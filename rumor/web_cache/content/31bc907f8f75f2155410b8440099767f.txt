Outbreak linked to infectious traveler
--------------------------------------

Among Colorado’s cases, 10 are associated with an out-of-state traveler who flew while infectious. Four were passengers on the Turkish Airlines flight with the index case, five were at Denver International Airport during the exposure period, and one is a contact of a secondary case.
