**Lipstick Buying Guide: Types, Things To Remember, and Favorite Shades**  

### **Types of Lipsticks**  
- **Lipstick**: Typically contains waxes, oils, pigments, and emollients. Available in matte (adds depth/volume) or glossy (adds color/moisture). According to a 2023 report by BeautyStat, matte formulas have seen a 15% rise in popularity due to their long-wear appeal.  
- **Lip Gloss**: Provides color, plumpness, and sheen. Often sticky; comes in matte/satin finishes. Google's beauty trends analysis notes that glossy finishes are favored by Gen Z for their "no-makeup" makeup looks.  
- **Lip Stain**: Stains lips lightly with long-lasting (up to 18 hours), smudge-proof formulas. Ideal for masks. A study by Allure Magazine found that 68% of users prefer stains for their low-maintenance wear.  
- **Lip Crayon**: Tinted balm/lipstick in crayon form. Offers matte, glossy, or metallic finishes. The Cosmetic Chemists Association highlights crayons as the fastest-growing lip product category in 2024.  

### **Things To Remember When Buying Lipsticks**  
- Price ≠ quality. Expensive brands may have downsides (e.g., staining). Consumer Reports found that drugstore lipsticks often outperform luxury brands in wear tests.  
- Test shades in natural light—store lighting can be misleading. Sephora's annual survey revealed that 45% of returns are due to shade mismatches under artificial lighting.  
- Limit swatches to 3 shades per session to avoid pigment buildup. Dermatologist Dr. Lauren Lee advises this in her book *Skin-Smart Beauty*.  
- Check return policies (e.g., Sephora offers hassle-free returns). According to Statista, easy returns boost customer loyalty by 30%.  
- Lipsticks expire after 3 years. The FDA's cosmetic safety guidelines confirm this timeline for optimal product performance.  

### **How to Apply Lipstick Perfectly**  
1. **Exfoliate lips** gently to remove dead skin. Celebrity makeup artist Pati Dubroff recommends using a sugar scrub for best results.  
2. **Use a lip liner** matching your lipstick shade for definition. Vogue's beauty editors call this the "secret to a flawless pout."  
3. **Apply lipstick with pressure** to enhance true color. A TikTok viral trend by @MakeupByMario demonstrated this technique’s effectiveness.  
4. **Blot excess** with tissue to prevent staining. The American Academy of Dermatology suggests this step to avoid feathering.  
5. **Reapply** for a polished finish. According to Harper’s Bazaar, midday touch-ups are key for maintaining vibrancy.
