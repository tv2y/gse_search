DIY love letter ideas.
----------------------

These DIY love letter ideas will help you find new ways to add a personal touch to your love letters. Surprise your significant other with something unexpected and thoughtful with these love letter ideas.

**A treasure box.**

If you’ve got a lot of time on your hands, then this DIY love letter idea is worth a try. Make a box out of cardboard with compartments or drawers. Each compartment will hold a letter. For example, if your anniversary is due in two weeks, consider making a box with 14 compartments to hold the same number of letters. Or you could just make a box with a few compartments. There’s no right or wrong way to go about this.

**A mini album.**

Make reprints of your favorite photos of you and your partner. Compile them in an album, where each photo opens up to reveal a short love letter. Consider including a QR code that leads to a shared online album where your partner can access a curated photo collection.

**Add it to a care package.**

Fill a box with your partner’s favorite things, whether it’s food or art materials. Then, make a pocket on the inside of the front panel so you can insert your love letter.

**A grand pop-up.**

Make an “explosion” box using this template from Jennifer Maker. What makes this DIY love letter idea so great is that, while it can be a bit time-consuming, it gives you plenty of room to express yourself. The box includes several pockets where you can stash away notes, letters, and trinkets. Plus, the middle part is perfect if you want to include a sweet gift. It’s like an advent calendar in box form.

Short love letter ideas.
------------------------

Writing a short love letter is a tricky challenge. How will you manage to fit all your ideas and feelings into a short letter? These short love letter ideas will help you write short yet memorable and impactful love letters.

**Come up with points.**

Brainstorm for some points you want to write about or include in your short love letter. These can include what you love about them or specific things they do that make you feel loved.

**Focus on what you feel.**

What do you feel when you think about your partner? Write about that. Use analogies or metaphors to help flesh out your ideas.

**Change your perspective.**

Writing a love letter, even a short one, can be intimidating. This is why reframing your letter and how you approach writing it helps. For instance, think of what you would include in a birthday or thank-you card for your significant other. Usually, you’ll be expressing your gratitude or respect for them. Now, in your short love letter, expand on those ideas and include the characteristics that you appreciate about your partner, for example.

**Find inspiration from your photos or mementos.**

Go back through your photos and the mementos you’ve collected over the years. This exercise can give you some much-needed inspiration to get started. Make a bullet list of what you love, appreciate, or are grateful for about your significant other.

Card love letter design ideas.
------------------------------

Make visually appealing love letters with these card love letter design ideas. These are perfect for larger celebrations like weddings or birthdays or for when you want to make each day more magical for your significant other.

**Make a flipbook.**

Create a card with mini-pages, with each page containing a new message, illustration, or sweet mementos like personalized photo collages or notes. Think you have a lot more to say? Why not make a companion video to your flipbook? Add a QR code to the card that links to a series of short video messages.

**The big reveal.**

Design a card with a “window” on the front. Using a piece of cardstock and a utility knife, carefully cut out the front in whatever shape you want to reveal a part of your letter or illustration inside the card. For an interesting effect, add clear cellophane or colored semi-transparent paper to create a stained glass look.

**A double-sided card.**

This is similar to conventional cards except that, with this one, you’ll be adding a custom work like your original illustration or a dramatic black and white photo collage to one side of the card, opposite your love letter.

**A work of art.**

Make a custom painting or illustration depicting one of your favorite moments with your partner. This serves as the card’s cover. On the back of the card, write your love letter.

Unique love letter ideas.
-------------------------

Want to make an unforgettable gesture? These unique love letter ideas will show you fresh and exciting ways to express your feelings.

**Make a series of letters.**

Remember the “Open When…” trend almost a decade ago? This might be a good time to revive that. Write letters that your significant other can open during specific periods. For example, on one envelope, write “Open When…you need some much-needed encouragement,” while another envelope can have something like “Open When…you’re feeling sad.” Other ideas include:

*   Open when we’re having a fight
*   Open when you need a quick pick-me-up
*   Open when you want to be reminded of a happy memory

**Gamify it.**

Turn your love letter into a puzzle or include a mini-game. This not only makes it unique but also engaging. Plus, the recipient will acknowledge the time and effort you put into making such a cool and sweet love letter.

**Embroider it.**

Transform your love letter into something truly unique and special with this unique love letter idea. Apply embroidery to the actual letter or make a fabric envelope to serve as a canvas for your embroidery work. The DMC website is a great place to start, as it offers tons of free patterns. Or use free coloring pages if you need quick patterns.

**Jazz up the envelope.**

The envelope is still a part of your letter, so it’s important to also pay attention to it. Make your letter stand out by customizing your envelope. Take a blank envelope and decorate it to reflect your or your partner’s personal style. For example, if they like arts and crafts, include an illustration featuring one of their hobbies.

You can also add a wax seal and botanical accents like herb sprigs, pressed flowers, or dried leaves. Or you could go the minimalist chic route by picking a solid-colored envelope and adding a dainty watercolor flower on the flap.

Tips to make memorable love letters.
------------------------------------

How do you write a love letter that effectively conveys your thoughts and feelings? Follow these tips to write love letters that your partner will want to read all over again.

*   **Exercise.** Your writing muscles, that is. Start writing down your thoughts. Don’t stop and make edits. Once you have the initial draft, start a fresh letter using your draft as inspiration.

*   **Keep it simple.** What’s important is being genuine. Being verbose can muddle your message. Even if you think it’s weird or corny, your partner will surely appreciate all the time and effort you’ve put into writing a heartfelt letter.

*   **Dig deep.** Think about why you love your partner or what made you fall in love with them. Then, go deeper. For example, you want to say that your partner is kind. What makes them kind, and in what specific instances did this trait make an impact on you?

*   **Start small.** Write love notes instead of letters. Once you get the hang of it, start writing longer notes, then short letters, and then transition to writing longer love letters.
