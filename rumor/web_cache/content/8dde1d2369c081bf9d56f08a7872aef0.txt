After more than a decade, it is rumored that Manchester United will visit Hong Kong at the end of May, possibly showcasing their skills at Kai Tak Sports Park.
===============================================================================================================================================================

AASTOCKS·Feb 19 05:42

MANU+0.33%

Manchester United, a Premier League club, has performed poorly this season, but rumors suggest that there will be a tour in Asia and the USA this summer, with hopes of returning to Hong Kong at the end of May after more than ten years, becoming the first European club to perform at the Kai Tak main venue.

According to foreign sources, Manchester United intends to tour Asia after the end of the season, with plans including Malaysia and Hong Kong, which, if successful, is expected to bring about 10 million USD in revenue to the club. Manchester United has previously visited Hong Kong multiple times for exhibition matches, the last being in 2013 when they won against Kitchee 5:2.

In addition to the Asia tour, foreign sources indicate that Manchester United intends to visit the USA at the end of July to participate in summer series matches against Bournemouth, West Ham, and Everton, but all of the above news has not been officially confirmed.
