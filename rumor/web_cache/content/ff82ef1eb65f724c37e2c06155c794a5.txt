Do you know?

That Steve Jobs' biological father was a Syrian Arab.

Here are the key facts:

Name: Steve Jobs’ biological father was Abdulfattah Jandali.

Origin: Abdulfattah Jandali was born in Homs, Syria.

He came to the United States as a student and later became a political science professor and businessman.

Steve Jobs was born in 1955 to Abdulfattah Jandali and Joanne Schieble, an American woman of Swiss and German descent.

Due to various personal and social circumstances, Jobs was placed for adoption shortly after birth and was adopted by Paul and Clara Jobs, who raised him.

So, while Steve Jobs was raised in an American family and identified culturally as American, his biological father was indeed Syrian Arab.
