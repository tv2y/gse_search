### FAQs:
1. **How long can SD cards store data?**  
   - Up to 5 years; recommended to offload data regularly.  

2. **Can I reuse an SD card?**  
   - Yes, after offloading and reformatting.  

3. **What can I store on an SD card?**  
   - Photos, videos, files; larger cards (e.g., 1TB) for heavy data.  

4. **Choosing the right SD card:**  
   - Capacity (128GB+ for 4K videos), speed class (UHS-II), compatibility, and brand reliability.  

5. **Lifespan factors:**  
   - Usage frequency, storage conditions, and quality.  

6. **Video storage on 32GB card:**
