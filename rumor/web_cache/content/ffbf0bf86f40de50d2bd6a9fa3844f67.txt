China wins men's 4x100m relay gold at Hangzhou Asiad
====================================================

 Xinhua | Updated: 2023-10-03 22:12 

Share

 Chen Guanfeng, Xie Zhenye, Yan Haibin and Chen Jiapeng of Team China celebrate after the Men's 4 x 100m Relay Final of Athletics at the 19th Asian Games in Hangzhou, East China's Zhejiang Province, Oct 3, 2023. [Photo by Wei Xiaohao/chinadaily.com.cn] 

HANGZHOU -- China clinched the gold medal in the men's 4x100m relay at the Hangzhou Asian Games on Tuesday.

Chen Guanfeng, Xie Zhenye, Yan Haibin and Chen Jiapeng helped China achieve a season-best 38.29 seconds to stand atop the podium.

Japan took silver in 38.44, while the bronze went to South Korea in 38.74.
