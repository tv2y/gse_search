What is my hairbrush made of?

+

Here is the list of the different materials that make up a Bonne Brosse:

*   Our **handle** is made of biodegradable cellulose acetate and is produced in France. This sustainable and responsible material is biodegradable, and not derived from petrochemicals. It is composed of 70% plant fibers and is sourced in the Jura. 

*   **Boar bristles**. Entirely natural organic fibers, rich in keratin, imported from Southeast Asia. Composed of 100% keratin, the fibers of our boar hair brushes are particularly hypoallergenic, resistant, and prevent static electricity. There is currently no synthetic equivalent to boar bristle. We have looked for one, and continue to work with manufacturers in the hope of one day finding an equivalent (as for makeup brushes ten years ago) but there is none to date. This bristle, made of 100% keratin, is the only one that has the ability to capture sebum at the hair root to distribute it throughout the hair. 

*   **Nylon fibers**. Precious nylon fibers, among the finest on the market, French and vegan. On our N.03 and N.04 models, they are topped with small epoxy beads, applied by hand, to make the scalp massage even more pleasant and effective. 

*   **The natural rubber cushion**. The comfort and quality of hair brushing are closely linked to the flexibility and bounce, called "shore", of the rubber that supports the hairbrush bristles. We have therefore chosen the softest shore quality. The one that offers the perfect bounce of our brushes and brushing without breakage, ultra comfortable. And finally, which perfectly fits the curves of the head and neck.

Our tips for taking care of your brush

+

A Bonne Brosse is a lifelong investment. You should take care of it, just as it takes care of you. Here is a small list of tips to follow:

If you have a hairbrush with boar bristles, we recommend not immersing it in water or using soap on it (with the exception of the N.04 and N.07, made entirely of nylon, which tolerate water very well) but you can use it on damp hair. Our cleaning brush is now available to help you maintain your Bonne Brosse as it deserves. Find all our care tips here.

If your hairbrush has an odor, it most likely comes from moisture on the boar bristles. Animal hair is hydrophobic, meaning it resists water thanks to its physical properties, but this resistance can sometimes release a slight odor. That said, no worries: the smell should fade after a few uses. You can continue to use your brush on damp hair. We do recommend towel-drying your hair well beforehand in order to: detangle gently, since the wetter the hair, the more elastic it becomes, which increases the risk of breakage, so it’s better to detangle when it is well towel-dried; and avoid soaking the boar bristles. After using on damp hair, make sure to dry your brush thoroughly, ideally with the bristles facing downward.

We also advise against using our brushes that contain nylon fibers with a high-powered hairdryer or any other heat source. For blow-drying, choose our round brush N.05, made with 100% boar bristles!

What to do in case of lice?

+

If this happens, remove the hair from the boar bristle brush, clean it following our advice listed here, place it in a freezer bag, then in the freezer for 48 hours.

Why are the boar bristles on my brush not all the same shape?

+

Our hairbrushes are made of high-quality natural bristles, but they are therefore of heterogeneous sizes and strength. This is also why they hug each hair strand by strand. The nylon fibers we have selected are very flexible to provide a very gentle massage, absolutely non-irritating and at the same time very effective on the microcirculation of the scalp. And this is what makes our thinning technique so effective when brushing!

The fact that some finer boar bristles can therefore move slightly over time does not affect the quality of the brushing in any way but is a sign that the hairbrush is alive. Especially since our ultra-soft rubber cushion allows each pin to orient itself during brushing to fit the shape of your skull. It is therefore rather a sign of effectiveness... The natural fibers should gradually regain their shape and you can gently guide them with your fingers to restore their straightness.

Why does my hair feel static after brushing it?

+

Depending on your hair type, the friction of our exceptionally tight fibers, which grip each hair to condition it, can create an electric effect. The more carefully and carefully your hair is brushed, the less this effect should appear. In the meantime, our advice is to lightly dampen your hands and run it through your hair right after brushing to immediately eliminate this effect. This way, you can continue to enjoy the benefits of your hairbrush without the static effect.

Where do our boar bristles come from?

+

Regarding the origin of the boar bristles, it was important for us not to work with intensive industrial breeding sectors. We chose an "upcycling" sector, as is the case for certain leathers.

Our partner recovers the hair from animals used primarily for food production, in agricultural farms in Southeast Asia. These are not animals killed for their hair, which is very important to us.

It is nevertheless an animal hair, which is why, to ensure each consumer has a hairbrush model that suits them, we have also developed our Nylon Brushes: the vegan hairbrushes, the N.04 and the N.07 to ensure that each consumer can choose a model that suits them.

### What is the best hairbrush?

It all depends on your hair type. So don’t hesitate to perform a hair diagnosis to select a model tailored to you. For example, a boar bristle hairbrush is ideal for fine hair, while a 100% nylon brush will be much more effective for thick hair.

### How to brush your hair properly

First and foremost, by doing it often enough—at least three times a week and twice a day. Make sure, during each brushing session, to start at the temples and work your way towards the back of the head, then restart from the top of the head and move towards the nape. And all of this with your head down to properly distribute the natural oils!
