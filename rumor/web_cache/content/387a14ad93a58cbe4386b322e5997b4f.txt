Clinton is in Nevada Tuesday. (Photo by Kevin Hagen/Getty Images) 

By Anne Gearan

LAS VEGAS -- Hillary Rodham Clinton will say Tuesday that any overhaul of the immigration system must include a way for undocumented immigrants to gain full citizenship.

"The standard for a true solution is nothing less than a full and equal path to citizenship," Clinton's campaign said in a preview of remarks she is expected to give during a visit to a largely Hispanic high school here.
