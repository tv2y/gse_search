### Abstract

The United Arab Emirates (UAE) is leveraging artificial intelligence (AI) to revolutionize its logistics and supply chain sector. This research provides a comprehensive examination of how AI technologies are being integrated into UAE logistics, through qualitative case studies of leading organizations including DP World, ADNOC, Emirates SkyCargo, and Aramex. The study situates these developments in the context of the UAE's national AI strategy and global logistics trends. Key findings indicate that AI adoption has led to significant improvements in operational efficiency, predictive capabilities, cost reduction, and sustainability in logistics operations. For example, AI-driven systems at DP World's Jebel Ali Port eliminated hundreds of thousands of unnecessary container moves and improved truck turnaround times by 20%, while route optimization algorithms at Aramex are enabling faster deliveries with lower fuel consumption. The research also identifies challenges such as high implementation costs, data integration issues, and skill gaps, and discusses how companies and policymakers are addressing these barriers. Overall, the paper highlights that AI is transforming UAE's logistics sector into a smarter, more resilient, and globally competitive hub. It concludes with practical implications for industry managers and policymakers, and suggests directions for future research on AI-driven logistics innovation in the region.

**Keywords:** Artificial Intelligence (AI), Logistics Optimization, Digital Transformation, Demand Forecasting, Thematic Analysis
