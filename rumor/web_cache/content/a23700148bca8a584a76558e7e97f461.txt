Revolutionizing public safety: the power of drones in modern security
=====================================================================

Unmanned aerial vehicles (UAVs) or drones are revolutionizing public safety as we know it. With their incredible versatility, these cutting-edge technologies are opening up a world of possibilities for enhancing safety in our communities and supporting the efforts of law enforcement and other public safety agencies. From search and rescue missions to crime investigations, SWAT operations, and firefighting efforts, these adaptable devices are proving invaluable in a wide range of scenarios.

We will talk about how UAVs are changing the approach to public order and security in the modern world. In this article, we will look at the areas of application of drones, their advantages and use cases by security services and law enforcement agencies.

What you need to know about drones for Public Safety

What are public safety drones?

Public safety drones are high-tech unmanned aerial vehicles that help law enforcement agencies, rescuers, and other city services perform their duties more efficiently. These compact and maneuverable aircraft are equipped with advanced technology, enabling them to autonomously carry out a wide range of tasks.

Equipped with high-resolution cameras, thermal imagers and other sensors, these drones become the eyes and ears of security services, providing them with critical information in real time.

Evolution of drone technology in security

Ten years ago, the use of unmanned aerial vehicle technology in law enforcement agencies was a novelty introduced only by some states, for example, the UAE. 

_Experts believe that the era of modern non-military drones began in 2006. It was this year that the US Federal Aviation Administration approved the flights of small custom drones._

Today, police departments worldwide have joined the drone revolution, employing unmanned aerial vehicles (UAVs) to perform a variety of critical tasks: from disaster response and crowd monitoring to firefighting operations.

Now, aerial monitoring to ensure public safety is not only individual drones, but also entire ecosystems, including ground control stations, data processing systems and integration with other elements of the smart city. 

Research shows that the market for drones for public safety is growing, and this trend will continue for a long time: 

_The global public safety drone market is projected to almost double in value by 2028, according to data by ResearchandMarkets. The industry is projected to be worth US_ _$2 billion_ _by 2028, up from $1.1 billion in 2023. This represents a combined annual growth rate (CAGR) of 13 per cent._

In the following sections, we will take a detailed look at the specific applications of drones in the field of public safety and the benefits they bring to cities and their residents.

10 Key applications of Drones in public safety 

Drones for emergency response and disaster response

Preventive measures are key to minimizing the impact of potential disasters, especially those affecting critical infrastructure. Autonomous unmanned aerial vehicles can conduct regular inspections of bridges, power lines, and gas pipelines, collecting vital data on wear and tear. This information is often crucial for predicting and preventing potential catastrophes. Drones can also map risk zones, helping emergency responders identify potential hazards and prepare comprehensive response plans.

During natural disasters, UAVs can assist with a key challenge faced by emergency response services – providing a complete picture of the emergency situation. They ensure continuous monitoring by transmitting real-time data. The accuracy of this data depends on the quality of the drone's equipment – cameras and sensors.

_At Microavia Drone-in-a-Box, we equip our drones with high-quality cameras that are resistant to severe weather conditions.They are equipped with 30x optical zoom and thermal imaging capabilities, providing users with accurate data even in poor visibility conditions. These features allow rescuers to quickly understand how to act, assess the scale of the emergency, and evaluate the level of damage._

Drones also aid in disaster recovery efforts. They help rescue teams monitor situations in hard-to-reach areas, conduct search and rescue operations efficiently, and analyze the aftermath.

Source: National Disaster Recovery Framework

Support for tactical operations

In tactical scenarios, the array of potential challenges is vast and unpredictable. That's where unmanned aerial vehicles (UAVs) come into play, revolutionizing situational awareness with their comprehensive insights. From identifying access points like doors and windows to detecting potential hazards such as animals or other individuals within the vicinity, UAVs provide invaluable intelligence to law enforcement officers on the ground.

With this critical information at their disposal, officers can navigate and approach buildings with heightened efficiency and safety. Moreover, as operations progress to the building entry phase, drone surveillance continues to prove their worth. Positioned overhead, these drones offer commanders a bird's-eye view of the entire scene, enabling real-time assessment and informed decision-making. Should the suspect attempt to flee, UAVs swiftly switch roles, seamlessly transitioning to tracking mode and ensuring a secure apprehension.

Drones for search and rescue operations

A standout advantage of unmanned aerial vehicles lies in their capacity to slash the response time of first responders dramatically. Through drone surveillance and reconnaissance, emergencies can be swiftly identified, offering rapid insights into the severity of the situation, assessing damages, and identifying potential hazards.

In the event of natural disasters such as floods, fires, or earthquakes, drones soar into action, capturing aerial imagery, surveying affected areas, and monitoring evolving conditions on the ground. This critical data serves as a linchpin for coordinating emergency services, optimizing resource distribution, and delivering effective aid to those in need.

Drones for law enforcement agencies and smart city technologies 

Law enforcement agencies are increasingly using drones in their operations, expanding their capabilities across various scenarios. For instance, forensic photogrammetry: in crime investigations, drones capture aerial photographs for forensic analysis, providing a comprehensive overview of crime scenes and aiding in evidence collection. Additionally, UAVs equipped with high-resolution cameras and thermal imaging conduct covert surveillance, tracking suspicious activities and suspects.

The smart city concept also incorporates the use of drones. Beyond monitoring the condition of buildings, railways, and other urban infrastructure, they are also employed in unconventional tasks. For example, drones equipped with specialized sensors can monitor air quality, detect pollution sources, and assess environmental impact in urban areas.

Drones that are used for firefighting

Collaborative efforts between law enforcement and firefighting units are not only standard practice but are actively promoted in the advancement of drone initiatives. Within structural firefighting, a range of innovative techniques are employed, empowered by drone technology:

*   Command and control enhancement. Drones furnish incident commanders with real-time, accurate data, facilitating informed decision-making.

*   Swift 360-degree building assessments. Drones swiftly survey structures from all angles, providing vital insights into potential hazards and safe entry points.

*   Smoke patch analysis. Drones aid in pinpointing the location and extent of smoke patches, guiding firefighting efforts with precision.

*   Fire movement prediction. By analyzing fire direction, drones optimize resource allocation, ensuring the most efficient response.

*   Crew tracking optimization. Drones enable seamless monitoring of firefighting teams, enhancing coordination and safety on the ground.

UAVs for border protection and monitoring, as well as coastal patrol

For border security, drones provide continuous surveillance of remote or hard-to-reach areas, allowing early detection of unauthorized border crossings. They support ground patrols by identifying potential threats and tracking environmental changes that may affect border security.

UAVs for traffic control and crowd control

For traffic control, drones transmit traffic information to dispatch centers, help redirect traffic flows during accidents or road closures, and assist in accident investigations. They can also monitor parking lots and identify available spaces, improving urban mobility.

In crowd management, drones provide support by monitoring mass gatherings, festivals, and sporting events. They can detect potential security threats or disturbances, direct emergency responders to specific crowd locations, and, if necessary, assist in coordinating evacuation procedures.

UAVs for correctional institutions and other public places 

In prisons, UAVs conduct perimeter surveillance to prevent escapes, monitor inmate activities outdoors, detect and intercept contraband, and provide surveillance during inmate transportation.

For other public spaces such as parks, shopping centers, and government buildings, drones provide crowd monitoring and management. They allow for quick incident response, deter criminal activity through visible presence, and support evacuation. 

UAVs to ensure safety on roads, railways

In road safety, unmanned aerial vehicles monitor traffic flows and congestion, quickly assess accident sites, inspect road conditions, and help coordinate emergency responses.

According to KRRI data, the defect detection rate by drones on railway tracks in South Korea exceeds 85%, with image recognition accuracy of over 90%.

In the railway sector, drones provide regular inspection of tracks, bridges, and tunnels, identify obstacles or damage on tracks, and assist in maintenance and repair. The autonomous operation of drones, such as Microavia's, allows for regular scheduled inspections without human involvement. High-resolution cameras with thermal imaging and built-in AI enable detailed examination of railway infrastructure: detecting overgrowth on tracks, finding breakdowns, and signaling the need for specialist intervention.

Drones for airport security

For airport security, UAVs monitor perimeter fences for breaches, inspect runways for debris or damage, help prevent bird strikes, and provide aerial support during emergencies.

Benefits of Implementing Drone Technology for Public Safety 

Enhanced situational awareness 

High-resolution cameras and thermal imaging capabilities allow drones to capture detailed images in various conditions, including low-light environments. This enhanced visual information enables public safety personnel to make more informed decisions, identify potential threats, and allocate resources more effectively.

For example, during large-scale events or emergencies, drones can quickly survey entire areas, providing real-time information about crowd movements, traffic flows, and potential hazards. This level of situational awareness was previously only possible with expensive helicopters, but drones now offer a more cost-effective and flexible solution.

Rapid response capabilities 

The ability of drones to quickly reach locations that may be difficult or dangerous for ground personnel to access makes them invaluable in emergency situations. In search and rescue operations, drones can rapidly cover large areas, using thermal cameras to locate missing persons even in challenging terrain or weather conditions. For firefighting, drones can be used to assess the extent of fires, identify hotspots, and guide firefighters to the most critical areas while keeping humans out of immediate danger.

The speed at which drones can be deployed also makes them indispensable in time-critical situations. For instance, in active shooter or hostage scenarios, they can provide immediate aerial reconnaissance, helping law enforcement respond more effectively and safely.

Improved data collection and analysis

Drones are not just flying cameras; they are sophisticated data collection platforms. Equipped with various sensors and imaging technologies, drones can gather vast amounts of information that can be analyzed to improve public safety strategies.

For example, drones used for traffic management can collect data on traffic flows, accident locations, and infrastructure conditions. 

This information can be analyzed to implement more effective traffic control measures, plan road improvements, and reduce the accident rates.

In environmental monitoring, drones can gather data on air quality, water pollution, and changes in vegetation, helping authorities identify and address potential health and safety risks more quickly.

Furthermore, the data collected by drones can be integrated with artificial intelligence and machine learning systems for real-time analysis. This integration allows for predictive policing, more efficient resource allocation, and improved emergency response planning.

Minimizing risks to human personnel

Perhaps one of the most significant advantages of drone technology in public safety is its ability to minimize risks to personnel. By sending drones into potentially dangerous situations, public safety services can gather crucial information without putting human lives at risk.

_According to statistics_ _, in the United States, over a thousand police officers die in the line of duty, in India – 1,731, and in Brazil – 5,804 police officers._

During natural disasters, drones can survey affected areas, locate survivors, and guide rescue teams while keeping rescuers safe from unstable structures and other hazards.

Law enforcement uses drones to monitor high-risk situations such as armed confrontations or drug raids, providing officers with vital information before they enter dangerous scenarios. This not only protects officers but can also lead to more peaceful resolutions of potentially dangerous situations.

Cost-effectiveness of drones and resource optimization to ensure public order 

In the traditional realm of law enforcement, officers rely on periodic patrols to keep neighborhoods safe and deter illegal activity. But this approach comes with its challenges: officers must physically traverse the area, either on foot or in vehicles, to maintain visibility and monitor for any signs of wrongdoing. This not only incurs high costs but also limits the scope of surveillance.

Saving tens of thousands of dollars using drones compared to agency helicopters. In many situations, drones are more versatile and effective than helicopters. The hourly cost of operating drones is significantly lower than traditional helicopters and will yield a significant return on our investment.

The development and proliferation of unmanned aerial vehicle (UAV) technology makes air support much more accessible and acceptable to public safety agencies. Compared to manned aircraft, UAVs are much less expensive to acquire and operate:

*   Acquisition costs: A new helicopter can cost millions of dollars, while professional-quality drones equipped with optical and thermal imaging cameras are considerably less expensive.

*   Training costs: Pilots of manned aircraft require extensive training, certification, insurance, and qualifications to perform police missions.

*   Maintenance Costs: Manned aircraft can require expensive maintenance, while unmanned aircraft usually only require software updates and replacement of minor parts, which can usually be performed by trained pilots/operators.

Given the cost savings, unmanned aircraft may be a viable option for agencies that want air support but cannot afford to deploy traditional manned aircraft.

Dubai's drone-powered smart city initiatives 

The smart city concept in Dubai is changing perceptions even about familiar objects. For instance, at the World Police Summit, a drone traffic light was presented. These unmanned aircraft can view, assess, and monitor accidents and search-and-rescue situations, minimizing the exposure of personnel and contractors to high-risk scenarios. This enhanced surveillance has strengthened maritime security for international vessels and cargo.
