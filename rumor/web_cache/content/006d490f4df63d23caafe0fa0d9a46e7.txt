Title: Sen. Sampson Statement on FEMA Payments to NYC Hotels Housing Illegal Immigrants

URL Source: https://www.ctsenaterepublicans.com/2025/02/sen-sampson-statement-on-fema-payments-to-nyc-hotels-housing-illegal-immigrants/

Published Time: 2025-02-10T20:12:39+00:00

Markdown Content:
**February 10, 2025**

Sen. Rob Sampson issued the following statement after news broke that **the Federal Emergency Management Agency (FEMA) sent payments of $59 million last week to luxury hotels in New York City to house illegal immigrants**.

“Was any FEMA money sent to _Connecticut_ for similar purposes?

It’s a simple question.

Gov. Lamont should get an answer, and he should inform state taxpayers.

He is already on record stating that all illegal aliens are ‘welcome’ in Connecticut.

Even the most violent.

So, how much is being spent to house them in Connecticut?

It’s our money. Tell us.”
