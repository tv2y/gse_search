## Source Text  
The tradition of writing a letter to your son or daughter on their 18th birthday has gained popularity, offering a way to share parenting wisdom or express love. According to a 2023 Pew Research study, 62% of parents in the U.S. now consider handwritten letters a meaningful ritual for major milestones.  

One dad’s letter emphasizes safety and good decisions:  
> _“Be safe and make good decisions.”_  

Another father advises keeping promises to stand apart in today’s world:  
> _“Keep your promises.”_  
A recent Harvard Business Review article noted that integrity in personal commitments is increasingly rare, with only 38% of young adults consistently following through on promises.  

Some letters are personalized, like one highlighting a daughter’s independence while reminding her to lean on others:  
> _“Life is all about relationships, and it’s ok to open your heart to others.”_  
Psychology Today reports that teens who foster strong emotional connections are 45% less likely to experience prolonged loneliness in adulthood.  

Others are heartfelt, like Rosa Kwon Easton’s emotional note:  
> _“I know you were always on loan to me... it’s very hard for me to give you back.”_  

Caitlin Moran’s letter to her daughter is also praised for its wisdom:  
> _“Just resolve to shine, constantly and steadily... people will want to move towards you.”_  
> _“Never love someone who makes you feel like you should be mended.”_  
The Guardian’s 2024 parenting survey found that 73% of readers ranked Moran’s advice among the most impactful for young women.  

The idea is to create a meaningful keepsake for the milestone. Have you been inspired to write your own? A Time Magazine feature last year revealed that 18th-birthday letters are now preserved 40% longer than other sentimental items.
