A mother in the Franklin Park Community Connection group seeks advice on finding the right foundation and concealer match for her skin tone and type. She hasn’t worn makeup since her son was born 2.5 years ago and is frustrated after receiving unhelpful guidance at Ulta and Sephora. She asks for recommendations on stores or methods to get proper assistance.  

Responses include:  
1. A former Ulta employee offers personalized help via message.  
2. Suggestions to visit a Mac counter for a makeup session and observe their techniques.  
3. A recommendation for Maybelline’s online color-matching tool.
