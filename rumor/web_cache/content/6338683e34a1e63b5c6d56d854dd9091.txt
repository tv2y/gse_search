Top GOP Presidential Front-Runner Donald J. Trump Doubles Down: Surveillance on Mosques, Database... I saw 'thousands and thousands' of muslims on 9/11 Cheering the Downing of our Twin Towers... Republican presidential candidate Donald Trump has shown he is not one to back down. He was questioned by ABC News George Stephanopoulos about his assertion that "thousands and thousands of people" cheered the collapse of the World Trade Center on Sept. 11 just across the river in New Jersey, Trump doubled down. "It did happen. I saw it. It was on television. I saw it," Trump said. Donald Trump claims that "thousands" of people in New Jersey "cheering" amid the September 11, 2001, terrorist attacks on US soil. "There were people that were cheering on the other side of New Jersey, where you have large Muslim populations. They were cheering as the World Trade Center came down," Trump said on Sunday. "I know it might be not politically correct for you to talk about it, but there were people cheering as that building came down — as those buildings came down. And that tells you something. It was well-covered at the time."
