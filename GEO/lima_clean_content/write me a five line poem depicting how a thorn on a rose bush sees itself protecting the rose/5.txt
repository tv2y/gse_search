**Poetry: Roses and Thorns**  

Roses, beauty in the garden’s bed,  
Soft petals, sweet fragrance, colors red.  
But hidden beneath the beauty fair,  
Are thorns that prick and cause despair.  

The thorns, a warning to the touch,  
A reminder of the pain too much.  
But without them, the rose would not be,  
Its beauty and its strength, set free.  

For every rose has its thorn,  
A symbol of the struggles born.  
But in the struggles, growth is found,  
And beauty’s true value is unwound.  

So, let us not just see the rose,  
But also, the thorn that it chose.  
For just like the rose and its thorn,  
We too, are both beauty and torn.
