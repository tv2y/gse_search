### The Best LCD/LED TV  

**Top Pick: Hisense U8QG Series**  
- **Screen Sizes:** 55", 65", 75", 85", 100"  
- **Key Features:**  
  - Mini-LED backlight with local dimming  
  - Quantum dots for enhanced color  
  - Supports HDR10, HDR10+, HLG, Dolby Vision  
  - 165Hz refresh rate, three HDMI 2.1 inputs  
  - Google TV smart platform  
  - Extremely bright (3,200 nits peak), excellent local dimming  
  - Great for gaming with low input lag and USB-C video input  

**Budget Pick: Hisense U75QG Series**  
- **Screen Sizes:** 55", 65", 75", 85", 100", 116"  
- **Key Features:**  
  - Mini-LED backlight with local dimming  
  - Quantum dots, supports all major HDR formats  
  - 165Hz refresh rate, four HDMI 2.1 inputs  
  - Google TV, bright (2,750 nits peak)  
  - Gaming-friendly with low input lag  

**Upgrade Pick: Samsung QN90F Series**  
- **Screen Sizes:** 43", 50", 55", 65", 75", 85", 98"  
- **Key Features:**  
  - Mini-LED backlight with local dimming  
  - Matte screen for reduced glare  
  - Supports HDR10, HDR10+, HLG (no Dolby Vision)  
  - 165Hz refresh rate, four HDMI 2.1 inputs  
  - Tizen Smart Hub, excellent out-of-the-box calibration  
  - Better viewing angles and reflection handling  

**Other Options:**  
- **TCL QM9K:** Best for performance in larger sizes (65"+, 4,000+ nits, Dolby Vision).  
- **Sony Bravia 9:** Premium accuracy and upscaling but expensive.  
- **Sony Bravia XR X90L:** Budget-friendly, highly accurate but less bright.  

**Notable Exclusions:**  
- 8K TVs (insufficient content).  
- TCL QM8K/QM7K (higher input lag, fewer features than Hisense).  
- LG QNED (IPS panel trade-offs).
