Hillary Clinton’s Pneumonia Forces Her to Leave 9/11 Event Early
================================================================

Sep 11, 2016 | Infectious Diseases, Other Infections, Testing |

> Hillary Clinton has been ill with pneumonia, her doctor revealed in a Sunday evening statement that shook up the presidential race.
> 
> 
> Dr Lisa R. Bardack released a statement via the campaign revealing the diagnosis on Sunday evening, hours after the Democratic presidential nominee was seen stumbling on video after exiting a 9/11 memorial event early.
> 
> 
> Bardack said Clinton had an examination at her home in Chappaqua after the incident, which was a result of dehydration and overheating. She said the candidate is “re-hydrated and recovering nicely.”
