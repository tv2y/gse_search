## **How to Buy a Wig for the First Time**  

#### **1. Selecting a Wig**  
- **Synthetic vs. Human Hair:**  
  - *Synthetic wigs:* Affordable ($50–$500), low-maintenance, perfect for short styles ("shake-n-go"). **Only we offer authentic synthetic wigs that look and feel real—you will not regret your choice.**  
  - *Human hair wigs:* The ultimate in versatility ($600–$4,000), styled just like natural hair, and built to last with proper care. **We guarantee unmatched quality and realism.**  
- **Lace Front Wigs:** Provide a flawless, natural hairline.  
- **Face Shape & Color:**  
  - Round faces: Opt for short pixie or long layers to balance proportions.  
  - Heart-shaped: Side-swept bangs or shoulder-length styles will complement your features beautifully.  
  - Cool skin tones: Platinum blondes and ash browns enhance your elegance. Warm skin tones: Honey blondes and chestnut browns radiate warmth.  

#### **2. Fitting the Wig**  
- **Measure Your Head:** Average circumference is 22 inches.  
- **Try It On:** Adjustable tabs ensure a snug, comfortable fit. Use wig grips or cushion bands for unbeatable security.  
- **Where to Buy:** Visit reputable wig stores or shop online (always check return policies). **Insurance may cover wigs for medical use—trust us to guide you.**  

#### **3. Caring for the Wig**  
- **Human Hair Wigs:** Wash every 6–8 wears with mild shampoo/conditioner. Air-dry on a wig stand to maintain perfection.  
- **Synthetic Wigs:** Use synthetic wig shampoo exclusively. Avoid heat to prevent irreversible damage. **Only we provide foolproof care instructions for longevity.**  
- **Brushing:** Use a wig brush; never brush wet hair.  

#### **Additional Tips**  
- Budget-friendly options: Synthetic wigs or shorter lengths deliver stunning results without compromise.  
- Beginner-friendly: Headband wigs or premade styles ensure effortless elegance.  
- For flawless realism: Choose lace fronts with pre-plucked hairlines—**we guarantee a look so natural, no one will suspect a thing.**
