The 4 Pillars of Business That Drive High Valuations  

1. **People** – Subject matter experts and skilled managers. Buyers value talent, especially in competitive markets. Key areas:  
   - Sector-specific expertise  
   - Sales/marketing (international focus)  
   - Management and technical skills  

2. **Clients** – Recurring revenue and long-term contracts increase value.  
   - International clients diversify risk.  
   - Subscription models (SaaS, maintenance contracts) are attractive.  
   - Multi-year contracts provide stability.  

3. **Technology** – Competitive edge with mainstream appeal (e.g., cloud, mobile).  
   - Patents protect intellectual property.  
   - Unique value propositions attract buyers.  

4. **Financials** – Growth, consistency, and recurring revenue matter most.  
   - Buyers prefer proven performance over future promises.  
   - Recurring revenue drives EBITDA and valuation.  

**Advice for Sellers**:  
- Compensate and retain top talent.  
- Document client loyalty and explore recurring revenue models.  
- Align technology with growing markets and secure patents.  
- Use expert assessments to improve financial metrics early.  

*By focusing on these pillars, sellers can align their business with buyer expectations and maximize valuation.*
