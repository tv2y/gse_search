### 100 Heroic Character Traits  

1. **Courage:** The fortitude to confront trepidation and adversities with valor.  
2. **Honesty:** An unwavering allegiance to veracity and forthrightness in deeds and speech.  
3. **Integrity:** Steadfast adherence to an unshakable moral compass and ethos.  
4. **Loyalty:** Fervent devotion and fealty to an individual, cause, or collective.  
5. **Perseverance:** The tenacity to endure and surmount formidable trials.  
6. **Selflessness:** A magnanimous focus on the well-being of others, eschewing personal aggrandizement.  
7. **Empathy:** The profound capacity to vicariously experience and comprehend others' emotions.  
8. **Tenderhearted:** Authentic solicitude and benevolence toward others' tribulations.  
9. **Determination:** The indomitable resolve to attain one’s aspirations, irrespective of impediments.  
10. **Kindness:** A genial and altruistic disposition toward fellow beings.

### 100 Villainous Character Traits  

1. **Cruelty:** Deriving perverse gratification from inflicting anguish or affliction upon others.  
2. **Deception:** The artful dissemination of fallacies to achieve self-serving ends.  
3. **Manipulation:** Machiavellian exploitation of others for egocentric motives.  
4. **Greed:** An insatiable avarice for opulence and material accumulation.  
5. **Selfishness:** A myopic prioritization of one’s own desires over communal welfare.  
6. **Ruthlessness:** A merciless and pitiless pursuit of objectives, devoid of compassion.  
7. **Malice:** A malignant desire to perpetrate harm or inflict suffering.  
8. **Arrogance:** Overweening hubris and an inflated sense of self-importance.  
9. **Ambition at any cost:** The unbridled pursuit of aspirations, heedless of ethical ramifications.  
10. **Vindictiveness:** A rancorous proclivity to exact retribution upon perceived adversaries.  
(...)  

### Personality Traits vs. Character Traits  

- **Personality Traits:** Superficial behavioral manifestations (idiosyncrasies, charisma, demeanor).  
- **Character Traits:** Foundational ethical tenets (valor, probity, egocentrism).  

### Heroes vs. Antagonists vs. Villains  

- **Villains:** Actively malevolent, perpetrating harm for personal aggrandizement.  
- **Antagonists:** Oppose protagonists yet lack inherently nefarious intent.  
- **Heroes:** Distinguished by virtuous attributes, though nuanced with imperfections for verisimilitude.  

### Hack for Character Development  
Infuse antithetical traits to engender complexity:  
- Heroes with foibles (e.g., hubris).  
- Villains with redemptive virtues (e.g., fealty).  

*The most compelling characters obfuscate the dichotomy between heroism and villainy.*
