To choose a PC case that fits your components, focus on the following:

1. **Motherboard Size**: Cases support specific motherboard form factors (e.g., ATX, Micro-ATX, Mini-ITX). A smaller motherboard will fit in a larger case, but not vice versa.  
2. **GPU Clearance**: High-end GPUs (like RTX 3060/3080 or above) require wider cases. Check the case’s maximum GPU length.  
3. **Cooler Height**: Ensure the case accommodates your CPU cooler’s height, especially for tower air coolers.  
4. **DVD Drive Support**: If needed, select a case with 5.25-inch bays (e.g., Corsair Spec-01). Otherwise, consider an external drive.  
5. **Airflow & Aesthetics**: Prioritize cases with good airflow (mesh fronts) and design you like (RGB options available). Mid-tower cases (e.g., Cooler Master MB511) balance compatibility and space.  

**Pro Tip**: Smaller builds (Mini-ITX) require more research—watch reviews for fitment. For future-proofing, choose a case with room for upgrades.
