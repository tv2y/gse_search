Desenio is a Swedish e-commerce emporium offering an extensive assortment of prints, posters, and art prints imbued with quintessential Scandinavian design. Their curated collection features celebrated designs by renowned artists, photographers, and illustrators, alongside exclusive creations from their in-house Design Studio.  

They furnish prints and posters in a myriad of sizes, extending up to 39” x 59”, empowering patrons to craft bespoke gallery walls by harmonizing diverse designs, dimensions, and materials. Pre-assembled sets featuring frames and prints are available for effortless aesthetic cohesion.  

Prints are meticulously categorized for seamless navigation, encompassing perennial bestsellers and room-specific selections such as bedrooms, living rooms, kitchens, or nurseries. Superior quality is guaranteed with uncoated 100lb premium paper, boasting a matte surface for an opulent, glare-free finish, and FSC certification underscoring their commitment to sustainability.  

The store prides itself on impeccable color fidelity and expedited shipping. Decorative inspiration abounds in the gallery walls section, where in-house stylists present artfully curated ensembles.  

For any exigencies, a dedicated customer service team stands ready to assist. Desenio’s prints, posters, and art prints are conceived to elevate home decor with sophisticated, high-caliber wall art.
