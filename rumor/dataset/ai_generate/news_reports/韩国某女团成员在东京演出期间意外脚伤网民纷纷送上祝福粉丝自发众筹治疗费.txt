**Korean Pop Star Injured During Tokyo Concert, Fans Rally Support**  

**October 15, 2023, at 8:45 PM JST**  
**Tokyo Dome, Tokyo, Japan**  

During a highly anticipated performance by the popular South Korean girl group "Starlight" at Tokyo Dome on Sunday evening, member Kim Ji-hyun, 23, suffered a severe ankle injury after slipping off the stage. The incident occurred midway through the group's hit song "Galaxy Tears," when Kim lost her footing during a choreographed dance sequence and fell approximately 1.5 meters onto the platform below. Concert organizers immediately halted the show, and medical personnel rushed to her aid.  

Eyewitnesses reported that Kim appeared visibly in pain but attempted to reassure fans with a smile before being carried offstage. She was subsequently transported to Tokyo Central Hospital, where doctors diagnosed her with a fractured ankle and ligament damage. Surgery is scheduled for later this week, with an estimated recovery period of six to eight weeks.  

News of Kim's injury spread rapidly across social media, with the hashtag #GetWellSoonJiHyun trending globally on Twitter. Fans worldwide expressed their concern and flooded her Instagram page with messages of support. Within hours, a fan-led crowdfunding campaign was launched to cover her medical expenses, surpassing its initial $50,000 goal in less than 12 hours.  

Starlight's agency, StarWave Entertainment, released an official statement thanking fans for their outpouring of love and confirming that Kim will temporarily pause all activities to focus on her recovery. The group's remaining tour dates in Japan have been postponed indefinitely.  

Despite the setback, fans remain optimistic, organizing virtual gatherings and sending handmade gifts to Kim's hospital room. "We just want her to know she’s not alone," said Yuki Nakamura, a longtime fan who traveled from Osaka for the concert. "Starlight’s music has helped so many of us—now it’s our turn to support her."  

As of press time, the crowdfunding total has exceeded $120,000, with proceeds set to cover advanced rehabilitation treatments and charitable donations in Kim's name.
