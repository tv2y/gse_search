Title: More than 80% of transgender employees in the US have experienced discrimination or harassment at work

URL Source: https://williamsinstitute.law.ucla.edu/press/trans-workplace-press-release/

Markdown Content:
Most transgender employees (82%) have experienced discrimination or harassment at work at some point in their lives, according to a new report by the Williams Institute at UCLA School of Law. This includes being fired, not hired, not promoted, or being subjected to verbal, physical, or sexual harassment because of their sexual orientation or gender identity.

Transgender people report taking steps to mitigate discrimination and harassment at work. About one-third of transgender employees (36%) are not open about being transgender with their current supervisor. Additionally, most (71%) engage in covering behaviors, such as changing where, when, or how frequently they use the bathroom (39%) and altering their voice or mannerisms (46%) at work.

Researchers analyzed survey data collected in the summer of 2023 to examine the workplace experiences of transgender adults.

Transgender employees were twice as likely as cisgender LGBQ employees to have left a job (67% vs. 31%) because of how they were treated based on their LGBTQ status. Due to the workplace environment at their current job _,_ 29% of transgender employees have considered leaving. Of those, most (79%) had taken steps toward finding another job.

“Transgender people are a particularly vulnerable and marginalized group in the workplace,” said lead author Brad Sears, Founding Executive Director at the Williams Institute. “Many are not bringing their full selves to work and face unsupportive workplace environments, which makes them less likely to fully invest in their current employer and job.”

Additional Findings

**Demographics**

*   An estimated 71% of transgender adults in the workforce are under the age of 35, compared to 51% of cisgender LGBQ adults and 87% of nonbinary adults.
*   Half (50%) of transgender adults in the workforce are people of color.
*   Six in ten (60%) transgender workers are making less than $50,000 a year.

**Discrimination and Harassment**

*   70% of transgender employees reported experiencing at least one form of employment discrimination (including being fired, not hired, or not promoted) based on their sexual orientation or gender identity at some point in their lives.
*   About two-thirds (68%) of transgender employees reported experiencing at least one form of harassment at work due to their sexual orientation or gender identity at some point in their lives.

**Recent Experiences of Discrimination and Harassment**

*   Forty-seven percent of transgender employees reported workplace discrimination or harassment in the past year compared to 15% of cisgender LGBQ employees and 29% of nonbinary employees.

**Out at Work**

*   One-third (36%) of transgender employees reported that they are not open about being LGBTQ to their current supervisor, and approximately one in eight (13%) reported that they are not out to any of their co-workers.
*   Only four in 10 (39%) transgender employees reported that they were out to all their co-workers.
