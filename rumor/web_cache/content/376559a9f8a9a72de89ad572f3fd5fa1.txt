Previously, the Hyundai Genesis was a luxury car included in Hyundai's vehicle lineup, but that vehicle was discontinued in 2016. Instead, Genesis became its own standalone brand separate from Hyundai's other vehicles. Today, the Genesis brand includes several luxury sedans and SUVs, like the GV70 and GV80.

Genesis vehicles can be pricey, with several models having starting prices well above the national average for all new vehicles. Unsurprisingly, that high price tag means Genesis models also tend to be more expensive to insure. On average, Genesis owners pay $130 per month for liability-only insurance and $245 per month for full coverage.

However, drivers can save on Genesis auto insurance by comparison shopping, taking advantage of insurance discounts, and adjusting their coverage. Some examples of car insurance companies with competitive rates for Genesis vehicles include USAA, GEICO, and State Farm.

What affects insurance rates for Genesis vehicles?
--------------------------------------------------

Genesis vehicles tend to have higher-than-average car insurance rates, but why is that? The following factors drive the higher rates.

*   **Genesis values:**As luxury vehicles, most Genesis models have higher-than-average sticker prices. As a result, they're more expensive to replace than other vehicles if they're stolen or totaled in an accident. The G70 and G80 are especially costly models to insure compared to the C90 and GV80.
*   **Higher theft rates:**Genesis has had some vulnerabilities that made its models easier to steal. As a result, Genesis vehicles — along with Kias and Hyundais — have had higher theft rates than other vehicles. Because of this risk, insurers charge higher premiums. 
*   **Repair costs:**Genesis vehicles are luxury cars that incorporate high-end parts and technology. Consequently, they're more expensive to repair than a basic sedan or SUV, so you'll pay more for comprehensive and collision insurance. 

Examples of other factors that affect your premiums include:

*   **Age:**Young drivers are inexperienced, so they're more likely to be in accidents. To offset that higher risk, insurers charge teens and young adults higher rates. 
*   **Gender**: Men tend to pay more for insurance since they have statistically higher accident rates than women, but some states don't allow insurers to consider gender.
*   **Location:** Areas with high traffic congestion levels, crime, and a higher cost of living will typically have higher insurance premiums. 
*   **Driving record:**If you have speeding tickets, accidents, or DUIs on your record, you'll pay a much higher rate for coverage. 
*   **Coverage options:**What coverage you choose (such as full coverage versus liability-only insurance) and your coverage limits can cause you to pay more for insurance. The same applies to the deductible amount you select.

How can I lower my Genesis insurance cost?
------------------------------------------

To lower your car insurance premiums, follow these tips.

*   **Avoiding unnecessary coverage:**While you should buy insurance that sufficiently protects your Genesis and your finances, carefully consider your needs and avoid unnecessary add-ons, as they can add up.
*   **Increase your deductible:**Your deductible — typically an amount between $200 and $2,000 — is how much you have to pay after a collision or incident for repairs to your own car before your insurer helps with the cost. The higher the deductible, the lower your premiums, so increasing your deductible can help you save money on your Genesis auto insurance policy. 
*   **Sign up for telematics or pay-per-mile programs:**If you're a safe driver, a telematics policy could help you get lower rates. Pay-per-mile policies base your rates on how much you drive, so those who drive fewer-than-average miles may benefit from them. 
*   **Ask about discounts:**There may be discount programs you qualify for but aren't currently taking advantage of, such as homeownership, good student, or policy bundling discounts. 
*   **Shop around:**Rates vary by company, so set reminders to shop for coverage at least once a year to ensure you're not overpaying for coverage. 

FAQs
----

### Are Genesis vehicles expensive to insure?

In general, Genesis vehicles are more expensive to insure than many other cars. Owners pay an average of $245 per month for full coverage insurance for the Genesis and $130 per month for liability-only policies.

### What's the best insurance company for Genesis insurance?

No company is the best for everyone. I found that USAA and GEICO offer the lowest rates for most drivers, but depending on your needs, you may find that another insurer is a better fit. For example, if you need GAP insurance, it's wise to consider an insurer like American Family.

### What coverage should I get for my Hyundai Genesis?

If you have an older Hyundai Genesis — which was in production between 2008 and 2016 — you may not need a full coverage policy. Depending on the value of your car, dropping collision and comprehensive coverage and opting for a liability-only policy can help you save money.

### Who typically has the cheapest car insurance for Genesis vehicles?

On average, USAA and GEICO typically have the lowest rates for all current Genesis models.

Bottom line
-----------

The Genesis lineup of luxury vehicles tends to be more expensive to insure, but you can save money by taking advantage of insurer discounts, completing safe driving courses, enrolling in telematics or UBI policies, or shopping around for the lowest rates.
