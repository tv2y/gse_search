WASHINGTON (TND) -- Chinese nationals are now the fastest growing demographic attempting to U.S. illegally.

In fiscal year 2021, U.S. Customs and Border Patrol only encountered 450 Chinese nationals at the Southern border. In 2022, that number grew to 2,176. In 2023, it ballooned to 24,314.

In the first four months of fiscal year 2024, border patrol apprehended nearly 19,000 Chinese citizens at the border.

“We need to be very concerned about this given that China is our number one adversary," explained Lora Ries, director of the Border Security & Immigration Center, at the Heritage Foundation.“Given the rapid increase of Chinese nationals crossing the border and how adversarial Beijing acts towards the U.S., we have to assume that much of this is state-sponsored, state-directed, and we need to act accordingly.”

One possible reason for the dramatic rise in illegal crossing attempts is the U.S. restriction on visas as tensions rise between the two nations.

In 2016, 2.2 million temporary visas were issued to Chinese migrants. In 2022 that dropped to just 160,000, according to data from the U.S. Department of State.

The Chinese are also much more likely to be granted asylum in the U.S. compared with the average applicant.

Republicans like Sen. Josh Hawley, R-Mo., are sounding alarm bells.

> Clearly it shows the border is completely open, that Joe Biden wants it open,” Hawley told Fox News on Tuesday.

Meanwhile, many Democrats chalk up GOP concerns to political theatre.

> They’re weaponizing immigration. They want to see if they can get votes for it,” said Rep. Adriano Espaillat, D-N.Y.

In San Francisco, a non-citizen Chinese immigrant was just sworn in to serve on the city’s election commission.

“That is shocking but it goes to show what is going on, on the left," said Ries. "Only U.S. citizens can vote in U.S. federal elections. Only U.S. citizens should vote. Citizenship should matter."

Americans increasingly see the border as a major issue and polling shows it’s a weak spot for Joe Biden’s re-election. The president reportedly is weighing issuing an executive order to show he’s taking Americans' concerns about the border seriously.
