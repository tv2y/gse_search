### Abstract

Crop quality and productivity are the most basic strategic resources of any agricultural practice and technology, so one of the priorities of the farm and its development is paying attention to new and innovative fields experiments and practices in production. Various factors affect the effectiveness of innovative practices and resources. The aim of this study is to propose a new quantum agriculture view and a homeopathy-based methodology that draws from the theories and concepts of quantum physics and takes a wave-based approach to the functional measurement phase.A new type of image technology as a thermal image is proposed to analyse the functional information about the plant; every pixel of the image indicates vibration parameters, frequency or amplitude of vibration. The relationship between the new Quantum Agriculture based method and the functional characteristics of the flour measured through bio-photonic emissions is also studied and the results of some preliminary studies are presented.

**Keywords:** quantum agriculture, crop productivity, functional information analysis
