Title: In Video: Bridge captured shaking in the wind in China

URL Source: https://jerseyeveningpost.com/morenews/worldnews/2020/05/06/in-video-bridge-captured-shaking-in-the-wind-in-china/

Published Time: 2020-05-06T13:19:14+00:00

Markdown Content:
Posted in More News, World News

by Adopstar

6 May 2020

A bridge in southern China’s Guangdong Province has been temporarily closed after it began shaking in the wind.

Experts did not see any signs of damage to the Humen Bridge, which links Guangzhou and Dongguan.
