### Meaning

English: pearl; Latin: great

### Historical & Cultural Background

In English, Maisy is often considered a diminutive of Margaret, which means 'pearl.' The name has been popularized in various cultures and is often associated with innocence and purity. In Latin, the name can be linked to the word 'magnus,' meaning 'great,' adding a layer of significance.

### Trend Summary

The name has seen a resurgence in recent years, often favored for its charming and playful sound.
