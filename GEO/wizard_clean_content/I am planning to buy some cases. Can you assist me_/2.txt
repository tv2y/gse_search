### **Places to Buy Phone Cases:**  
1. **Online Retailers:** Amazon, eBay, Walmart, Best Buy, Target, Etsy (for unique/handmade cases).  
2. **Cell Phone Carrier Stores:** AT&T, Verizon, T-Mobile, Sprint.  
3. **Electronics Stores:** Best Buy, Walmart, Target, Apple Store (for iPhone cases).  
4. **Specialty Brands:** Case-Mate, OtterBox, Speck, LifeProof.  
5. **Department Stores:** Macy’s, Kohl’s, JCPenney.  
6. **Mobile Accessory Kiosks:** Found in shopping malls.  
7. **Phone Repair Shops:** Some also sell cases.  
8. **Online Marketplaces:** AliExpress, DHgate (for budget-friendly options).  

### **Additional Tips:**  
- Choose sturdy cases with flat corners (better impact protection).  
- Read reviews to ensure proper fit (holes align with camera/ports).  
- Avoid silicone cases if durability is a priority.  
- Check both online and local stores for price comparisons.
