About LIMA 2025
---------------

### Discover Innovations for 
DEFENCE COMMERCIAL AEROSPACE MARITIME INNOVATION SUSTAINABILITY EDUCATION

* * *

The only exhibition of its kind in the world, the Langkawi International Maritime & Aerospace Exhibition, or LIMA for short, is a platform for defence, commercial, and industrial uses. The biennial exhibition for the maritime and aerospace sectors, serving the Asia Pacific region, stands out as a unique trade showcase and specialised event, unparalleled by any other worldwide. The 17th edition of LIMA 2025 will be held from 20 - 24 May 2025, in Langkawi Island.
