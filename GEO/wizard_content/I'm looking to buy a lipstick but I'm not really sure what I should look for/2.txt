It’ll save you money if you do try it before you buy it!

Getting advice is so plentiful these days from asking a makeup consultant at major department stores, cosmetic stores like MAC, Sephora or Ulta and there are abundant DIY/tutorials on YouTube with many experts like Wayne Goss, James Charles and Manny Mau!

Personally I’m a “baptism by fire” kind of guy and found the only way to learn is by DIY! When I first started getting into wearing makeup and lipsticks it was a gradual approach. I would go to drug stores and purchase tinted lip balms like Burt’s Bee Wax which has numerous shades and I could take them to the counter with no questions asked. This satisfied my curiosity for a bit but found I wanted more of a color to my lips than simply a tint so I went to M·A·C Cosmetics Homepage and started reviewing different lipsticks when I noticed MAC had teamed up with Lady Gaga to issue Viva Glam! And all for a noble cause: to spread awareness about HIV virus and raise funds to help men, women and children around the world living with the illness. I was in! I ordered my first tube from the MAC website and anxiously waited for it to arrive!

It was a tube of bliss and loved the color on my now pinkish-beige mauve lips! I was going to go through that lipstick in about two weeks and wanted another but didn’t want to wait for the delivery time so I went to my local MAC store to purchase in person!

I hadn’t ever been inside an actual cosmetic store but was looking forward to the experience. Of course if you’ve ever been to a MAC store on a mid Saturday afternoon you know what it’s like and if you haven’t it’s complete pandemonium! MAC beauty consultants everywhere and women at every counter swabbing, applying, spritzing and trying everything thing on! I was in a space I hadn’t prepared myself for! Luckily a young woman approached me asking if I needed any help which I replied with “Yes! Please!”. I told her I was looking for the Viva Glam lipstick and would she help me locate it. She then proceeded to walk and ask was it for my girlfriend, wife and I replied with “No. It’s for me!” She smiled and stated “Oooo, fun! I love men who aren’t afraid of makeup!”

We then spent the next hour playing with other shades of lipstick as well as primers, concealers, eyeshadows and bronzers powders! I never felt so relaxed and totally at ease the entire time! She never judged or snickered but we did laugh at a couple of lipsticks which looked way too red for my taste though she thought they looked “Hot”.

She helped me with how to apply makeup so it enhanced my overall look without looking too made up! She allowed me to freely sample by myself while she attended to other customers but always keeping an eye on me!

Since then I’ve gone to MAC numerous times as they’re always so welcoming and ever so ready to answer any questions. I now go to other cosmetic stores or department stores and feel totally at ease while I’m browsing or trying on a lipstick. If anyone gives me a second glance what do I care. I’m having fun being myself and if you don’t like it then that’s a you problem not a me problem.

I hope and wish you lots of fun playing, sampling and of course wearing makeup!

The difference between liquid lipstick and regular lipstick can mostly be about the finish, smudging, transfer and possibly longevity.

Regular lipstick has many different finishes and are more moisturizing than most liquid lipsticks. Now liquid lipsticks are more matte, can be longer lasting, usually wear much longer and sometimes can be more drying over time. They also are less likely to transfer or smudge depending on the brand. At least that is my findings.

I have tried a few that are kind of a between the two but they don’t last as long as liquid lipstick but are not as drying either.

The disadvantages of regular lipstick would be possibly having to reapply more frequently, as well as they transfers more easily and they are more easy to smudge. The disadvantages of liquid lipstick is that they can be more drying on the lips, is a more matte finish, and can be a little harder to apply accurately on the lips.
