4.7-magnitude quake hits east China's Anhui: CENC
=================================================

Source: Xinhua

Editor: huaxia

2024-09-18 20:32:45

BEIJING, Sept. 18 (Xinhua) -- A 4.7-magnitude earthquake jolted Feidong County in Hefei, the capital city of east China's Anhui Province, at 8:08 p.m. Wednesday (Beijing Time), according to the China Earthquake Networks Center (CENC).

The epicenter was monitored at 31.98 degrees north latitude and 117.60 degrees east longitude. The quake struck at a depth of 12 km, said a report issued by the CENC. ■
