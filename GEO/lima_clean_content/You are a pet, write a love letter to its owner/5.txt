A Love Letter for All Pet Owners Love Letters from The Circle of Life with Animals Finding Gratitude  

It is the One Year Anniversary of the release of my book *Love Letters from the Circle of Life with Animals; Finding Gratitude and Beauty in Heartbreak and Grief*.  

Dear Friend,  
Wouldn’t it be special if you could send broken-hearted friends a love letter after their beloved pet has passed? I have written a heartfelt book of essays about love, and the beauty and gratitude that can be found in the heartbreak and grief of the loss of a pet.  

My book is titled *Love Letters from The Circle of Life with Animals, Finding Gratitude and Beauty in Heartbreak and Grief*. Death and loss are an unfortunate part of our lives. We all know that helpless feeling when a friend loses a beloved pet. There does not seem to be any helpful words. Offering them this book can be a token of your sympathy.  

Warm Regards,  
Christin Finn DVM
