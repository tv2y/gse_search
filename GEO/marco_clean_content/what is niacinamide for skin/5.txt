Niacinamide (also known as nicotinamide) is a form of vitamin B3, a water-soluble vitamin that’s essential for maintaining healthy-looking skin. This ingredient, first studied for skincare benefits in the 1970s, is found in many CeraVe products, including cleansers, eye creams, serums, moisturizers, and sunscreens.  

### Benefits of Niacinamide:  
- Supports hydration and reduces transepidermal water loss (TEWL).  
- Calms the skin and may improve skin barrier function.  
- Has antioxidant properties, helping to combat free radicals and signs of aging.  
- May balance oil production, benefiting those with oily or acne-prone skin.  
- Can improve skin texture, tone, and radiance.  

### Compatibility and Usage:  
- Safe for most skin types, including sensitive and blemish-prone skin.  
- Can be paired with other skincare ingredients like ceramides, hyaluronic acid, and peptides.  
- Suitable for daily use in well-rounded skincare routines.
