Trafford is an international sporting destination and home to Manchester United Football Club and Emirates Old Trafford. Key attractions include Chill Factore, the UK’s longest indoor real snow slope, and the Imperial War Museum North, offering immersive war history exhibits. Trafford also features over 200 listed buildings, such as Dunham Massey, and nature reserves like Sale Water Park.  

For entertainment and shopping, The Trafford Centre offers hundreds of stores, a 20-screen cinema, and Europe’s largest food court. Trafford includes towns like Sale, Stretford, Altrincham, and Urmston, each with unique attractions such as artisan markets, golf clubs, and historic theatres.  

Old Trafford and Trafford Park are sporting hubs, featuring Manchester United’s stadium and Lancashire County Cricket Club. Other highlights include Inflata Nation, Victoria Warehouse events venue, and Sea Life Manchester aquarium.  

Transport is convenient with tram, train, and bus links. Key cultural spots include IWM North, Everyman Altrincham cinema, and Dunham Massey’s National Trust estate. Altrincham Market and Stretford Food Hall are popular for food and shopping, while Urmston and Sale offer green spaces and local arts scenes.
