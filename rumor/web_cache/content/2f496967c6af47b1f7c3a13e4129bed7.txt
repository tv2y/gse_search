### **Annette Lawless**

August 28 · 

What's your take on "thoughts and prayers?"

Vice President JD Vance shared his thoughts, as he responded to comments made by former White House Press Secretary Jen Psaki. This is what she posted online after the deadly shooting at Annunciation Catholic Church this week that killed two children and injured over a dozen other people.

Psaki, who served as press secretary under President Biden, wrote on “X”/Former Twitter yesterday after the Minneapolis shooting, saying, “Prayers does not end school shootings. prayers do not make parents feel safe sending their kids to school.”

Vance responded directly to Psaki’s post, and he wrote, “We pray because our hearts are broken. We pray because we know God listens. We pray because we know that God works in mysterious ways, and can inspire us to further action.”

Charles M. Carter

“Thoughts and prayers” makes me think of these words of James:

“What good is it, my brothers, if a man says he has faith, but has no works? Can faith save him?

“And if a brother or sister is naked and in lack of daily food, and one of you tells them, ‘Go in peace. Be warmed and filled;’ yet you didn’t give them the things the body needs, what good is it?

“Even so faith, if it has no works, is dead in itself.”

‭‭James‬ ‭2‬:‭14‬-‭17‬ (WEBUS‬‬)

“Go in peace, be warmed and filled” is the kind of easy thing that rolls off the tongue that a person might say who has no real intention to do anything substantive to help.

“Thoughts and prayers” could easily be the same kind of cliche.

Replace “if a brother or sister is naked and in lack of daily food” with “if a brother or sister has lost a child in another school shooting,” and replace “go in peace, be warmed and filled” with “thoughts and prayers,” and a description of a dead, phony faith writes itself.

According to the Washington Post, in the past 26 years in the US, starting with Columbine High School, there have been 390 K-12 shootings, yielding 203 deaths and 441 injuries.

The inaction to ban military-grade assault rifles in the US in that time is testament to a dead, hypocritical faith, and what has turned “thoughts and prayers,” for the most part, into a cruel cliche.

The children who were killed in Minneapolis were literally thinking about God, and praying to Him, when they were shot. That didn’t save them from the inaction of “thoughts and prayers” politicians.
