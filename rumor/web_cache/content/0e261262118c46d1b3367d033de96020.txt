#### **Drone World Congress 2025: The Premier Event for Low-Altitude Economy and Unmanned Systems**

📅 **Dates:** May 23–25, 2025

📍 **Venue:** Shenzhen Convention and Exhibition Center (Futian)

**For the First Time: Dual Halls and 50,000 Square Meters of Drone Innovation**

The highly anticipated World Drone Conference and UAS Drone Exhibition return in 2025, marking a new milestone with an expanded scale across two massive exhibition halls. Covering 50,000 square meters, this event will feature groundbreaking innovations in unmanned systems and the low-altitude economy. Expect participation from **800+ global exhibitors** and **76,000 professional visitors** representing **over 100 countries**, making it an unmissable event for industry leaders, innovators, and enthusiasts.

‍

### **Why the 2025 UAS Drone Show is a Must-Attend Event**

*   **Dual Halls, Expanded Scope**

For the first time, the exhibition spans **50,000 square meters**, showcasing a comprehensive range of technologies including drones, aviation models, intelligent unmanned vehicles, artificial intelligence robots, and more.
*   **Global Collaboration and Networking**

With participants from countries like the U.S., Germany, Japan, South Korea, and India, as well as leading domestic institutions like the Ministry of Industry and Information Technology, the event fosters meaningful international cooperation.
*   **Comprehensive Industry Coverage**

The exhibition highlights advancements across key sectors such as agriculture, logistics, emergency rescue, autonomous driving, and artificial intelligence, offering an unparalleled platform to explore the full spectrum of the unmanned systems industry.

### **Event Highlights**

#### **1. Exhibition Zones**

The exhibition features specialized zones for various industry segments:

*   **Industrial Drones:** Police drones, agricultural UAVs, logistics drones, firefighting UAVs, and more.
*   **Consumer Drones:** Aerial photography drones, racing drones, and recreational UAVs.
*   **Unmanned Systems:** Land-air integrated systems, underwater vehicles, and flying motorcycles.
*   **Intelligent Vehicles:** Autonomous cars, delivery robots, and intelligent cockpits.
*   **AI Robots:** Service robots, firefighting robots, and security inspection robots.
*   **Industry Chain Solutions:** Navigation systems, mission payloads, and energy innovations.

#### **2. Conferences and Forums**

The event includes **two plenary sessions**, over **40 parallel forums**, and keynotes from leading experts, government officials, and industry pioneers.

*   **9th World Drone Conference:**

Theme: "Entering a New Era of Low-Altitude Economy."

Topics include industry policy insights, technological challenges, and future trends.
*   **Global Awards Ceremony:**

Recognition for contributions to drone technology with awards such as:
    1.   Global Drone Contribution Award
    2.   International Unnamed System Innovation Award
    3.   Drone System Design Award

#### **3. Exciting Events**

*   **Drone Racing Championship**

A thrilling competition showcasing cutting-edge drone technology while promoting talent development and regional collaboration.
*   **Live Demonstrations:**

Witness live applications of advanced UAVs, including manned drones and vertical take-off fixed-wing systems.

### **Why Exhibit or Attend?**

*   **Industry Benchmark:**

Stay updated on the latest developments in drones and unmanned systems.
*   **Global Exposure:**

With participants from 100+ countries, connect with international markets and expand your business.
*   **Policy Support:**

Leverage opportunities created by national strategies promoting the low-altitude economy.
*   **Unparalleled Networking:**

Engage with 800+ exhibitors, 76,000+ professionals, and 400+ industry leaders.
