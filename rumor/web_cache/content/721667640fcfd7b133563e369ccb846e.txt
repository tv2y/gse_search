No, the American Heart Association did NOT publish a study saying that 90 percent of people who were vaccinated against COVID-19 have deadly heart defects. In fact, the study was unrelated to vaccines! However, teen fact-checker Sameer did see the claim circulating on social media. Watch to find out how Sameer verified that the claim is false and see what media literacy tip he has for when something you see online provokes a strong emotional reaction. Sameer is a participant in Canada's Teen Fact-Checking Network program at MediaSmarts.
