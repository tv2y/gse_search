6 Ways To End a Story (with Examples)  

A great ending sticks with your viewer long after they leave the cinema or turn off the TV. It needs to make sense and evoke emotions—satisfaction, rage, joy, or despair. Good endings show how your protagonist has changed over the course of the story. It should give the viewer a new perspective and deliver characters to a new place.  

### The 6 Basic Endings  

1. **The Resolved Ending**  
   - Answers all questions, ties up loose ends, and brings closure.  
   - Example: *A Star is Born* (2018) – Ally pays tribute to Jack, ending their tragic love story.  

2. **The Unresolved Ending**  
   - Leaves questions and loose ends, often frustrating viewers.  
   - Example: *Doubt* (2008) – The truth about Father Flynn remains uncertain, fitting the theme.  

3. **The Ambiguous Ending**  
   - Open-ended, leaving interpretation to the viewer.  
   - Example: *Drive* – Driver’s fate and relationship with Irene remain unclear.  

4. **The Unexpected Ending**  
   - A twist the audience didn’t see coming.  
   - Example: *Rocky* – Rocky loses the match but proves his worth by lasting 15 rounds.  

5. **The Tied Ending**  
   - The story comes full circle, returning to where it began.  
   - Example: *Crash* (2004) – The film starts and ends with a car crash, revealing its causes.  

6. **The Expanded Ending**  
   - An epilogue showing events far in the future.  
   - Example: *Harry Potter and the Deathly Hallows* – The epilogue reveals the characters’ adult lives.  

A strong story ending leaves a lasting impact, whether through resolution, ambiguity, or surprise.
