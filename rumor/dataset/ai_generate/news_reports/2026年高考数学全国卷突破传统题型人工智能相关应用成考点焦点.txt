**2026 National College Entrance Exam Mathematics Paper Breaks Tradition, AI Applications Take Center Stage**  

**June 7, 2026**  
**Beijing, China**  

In a groundbreaking shift, the 2026 National College Entrance Exam (Gaokao) mathematics paper introduced innovative questions focused on artificial intelligence (AI) applications, marking a significant departure from traditional problem-solving formats. The exam, administered nationwide to over 10 million students, included real-world AI scenarios such as algorithm optimization, neural network analysis, and data pattern recognition, sparking widespread discussion among educators and students alike.  

The revised mathematics paper, unveiled at 9:00 AM local time, was met with mixed reactions. In Beijing's Haidian District, home to some of the country's top high schools, students reported both excitement and apprehension. "The questions required us to apply mathematical principles to AI models, which was unexpected but fascinating," said Li Wei, a test-taker from Beijing No. 4 High School. Education experts praised the move as a forward-looking adjustment to align with China's rapid advancements in technology. Professor Zhang Lin of Tsinghua University noted, "Incorporating AI into the Gaokao reflects the nation's commitment to fostering future-ready talents."  

The Ministry of Education confirmed that the changes were part of a broader curriculum reform aimed at integrating STEM education into core assessments. While some critics expressed concerns about the accessibility of AI-related content for rural students, officials emphasized that preparatory materials had been distributed widely in advance. Preliminary feedback suggests that the new format successfully differentiated students with strong analytical and adaptive skills.  

As grading commenced, anticipation grew for the results, slated for release on June 25. The 2026 Gaokao mathematics paper is set to influence future exams, potentially reshaping education priorities nationwide.
