### Welcome to

York County

York County, PA, is a rich tapestry of history, from our colonial roots to our pivotal role in the American Revolution and Civil War. Today, we thrive as a vibrant community, blending charming small towns with modern amenities and scenic countryside.

### Honey Run Golf Course

Honey Run Golf Course in York County, PA, offers golfers a picturesque escape with its lush green fairways nestled amidst rolling hills. Designed to challenge players of all skill levels, this scenic course provides a delightful blend of natural beauty and enjoyable gameplay.

Learn More

### Royal Square District

The Royal Square District, home to Mural Park, is a dynamic urban hub brimming with eclectic shops, trendy eateries, and vibrant arts venues. Its historic charm blends seamlessly with modern creativity, offering visitors a unique and lively cultural experience in the heart of the city.

Learn More

### Central Market House

With its bustling atmosphere and diverse array of vendors offering fresh produce, artisanal goods, and culinary delights, Central Market is a quintessential destination for food enthusiasts and community engagement.

Learn More

### Ma & Pa Railroad

As one of the last vestiges of the Maryland and Pennsylvania Railroad, Ma & Pa Railroad provides visitors with a unique opportunity to step back in time and experience the wonderment of rail travel.

Learn More

### Stewartstown Railroad

The Stewartstown Railroad is a historic gem transporting passengers on a journey through time, with vintage locomotives and charming scenery meant to be enjoyed by visitors of all ages.

Learn More

### Northern Central Railway of York

Northern Central Railway boasts restored train cars from various times in history, authentic experiences, and fun themed excursions through scenic routes along the Heritage Rail Trail.

Learn More

### Dill's Tavern

Steeped in colonial charm, visitors can step back in time to immerse themselves in the ambiance of the 18th century. Learn about the history of distilling and our area's fascinating colonial past.

Learn More

### Mason-Dixon Trail

Traverse through picturesque landscapes to experience a diverse and scenic journey spanning more than 200 miles. From lush forests to historic landmarks, this trail is an outdoor enthusiast's ideal adventure.

Learn More

### Gifford Pinchot State Park

This is a nature lover's paradise. Encompassing over 2,300 acres of rolling hills, lush forests, and serene lakes, Gifford Pinchot State Park boasts hiking, boating, picnicking, and more in a beautiful natural setting.

Learn More

### Codorus State Park

This sprawling outdoor playground lives on more than 3,500 acres of pristine wilderness and scenic lake vistas packed with recreational activities for all ages. Whether you're camping under the stars, fishing Lake Marburg, or exploring the miles of beautiful hiking & biking trails, this park promises a beautiful adventure in nature.

Learn More

### Roundtop Mountain Resort

Roundtop Mountain Resort offers thrilling adventures year-round. From skiing and snowboarding in the winter to paintball and mountain biking in the summer, you'll find endless excitement and breathtaking views throughout your adrenaline-fueled fun.

Learn More

### York County History Center

The York County History Center encompasses the Agricultural and Industrial Museum, Colonial Complex, and Fire Museum. Check out the BRAND NEW, state-of-the-art location for the Center slated to open this summer! It will offer enhanced exhibits and modern amenities to showcase the region's diverse heritage.

Learn More

### Haines Shoe House

You've heard of the woman who lived in a shoe, but I bet you didn't expect it to look like this! The Haines Shoe House has taken on many roles over the years, from a tourist attraction to private residence, ice cream stand to gift shop, it has never failed to amaze onlookers. Today, it's a beautiful VRBO home that you can actually rent and experience for yourself!

Learn More

### Snyder's of Hanover Factory Store

Who doesn't love a good snack? The Snyder's of Hanover Factory Store offers your favorite baked pretzels, snacks, and branded merch. Pretzel lovers and snack enthusiasts come from all over to enjoy their favorite goodies straight from the source.

Learn More

### Susquehanna National Heritage Area - Zimmerman Center

This gateway to the region's rich history and natural beauty offers interactive exhibits, educational programs, and access to scenic trails along the Susquehanna River. Climb aboard the Chief Uncas, a fully restored yacht that dates back to 1912, for a gorgeous tour on the river.

Learn More

### York State Fair

America's first fair! This beloved event showcases the rich agricultural heritage and captivating community spirit of the region. With huge headliner concerts, thrilling rides, educational exhibits, and delicious food, you're certainly in for a good time.

Learn More

### Heritage Rail Trail

Extending more than 30 miles between the City of York and the Mason-Dixon Line, this ADA-accessible trail weaves through 11 municipalities while following along centuries-old train tracks. Travel along the wide paths of compacted stone and asphalt for a leisurely stroll or a relaxing bike ride.

Learn More
