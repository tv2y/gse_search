### The Definitive Source Behind the News

Calling the 2024 presidential race state by state
=================================================

At 5:34 a.m. ET on Wednesday, The Associated Press declared Donald Trump the winner of the U.S. presidential race.
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The following is a chronology of AP’s presidential race calls (all times Eastern). At this time, AP has not called Alaska, Arizona and Nevada.

**Nov. 5, 2024**

19:05: WASHINGTON (AP) – Donald Trump wins Indiana

19:06: WASHINGTON (AP) – Kamala Harris wins Vermont

19:06: WASHINGTON (AP) – Donald Trump wins Kentucky

19:30: WASHINGTON (AP) – Donald Trump wins West Virginia

20:00: WASHINGTON (AP) – Donald Trump wins Mississippi

20:00: WASHINGTON (AP) – Donald Trump wins Alabama

20:00: WASHINGTON (AP) – Kamala Harris wins Maryland

20:00: WASHINGTON (AP) – Donald Trump wins Oklahoma

20:00: WASHINGTON (AP) – Donald Trump wins Tennessee

20:00: WASHINGTON (AP) – Kamala Harris wins Connecticut

20:00: WASHINGTON (AP) – Kamala Harris wins Massachusetts

20:00: WASHINGTON (AP) – Kamala Harris wins Rhode Island

20:01: WASHINGTON (AP) – Donald Trump wins Florida

20:01: WASHINGTON (AP) – Donald Trump wins South Carolina

20:30: WASHINGTON (AP) – Donald Trump wins Arkansas

20:30: WASHINGTON (AP) – Kamala Harris wins New Jersey

20:34: WASHINGTON (AP) – Kamala Harris wins Delaware

20:38: WASHINGTON (AP) – Kamala Harris wins Illinois

21:00: WASHINGTON (AP) – Donald Trump wins the statewide vote in Nebraska and two electoral votes

21:00: WASHINGTON (AP) – Kamala Harris wins New York

21:00: WASHINGTON (AP) – Donald Trump wins North Dakota

21:00: WASHINGTON (AP) – Donald Trump wins South Dakota

21:00: WASHINGTON (AP) – Donald Trump wins Louisiana

21:00: WASHINGTON (AP) – Donald Trump wins Wyoming

21:08: WASHINGTON (AP) – Donald Trump wins Ohio

21:12: WASHINGTON (AP) – Donald Trump wins Texas

21:25: WASHINGTON (AP) – Donald Trump wins the 3rd Congressional District in Nebraska and one electoral vote

21:49: WASHINGTON (AP) – Donald Trump wins Missouri

22:00: WASHINGTON (AP) – Donald Trump wins Montana

22:00: WASHINGTON (AP) – Donald Trump wins Utah

22:08: WASHINGTON (AP) – Kamala Harris wins Colorado

22:20: WASHINGTON (AP) – Kamala Harris wins the District of Columbia

22:39: WASHINGTON (AP) – Donald Trump wins Kansas

22:40: WASHINGTON (AP) – Donald Trump wins Iowa

22:45: WASHINGTON (AP) – Kamala Harris wins the 1st Congressional District in Maine and one electoral vote

23:00: WASHINGTON (AP) – Kamala Harris wins California

23:00: WASHINGTON (AP) – Donald Trump wins Idaho

23:00: WASHINGTON (AP) – Kamala Harris wins Washington

23:18: WASHINGTON (AP) – Donald Trump wins North Carolina

23:23: WASHINGTON (AP) – Kamala Harris wins Oregon

23:33: WASHINGTON (AP) – Kamala Harris wins New Mexico

23:42: WASHINGTON (AP) – Kamala Harris wins Virginia

**Nov. 6, 2024**

00:00: WASHINGTON (AP) – Kamala Harris wins Hawaii

00:08: WASHINGTON (AP) – Kamala Harris wins the 2nd Congressional District in Nebraska and one electoral vote

00:58: WASHINGTON (AP) – Donald Trump wins Georgia

01:01: WASHINGTON (AP) – Donald Trump wins the 1st Congressional District in Nebraska and one electoral vote

01:16: WASHINGTON (AP) – Kamala Harris wins New Hampshire

01:51: WASHINGTON (AP) – Donald Trump wins the 2nd Congressional District in Maine and one electoral vote

02:24: WASHINGTON (AP) – Donald Trump wins Pennsylvania

02:47: WASHINGTON (AP) – Kamala Harris wins Minnesota

05:34: WASHINGTON (AP) – Donald Trump wins Wisconsin

05:34: WASHINGTON (AP) – Donald Trump is elected the 47th president of the U.S.

12:54: WASHINGTON (AP) – Donald Trump wins Michigan

See more about AP’s essential role in U.S. elections.

By **Nicole Meir**, 

The Associated Press

NOV. 6, 2024
