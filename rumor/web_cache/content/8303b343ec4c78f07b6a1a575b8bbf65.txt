Four killed in China school building collapse
=============================================

Advertisement

Press Trust of India Beijing 

 Last Updated : Jun 20 2014 | 8:31 PM IST

Four members of a family, including two children, were killed today when a primary school building collapsed after incessant rain in China's eastern Jiangxi Province. 

 The four - grandparents and their two grandchildren, aged five and seven - were in the two-story structure in Dangkou Village, Yihuang County when a landslide triggred by a rainstorm flattened it. 

 About 2.12 lakh people have been affected till today because of the downpour that started in Jiangxi on Monday. More than 3,000 people have been relocated, state-run Xinhua news agency reported.

 A rescue operation is under way.
