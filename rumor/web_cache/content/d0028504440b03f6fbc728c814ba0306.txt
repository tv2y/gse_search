Description
-----------

Share your opinion and emotions with other Manchester United fans – from Los Angeles to Tokyo. You will be able to celebrate every Rashford's goal with a pal who lives on the opposite side of the planet.

Our editorial team works hard to make sure that United fans always have something to discuss and read. We create a constant flow of soccer news, editorial articles, opinion pieces from our users.

This is what you get right now:

√ Match updates, live scores and results.

√ Chat rooms with heated discussions!

√ Pre-mach polls. From our side, we will tell you which team has the best chances to win the match.

√ Latest news including confirmed transfers and potential new signings.

√ Match previews, line-ups and goal notifications.

√ Post-match reports, tactical analysis, editorial columns and experts' opinions.

√ Videos. Unfortunately, we can't broadcast any live games, but we deliver video highlights when we can.

√ Tools for creating your own posts and sharing them with other users inside the app.

√ Calendar and standings for all major leagues and cups.

√ Team statistics and players' career. See who has the best performance on the pitch in today's match.

√ Penalty shoot-outs in matches.

√ Your own push notifications for top news, starting lineups, kickoff, goals, yellow and red cards, results, replies in chats. Silent mode is available as well.

√ Dark mode.

√ Pure emotions!

Follow every tournament where Man United takes part – English Premier League, UEFA Champions League and Europa League, The FA Cup, EFL Cup, friendly matches.

We will add more features will our next updates, so stay with us and keep faith in the Club. Glory Glory MAN UNITED!

For all the statistics lovers out there, we're happy to offer extended data, including:

• Updated match centre. More team information is now displayed during any match. Including head-to-head info.

• Players' injuries;

• Information about players on loan;

• Coach career as a player;

• Transfer prices.

We have finally added an option to disable ads in the application with a paid subscription. The following options are available:

• Disable ads for 1 year;

• Disable ads for 1 month;

• Free trial for 7 days.

Choose yours!

Our football app is created and supported by Manchester United fans for other Man Utd fans. This is not an official app, it’s not in any way affiliated to the club. Stay with us as we keep developing more features for you.

We're open for cooperation. You can contact us with any questions or suggestions at support.90live@tribuna.com.

Let's enjoy every Manchester United moment together!
