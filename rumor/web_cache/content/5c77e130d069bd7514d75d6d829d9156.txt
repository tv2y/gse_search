The Program Year (PY) 2025 Community Development Homeless Services application is available online through the Department of Community Development’s PowerApps grants management portal.

The link to the application is here: https://esguniversalpartner.powerappsportals.us/

Applications must be submitted to the PowerApps portal by October 31, 2025 at 5:00 PM. Eligible organizations may use this single application to request the Emergency Solutions Grant (ESG) and Community Development Block Grant (CDBG) homeless services funds. These funds support the following services:

*   Street Outreach – engagement and assessment services for persons experiencing unsheltered homelessness
*   Emergency Shelter – safe and secure facility-based crisis housing and services
*   Rapid Rehousing – rental subsidy, housing case management, and supportive services for households experiencing homelessness
*   Coordinated Intake – the single point of entry into the Cuyahoga County homeless services system
*   Homeless Prevention – assistance to help imminently homeless households maintain or regain housing stability

Late submissions will not be accepted.

To qualify for funding, organizations must be in operation at least two years and all proposed activities must align with the goals of the Cleveland/Cuyahoga County Continuum of Care Strategic Action Plan to End Homelessness. Programs must also comply with Continuum of Care standards and participate in HMIS and Coordinated Intake.

ESG-funded programs require a dollar-for-dollar match. There is no match requirement for CDBG homeless services grants.

Applications will be scored on the program’s effect on homeless individuals and families, agency financial and programmatic capacity, collaboration with other providers, and past performance on Community Development contracts.

Questions regarding the application should be addressed to David Steinmuller, Acting Manager - Emerging Programs at dsteinmuller@clevelandohio.gov

The City of Cleveland (City), Department of Public Safety, is seeking proposals from qualified conservation specialists for the cleaning and preservation of the historic bronze ship’s bell from the USS Cleveland. The bell is a historical artifact crafted from a solid cast copper alloy and features intricate relief imagery that represents a valuable component of naval pageantry and civic history. Currently mounted on a wooden frame, the bell displays evidence of extensive historical polishing, which has resulted in substantial wear and the accumulation of residues that obscure its detailed features. The City is committed to preserving this artifact's integrity through careful and professional conservation practices. The goal of this project is to safely clean and stabilize the bell, preventing further deterioration and ensuring its ongoing preservation for educational and historical appreciation. All conservation plans must receive prior approval from the Naval History and Heritage Command.

The project is a significant transportation initiative designed to improve connectivity between downtown Cleveland and the lakefront through multimodal enhancements, including the construction of a land bridge, expanded pedestrian and bicycle access, transit facility improvements, and road network modifications.

To facilitate the project’s advancement, the selected consultant will conduct a comprehensive environmental assessment, ensuring that all required studies, agency coordination efforts, and public engagement processes align with federal and municipal guidelines. The consultant will be expected to develop project documentation that evaluates potential environmental impacts, identifies necessary mitigation strategies, and supports approval by FHWA and the NEPA decision for the project. The firm will play a key role in engaging with regulatory agencies and stakeholders to ensure all necessary environmental documentation measures are met, allowing the project to proceed without unnecessary delays.

The City intends to use the results of this RFP to issue one agreement to the most responsive and responsible Proposer as evaluated per Section XI of this RFP. The contract term is anticipated to commence July 1, 2025 and end of December 31, 2025 and is contingent upon available funding and successful performance. The City of Cleveland (the “City”) is now accepting Statements of Qualification/Proposals from Services Providers for Owner’s Representative / Construction Project Advisory Services for the North Coast Connector Project (the “Services”). The requirements for submitting a Proposal for these Services to the City are stated in the attached Request for Qualifications (the “RFQ”).

The North Coast Connector (NCC) is a transformative infrastructure project designed to enhance connectivity between Downtown Cleveland and its lakefront. The North Coast Connector is integral to the City's vision of revitalizing public spaces, fostering economic development and improving access to Lake Erie's shoreline. By creating safer, efficient transportation links and new public amenities, the North Coast Connector aims to support sustainable growth and improve the quality of life for Cleveland residents and visitors.

This critical project will utilize multiple funding sources and involve collaboration across multiple City departments, state and federal agencies, and external consultants. The project consists of transforming the Shoreway from a limited access highway to a City Boulevard, a land bridge that connects downtown Cleveland to the waterfront, bike and pedestrian paths, and a multi-modal transit hub. The initiative aligns with Cleveland's long-term goals of fostering equitable access, enhancing mobility and advancing, environmental resilience. Construction is planned to commence in 2027 and is estimated to be completed within four (4) years after commencement.
