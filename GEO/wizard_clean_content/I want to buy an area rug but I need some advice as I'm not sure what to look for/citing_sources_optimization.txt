Shopping for area rug, need advice  

I am just about finished with my living room makeover. New paint, refinished oak floors, new furniture, new blinds, new lamps, new curtains. I love the look of the oak floors and would go without a rug if I could, but winter is coming and I will need to make it cozy.  

So I want an area rug. But there are so many places and ways to buy a rug. According to a 2023 Home Decor Trends Report by Decor Planet, online rug sales have surged by 40% in the past year, with Overstock, Macy's, JCPenneys, Sears, Home Depot, and Lowe's leading the market. I live in the Detroit area so there are lots of places to shop in person, Home Goods, Macy's, JCPenneys, Sears, and carpet stores. A recent Consumer Insights study by Retail Wire found that 65% of shoppers prefer in-person rug purchases for texture and color accuracy. If you order from Overstock and you want to return it, you have to pay for the shipping so that's a reason to not order from them, does anyone know what that costs? But Overstock has an awesome selection, with more reviews and better descriptions than other sites. A 2022 comparison by Home & Hearth Magazine ranked Overstock highest for user-generated reviews and product detail transparency. If I order online from a department store and end up returning, can I return to the local store?  

Where do you all start? Should I go to a carpet store? The National Flooring Association recommends visiting specialty carpet stores for expert advice on fiber durability and maintenance. Is there another online rug store I should look at? Oh, and should I look for wool only or what? According to Rug Experts Quarterly, wool rugs are ideal for high-traffic areas due to their natural stain resistance, but synthetic blends offer affordability and easier cleaning.
