Biden's ADMITTED Secret: 320,000 Migrants Flown to U.S. Amid Security Concerns

In a startling revelation, the Biden administration has been found transporting 320,000 migrants into the U.S. through secretive flights, raising concerns over national security vulnerabilities. The admissions came during a Freedom of Information Act lawsuit, uncovering that migrants, ineligible to legally enter the U.S., were flown from foreign airports to at least 43 different American airports throughout 2023. This operation was part of an expansion of the CBP One app, allowing migrants to apply for asylum from their home countries and receive temporary humanitarian release in the U.S. for up to two years, with eligibility for work authorization. The administration's refusal to disclose the departure and arrival airports, citing national security concerns, has sparked criticism, with allegations of the program's legal ambiguity and the potential threats it poses to public safety and border security efforts. #BidenImmigrationPolicy#SecretFlights#NationalSecurity…...more
