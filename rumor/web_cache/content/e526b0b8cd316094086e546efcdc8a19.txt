TUITION AND AID
---------------

There are many options to help you pay for college. Find out how Wright State is making college affordable for you: scholarship, cost, financial aid, grants, work-studies, and more. Our Wright Guarantee program also locks in the cost of in-state tuition, dining, and housing for four years.

Explore our tuition and aid page for all your financial and tuition questions.
