**Posts on social media say that NASA confirmed that there will be 15 straight days of darkness in November.**

**The facts:** Don't fret. No such astronomical event will happen.

On July 14, the website Newswatch33 published an article titled "NASA Confirms Earth Will Experience 15 Days of Complete Darkness in November 2015," Snopes.com reported.

The article claimed that the total darkness would occur from 3 a.m. Sunday, Nov. 15, until 4:15 Monday, Nov. 30, and that such an event hasn't occurred in more than 1 million years.

"Officials" are quoted as saying the blackout will be caused by an "astronomical event" between Venus and Jupiter.

The article says that Charles Bolden, the head of NASA, issued a 1,000-page document explaining the occurrence to the White House.

While Bolden is the legitimate chief of NASA, the Newswatch33 website is not a legitimate news outlet, Snopes.com notes. Newswatch33 is a fake news site that began shortly after the nearly identical NewsWatch28 fake news site shut down in mid-2015.

And, Snopes.com points out, the "15 days of darkness" tale was lifted from an older, debunked viral rumor that has crossed the Internet several times before.

Snopes.com reports that Newswatch33 has fooled social media users a number of times, falsely claiming, for example, that a girl was electrocuted by her iPhone's earbuds, that musical couple Jay-Z and Beyonce bought the rights to the Confederate flag, and that the federal government had put listening devices into inexpensive cigars.
