How much does Genesis G70 insurance cost?
-----------------------------------------

Average car insurance rates for the Genesis G70 cost $2,512 per year, which is about $209 per month. When priced by coverage type, comprehensive (or other-than-collision) coverage costs about $624 a year, liability/medical will cost around $636, and the remaining collision insurance costs around $1,252.

What is the cheapest Genesis G70 insurance?
-------------------------------------------

The cheapest trim level of Genesis G70 to insure is the 2.0T Standard Sedan at an estimated $2,350 per year, or about $196 per month.

The second cheapest model is the 2.0T Standard AWD at $2,406 per year, and the third cheapest trim to insure is the 2.0T Sport Prestige at $2,456 per year.

The most expensive models of Genesis G70 to insure are the 3.3T Sport Prestige AWD at $2,660 and the 3.3T Sport Advanced AWD at $2,596 per year. Those models will cost an extra $310 over the lowest cost 2.0T Standard Sedan model.

Additional rates and information regarding the cost of insurance for a Genesis G70 include:

*   **The older you get, the cheaper insurance rates become.** The difference in G70 insurance rates between a 40-year-old driver ($2,512 per year) and a 20-year-old driver ($5,070 per year) is $2,558, or a savings of 67.5%.
*   **Genesis G70 insurance rates for teens are expensive.** Average rates for full coverage G70 insurance costs $8,876 per year for a 16-year-old driver, $8,644 per year for a 17-year-old driver, and $7,822 per year for an 18-year-old driver.
*   **Tickets and violations can cost a lot.** If you want to get the most affordable G70 insurance rates, it pays to not be an aggressive driver. As few as two minor driving offenses could increase the price of a policy by at least $664 per year.
*   **Get cheaper rates because of your employment.** Most car insurance providers offer policy discounts for having a job in professions like farmers, architects, college professors, lawyers, engineers, police officers and law enforcement, and other occupations. If you can earn this discount, you may save between $75 and $233 on your annual insurance cost, subject to policy limits.
*   **Careful drivers have lower rates.** Too frequent at-fault accidents can raise rates, possibly as much as $1,208 per year for a 30-year-old driver and as much as $736 per year for a 50-year-old driver.
*   **Bring up your credit score and save.** In states that have car insurance regulations that allow credit scores to be used as a policy cost factor, having an excellent credit rating above 800 could possibly save as much as $394 per year when compared to a rating of 670-739. Conversely, a weaker credit score below 579 could cost as much as $457 more per year.
*   **High risk insurance is expensive.** For a 30-year-old driver, having a high frequency of accidents or violations could end up with a rate increase of $3,102 or more per year.
