Multiple Measles cases confirmed in Wisconsin, officials say
============================================================

**By WDIO**

August 2, 2025 - 9:04 PM 

On August 2, the Wisconsin Department of Health (DHS) and county officials have confirmed nine cases of measles in Oconto County, in the northeastern part of the state.

The DHS says all of the cases were exposed to a common source during travel outside of Wisconsin. The first case was verified through testing at Wisconsin’s State Laboratory of Hygiene, with the other eight being confirmed through both exposure and showing symptoms. Citing privacy laws, the DHS says no further information will be released.

DHS and Oconto County officials say they’re coordinating to notify anyone who may have been exposed, though they say risk to the public is low.

Measles, the DHS says, is highly contagious and can be transmitted through the air – and can stay in the air for two hours after aa contagious person coughs or sneezes. They explain that 90% of people around an infected person can become infected themselves if not vaccinated.
