**What to Look For When Buying a Makeup Mirror**

Choosing the best makeup mirror for your vanity is tricky. Here’s a guide to makeup mirrors with lights, magnification, and all the features you need for your best makeup application.

### **Mirror Styles and Designs**  
- **Wall-Mounted Extending Mirror:** Attaches to the wall with hinged or accordion-style arms for adjustable positioning.  
- **Tabletop Mirrors:** Portable options in trifold or round designs, ideal for travel or mobile use.  
- **Hollywood Mirror:** Large mirror surrounded by lightbulbs for even, distortion-free illumination (e.g., Lumina Pro’s Hollywood Mirror).  
- **Crystal Vanity Mirror:** Adjustable color temperature with full-face illumination, available as wall-mounted or tabletop.  

### **Lighting and Magnification**  
#### **Magnification:**  
- **Basic makeup:** 2x-3x magnification for clarity without strain.  
- **Sight-impaired/distant use:** 5x-10x magnification for detail.  
- **Intricate makeup (fantasy/fashion):** 15x magnification for precision (partial face view).  

#### **Lighting:**  
- **Side illumination** prevents shadows (avoid above/below-head lighting).  
- **Daylight-spectrum light** (1000–1400 lumens, 4800–5000K) shows true colors.  
- **Adjustable color settings** (warm/cool, presets for indoor/office/evening) help match real-world lighting conditions.  

### **Size and Mounting**  
- **Mobile mirrors:** Freestanding or foldable, battery/plug-in options (e.g., Lumina Pro’s Professional Ring Light with detachable mirror).  
- **Permanent setups:** Wall-mounted mirrors (require outlets) offer full-face reflection and multiple light options. Larger sizes suit glam rooms (Hollywood/Crystal Vanity Mirrors).  

### **Final Tips**  
Invest in quality—cheap mirrors may break, blur, or malfunction. Choose based on your needs for artistry and self-care.
