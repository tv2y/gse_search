A gas tanker parked in southwestern China’s Sichuan province exploded after the gas inside began to rapidly leak out. The driver and his assistant who were working on the tanker at that time lost their lives. The flames quickly spread to a wide area and damaged another vehicle. The flames were extinguished after an hour. The cause of the explosion was being investigated.

Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelensky was officially received by King Charles III at Windsor Castle during his visit to England for the Coalition of the Willing meeting.

The ceremony included the Ukrainian national anthem and a guard of honor. This marks the third meeting between King Charles and Zelenskyy this year.
