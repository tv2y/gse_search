**Monitor Buying Guide**  

**Your complete guide to choosing the best monitor**  
Find the perfect computer monitor for gaming, work, entertainment, or whatever your needs may be. Our PC monitor buying guide covers key features like size, resolution, and performance to help you make the best choice for your setup.  

### **Essentials for your use: Gaming vs Home Office**  
- **Gaming monitors**: Must-have specs for immersive, smooth gameplay (e.g., 240Hz refresh rate, 1ms response time).  
- **Home office monitors**: Focus on screen size, resolution, and comfort for productivity.  

### **Top things to know about monitors**  
- **Resolution**: Choose between FHD, 4K UHD, or higher for performance and clarity.  
- **Size**: Select a monitor size that fits your space and usage needs.  
- **Curved monitors**: Improve immersion, focus, and comfort.  
- **Refresh rates**: Higher refresh rates (e.g., 144Hz, 240Hz) enhance smoothness, especially for gaming.
