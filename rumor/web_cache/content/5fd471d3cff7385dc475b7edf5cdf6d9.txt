Denouncing Gov. Abbott’s call for special session targeting minority voting rights
==================================================================================

by VENTON JONES July 17, 2025 July 17, 2025

(_Texas House of Representatives_) – Gov. Abbott’s call for a special legislative session set to convene on July 21 includes an alarming 18 agenda items, most notably the premature redistricting of four congressional seats currently held by minority representatives: TX-09, TX-18, TX-29 and TX-33.

The governor’s attempt to prematurely redraw our district lines, particularly targeting the four minority congressional seats identified in the Department of Justice’s July 7, letter, is an egregious act that undermines the very foundation of our democracy. While the DOJ’s Civil Rights Division, under the leadership of Assistant Attorney General Kristen Clarke, rightfully raised serious concerns about existing unconstitutional race-based gerrymandering in these districts, the governor has twisted these concerns into a pretext for a new, politically motivated assault on the voting rights of Texans.

These actions are deeply concerning in nature. The DOJ’s letter explicitly states that districts TX-09, TX-18, TX-29 and TX-33 currently constitute ‘unconstitutional coalition districts’ and involve unconstitutional racial gerrymandering, in violation of the _U.S. Constitution_ and established legal precedents. The DOJ is calling for the State of Texas to bring its current redistricting plans into compliance. Instead, the governor is using this legitimate call for constitutional adherence as an excuse to further disenfranchise Black and minority voters, effectively engaging in reverse racism by misrepresenting the DOJ’s intent to justify his own partisan agenda.

This is not about correcting past wrongs in a just manner; it’s about seizing an opportunity to manipulate district lines to silence the voices of communities of color. Texas, a state with one of the largest Black populations in the country, deserves fair representation, not cynical schemes designed to dilute our electoral power. The governor’s narrative is not only wrong but profoundly hurtful, using what appears to be a distorted interpretation of federal concerns to justify the disenfranchisement of Texans.

Let me be unequivocally clear: I will not stand idly by while the rights of Texas voters are trampled upon. We are ready and willing to fight at all costs to protect all the citizens of Texas. This is a critical moment for our state, and the future of our democracy hangs in the balance.

I urge all Texans to pay close attention to the unfolding events of this special session. It is imperative that the people of Texas understand the gravity of what is happening. We must all be prepared to take action, to speak out and to defend our democratic principles. Your voice, your vote and your engagement are more important now than ever before.

_Venton Jones is a Texas State Representative and House Minority Whip._
