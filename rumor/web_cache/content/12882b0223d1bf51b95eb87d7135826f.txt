The International Experts Summit on Aerospace and Aeronautics Engineering (AAE-2025) is a prestigious global conference bringing together leading minds in aerospace and aeronautics. Scheduled to take place from May 26-28, 2025, in the beautiful city of Valencia, Spain, this summit will serve as a platform for professionals, researchers, engineers, and academics to exchange insights, present research findings, and discuss the latest advancements in aerospace and aeronautics engineering. The event will feature a series of keynote speeches from renowned experts, technical sessions, panel discussions, and networking opportunities designed to foster collaboration and innovation.
