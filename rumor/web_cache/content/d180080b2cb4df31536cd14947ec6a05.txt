A total of _4584 earthquakes_ with a magnitude of four or above have struck within 300 km (186 mi) of China in the past 10 years. This comes down to a yearly average of 458 earthquakes per year, or 38 per month. On average an earthquake will hit near China roughly every 0 days.

Frequently Asked Questions
--------------------------

These questions are commonly asked in relation to earthquakes occurring near China.

#### When was the last earthquake in China?

A 4.5 magnitude earthquake hit near China on the afternoon of October 26, 2025 at 15:04 local time (Asia/Urumqi). The center of this earthquake was located 75km north-northeast of Xinyuan at a depth of 10km under land. Check the list on our website for any earthquakes occurring near China in the past hours.

#### What was the strongest earthquake near China?

A 8 magnitude earthquake hit near China on the afternoon of January 15, 1934 at 16:43 local time (Asia/Shanghai). The center of this earthquake was located 291km southwest of Rikaze at a depth of 15km under land. This is the strongest earthquake that occurred near China since the year 1900.

#### How often do earthquakes occur near China?

In the past 10 years, 4584 earthquakes with a magnitude of four or higher occurred within a 300 kilometer range from China. This averages to 458 earthquakes yearly, or one earthquake every 1 days.
