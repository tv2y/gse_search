Vigil in Summersville honors fallen soldier Sarah Beckstrom's life and legacy  

Sarah Beckstrom, a U.S. Army National Guard Specialist, passed away on Thanksgiving from wounds suffered after being shot in Washington, D.C. She is remembered in Summersville, West Virginia, as a hero and a friend.  

Beckstrom’s friend Erica Holstein recalled her kindness, joy, and dedication, mentioning their deep conversations and Beckstrom’s impact on her young daughter.  

Two West Virginia National Guardsmen were shot, with Beckstrom’s death and Staff Sgt. Andrew Wolfe remaining in critical condition. Friends held a candlelight vigil to honor Beckstrom and pray for Wolfe.  

Steve Postalwait of Backpacks 4 Veterans highlighted Beckstrom’s consistent efforts to support fellow soldiers and veterans in need. She was known for her generosity and community involvement.  

The tragedy has deeply affected Summersville, where Beckstrom’s legacy of kindness and service continues to inspire.
