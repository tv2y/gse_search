Open Streets in full swing on Franklin Avenue
=============================================

###### August 11, 2025 Brooklyn Eagle Staff

Share this:

Facebook Twitter Reddit Flipboard Email Share

**CROWN HEIGHTS — FRANKLIN AVENUE’S OPEN STREETS event on Saturday** transformed the stretch between St. Marks Avenue and Eastern Parkway into a carefree, car-free zone. Part of NYC DOT’s 2025 Open Streets program, the event featured a Brooklyn celebrity pop-up, a giveaway by Brooklyn Book Bodega, an author book signing, one-man band and street performer Murimi Yuma and more.

_Launched June 7 and running through Oct. 25, the full street closure fosters_ _community_ _activities and supports local businesses and residents._
