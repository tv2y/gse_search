A love letter can convey deep affection without explicitly using the word "love." Here are key approaches:

1. **Focus on Specific Emotions and Details**  
   - Describe how the person makes you feel: "Your laughter brightens even the cloudiest days."  
   - Mention their qualities: "It’s the strength in your character, the honor in your word."  

2. **Use Sensory and Poetic Language**  
   - Paint vivid imagery: "Your smile ignites a warmth that wraps around me like a cherished embrace."  
   - Compare feelings to nature or shared moments: "Like the dusk, where the sun and moon meet despite their duties."  

3. **Emphasize Actions Over Words**  
   - Highlight what you admire: "Every time you turn toward me, you find my attention fastened on you."  
   - Express longing: "I long to fold myself inside the circle of your arms."  

4. **Share Personal Memories**  
   - Recall meaningful experiences: "I remember our first sight—your blue shirt, those deep eyes."  
   - Note small gestures: "Your good morning texts, especially with the ❤️ emoji, became my joy."  

5. **Convey Commitment**  
   - Assure permanence: "I am and always will be yours."  
   - Show vulnerability: "Forgive me if I sound silly; you own my every breath."  

**Examples from the Text**:  
- "Your cool hand relieves the fever of my heart."  
- "I notice every single thing about you, even when you don’t realize it."  

Avoid clichés; sincerity and specificity make the letter powerful.
