### The Global Alliance

The Global Alliance Against Poverty and Hunger represents an innovative approach to accelerate efforts to implement the Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs) 1 (No Poverty) and 2 (Zero Hunger).

It aims to provide continuous political drive and foster collective action, building synergies with pre-existing initiatives to combat hunger and poverty. Its mission is to facilitate mobilization and enhance alignment between national and international support, including public and private financial resources as well as knowledge-sharing, to enable the large-scale implementation of evidence-based policy instruments and programs, led by and owned by countries, centred around those most affected by extreme poverty and hunger and people in vulnerable contexts.

The Global Alliance Against Hunger and Poverty welcomes the participation of countries, organizations, and platforms, aiming to leverage innovation, collaboration, and evidence-based decision-making to support the implementation of policies and programs.

*   Our Mission

Our mission is to s upport and accelerate efforts to eradicate hunger and poverty while reducing inequalities and contributing to revitalising global partnerships for sustainable development.

POLICY BASKET
-------------

The Policy Basket is the heart of the Global Alliance Against Hunger and Poverty. It provides a menu of policy instruments and programs that can be applied as-is or adapted to specific contexts, with financial and technical support from Alliance members upon request. The policy instruments and programs are selected based on the following criteria: a clear scope; implementable/implemented by governments; evidence-based; primarily reaching out to persons experiencing hunger and poverty; and contributing primarily to reach SDGs 1 and 2.

MEMBERS
-------

The members of the Global Alliance Against Hunger and Poverty are governments and institutions committed to an innovative approach to mobilize resources and knowledge for the implementation of programs, policies, and social technologies focused on eradicating hunger and poverty.
---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

### Members of the Global Alliance

Membership in the Alliance is open to governments, international organizations, knowledge institutions, development funds and banks, ​as well as​ philanthropic institutions. Member countries can adopt the Alliance’s proposed policy instruments and programs and receive technical and financial support from other members.

Membership is formalized through the issuance of tailored Statements of Commitment. Depending on their nature and capacities, members join one or more of the three Pillars of the Alliance – National, Financial and Knowledge.
