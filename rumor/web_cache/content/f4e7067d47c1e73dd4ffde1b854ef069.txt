UPDATE: At least 19 dead in E China LNG tanker explosion

By Agencies Published: Jun 13, 2020 09:36 PM Updated: Jun 14, 2020 08:00 AM

*    

UPDATE: At least 19 people were killed and 172 injured in Saturday's incident, a local government official told a press conference on Sunday morning. 

Source: social media

An oil tanker reportedly caught fire and exploded on a highway in east China's Zhejiang Province on Saturday.

At about 16:40 Sat, the truck exploded on the highway near Liangshan Village, Zhejiang Province. Part of the surrounding houses and factories collapsed, which has killed tenpeople and injuried more than 117 by 9 pm. Rescue work is underway.
