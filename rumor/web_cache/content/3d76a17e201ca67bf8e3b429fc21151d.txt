#### October

#### Weather in October

October continues the trend of dropping temperatures as autumn deepens. Leaves change to hues of orange and red, decorating Xuchang with a spectrum of autumnal shades. As temperatures continue to calm down from the harsh summer heat, the overall weather remains pleasant, though slightly chillier than the month before.

### Temperature

October comes with a drop in the average high-temperature, falling from September's warm 27°C (80.6°F) to a pleasant 21.7°C (71.1°F). In the month of October, the nighttime temperature in Xuchang dips to a steady average of 10.5°C (50.9°F).

### Rainfall

In Xuchang, in October, during 7.7 rainfall days, 51mm (2.01") of precipitation is typically accumulated. In Xuchang, during the entire year, the rain falls for 86.2 days and collects up to 706mm (27.8") of precipitation.

### Daylight

In October, the average length of the day is 11h and 20min.

On the first day of October, sunrise is at 06:19 and sunset at 18:09. On the last day of the month, sunrise is at 06:43 and sunset at 17:32 CST.
