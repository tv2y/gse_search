Writing Exercise: The Letter  

Letter writing is an excellent exercise for character development and understanding relationships between characters. It exists outside the world of the play but provides deep insight into a character’s thoughts and feelings.  

Instructions: Choose a character and write a letter from their perspective. The letter could be to a parent, loved one, or friend, explaining an action, revealing a secret, or discussing a past event. Alternatively, write a series of letters between two characters to show their relationship's development. The letter could also be addressed to someone not in the play, such as a lost loved one.  

Consider details like the paper used (stationery, napkin) and the writing style (neat, scrawled, scratched-out words). Analyze whether the letter contains lies, hidden emotions, or clear feelings toward the recipient. Pay attention to grammar, vocabulary, and spelling, as these reflect the character’s voice.  

This exercise helps improve dialogue writing by developing a unique and specific voice for each character. A well-crafted letter reveals much about a character’s personality and mindset.
