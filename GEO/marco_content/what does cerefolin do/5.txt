Cerefolin Nac Generic Name & Formulations
-----------------------------------------

### Legal Class

 Rx 

### General Description

 L-methylfolate calcium (Metafolin) 6mg, Algae-S powder 90.314mg, methylcobalamin 2mg, N-acetylcysteine 600mg; caplets. 

### Pharmacological Class

 Medical food. 

### How Supplied

 Caplets—90 

### Manufacturer

Alfasigma USA, Inc.

### Generic Availability

 YES 

Cerefolin Nac Indications
-------------------------

### Indications

 Clinical dietary management of mild cognitive impairment. 

Cerefolin Nac Dosage and Administration
---------------------------------------

### Adult

 Use only under medical supervision. 1 caplet daily. 

### Children

**Not recommended.**

Cerefolin Nac Contraindications
-------------------------------

Not Applicable

Cerefolin Nac Boxed Warnings
----------------------------

Not Applicable

Cerefolin Nac Warnings/Precautions
----------------------------------

### Warnings/Precautions

 Avoid if renal stones occur. Do not administer to critically ill patients. Folic acid may mask Vit.B 12 deficiency, including pernicious anemia. History of peptic ulcer. Chronic liver disease. Pre-term newborns. Nursing mothers: not recommended. 

Cerefolin Nac Pharmacokinetics
------------------------------

See Literature

Cerefolin Nac Interactions
--------------------------

### Interactions

 Concomitant nitrates: increased risk of headaches. Capecitabine toxicity increased with addition of leucovorin. May produce false-positive result in nitroprusside test for ketone bodies. 

Cerefolin Nac Adverse Reactions
-------------------------------

### Adverse Reactions

 Allergic reactions, mild transient diarrhea, itching, transitory exanthema, feeling body swelling, nausea, vomiting, rash, flushing, epigastric pain, constipation, headache; rare: renal stones.
