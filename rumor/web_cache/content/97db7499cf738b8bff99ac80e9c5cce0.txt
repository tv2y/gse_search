The Packers and Steelers meet Sunday night at Acrisure Stadium in Pittsburgh. Kickoff is set for 8:20 PM ET on NBC, with cool fall weather expected around 59 degrees. From underdogs to point spreads, we’ve got the sharpest NFL picks.

Packers Preview
---------------

Green Bay enters with a balanced attack that has carried them through a tough stretch of games. Love has been efficient, throwing for 1,438 yards with 10 touchdowns and only two interceptions on 69 percent passing. His connection with tight end Tucker Kraft, who’s caught 23 passes for 326 yards and four scores, gives this offense a reliable red-zone option. Running back Josh Jacobs has provided muscle on the ground with 414 yards and eight touchdowns, allowing the Packers to control tempo and keep their defense on the sideline. The front seven has been productive, with Rashan Gary’s 5.5 sacks setting the pace up front, while linebacker Quay Walker continues to lead the unit with 59 tackles.

Steelers Preview
----------------

Pittsburgh has relied heavily on veteran quarterback Aaron Rodgers, who’s thrown 14 touchdown passes through six games. Even at 40, Rodgers still reads coverages at an elite level, and wideout DK Metcalf’s 406 receiving yards and four touchdowns have given him a trusted target in key situations. Jaylen Warren has rushed for 311 yards but hasn’t found the end zone much, leaving some of the scoring burden on the passing game. Linebacker Nick Herbig’s 4.5 sacks show he’s quickly becoming a problem for offensive linemen, and Patrick Queen anchors the middle of a defense that can generate turnovers when playing from ahead. However, the Steelers’ run defense has been a concern, especially with injuries limiting depth in the front four.

Steelers vs Packers Prediction
------------------------------

Oddsmakers opened the Packers as slight favorites around -2.5, with the total hovering near 44.5 points. Green Bay’s superior offensive and red-zone finishing give them an edge, though Pittsburgh’s home crowd and Rodgers’ experience make this a competitive clash.

**Prediction: Packers 27, Steelers 23**
