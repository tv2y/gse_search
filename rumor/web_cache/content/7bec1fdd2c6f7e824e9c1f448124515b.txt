### Guyu Lake

_We***59

Guyu Lake is a great place for a relaxing vacation! The spacious road around the lake is perfect for cycling. Cycling with friends, enjoying the scenery and chatting, stopping by the lake when tired, and feeling the tranquility and beauty of nature, it truly relaxes both body and mind.

__
### Longchang Memorial Stone Archways

_We***59

Coming to the Longchang Stone Archways is like stepping into an open-air art museum. The stone archways here vary in style, from simple and elegant to ornate and exquisite. It's a true pleasure to admire the beauty of art here with your partner, experiencing the perfect fusion of history and art.

__
### Nanguan Memorial Arch Ancient Town

M59***93

The surrounding transportation is convenient and the environment is very good. I went there with friends on the weekend. The ticket price is not very expensive and the cost performance is relatively high. Overall, I had a lot of fun. It is very good. As the hometown of ancient Chinese archways, it can also let us learn more about history.

__
### Guyu Lake Confucius Cultural Park

某某市民

Guyu Lake Confucius Cultural Park is a green and vibrant leisure resort. The lake is clear, reflecting the blue sky and white clouds, and the lake surface is sparkling, like a huge mirror. Surrounded by green trees, colorful flowers, and the fragrance of flowers, it is like being in a sea of ​​flowers, making people happy and reluctant to leave.

__
### Yundingzhai Sceneic Area

霓凰。

Yunding Village boasts a unique architectural style, a treasure of traditional Chinese architecture. It blends diverse styles, including those of Southern Sichuan, Hakka, and Huizhou, creating a unique charm. The buildings within the village are strategically arranged and arranged in a well-organized pattern, creating a sense of both overall unity and individual diversity. Each building has been meticulously designed and constructed, reflecting the exquisite craftsmanship and aesthetic taste of the ancients, from the roof tiles to the carved doors and windows.

__
### Lantern City of China

biketripper

The park is quite large, and the lighting is very beautiful. The atmosphere of the festival is immediately there, but there are really a lot of people. You need to park your car on the outer roadside according to the instructions, but there are free shuttle buses along the way.

__
### Fantawild Dino Kingdom

丹麦的约翰尼斯

I've bought here multiple times, and the products are great. My kids love them, and it's trustworthy. I recommend arriving around 9:30 AM (it says it opens at 10 AM, but it actually opens earlier) so you can enjoy the scenery until after the fireworks at 10 PM. I recommend going to Flying Fly first, then Dinosaur Adventure, as these two attractions often have long lines. The rest of the attractions, like Journey to the Center of the Earth, Crossing the Volcano, and Dino Crisis, are all good.

### Yangtze River Cableway

M28***69

The scenery you see on the cable car is different from that outside the cable car. Although there are more than eleven people, the staff are very organized and efficient in their guidance. When you take a number, they will tell you approximately how long it will take to get in line. You can go shopping elsewhere while waiting. The nearby restaurants are also delicious. You can drink tea and eat without delaying anything. It is a rare and comfortable experience. The crispy plums in the nearby Baba tea are delicious and sweet. The night view is also super beautiful. The nearby restaurants are good.
