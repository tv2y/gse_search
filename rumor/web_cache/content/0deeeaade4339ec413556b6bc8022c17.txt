Tour 2025
---------

Manchester United is preparing to embark on a summer like no other, with Ruben Amorim’s Reds set to play seven matches in front of our fans on three continents between May and August.

Tour 2025, presented by Snapdragon, will kick off with two fixtures in Asia following the conclusion of the current Premier League campaign, with the visits to Kuala Lumpur and Hong Kong marking the first post-season trip in the club’s modern era.

After the summer break, our pre-season preparations for the 2025/26 term will then commence back on European soil with a July friendly against Leeds in Sweden, before Tour resumes in the United States of America with three games as part of the latest Premier League Summer Series.

That is before the seven-match schedule concludes on home soil in Manchester, when we welcome Italian side Fiorentina to Old Trafford for the Snapdragon Cup on Saturday 9 August.

You can find out more information on all the dates, locations and opponents below, along with details on how to buy tickets and register for further news alerts…

May: United in Asia
-------------------

#### 28 May: v ASEAN All Stars (Kuala Lumpur) - 8.45pm local time

United’s post-season tour, arranged by ProEvents, will get under way against ASEAN All Stars in the Malaysian capital of Kuala Lumpur. There, the 84,000-capacity Bukit Jalil National Stadium will be the venue for our first game in the city since 2009. The arena was initially built to host the 1998 Commonwealth Games and has since become the national home of Malaysian football.

#### 30 May: v Hong Kong, China (Hong Kong) - 8pm local time

The second of our May matches will see us face Hong Kong, China, at the Hong Kong Stadium. The ground, residing in Causeway Bay on Hong Kong Island, can seat up to 40,000 fans and will host United's first outing in the region since 2013. While staging most of Hong Kong's international football matches, rugby fixtures have also regularly been played at the stadium over the years.

July: United in Sweden
----------------------

#### 19 July: v Leeds (Stockholm) - 3pm local time

After a summer break for Ruben's Reds, preparations for 2025/26 will really kick into gear with our first pre-season test in Stockholm. Old foes and newly promoted Premier League outfit Leeds are the opponents, with rivalries set to be renewed at the recently renamed Strawberry Arena. Formerly known as the Friends Arena, it's where we lifted the Europa League in 2017 after beating Ajax in the final.

July - Aug: United in the USA
-----------------------------

#### 26 July: v West Ham (New Jersey) - 7pm local time

Our Summer Series opener sees us head back to the USA and New Jersey, where we faced Arsenal in front of more than 82,000 supporters during Tour 2023. This time, we'll take on another London opponent in the form of West Ham United, at MetLife Stadium. As well as being home to New York's two NFL teams - the Giants and the Jets - the arena is also set to host next year's FIFA World Cup final.

#### 30 July: v Bournemouth (Chicago) - 8.30pm local time

Stateside game two will be a meeting with Bournemouth at the iconic Soldier Field in Chicago. The stadium holds just over 62,000 fans at full capacity and is currently 100 years old, having first opened in October 1924. United's visit in July will mark our return to The Windy City for the first time since pre-season 2015.

#### 3 August: v Everton (Atlanta) - 5pm local time

Our final stop on Tour 2025 will be Atlanta, for an all-North-West affair with David Moyes's Everton at the state-of-the-art Mercedes-Benz Stadium. The game will be our first-ever in the state of Georgia's most populous city and will therefore be a maiden United outing at the aforementioned arena, which opened in 2017 and features a retractable roof as part of its stunning architecture.

August: United in M16
---------------------

#### 9 August: v Fiorentina (Manchester) - 12.45pm local time

United's pre-season schedule concludes at Old Trafford, where the winner of our game with Fiorentina will be crowned champions of the Snapdragon Cup - a fixture which has been a feature of the men's summer programme since 2023. The Reds most recently played the Florence outfit in 2000, with connections between the clubs in more recent years being through players - the most notable being David De Gea who joined the Italian side last year.
