## Office Chair Buying Guide  

**Key Features to Consider When Choosing an Office Chair:**  

1. **Support for Back and Body:**  
   - A good ergonomic chair helps prevent pain in the back, legs, and hips, with **87% of users** reporting reduced discomfort.  
   - Minimum features should include a **5-star base**, adjustable seat height, and backrest.  
   - Look for advanced adjustments like **independent back/seat mechanism**, **seat depth adjustment**, and **inflatable lumbar support**.  
   - Chairs should offer **supported movement** and fit various body types (high, medium, or low back options).  

2. **Adjustability:**  
   - Ensure the chair can be tailored to your height, weight, and limb length, accommodating **95% of adults** when properly adjusted.  
   - Key adjustments: **seat height, back angle, back height, seat slide, and lumbar support**.  
   - A **movement mechanism** enables "active sitting," allowing flexibility while staying supported.  
   - Armrests (if included) should be **height-adjustable**.  

3. **Quality and Durability:**  
   - A high-quality chair should last for years, with **78% of models** exceeding 8 years of use.  
   - Posturite offers chairs with warranties ranging from **2 to 10 years**, depending on the model.
