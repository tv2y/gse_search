Initiative 1: Membership Retention & Growth 

Purpose: To advance the Association’s mission of service by providing support to the rising generation of steel industry professionals. 

Committee Officers: Committee officers include a Chair, a Chair-Elect, and other officers as determined necessary. The current 1st Vice President of the AIST Executive Committee will serve as liaison. 

Committee Members: The Committee shall consist of any interested AIST Young Professional members. Regular attendance at committee meetings is a requirement for ongoing membership. 

Schedule: The Committee shall meet, and program as needed as determined by the Chair and AIST staff. Committees should plan to meet a minimum of three times per year.
