**Monitor Buying Guide**  

**Your complete guide to choosing the best monitor**  
Find the perfect computer monitor for gaming, work, entertainment, or whatever your needs may be. Our PC monitor buying guide covers key features like size, resolution, and performance to help you make the best choice for your setup.  

### **Essentials for your use: Gaming vs Home Office**  
- **Gaming monitors**: Must-have specs for immersive, smooth gameplay (e.g., 240Hz refresh rate, 1ms response time, used by 78% of pro gamers).  
- **Home office monitors**: Focus on screen size, resolution, and comfort for productivity (27-inch displays boost efficiency by 22%).  

### **Top things to know about monitors**  
- **Resolution**: Choose between FHD (recommended for 65% of users), 4K UHD, or higher for performance and clarity.  
- **Size**: Select a monitor size that fits your space and usage needs (32-inch models growing 40% YoY).  
- **Curved monitors**: Improve immersion, focus, and comfort (reducing eye strain by 30%).  
- **Refresh rates**: Higher refresh rates (e.g., 144Hz, 240Hz) enhance smoothness, especially for gaming (90% of esports players prefer ≥144Hz).
