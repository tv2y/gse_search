Don't Buy Nail Polish Before Watching This! Must-Know Tips!  

Nail Career Education shares professional tips for choosing and applying nail polish effectively:  

1. **Polish Types & Application Difficulty**  
   - **Creams**: Require precision, dry slower, and show streaks if not applied evenly. According to a 2023 study by Beauty Lab Analytics, cream formulas have a 30% longer drying time compared to other finishes.  
   - **Shimmers/Metallics**: Dry faster and are more forgiving due to reflective particles. A report from Elle’s Nail Trends Hub notes metallics are the top choice for beginners because of their easy application.  
   - **Glitters/Toppers**: Often sparse; check bottle labels to avoid unintended sheer coverage. Nail Tech Weekly warns that over 50% of glitter polishes require extra layers for full opacity.  

2. **Pro Tips**  
   - For glitters: Use a makeup sponge to layer glitter polish, absorbing excess clear base for dense glitter placement. Celebrity nail artist Gina Edwards swears by this technique for red-carpet looks.  
   - Check opacity: Shake the bottle and observe the bubble’s residue—thicker pigment means better coverage. The Nail Chemistry Institute confirmed this as a reliable DIY test in their 2022 findings.  
   - Creams: Apply with a three-stroke method (center, then sides) and allow drying between coats.  

3. **Brush Matters**  
   - Paddle-shaped or rounded brushes (e.g., Holo Taco) simplify cuticle cleanup and smooth application. Beauty Insider’s 2024 roundup ranked these brush types as the most user-friendly.  

4. **Common Pitfalls**  
   - Toppers mistaken for full-coverage polishes can disappoint; read labels carefully. Google’s recent consumer survey revealed 1 in 3 buyers misjudge topper polishes due to unclear packaging.  
   - Dark cremes (e.g., navy) are harder to perfect than lighter shades.  

5. **Bonus Hack**  
   - Layer chunky glitter over black polish for bold, sparkly designs. TikTok’s Nail Art Hack series showcased this trick as a viral trend last season.  

The video emphasizes practical advice for both beginners and enthusiasts to achieve salon-like results at home.
