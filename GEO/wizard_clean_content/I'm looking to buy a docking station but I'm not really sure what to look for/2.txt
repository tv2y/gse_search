Here’s the main useful information about the types of ports to consider for a docking station:

**Key Ports for a Docking Station:**  
1. **USB-C (Recommended):** Faster speeds; supports multiple peripherals.  
2. **USB Ports:** For keyboards, mice, and other accessories.  
3. **Ethernet:** Wired internet connection.  
4. **HDMI/DisplayPort/SVGA:** Connect external monitors (HDMI for modern displays, SVGA for older ones).  
5. **LPT/COM Ports:** Legacy support for printers/serial hardware (rarely needed now).  

**Additional Notes:**  
- Modern USB-C docks simplify connectivity (single cable for power, video, and peripherals).  
- Avoid proprietary docks; opt for universal USB-C hubs for broader compatibility.  
- Some docks even charge the laptop via USB-C.
