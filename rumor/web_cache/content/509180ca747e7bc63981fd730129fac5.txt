### **Christina Lorey**

December 6, 2025 · 

ABC News reporter Jonathan Karl has a new book out in which he interviewed the Secret Service agents who were with Trump the day of his “attempted assassination” — and they revealed things that should raise some red flags…

First, after Trump was “shot,” the agents immediately tackled them and told him to STAY DOWN as they piled on top of him — worried the bullet might keep coming.

Trump IGNORES them and, instead, starts wrestling against them to stand up so he can clench his fist towards the crowd and appear “tough” — putting his and all Secret Service agents’ lives in danger.

Then, when they’re safely in a car, one agent remembers Trump asking, “How did that look?”

An unusal first reaction to coming “within millimeters” of your death…
