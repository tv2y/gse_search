Get paid up to 2 days faster* with direct deposit
=================================================
---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Paydays never seem to arrive fast enough. Set up direct deposit and enjoy access to your paychecks up to two days faster.*

Many employers make payroll funds available a day – or even two days – before payday. Golden 1 puts direct deposits in your account as soon as funds are available. If direct deposit is available at your job, here's all they need to get you set up:

1. Your Golden 1 checking or savings account Number

 2. Golden 1’s routing number: 321175261

We've made it easy if you'd like to track it in one place. Fill out the form below and give it to your employer.

*Early deposit of funds is not guaranteed and is dependent on the timing of funding by the payer.

Direct Deposit request form

Direct deposit works for more than just paychecks
-------------------------------------------------

### Recurring payments

Automatically receive recurring payments from Social Security, VA benefits, pension payments, dividend checks, and more.

### Faster tax refunds

You can speed up your tax refunds by requesting direct deposit when filing your taxes.

### Earlier paydays

If your payday falls on a Friday and we are in receipt of your funds, you may be able to access your money as early as Wednesday.

Find Account Number

### Paper Check

1. Locate your paper checks

 2. Account number is the middle 10-digit number at bottom of check after the routing number

Find Account Number

### Online & Mobile

1. Log into Mobile Banking on the G1 app

 2. Click on the specific account

 3. For mobile banking, click on the **Account Details** tab

 4. Click the **eyeball icon**next to**Account number**to show the full number

 5. Your Savings account number will have a suffix "=X". Please do not include.

Find Account Number

### Monthly Statement

1. Locate your monthly statement

 2. Locate the page that lists the name of the account. Your **Checking**account number is the **Book No**.

 3. Your **Savings**account number listed on top corner of the first page under **Account Number**

How to enroll
-------------

* * *

*   ### Enrolling is easy

If direct deposit is available at your job, simply provide them with the following:

1. Your Golden 1 checking or savings account number

2. Golden 1's routing number: 321175261

We've made it easy if you'd like to track it in one place, fill out this form and give it to your employer. 
*   ### Find your account number

To find your account number on a paper check, in Online Banking, or on your monthly statement, refer to the three examples above. 
*   ### For employment paychecks:

Contact your employer’s payroll department to find out if they offer direct deposit and give them our routing number: 321175261. Or, choose and complete the appropriate form below, and submit it to your payroll department: Regular Form California State Employee Form 
*   ### For payments from federal agencies:

If you receive payments from Social Security, the VA, or other agencies, use this form. See instructions on the form for mailing address plus alternate ways to sign up online or by phone. Federal Benefit Payments Our routing number is 321175261.
