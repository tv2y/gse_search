### Answer

True.

### Explanation

1.   Analyze the statement. The statement claims that drone delivery systems are designed to avoid the problems of road conditions and traffic in cities.

2.   Evaluate the statement's truthfulness. Drone delivery systems utilize airspace, bypassing ground-based traffic and road conditions. This makes the statement true.

3.   Refine the explanation. While drones offer advantages in bypassing traffic, the provided explanation includes irrelevant information about cost, weather resistance, and sensor capabilities. These are not directly related to the core functionality of overcoming traffic and road issues.
