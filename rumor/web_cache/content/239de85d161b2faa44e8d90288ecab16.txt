Many provinces in China have been experiencing high temperatures for days, and university dormitories have become "steamers." A college student in Yantai, Shandong Province was sent to the hospital, and the nearby homestays were full.

High temperatures continue in many places in China. Many colleges and universities in Shandong province have suffered from heat stroke and school workers died of heat stroke due to the failure to install air conditioners.

The dormitory administrator who died suddenly worked at the Fushan Campus of Qingdao University. According to students, Master Cebu usually lives in the bungalow of the duty room at the door of the dormitory. There are only electric fans and no air-conditioning inside. You can see the uncle outside the door in the cold.

On the 6th, some netizens broke the news that a dormitory staff member of Qingdao University was found unconscious in the duty room. After being sent to the hospital, the rescue failed and died. Related topics rushed to the social platform Weibo hot search.

On the 5th, a student from the Donghai Campus of Yantai Nanshan College told the reporter of Jimu News that the high temperature weather has been going on for many days. The dormitory building of the school has no air-conditioning, only a small hanging fan, and the wind is limited. Because the temperature is also hot, they can only cool down by flushing.

On July 5, some netizens broke the news that the student dormitory of the Donghai Campus of Yantai Nanshan College was not equipped with air conditioning, and some students were sent to the hospital with heat halo.

On the evening of July 7, several students from the school released footage saying that the dormitory was hot and hot, and the students were exposed to the high temperature. On the evening of the 6th, ambulances appeared to enter and exit intensively. "One night drove in. Five or six ambulances. Some students were carried to stretchers."

On July 5, the highest temperature in most parts of Yantai City was 36 to 39 ° C, and high temperatures above 40 ° C occurred in some areas.

A 60-year-old Cebu Lao from Qingdao University was found unconscious in the duty room on the 6th. He was sent to the hospital and died. The death certificate said "heat injection disease."

He also told reporters that on the morning of July 6, another student in Yingyuan dormitory was taken to hospital by ambulance for heatstroke.
