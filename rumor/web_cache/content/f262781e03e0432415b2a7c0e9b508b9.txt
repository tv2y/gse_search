### Huang Bai Yuan

性别男，爱好女

Huangbaiyuan, located in Taibai County, Baoji City, Shaanxi Province, is a tourist destination in the heart of the Qinling Mountains, known as the "Little Jiuzhaigou of Qinling" and a "Natural Oxygen Bar." The scenery here varies with the seasons: wildflowers bloom in spring, summer is cool and pleasant, autumn paints the forests in vibrant colors, and winter blankets the landscape in snow. Within the scenic area, Dajian Valley boasts beautiful mountains and waters, with colorful streams, babbling brooks, and a glass viewing platform; Walnut Flat is pristine and ancient, with high levels of negative oxygen ions. Numerous farmhouses and guesthouses are located nearby. Huangbaiyuan Inn, for example, is clean and hygienic, with a welcoming owner, making it suitable for family trips. However, travel from Xi'an and other areas is time-consuming, and some areas of the scenic area require improvement, such as unclear parking signage. It is recommended to visit early and return early, and a 1-2 day trip is advisable. Overall, it is a great place for leisure, relaxation, and getting close to nature.

### Qin Ling

M27***06

The Qinling Mountains can be divided into the Qinling Mountains in a narrow sense and the Qinling Mountains in a broad sense. The Qinling Mountains in a narrow sense are limited to the mountains between the Weihe River and the Hanjiang River in the southern part of Shaanxi Province. The Qinling Mountains in a broad sense start from Kunlun in the west, pass through Longnan and southern Shaanxi in the middle, and end at the Dabie Mountains of Hubei, Henan, Anhui and Zhangbaling near Bengbu in the east. It is the watershed between the Yangtze River and the Yellow River. The Qinling Mountains are not only the boundary between the north and south of China, but also the dividing line of the differences in geography, climate and resources between the north and the south. Different climates, different water and soil, different social living habits such as diet, architecture and transportation, have evolved into differences in the overall customs and habits of the society over time. The Qinling Mountains are revered as the dragon vein of Chinese civilization. The main peak, Taibai Mountain, is 3771.2 meters above sea level and is located in Baoji City, Shaanxi Province. The Qinling Mountains are the boundary between the Guanzhong Plain and southern Shaanxi in Shaanxi Province.

### Taibai Qingfengxia Forest Park

无为的无心草

The snowy landscape of Qingfengxia Forest Park in Taibai County resembles a breathtaking winter painting, offering a dreamlike experience. The following describes its unique characteristics from various perspectives: Overall Atmosphere After the snow falls, the entire Qingfengxia Gorge is blanketed in a blanket of silver, transforming into a fairytale world. Morning mist shrouds the distant snow-capped mountains and forests, creating a hazy, almost ethereal ink painting, creating a tranquil and ethereal atmosphere that evokes a sense of wonderland. Tree Landscape Snowflakes gently blanket the branches, making each tree appear draped in a pure white veil. The evergreen pine trees are also covered in snowflakes, which fall in a gentle breeze. Branches and trunks resemble ice sculptures, each shaped in a unique pattern, shimmering in the sunlight. Mountain Trails The mountain trails are lightly dusted with fine snow, and the wooden plank roads appear to be covered with a thick blanket. Walking along the plank road, the snow in the near distance becomes clear, while the snow in the distance gradually blurs. Each step is imbued with a profound poetic quality, leaving visitors feeling as if they are "walking through a painting." Icefalls and Rime Influenced by the scenic area's unique climate, Qingfeng Gorge in winter boasts not only beautiful snowscapes but also spectacular icefalls and rime landscapes. The cascading waterfalls, frozen in the cold, create a variety of majestic icefalls, while the rime adds a unique beauty to trees like pine trees: graceful, charming, soft, translucent, and shimmering. Moments of Life In this pristine white world, there are many moving moments. Take a foraging squirrel or a couple cuddling together to enjoy the snow. They inadvertently appear in the camera, becoming the warmest winter moments and bringing life and energy to the tranquil snowscape. If you want to go to Qingfengxia Forest Park to enjoy the snow, please note that the scenic area is open from 8:30 to 18:00. The temperature difference between morning and evening is large in the scenic area, so it is recommended to bring a warm jacket, non-slip shoes, hiking poles, etc., and pay attention to travel safety.

### Baiji Bronze Ware Museum

折翼中坠落的燕子

Baoji, known as the "Hometown of Bronze Ware," boasts a vast collection of high-quality bronze artifacts. The China Bronze Ware Museum, located in Baoji, is a highly recommended destination by local friends. 👍 The museum houses nearly a thousand bronze artifacts from the Shang and Zhou dynasties, including national treasures such as the He Zun, Qin Gong Bo, and Lai Pan. Each piece deserves your careful appreciation. I spent over three hours there and still felt I hadn't seen enough (I definitely need to stay in Baoji for a while to come back and enjoy them more thoroughly). Here, you can not only feel the weight of history but also appreciate the charm of art. Even if you know nothing about bronze ware, as long as you calm down and follow the introductions to each section, you can understand it. As a national-level museum, its curatorial capabilities are exceptional, with a clear and well-structured presentation. [Thumbs up] The inscriptions on the artifacts are the highlight of each treasure. We usually just look at them for the spectacle, but this time, with annotations of bronze inscriptions to aid understanding, it's a must-see for anyone interested. This exhibition shows considerable effort and is well worth the visit. The exhibition is located on the third floor and divided into four sections: Hometown of Bronze Ware, Land of Zhou Rituals, The Road to Empire, and The Light of Wisdom. There is also a temporary exhibition on the second floor: Vessels and Appraisals, which is also highly recommended.

### Zhonghua Shigu Yuan

郭润龙浪迹天涯

20230405 afternoon: 1. "China Stone Drum Park" is a comprehensive park with the theme of "China's first ancient artifact" stone drum, integrating culture, catering, entertainment, sightseeing and leisure. The stone drum is one of the "nine national treasures of China". The text on the stone drum records the history before Qin Shihuang unified the country, filling the historical gap. It is the earliest stone-carved poetry and seal script in China. It was first discovered in the first year of Zhenguan in the Tang Dynasty (627) in Baoji City's "Shigu Mountain" (anciently known as "Chen Cang Mountain", the scenic spot was built on this mountain). There are 10 faces. At first, it was regarded as auspicious and other gods. It was studied and studied by emperors and literati of all dynasties. After the Ming and Qing Dynasties, one word was worth ten thousand gold. The scenic area is divided into two major areas: "Baoji Bronze Museum" and "Shigu Pavilion". Admission is free. ① Address: Binhe Avenue, Weibin District, Baoji City, Shaanxi Province. ② Transportation: Direct bus, easy to park by car ③ Peak season: None. ④ Duration: 0.5 days. 2. Baoji Bronze Museum is a national first-class museum with bronze culture as its theme. It houses countless bronze cultural relics from more than 2,000 years ago. The building is a four-story square built against the mountain, surrounded by a sloping dome in the center like a stone drum. You can hire a guide in the exhibition hall, which is divided into 7 halls, namely: "Hometown of Bronze" (summarizes the unearthed bronzes near Baoji and displays a large number of precious cultural relics), "State of Zhou Li" (bronze products of the Zhou Dynasty, including wax statues, tombs restored according to the real placement, and pre-Qin chariots and horses, which are very immersive), "Road to Empire" (showing the migration trajectory of the Qin people and the process of the Qin State from weak to strong and finally unifying China. The Qin people have taken root in Baoji for more than 400 years), "Light of Wisdom" (the center is a circle of bronze making process Sculpture), "Looking at the Mirror with Yellow Flowers" (displaying ancient mirrors, the sculpture of a lady looking at the mirror in the center is very beautiful, it is a photo spot), "Pottery Language Tells the Spring and Autumn Period" (displaying ancient pottery, the small clay sculpture of the pottery making process in the center is very vivid), "The Moon Shines on the Jade" (displaying ancient jades, the crystal jades are dazzling under the ancient background and lighting), and there are graphic exhibitions "Wandering Wanderers" and "Homesickness" in the hall, both of which introduce the Baoji bronzes lost overseas, as well as Chinese and foreign celebrities who have made great contributions to the protection and recovery of Chinese cultural relics. The spectacular "Zhou Culture Wall" is embedded in the natural cliff on the north side of the museum, telling a series of important events in the Zhou Dynasty starting from the "birth of Hou Ji", showing the development and growth history of the Zhou and Qin nationalities. The pink flowers beside the wall are brilliant and beautiful. 3. Go west out of the museum and pass through the garden sculpture area first. This area is composed of many small gardens, including the "Five Virtues Garden" (composed of 5 small gardens: the "Ren Garden" has a sculpture "Yu Rui Dispute", which tells the story of the kings of Yu and Rui going to the Zhou Dynasty to dispute over land and being influenced by the Zhou Dynasty's etiquette and benevolence; the "Yi Garden" has a sculpture "Tai Bo Runs to Wu", which tells the story of Tai Bo and Zhong Yong giving up the throne of Wu to their younger brother Ji Li; the "Li Garden" has a sculpture "Duke Zhou Establishes Rituals" to commemorate the brother of King Wen of Zhou, Duke Zhou Ji Dan, who established the ritual and music system...
