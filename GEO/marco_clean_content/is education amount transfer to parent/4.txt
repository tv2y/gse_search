Students can transfer unused tuition tax credits to their parents, spouse, common-law partner, or grandparent. To transfer credits, students must complete the transfer section of the tax certificate and provide it to the recipient, who then enters the amount on line 32400 of their tax return.  

Tuition credits can be claimed even if parents paid the tuition, unless an employer reimbursed it. Unused credits can be checked via CRA My Account or the Notice of Assessment. Parents cannot directly claim a child’s tuition; the student must first claim and then transfer unused amounts (up to $5,000 federally and provincially in Ontario).  

Unused credits from previous years can be carried forward, but any missed claims require amending prior tax returns. Laptops may qualify as educational expenses if deemed necessary for enrollment.  

Tuition credits can be transferred for both federal and provincial tax purposes. Attending a designated institution like Robertson College qualifies students for tuition tax credits. Robertson offers short-duration programs in fields like Community Services, Health, Business, and Technology.
