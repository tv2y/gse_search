New Perspectives on The Physiological Basis of Muscle Loss
----------------------------------------------------------

**04 Sep 2024 09:00 - 05 Sep 2024 16:00 - University of Exeter Life Sciences Institute Exeter EX4 4QR**

This meeting will probe new perspectives on the underlying causes of muscle loss in the context of ageing, disuse, inactivity, and malnutrition, whilst also exploring intervention strategies to mitigate such losses. Across the two days, there will be a varied programme of evidence, ranging from mechanistic data to clinical application.
