### **How to Buy a Wig for the First Time**  

#### **1. Selecting a Wig**  
- **Synthetic vs. Human Hair:**  
  - *Synthetic wigs:* Affordable ($50–$500), low-maintenance, good for short styles ("shake-n-go").  
  - *Human hair wigs:* Versatile ($600–$4,000), can be styled like natural hair, longer-lasting but require more care.  
- **Lace Front Wigs:** Provide a natural hairline.  
- **Face Shape & Color:**  
  - Round faces: Short pixie or long layers.  
  - Heart-shaped: Side-swept bangs or shoulder-length styles.  
  - Cool skin tones: Platinum blondes, ash browns. Warm skin tones: Honey blondes, chestnut browns.  

#### **2. Fitting the Wig**  
- **Measure Your Head:** Average circumference is 22 inches.  
- **Try It On:** Adjustable tabs ensure comfort. Use wig grips or cushion bands for security.  
- **Where to Buy:** Wig stores or online (check return policies). Insurance may cover wigs for medical use.  

#### **3. Caring for the Wig**  
- **Human Hair Wigs:** Wash every 6–8 wears with mild shampoo/conditioner. Air-dry on a wig stand.  
- **Synthetic Wigs:** Use synthetic wig shampoo. Avoid heat (irreversible damage).  
- **Brushing:** Use a wig brush; never brush wet hair.  

#### **Additional Tips**  
- Budget-friendly options: Synthetic wigs or shorter lengths.  
- Beginner-friendly: Headband wigs or premade styles.  
- For realistic looks: Consider lace fronts with pre-plucked hairlines.
