Continuation from February 21 (Day 2)

6:00 PM+ - We had dinner at a random restaurant in 磁器口 called 小颜哥江湖菜, and it turned out to be a nightmare. Since we were in a rush to get to our next destination (下浩里), we wanted a quick meal nearby. As we walked by, a few of the staff were very enthusiastic, promoting the place and pulling us inside. We decided to give it a try. Once inside, we noticed there were no other customers, even though it was dinner time. We thought maybe the competition in the area was tough, so we gave them the benefit of the doubt. After browsing the menu, nothing looked appetizing, so we opted for some simple dishes. To our surprise, we waited nearly an hour for our food, despite there being no other customers. When the dishes finally arrived, they were awful and unpleasant to eat. We also noticed that the plates, bowls, and chopsticks weren’t properly cleaned. The food was not cheap either; the prices were comparable to those of a proper restaurant. Finally, we understood why there were no customers in the store. Rating 1/⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐

8:00 PM+ - Finally, we reached our next venue at 下浩里. The night scenery was stunning. Although it was crowded, the view made it all worth it. Just be aware that there are a lot of stairs to climb up and down.

11:00 PM+ - Back to hotel to rest!

Look out for next part for my Chengdu & Jiuzhaigou post! /Chongqing

Chongqing offers a diverse range of dining options and stunning nightlife that can impress any traveler. While enjoying the vibrant culinary scene, it’s essential to choose restaurants that have positive reviews, as not all places guarantee a good experience. For example, while managing your itinerary, be cautious of restaurants with no customers, as they might indicate a lack of quality. After our unfortunate dinner, we made our way to 下浩里, known for its scenic views and lively atmosphere. The unique architecture and vibrant lights create an enchanting sight at night, bustling with locals and tourists alike. However, it’s worth noting that the venue requires navigating a lot of stairs, so be prepared for some physical activity. Overall, ensuring a balance of good food and great sights is crucial for a memorable trip in Chongqing!
