My idea of a perfect cozy night in involves a clean space, my favorite candle lit, my latest reality TV obsession on, and a bowl of my favorite snacks.The glue that holds it all together? A cozy throw blanket draped over me. No matter what, every time I try to chill out and relax, I seem to always scold myself for not having picked up a throw blanket yet. They’re functional and decorative and offer you and your guests a useful resource to turn to if you’re looking to achieve maximum cozy vibes.

Throw blankets are also a great way to quickly change the vibe of your space from season to season.You can welcome in fall with a chunky knitted blanket, ease into winter with sherpa or faux fur, and bring on the spring and summer vibes with a lightweight gauzy throw. You can even opt for more seasonal-leaning patterns like plaids or florals, depending on the time of year. I’m serious when it comes to finding the perfect throw blanket, and I’ve got 48 of the best throw blankets you can snag below.
