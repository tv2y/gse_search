President Donald Trump Speaks to Reporters on Air Force One in Qatar Today!

U.S. President Donald Trump spoke with reporters on Air Force One during a refueling stop in Qatar on October 25, 2025, while en route to a multi-nation tour of Asia. Onboard the plane at Al Udeid Air Base, Trump also held a meeting with Qatar's Emir Sheikh Tamim bin Hamad Al Thani and Prime Minister Mohammed bin Abdulrahman Al Thani. Key topics from the gaggle and meeting included: Middle East Peace Efforts: Trump praised Qatar for its "very big factor" role in helping broker peace deals in the region. He told reporters that if the Gaza ceasefire between Israel and Hamas does not hold, Hamas would have a "very big problem". International Peacekeeping: He stated that Qatar was willing to contribute troops to an international peacekeeping or stabilization force for Gaza if needed. India-Pakistan Ceasefire: Trump claimed credit for brokering a ceasefire between India and Pakistan following a terror attack in April 2025. Russia-Ukraine Conflict: When asked about a potential meeting with Russian President Vladimir Putin, Trump said he would need assurance of a deal and would not "be wasting my time". U.S.-China Trade: In anticipation of his meeting with Chinese President Xi Jinping, Trump expressed confidence in negotiating a trade deal and possibly discussing Russia-Ukraine issues. Canada Tariffs: He also announced that he was imposing an additional 10% tariff on Canadian goods. Trump's stop in Qatar occurred as he traveled to attend the ASEAN summit in Malaysia, followed by visits to Japan and South Korea.
