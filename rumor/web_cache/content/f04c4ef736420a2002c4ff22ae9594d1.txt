About the event
---------------

**PPP Americas is the most important event in Latin America and the Caribbean on public-private partnerships (PPP).** Held every two years in member countries, it has established itself as a strategic space for dialogue and the promotion of sustainable projects between the public and private sectors.

The Inter-American Development Bank (IDB), in collaboration with the Ministry of Economy and Finance of Peru (MEF) and ProInversión, presents this 12th edition of PPP Americas, which will take place in Lima, Peru, from April 8 to 10, 2025. This event brings together government leaders, investors, and international experts to discuss the main challenges and opportunities in the region.

**Modalities of participation:**

*   Virtual live streaming: April 8 and 9
*   On-site participation (by invitation only): April 8, 9, and 10 (technical visits to PPP projects will take place on the last day)

**The event will have simultaneous interpretation in Spanish, English and Portuguese.**

Watch this session here

Latin America has been leading the global movement to diversify the type of assets built and managed by the private sector. This process has also led to a diversification of the type of public authorities that sign PPA contracts. In fact, several countries in the region have transferred the role of their PPP programs to subnational entities, such as territories, states, or municipalities. This event will discuss how private participation in infrastructure at the local level can bring the benefits of PPPs closer to public service users and generate value for money for important sectors of the public sector (such as health and education). In addition, the event will explore the challenges that subnational entities face, such as credit problems and institutional capacity building. Participants will present some of the most successful experiences in the region and reflect on the future of subnational PPPs in Latin América.
