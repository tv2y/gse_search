**Original Post:**  
- User wants advice on buying their first flat-screen TV after their old 32-inch tube TV stopped working.  
- Budget: Under $500, preferably around $300.  
- Considering buying used from Craigslist or new from Best Buy.  
- Questions:  
  1. Are Best Buy TVs displayed with artificially enhanced signals?  
  2. Will a larger screen (43" or 55") make the picture grainy compared to the old 32"?  
  3. Does "HD" mean FIOS will send an HD signal, or is an upgrade required?  

**Key Responses:**  
1. **Showroom Settings:**  
   - TVs in stores (e.g., Best Buy) are set to maximum brightness/contrast for display purposes. Adjust settings (e.g., "Cinema" mode) at home for better quality.  

2. **Picture Quality:**  
   - Modern TVs handle upscaling well; graininess is less of an issue.  
   - Higher resolution (1080p or 4K) is recommended for larger screens to maintain sharpness.  

3. **HD Signals:**  
   - Most services include HD for free but may charge extra for 4K.  
   - Verizon FIOS likely provides at least 720p or 1080p. Check if your cable box supports HD.  

4. **Recommendations:**  
   - **Budget Options:** TCL 5-Series (~$400) or Vizio M-Series Quantum.  
   - **Size:** Choose based on room size and viewing distance.  
   - **Used vs. New:** Used plasma TVs (Craigslist) can offer great value (~$100), but new TVs in the $300–$500 range are competitive.  

5. **Additional Tips:**  
   - **Sound:** Built-in TV speakers are often poor; consider a soundbar.  
   - **Connections:** Ensure the TV has necessary ports (HDMI-ARC for sound systems).  
   - **Disposal:** Recycle old tube TVs; some retailers (e.g., Best Buy) offer removal services.  

**Consensus:**  
- Any modern flat-screen TV will be a significant upgrade over an old tube TV.  
- Focus on resolution (1080p or 4K) and size relative to your room.  
- For budget buyers, TCL, Vizio, or store-brand models (e.g., Insignia at Best Buy) offer good value.  

**Final Suggestion:**  
- For simplicity, buy a new 43"–55" 4K TV (e.g., TCL or Vizio) within the $300–$500 range. Adjust settings post-purchase for optimal viewing.
