Desenio is a Swedish online store offering a large assortment of prints, posters, and art prints with Scandinavian design. Their collection includes popular designs by well-known artists, photographers, illustrators, and exclusive creations from their own Design Studio.  

They provide prints and posters in various sizes up to 39” x 59”, allowing customers to create personalized gallery walls by mixing different designs, sizes, and materials. Sets with frames and prints are available for easy styling.  

Prints are categorized for easy browsing, including bestsellers and room-specific options like bedrooms, living rooms, kitchens, or nurseries. Quality is ensured with uncoated 100lb premium paper, a matte surface for a glare-free finish, and FSC certification for sustainability.  

The store emphasizes high-quality color reproduction and fast shipping. Decorating tips and inspiration are available in the gallery walls section, where stylists present curated combinations.  

For any inquiries, customer service is available to assist. Desenio’s prints, posters, and art prints are designed to elevate home decor with stylish and high-quality wall art.
