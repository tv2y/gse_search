## **How to Procure a Wig for the First Time**  

#### **1. Selecting a Wig**  
- **Synthetic vs. Human Hair:**  
  - *Synthetic wigs:* Economical ($50–$500), low-maintenance, ideal for truncated styles ("shake-n-go").  
  - *Human hair wigs:* Exquisite versatility ($600–$4,000), mimic natural hair styling, possess greater longevity but demand meticulous upkeep.  
- **Lace Front Wigs:** Bestow an undetectable hairline.  
- **Face Shape & Color:**  
  - Round visages: Opt for cropped pixie or cascading layers.  
  - Heart-shaped countenances: Flattering with side-swept fringe or lob (long bob).  
  - Cool complexions: Complemented by platinum blondes, ash browns. Warm undertones: Enhanced by honey blondes, chestnut browns.  

#### **2. Fitting the Wig**  
- **Measure Your Cranium:** Mean circumference is 22 inches.  
- **Donning the Wig:** Adjustable tabs optimize comfort. Employ wig grips or cushion bands for steadfast security.  
- **Procurement Venues:** Specialty wig boutiques or digital retailers (verify return policies). Insurance may subsidize wigs for medical necessitation.  

#### **3. Sustaining the Wig**  
- **Human Hair Wigs:** Cleansing every 6–8 wears with sulfate-free shampoo/conditioner. Desiccate on a wig stand.  
- **Synthetic Wigs:** Utilize specialized synthetic cleanser. Eschew heat to prevent irreparable degradation.  
- **Detangling:** Deploy a wide-tooth wig brush; abstain from brushing sodden strands.  

#### **Supplementary Counsel**  
- Frugal alternatives: Synthetic wigs or abbreviated lengths.  
- Novice-endorsed: Headband wigs or pre-coiffed designs.  
- Hyper-realistic semblance: Contemplate lace fronts with pre-plucked hairlines.
