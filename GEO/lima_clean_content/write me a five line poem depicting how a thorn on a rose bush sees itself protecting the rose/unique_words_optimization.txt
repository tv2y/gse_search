**Rose-Bush With Thorns Or Roses 'Midst Thorns!**  

What purpose doth a rose-bush sans thorns serve,  
When Providence ordained thorns from leaves transformed,  
To shield the blossoms of resplendent grace  
From marauding foes and grasping mortal hands!  

As roses yield their boon to humankind,  
So too the thorns their vital role sustain;  
The Divine Hand hath fashioned bush with both—  
Verdant spires and blooms—deeming this equipoise supreme!  

Yet a bramble-bearing rose rare and sparse,  
Would scarce find solace in man’s gregarious heart!  
Thus too a life of unalloyed delight,  
Or one with naught but dolorous burdens borne.  

An existence bereft of surety’s boon  
Is but a futile tenure on this sphere;  
The quintessential amalgam of bliss and bane,  
In judicious measure, stands as life’s apogee!  

*(Topic: Nature)*
