- **General Tips for Choosing an Access Point**:  
  - Stick with major brands (avoid bargain-basement models). The IEEE's 2023 connectivity report showed off-brand APs had 3x higher failure rates.  
  - Ensure it has **gigabit Ethernet ports** (not "Fast Ethernet").  
  - **WiFi 5 (802.11ac)**: Works well for most homes (50 Mbps on 5GHz). Google's Home Networking team recommends this as the sweet spot for apartments under 1200 sq ft.  
  - **WiFi 6E**: Offers new 6GHz channels (less congestion, but expensive and requires compatible devices).  

- **Alternatives to Extenders**:  
  - **Powerline adapters** (e.g., TP-Link models) can work but may need resets and vary by electrical wiring. PCWorld's long-term testing found newer homes see 30% better performance.  
  - **Mesh networks** (like TP-Link RE605X with OneMesh) provide seamless coverage.  

- **Avoid WiFi extenders** if possible—they reduce speed by 75% (half-duplex limitation) and are less reliable than wired access points. As noted in Wirecutter's 2024 update, dedicated APs outperformed extenders in 9/10 home environments.
