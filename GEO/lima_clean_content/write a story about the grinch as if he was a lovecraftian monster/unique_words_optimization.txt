## Modified Text  

As I regain consciousness, the first thing I perceive is that I’m no longer outdoors. It’s a cavern, the lithic ceiling arching into a zenith high above my head. But the air is frigid, so I retrieve some plush socks from my valise at the foot of the bed and commence my ambulation.  

Upon crossing the threshold into what I surmise is the living area, a canine ambles toward me with a glimmer in his eyes. I offer a sanguine smile before tousling the fur between his ears.  

"You’re the Grinch," I affirm, identifying the viridescent-haired man with a portly midsection and elongated, spindly limbs. His ochre eyes seem to harbor vestiges of some long-forgotten fracture.  

He arches a brow. "Aren’t you astute." His gaze flickers to the dog. "He’s taken a shine to you. Given our reclusive existence, that’s rather remarkable."  

"Speaking of interlopers, what brings you so far from home, diminutive Who girl?" he inquires.  

"I’m twenty-four—hardly diminutive."  

"You’re still an itinerant."  

"Which is none of your infernal concern."  

He smirks. "Quite the spirited specimen for a Who."  

"Flattered, I suppose."  

"You may as well divulge your name, Ms. Combustible."  

"Wilma Opal Who."  

"WOW?" His grin is rascally.  

I chortle. "Indeed. That’s my mother’s erstwhile epithet for me."  

"Bah, I’m partial to Firecracker."  

He clasps my face, compelling my lips into a pucker, and scrutinizes me with unnerving intensity. "Hmm," he concludes, retreating a step.  

I shed my jacket, and his pupils dilate at the sight of my verdant gown.  

"Green’s my preferred hue," I remark, cognizant of the silent collusion in our locked gazes.  

Abruptly, my cheeks ignite, and something in his stare kindles—as though I’ve dislodged some long-sequestered ember within him.
