The end table is a piece of furniture you really can’t do without in your living room. If you don’t have an end table yet, here are some things to consider when buying one.  

**Decide on a Shape**  
There are two main shapes to choose from: circle and square. Square end tables offer more table space, while circular versions are easier to maneuver around and fit better with room flow.  

**Pick Out a Style**  
Choose a style that matches your current décor, considering materials, colors, and design. End tables can also serve as accent pieces without needing to fully match the room’s theme.  

**Find the Right Size**  
Consider whether you need storage space or just a surface for drinks. Larger tables offer more utility, but avoid sizing that extends beyond your couch or chair to prevent awkward placement and accidents. Measure your space before purchasing.
