China to step up earthquake preparedness efforts in 2026
========================================================

Source(s):China Daily

Upload your content

China will intensify efforts in 2026 to improve earthquake disaster prevention and mitigation, emergency response and early-warning capabilities.

[...]

In 2026, the country will release a national plan on earthquake disaster prevention and mitigation for the 15th Five-Year Plan period (2026-2030).

[...]

Priorities will be given to improving earthquake resistance in urban and rural areas, and strengthening the seismic resilience of major infrastructure and engineering projects.

[...]

By the end of 2025, the country's earthquake early-warning system had more than 240 million users. In 2025, it issued around 300 alerts, and served over 18 million people.
