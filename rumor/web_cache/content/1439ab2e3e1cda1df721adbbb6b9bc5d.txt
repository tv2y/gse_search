The Cook Political Report

Below is our 2025 Cook Partisan Voter Index (Cook PVI℠) score for all 50 states and the District of Columbia. The 2025 Cook PVI scores were calculated using the 2020 and 2024 presidential election results. Learn more about PVI and how it's calculated.
