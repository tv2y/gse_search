Texas: Bomb-Making Material (BMM) and Explosive Device Discovered During Search, One Arrested
---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

_Submitted by NL0387 on Fri, 08/29/2025 - 15:36_

On 25 July 2025, a 48-year-old Hill County man was arrested for allegedly possessing bomb-making material (BMM) and explosive devices at his residence. The Hill County Sheriff’s Office stated the suspect “intentionally and knowingly possessed components of an explosive weapon with the intent to combine the components into an explosive weapon for use in a criminal endeavor.” Inside his home, investigators found fuse sections, small pill bottles used to contain the explosives, electrical tape, as well as mixed and unmixed exploding target material (ETM).

Arkansas: 19-year-old Arrested for Building Pipe Bomb
-----------------------------------------------------

_Submitted by NL0387 on Fri, 08/29/2025 - 15:31_

On 9 July 2025, a 19-year-old man was accused of constructing a pipe bomb and hiding it under a dresser in his bedroom. According to an affidavit for probable cause, the suspect informed his ex-girlfriend about the device and its location. The suspect’s ex-girlfriend then notified his mother, who subsequently located and removed the device, placing it on the kitchen table. She contacted the suspect’s probation officer via email to report the situation. The probation officer then notified the Baxter County Sheriff’s Office.

Texas: Homemade Explosives (HME) Lab Required On-site Disposal; One Arrested
----------------------------------------------------------------------------

_Submitted by chaunceym on Mon, 06/16/2025 - 15:23_

On April 15, 2025, in Austin, Texas, Austin Police responded to reports of explosives manufacturing at a home on Vintage Stave Road. The confirmation of explosives manufacturing prompted evacuations in the neighborhood, and the bomb squad was called in to investigate. After a thorough examination, the bomb squad safely detonated the sensitive explosives on-site. The incident caused temporary road closures. A suspect was taken into custody.

Texas: Pipe Bomb Discovered Near Bridge
---------------------------------------

_Submitted by emcjohnson on Wed, 02/12/2025 - 09:57_

On 19 January 2025, at approximately 1400 hours local time, Wichita County Sheriff’s Office deputies responded after a passerby reported seeing a suspicious device on the bank of the Wichita River at the bridge over FM Road 369 in Wichita Falls. Deputies used a UAS to examine the device and called the Fort Sill bomb squad, Wichita West volunteer firefighters, and a Wichita Falls police officer associated with the ATF for assistance. The bomb squad used an X-ray to examine the device and determined that it was a live IED, described as a pipe bomb.

Louisiana: RCIEDs and Explosive Material Found Following Vehicle Ramming, 14 Killed and Over 30 Injured
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

_Submitted by emcjohnson on Tue, 02/11/2025 - 18:32_

On 1 January 2025, at approximately 0315 hours local time, the New Orleans Police Department (NOPD) responded to reports of a vehicle ramming into a crowd of pedestrians on Bourbon Street, which killed 14 people and injured over 30 others. Texas: BMM Found Home and Storage Unit of New Orleans Vehicle-Ramming Attacker
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

_Submitted by emcjohnson on Tue, 02/11/2025 - 17:50_

On 7 January 2025, Harris County Sheriff’s Office deputies and agents from the FBI’s Houston field office discovered BMM while searching a storage unit on the 11900 block of Walters Road in Houston that was connected to the suspect in the New Orleans vehicle-ramming attack. The BMM, described as “bottles of sulfuric acid,” were reportedly purchased at a nearby hardware store and were discovered inside a cooler within the storage unit.

Texas: Suspect Threatened Police with IED
-----------------------------------------

_Submitted by AChevez1 on Tue, 02/04/2025 - 13:13_

On 13 January 2025, at approximately 0820 hours local time, the Teague Police Department responded to a residence in Teague after a suspect reportedly threatened that they were "going to blow up the police when they come back." Officers observed an IED on the property and called the McLennan County Sheriff’s Office Special Operations Unit, ATF, and other nearby law enforcement authorities to assist. Officials executed a search warrant at the residence and the special operations unit rendered the device safe without incident.

Texas: Explosives Seized After Police Standoff, 1 Arrested
----------------------------------------------------------

_Submitted by AChevez1 on Mon, 01/06/2025 - 12:40_

On 11 December 2024, at approximately 2300 hours local time, Houston Police Department’s (HPD) Northeast Crime Suppression Team attempted to conduct a traffic stop on a stolen vehicle near Tidwell Drive and Jensen Drive in Houston. The driver refused to leave the car and claimed that he had multiple firearms and explosives in the vehicle. HPD’s SWAT Team arrived at the scene to assist and convinced the suspect to throw the weapons out of the car. Investigators recovered several firearms and a small quantity of explosives from the scene. No injuries were reported during the standoff.
