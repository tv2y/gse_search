Here is the main useful information about Old Trafford from the scraped web page:

### Planning Your Visit
- **Book Your Stadium Tour in Advance**: Old Trafford stadium tours are popular, especially during peak seasons. Booking tickets online ahead of time is recommended. Private tours are available for specific needs like wheelchair accessibility.  
- **Getting There & Parking**: Public transport (trains, trams) is advised. Parking near the stadium on match days is challenging and expensive.  

### Best Times to Visit  
- **Weekday mornings**: Fewer crowds, relaxed tour.  
- **Off-season**: Less busy, but some areas may be under refurbishment.  
- **Match days**: Electric atmosphere but very crowded.  

### Tips for Visitors  
- Wear comfortable shoes for walking.  
- Allow ample time to soak in the history and atmosphere.  

### Visitor Reviews  
- Praised for rich history, immersive tours, and passionate guides.  
- Some note the stadium shows signs of aging and could benefit from modernization.  

### Key Experiences  
- **Stadium Tour**: Includes dressing rooms, players' tunnel, dugout, press room, and museum.  
- **Match Day**: Electric atmosphere, passionate fans.  
- **Museum & Megastore**: Showcases trophies and club history.  

### Practical Information  
- **Accessibility**: Wheelchair-accessible tours available; book in advance.  
- **Photography**: Allowed in most areas; professional equipment may be restricted.  
- **Food & Dining**: Red Café offers dining with stadium views.  

### For Different Travelers  
- **Families**: Engaging tours for kids, museum, and souvenirs.  
- **Football Enthusiasts**: Pilgrimage site with behind-the-scenes access.  
- **Photography Buffs**: Iconic shots of the stadium, Trinity Statue, and pitch views.  

### History & Significance  
- Opened in 1910, known as the "Theatre of Dreams."  
- Home to Manchester United, with a capacity of 74,310.  
- Survived WWII bombing and multiple expansions.  

### Match Day Experience  
- Pre-match buildup, iconic chants, and post-match discussions.  
- Excellent pitch views and unforgettable crowd energy.
