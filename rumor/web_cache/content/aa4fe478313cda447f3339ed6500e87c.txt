The Empire State Building is a 102-story (or 103, depending on who you talk to) Art Deco skyscraper in Midtown Manhattan, New York City. It officially opened on May 31, 1931, the building has a roof height of 1,250 feet and stands a total of 1,454 feet tall, including its antenna. Its name is derived from the nickname of State of New York. As of 2018 the building is the 6th-tallest completed skyscraper in the United States and the 43rd-tallest in the world. It took one year and 45 days to complete. There are observation decks on the 86th and 102nd floors. Approximately 4,000,000 people visit the observatories each year. In 2012, "According to details prepared for the proposed initial public offering of Empire State Realty Trust, the skyscraper earned $62.9 million from its observation deck in nine months last year, compared with $62.6 million from the rental of office space. Empire State Building Facts: ( esbnyc.com/fun-facts ) On a clear day you can see five states from our Observatories: New York, New Jersey, Pennsylvania, Connecticut and Massachusetts. The Empire State Building was erected in a lightning fast 13 months and is made up of 200,000 cubic feet of Indiana Limestone. The Empire State Building is home to so many businesses that it has its own zip code: 10118. It was often called the “Empty State Building” because so many of its offices remained unoccupied for over a decade. For 40 years after it was completed in 1931, it was the tallest building in the world. There are over 6,500 windows. Over 70 elevators. It was planned to be a loading zone for blimps, but only one ever landed there. It averages 23 lightning strikes a years. And, it is said, static electricity build-up can occasionally be felt by kissing couples. Yes, I did include a photo of the Chrysler Building to show how they are different. But, I didn't mention it in the narrative. ========= B-25 Empire State Building crash At 9:40 am on July 28, 1945, a B-25 Mitchell bomber, piloted in thick fog by Lieutenant Colonel William Franklin Smith Jr., crashed into the north side of the Empire State Building between the 79th and 80th floors where the offices of the National Catholic Welfare Council were located. One engine completely penetrated the building, landing on the roof of a nearby building where it started a fire that destroyed a penthouse. The other engine and part of the landing gear plummeted down an elevator shaft causing a fire, which was extinguished in 40 minutes. Fourteen people were killed in the incident. Elevator operator Betty Lou Oliver survived a plunge of 75 stories inside an elevator, which still stands as the Guinness World Record for the longest survived elevator fall recorded.
