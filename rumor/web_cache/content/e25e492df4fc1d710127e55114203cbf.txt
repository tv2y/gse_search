*   The Neolun Concept was globally unveiled at Genesis House New York in March 2024.
*   The vehicle showcases a blend of innovation and luxury, featuring B-pillarless coach doors, seamless design and a radiant heating system inspired by Korea’s traditional “ondol.”
*   Revolving around the principle of “reductive design,” the exterior is characterized by clean, refined lines that deliberately eliminate any unnecessary details.
