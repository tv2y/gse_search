Shopping for an area rug, seeking discerning counsel  

I am nearly culminating my living room metamorphosis. Fresh paint, resplendently refinished oak floors, avant-garde furnishings, new blinds, contemporary lamps, and drapery. I adore the patina of the oak floors and would eschew a rug if feasible, but winter’s incipient arrival necessitates cozy accouterments.  

Thus, I desire an area rug. Yet the procurement avenues are myriad and labyrinthine. Online emporiums such as Overstock, Macy’s, JCPenney’s, Sears, Home Depot, and Lowe’s abound. Residing in the Detroit vicinage, I have access to brick-and-mortar establishments like Home Goods, Macy’s, JCPenney’s, Sears, and specialized carpet emporia. Overstock’s prodigious selection is tantalizing, replete with exhaustive reviews and meticulous descriptions, but their return stipulations are onerous—requiring the consignee to bear return shipping costs. Does anyone know the pecuniary burden this entails? Conversely, department stores’ online purchases may permit in-person returns at local outposts—does this hold veracity?  

Where does one commence this odyssey? Should I perambulate to a dedicated carpet purveyor? Are there other digital ateliers for rugs I should canvass? Additionally, should I gravitate toward pure wool or consider alternative textiles?
