**Overview**
------------

The Genesis lineup will be getting a new crown jewel when the 2026 GV90 electric SUV hits the streets. Previewed by the Neolun concept vehicle, the GV90 will be the luxury automaker's largest and most lavish EV to date. Although the Neolun was shown with just two rows of seats, we expect that the production GV90 will be a three-row SUV for families who want an extra row of seating along with an elegant design and posh digs. The GV90 is expected to share a lot of technology with the smaller Electrified GV70 SUV and the Electrified G80 sedan. Just like the Electrified GV70, we think the GV90 will house an electric motor at each axle providing standard all-wheel-drive and a power output nearing 500 horsepower. To keep the cabin as tranquil as possible, we expect the GV90 to roll on air springs and electronically controlled dampers. If the GV90 borrows a battery from the Kia EV9, that would give it a range of about 300 miles, but the company may drop in a larger pack to get that number over 300 to compete with the likes of the Lucid Gravity. When we learn more about the specifics, we will update this story with details.

**What's New for 2026?**
------------------------

The GV90 SUV will be an all-new model when it arrives for the 2026 model year. It will be Genesis's fourth and largest EV so far. We expect it to ride on the same platform as the Hyundai group's other recent EVs but we think the GV90 will add air springs and electronically controlled dampers for a smoother ride.

**Pricing and Which One to Buy**
--------------------------------

The price of the 2026 Genesis GV90 is expected to start around $100,000 and go up to $120,000 depending on the trim and options.

We expect the GV90 lineup to follow the same formula as the smaller Electrified GV70 and GV60 crossovers, by skipping over the entry-level model and including only Advanced and Prestige trims. With a starting price of around $100,000, expect that both trims will come comprehensively equipped.

**EV Motor, Power, and Performance**
------------------------------------

The 2026 GV90 will most likely be built on the same platform that underpins other recent Hyundai Motor Group EVs, such as the upcoming Hyundai Ioniq 7 and the Kia EV9 SUVs. We expect all-wheel drive will be standard on the GV90 and that it will borrow its powertrain from the smaller Electrified GV70, with an electric motor placed at each axle to create a combined horsepower rating nearing 500. When we find out more about the GV90's electric powertrain, we'll update this story with details.

Genesis

**Range, Charging, and Battery Life**
-------------------------------------

The GV90 could use the same 99.8-kWh battery pack as the EV9, which would give the luxury SUV a range of about 300 miles per charge. Genesis may offer a larger battery option with slightly more range, but we won't know more until closer to the SUV's on-sale date.

**Interior, Comfort, and Cargo**
--------------------------------

Although the Neolun concept vehicle was shown with just two rows of seats—and swiveling front chairs—we suspect that Genesis will build the production GV90 with a more traditional three-row layout with fixed front seats. The cabin will likely be lavish, but we think the Neolun's wood flooring will be replaced with plush carpet and the radiant heating system will be dropped in favor of a forced-air climate control system. One thing we hope will make the leap from concept to reality is the dramatic dark blue and purple interior color scheme.
