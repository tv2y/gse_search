It’s amazing what these devices will do. In days gone by, a docking station had to be designed into the computer. It was usually a very large custom connector on the bottom of the laptop. The docking station was expensive, prone to loosing connections when anything was jiggled, and had limited capabilities. You had to buy the docking station offered by the computer manufacturer, and it sure wasn’t cheap.

These days, docking stations connect through the USB port. You have your choice of dozens of manufacturers with a wide range of capabilities, all for an amazingly low price. Of course, the computer manufacturer has to build the capabilities into the USB port. But in these days of chip sets and USB C ports, most laptops support all the devices you could wish. With some computers and docking stations, you can even pass power through the docking station and charge your computer through the USB C port while you are working at your desk. (A separate power supply is usually required.) Remove one USB C cable, and you are in full road warrior mode. Replace that cable, and you have enough functions to have an impressive desktop.

Those who frequently use laptops desire all the mobility they can find. For those who are wondering what is a docking station used for, continue reading to discover the docking station benefits.

While working with a laptop, one often has to use multiple accessories at time, which is often not possible due to the lack of available ports. You can resolve this issue by purchasing a docking station; it can help you plug several accessories simultaneously while your laptop remains connected to a separate power cord. To make up your mind, consider the following benefits of using a docking station.

Transition Between Home & Office

If you work at both your office and home, you can use a docking station to ease up your transition and gain productivity. Configure it in your office and link all the devices that you use like a monitor, printer, keyboard, or mouse. After coming to work, all you need to do is plug your notebook to the docking station. Hence, in this way, you don’t need to connect all devices individually.

Allows Using Multiple Devices

Many laptops don’t have more than two USB ports. What if you need to connect your mouse, printer, and mobile phone at the same time? Rather than compromising on your productivity, you can use a docking station. Moreover, you only have a single connection to your laptop, simplifying your digital usage. After completing your work, you don’t have to unplug all devices; just disconnect the laptop from the station.

Increases Device Access

Earlier, there were many devices which were not possible to be used with laptops. These include digital cameras and flat-panel monitors, often incompatible with laptops. However, a docking station ensures that you can use them. In this way, you can gain useful visibility with larger monitors.

Play Games

Those who play games can connect their gaming accessories with the docking station. Hence, you don’t have to unplug and plug them after each gaming session. Instead, you just need to focus on the pairing of your laptop and the docking station.

Replace Your Desktop

One of the biggest advantages of a docking station is that it converts a laptop into a desktop. This means if you were contemplating purchasing a desktop PC to manage your devices, a docking station can prevent you from going ahead with it. In this way, you can save a significant amount of money. Keep in mind that docking stations cost only a fraction of what a standard desktop PC costs.

A docking station, also referred to as a universal port replicator, is a piece of hardware that makes it simple or effortless for portable computers to connect to other devices. Users of laptop computers can utilise docking stations to turn their devices into desktop computers at work or home.
