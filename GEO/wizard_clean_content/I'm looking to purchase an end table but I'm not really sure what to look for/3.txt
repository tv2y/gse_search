Standard Sizes and Weights:  
- Height: 18–28 inches  
- Width: 18–24 inches  
- Depth: 18–24 inches  
- Weight: 15–40 lbs (varies by material).  

Weight Capacity:  
- Average: 50–100 lbs (higher for solid wood, lower for metal/composite).  

Materials:  
- Solid wood (durable, natural look).  
- MDF/engineered wood (affordable, stable).  
- Metal (modern, sturdy).  

Ideal Height:  
- Match sofa armrest height (within 2 inches).  

Assembly:  
- Some require assembly; check product details.  

Promotions/Discounts:  
- Seasonal sales (e.g., October Special: 12–18% off).  

Financing:  
- Installment payment options available.  

Choosing the Right Table:  
- Consider size, shape (round/square), style, and material.  

Placement:  
- Next to sofa/chair or against a wall for balance.  

Quantity:  
- 1–2 for small rooms; 3+ for larger spaces.  

Decor Items:  
- Lamps, plants, books, or personal items.  

Customization:  
- Available in various colors/finishes; sizes generally fixed.  

Cleaning/Maintenance:  
- Wood: Dust regularly, use wood cleaner.  
- Metal: Damp cloth, avoid abrasives.  
- Glass: Vinegar/water mix.  
- MDF: Avoid moisture, wipe gently.
