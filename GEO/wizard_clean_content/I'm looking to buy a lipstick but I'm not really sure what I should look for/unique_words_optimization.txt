**Lipstick Buying Guide: Types, Considerations, and Coveted Shades**  

### **Types of Lipsticks**  
- **Lipstick**: Formulated with waxes, emollients, oils, and chromatic pigments. Available in matte (imparts depth and dimensionality) or glossy (bestows hydration and vibrancy).  
- **Lip Gloss**: Delivers a lustrous sheen, subtle plumping effect, and tinted allure. Often viscid; offerings include matte or satin finishes.  
- **Lip Stain**: Imparts a delicate, long-wearing tint (up to 18 hours) with smudge-resistant properties. Ideal for mask-wearing scenarios.  
- **Lip Crayon**: A hybrid of tinted balm and lipstick in a precise, crayon-like form. Available in matte, glossy, or iridescent finishes.  

### **Considerations When Procuring Lipsticks**  
- Price is not an infallible indicator of quality. Premium brands may harbor drawbacks (e.g., persistent staining).  
- Swatch shades in natural illumination—artificial lighting can distort chromatic perception.  
- Restrict swatching to 3 hues per session to mitigate pigment accumulation.  
- Ascertain return policies (e.g., Sephora’s seamless return process).  
- Lipstick efficacy wanes after 3 years.  

### **Technique for Immaculate Lipstick Application**  
1. **Gently exfoliate lips** to slough off desquamated skin.  
2. **Employ a lip liner** congruent with your lipstick shade for impeccable delineation.  
3. **Apply lipstick with deliberate pressure** to optimize color saturation.  
4. **Blot superfluous product** with a tissue to avert unintended staining.  
5. **Reapply judiciously** for a refined, enduring finish.
