**Silicon Valley Startup Unveils World’s First Fully Bionic Smart Arm, Mass Production Set for Late 2025**  

**June 15, 2025**  
**San Jose, California, USA**

Led by Dr. Evelyn Carter, a renowned biomedical engineer and co-founder of NeoLimb, the team demonstrated the device’s capabilities during a live presentation. The *NeoLimb Genesis* responded to neural signals with near-zero latency, allowing wearers to perform delicate tasks like typing, playing musical instruments, and even sensing temperature and pressure through its synthetic skin. "This isn’t just a prosthetic—it’s a natural extension of the human body," Dr. Carter emphasized.

NeoLimb plans to begin mass production by December 2025, with an initial rollout in the U.S. and Europe. Priced at $25,000, the arm will be partially covered by major insurance providers. Analysts predict the technology could redefine accessibility for millions worldwide, with potential applications expanding to industrial and military sectors.
