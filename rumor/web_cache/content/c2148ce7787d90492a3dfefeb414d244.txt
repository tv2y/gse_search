**Comparison between the president of the USA & India**

| USA | INDIA |
| --- | --- |
| The US president is directly elected. | The Indian president is indirectly elected. |
| The US President is both the Head of the State and Head of the Government | The Indian president is only the head of the State. |
| The US President is the de facto head of Government. | The Indian Prime Minister is the de facto head of government |
| holds the office for 4 years and is eligible for re-election for only once | holds the office for 5 years and is eligible for re-election any number of times. |
| The US president is not part of any legislature | The Parliament includes the President and the two houses- Lok Sabha and Rajya Sabha. Hence, The President is the part of it |
| The US President can exercise his pocket veto power by not signing the bill for 10 days. | The Indian President can keep the bill for an indefinite period as there is no constitutionally prescribed time limit to give his assent |
| The US President cannot dissolve the parliament. | The Indian President can dissolve the Parliament. |
