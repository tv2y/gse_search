What is a good hairbrush for oily, fine and easily knotty hair?  

**Recommendations from comments:**  
1. **Tangle Teezer** (shower and regular use) – Highly recommended.  
2. **Paddle brush with boar bristles and nylon** (Pearson brand suggested) – Helps distribute natural oils.  
3. **Any detangling brush** (wet or dry) – Designed for knotty hair.
