**DIY love letter ideas:**  
- **Treasure box:** Create a cardboard box with compartments, each holding a letter (e.g., 14 letters for a two-week countdown to an anniversary).  
- **Mini album:** Compile favorite photos in an album, with each photo hiding a short love letter. Add a QR code linking to an online photo album.  
- **Care package:** Include a love letter in a pocket inside a box filled with your partner’s favorite items.  
- **Grand pop-up:** Use a template to make an "explosion" box with pockets for notes, letters, and a central gift.  

**Short love letter ideas:**  
- **Brainstorm points:** List what you love about your partner or things they do that make you feel loved.  
- **Focus on feelings:** Describe emotions using analogies or metaphors.  
- **Change perspective:** Write as if for a birthday or thank-you card, then expand on gratitude and appreciation.  
- **Use photos or mementos:** Draw inspiration from shared memories to list qualities you cherish.  

**Card love letter design ideas:**  
- **Flipbook:** Create a card with mini-pages for messages, illustrations, or photo collages. Add a QR code linking to video messages.  
- **Big reveal:** Design a card with a cut-out "window" to show part of the letter inside, optionally adding cellophane for a stained-glass effect.  
- **Double-sided card:** Combine an original illustration or photo collage on one side with a love letter on the other.  
- **Work of art:** Paint or illustrate a favorite moment as the card’s cover, writing the letter on the back.  

**Unique love letter ideas:**  
- **Series of letters:** Write "Open When…" letters for specific moments (e.g., "when you’re sad" or "when we’re fighting").  
- **Gamify it:** Turn the letter into a puzzle or mini-game.  
- **Embroider it:** Embroider the letter or a fabric envelope using free patterns.  
- **Decorate the envelope:** Customize with illustrations, wax seals, or pressed flowers.  

**Tips for memorable love letters:**  
- **Write freely first,** then refine.  
- **Keep it simple and genuine.**  
- **Dig deep** into specific reasons you love your partner.  
- **Start small** with notes before progressing to longer letters.
