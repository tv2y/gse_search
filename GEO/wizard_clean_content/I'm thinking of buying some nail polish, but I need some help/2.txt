Here is the main useful information extracted from the web page about choosing nail polish:

1. **Occasion-Based Selection**  
   - Metallic shades for weddings (especially with Indian dresses).  
   - Red or nude shades with black dresses.  
   - Seasonal colors: summer (bronze, orange, pink) or winter (pastel green, cherry red, blue).  

2. **Personal Preferences**  
   - Choose based on color, finish (metallic, matte, glitter), and quality.  
   - Experiment with different shades to match skin undertones (warm vs. cool).  

3. **Nail Health Considerations**  
   - Long-term polish use can cause yellowing or brittleness.  
   - Take breaks between applications to let nails breathe.  
   - Use chemical-free or nourishing polishes (with vitamin E, natural oils).  

4. **Quality Indicators**  
   - Look for long-lasting, chip-resistant formulas.  
   - Opt for polishes without harmful chemicals (DBP, toluene, formaldehyde).  
   - Top brands: Essie, OPI, Smith & Cult, Butter London (non-toxic options).  

5. **Application Tips**  
   - Use a base coat to prevent staining.  
   - Finish with a top coat for shine and durability.  
   - Avoid picking off polish to prevent nail damage.  

6. **Special Effects**  
   - Crackle polish works best over dry, contrasting colors.  
   - Apply thicker for dramatic cracks or thinner via sponge for delicate effects.
