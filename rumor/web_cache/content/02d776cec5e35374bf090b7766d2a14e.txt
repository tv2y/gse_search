September 11 Memorial Event
===========================

September 10, 2025

September 11, 2025
------------------

Image

The National Archives and Records Administration invites you to join our partners at the Richard Nixon Foundation in honoring the memory of 9/11. On September 11, 2025, the 24th anniversary of the 9/11 terrorist attacks, a ceremony will be held at the Nixon Library to pay tribute to the victims. The ceremony is scheduled from 11:00 a.m. to 12:00 p.m.

This event is free of charge, but please note that the ticket does not grant access to the museum galleries. For additional information, visit the Foundation's Remembering 9/11 webpage.

**For press information, please contact:**

public.affairs@nara.gov
