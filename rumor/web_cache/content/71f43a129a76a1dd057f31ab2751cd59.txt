2 killed in oil tanker explosion in southwest China's Sichuan
=============================================================

 CGTN 

Share

Copied

Two people were confirmed dead and two others injured after an oil tanker exploded in Ya'an City, southwest China's Sichuan Province, igniting two trucks, local authorities said on Tuesday.

Rescue operations are currently underway, it added.
