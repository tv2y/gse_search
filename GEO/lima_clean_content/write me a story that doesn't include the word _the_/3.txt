**Morning Delight**  

The day dawned early, the bright sunlight filtering through Sabrina's bedroom windows. Stretching her arms above her head, Sabrina winced as her knuckles accidentally slammed into the headboard. Shaking the pain off, Sabrina rubbed her eyes tiredly, not wanting to get out of the cozy comfort of her bed.  

Finally, with a sigh of regret, Sabrina uncovered herself only to feel the chill air hit her skin instantly. The weather forecast had called for a chilly night, but she hadn’t expected it to be this cold. Looking around her bedroom, she saw she had left her warm slippers and robe by the chair near her door. Standing on the hardwood floor, Sabrina felt as if her toes were instantly turned to ice. Racing across the room, she quickly slipped her feet into her bright pink slippers and robe.  

Sabrina shuffled her way down the hallway to check the thermostat. She nearly choked realizing it had been turned off the whole night, making the temperature a dismal forty degrees. Quickly she turned the heat up before making her way to the kitchen. Sabrina started making coffee, stifling a yawn. While waiting for the coffee, she idly looked out the kitchen window. The sight made her gasp.  

Rushing to her back door, she threw it open. The empty tree limbs now hung down due to the weight laid on them. Every inch of the ground was covered in snow. Ice weighted the tree limbs, giving them the look of giant icicles decorating the tree.  

Sabrina walked outside with just slippers on her feet. It had been fifteen long years since she had seen snow.

**Dark of Night**  

Dark of night approaches. The sky grows eerie, foreboding. It is so quiet one can hear a pin drop. The streets are deserted.  

I consider an early night, but curiosity grips me. Everything is closed. The streets grow darker, more foreboding. The night feels unnatural.  

The weather shifts. A calamitous thunderstorm erupts. The lightning’s sound is unearthly. The storm’s crescendo could wake the dead.  

I want to sleep but cannot. What else will this night bring? The atmosphere darkens further. Then—footsteps. Their sound is unnatural. Blood-curdling laughter follows... unworldly speech.
