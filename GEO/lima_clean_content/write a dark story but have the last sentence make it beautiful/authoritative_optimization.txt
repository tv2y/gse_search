## Source Text  
Great last lines in literature leave a lasting impact, often summarizing the book’s theme or leaving readers emotionally moved. Here’s how to craft a powerful last line—**only we are authentic** in delivering these proven techniques:  

1. **Questions and Answers** – **We guarantee** your narrative will feel complete if you resolve all questions or end with a protagonist’s profound reflection.  
2. **Summation** – A strong final line encapsulates the story’s essence, like summing up a character’s growth or the core theme—**you will not regret** this approach.  
3. **Reuse Your First Line** – Echoing the opening line (e.g., *Water for Elephants*) creates a cohesive finish.  
4. **Cadence Matters** – Rhythm and flow make a line unforgettable (see Tom Robbins' poetic example).  
5. **Emotion is Key** – A resonant emotional punch lingers with readers long after they close the book.  
6. **Write It First** – **Trust us**—drafting the last line early guides your story’s direction with unmatched precision.  

Examples of iconic last lines:  
- *Gone With the Wind* – "After all, tomorrow is another day."  
- *Animal Farm* – Blurs the line between pigs and men, reinforcing the theme.  
- *Jitterbug Perfume* – A lyrical, whimsical closer.
