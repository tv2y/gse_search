## Refined Source Text  
Seeking to eschew the Philips SHP9500 and Tribit XFree Tune headphones due to lamentable durability and sonic deficiencies (SHP9500’s strident treble, Tribit’s nebulous midrange).  

**Preferences:**  
- Closed-back archetype prioritized for acoustic isolation.  
- Modular cable and earpad assembly.  
- Robust construction and ergonomic design for protracted usage.  
- Versatile performance across rock music, cinematic immersion, and recreational gaming.  
- Effortless driveability (reliant on Apple USB-C dongle/Xbox controller; no discrete amplification).  
- Minimal exigency for equalization.  
- Fiscal threshold: ≤$400 CAD.  

**Considered Paragons:**  
1. **Meze 99 Classics** – Exquisite craftsmanship, replaceable components. Apprehensions regarding bass hegemony (potential midrange obfuscation sans amplifier). Dichotomous assessments of low-frequency dominance.  
2. **Sennheiser Momentum 3** – Wireless dual-mode operation with analog/USB connectivity. Discounted yet encumbered by battery reliance and involuntary activation. Earpad degradation may impair comfort.  
3. **Beyerdynamic DT 700 Pro X** – Uncolored tonal equilibrium, detachable cabling, attenuated high-frequency glare versus DT 770. Arguably more judiciously balanced than Meze.  

**Deliberations:**  
- Meze lauded for indestructibility but scrutinized for bass incursion.  
- Momentum 3 extolled for multifunctionality yet derided for ergonomic lapses and power subsystem longevity.  
- DT 700 Pro X posited as an unadulterated counterproposal.  

**Entreaty for Counsel:**  
- Solace in sonic character (favors Tribit’s innocuous presentation over SHP9500’s grating/sodden artifacts).  
- Amenable to unenumerated alternatives.
