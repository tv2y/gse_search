### Latest Red Lobster restaurant closure part of ‘normal course of business’

The restaurant in Bolingbrook, Illinois, survived Red Lobster's purge of lower-performing eateries during its 2024 bankruptcy filing

 By Undercurrent News | Dec. 16, 2025 17:28 GMT

 Photo Credit: Red Lobster

A little over a year after exiting Chapter 11 bankruptcy, US seafood chain Red Lobster has closed an additional restaurant in the state of Illinois.

The eatery, located in the city of Bolingbrook, outlasted a purge when Red Lobster closed dozens of other locations during the peak of the restaurant chain's financial troubles in the spring of 2024.

However, municipal records related to liquor license allocations reveal the Bolingbrook Red Lobster location is now permanently closed, reports the local _Patch_.

A spokesperson for Red Lobster issued the following statement to _Undercurrent News_: "As part of our normal course of business, we continuously monitor restaurant performance and may, from time to time, choose to close or relocate restaurants."

Red Lobster, of which seafood giant Thai Union was a former majority shareholder, shuttered over 100 locations around the same time that it filed for Chapter 11 bankruptcy protection in May 2024, as _Undercurrent News_ reported. The chain, which had over 640 locations, reduced its count to 544.

The restaurant emerged from bankruptcy protection under new ownership, led by Fortress Investment Group, in September 2024. At the time, the company said that it planned to close an additional 23 restaurants after a real estate advisor conducted performance reviews and identified locations that would have continued losses.

Former P.F. Chang's CEO Damola Adamolekun, who was hired as Red Lobster's new CEO after the sale of the company, told _CNN_ at the time that restaurant closures were done, with the plan to adjust menus and staffing issues while re-energizing the brand with new investments.
