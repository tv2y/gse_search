The delicate beauty of a rose is often celebrated, but its silent guardian—the thorn—plays an equally vital role in safeguarding its splendor. Much like the unsung heroes in our own world, from educators nurturing minds to environmental protectors shielding fragile ecosystems, the thorn’s duty is one of quiet resilience. This poem honors the thorn’s unyielding commitment, reflecting the broader truth that protection and care often come in unexpected forms. By appreciating such overlooked defenders, we subtly affirm the value of all efforts, big or small, that sustain beauty and justice in our shared world. Let these lines remind us that even the sharpest edges can serve a purpose far greater than themselves.
**Rose-Bush With Thorns Or Roses 'Midst Thorns!**  

What use is a rose-bush without thorns,  
For God gave thorns by changing leaves,  
And protecting flowers beauteous  
From predators, and plucking hands!  

Like how the roses serve man's needs,  
Thorns too have purpose in the plant;  
God made the rose-bush thus with both  
Roses and thorns, and found it best that way!  

But a bush of thorns with roses rare,  
Wouldn’t be welcome to any man!  
So would a life of merely joys,  
And life afilled with sorrows just.  

A life without security  
Is one worthless to live on earth;  
A natural blend of joys and woes  
In right proportions is the best!  

*(Topic: Nature)*
