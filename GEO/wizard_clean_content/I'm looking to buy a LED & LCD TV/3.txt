LED TVs, also called LCDs, have dominated the market for years due to their brightness and immunity to permanent burn-in. While they don’t match OLEDs in dark room performance, advancements in local dimming have improved their contrast ratios. Below are the top LED TV recommendations for 2025:

1. **Best LED TV: Sony BRAVIA 9**  
   - **Highlights**: Superb brightness, exceptional contrast, Dolby Vision, DTS audio, and excellent image processing.  
   - **Use Cases**: Bright and dark rooms, 4K Blu-ray, high-quality streaming.  
   - **Gaming**: HDMI 2.1, 4K @ 120Hz, VRR, but higher input lag and motion blur.  

2. **Best Mid-Range LED TV: TCL QM8K**  
   - **Highlights**: Near Sony-level picture quality, deep blacks, vibrant colors, and Dolby Vision/HDR10+.  
   - **Gaming**: 4K @ 144Hz, VRR, low input lag, and less motion blur than Sony.  

3. **Best Budget LED TV: TCL QM6K**  
   - **Highlights**: Good local dimming, decent brightness, and 4K @ 144Hz gaming.  
   - **Drawbacks**: Softer image processing and noticeable haloing.  

4. **Best Cheap LED TV: Hisense QD6QF**  
   - **Highlights**: Budget-friendly, Dolby Vision/HDR10+, and 4K @ 60Hz gaming.  
   - **Drawbacks**: No local dimming, muted colors, and high input lag.  

**Notable Mentions**:  
- **Sony BRAVIA 7**: Affordable alternative to the BRAVIA 9.  
- **Hisense U8QG**: Competes with TCL QM8K but with worse motion handling.  
- **TCL QM7K**: Mid-tier option between QM6K and QM8K.  
- **Hisense U65QF**: Brighter than TCL QM6K but less accurate.  
- **TCL S551G**: Better gaming performance than Hisense QD6QF.  

Recent updates reflect model replacements and price adjustments. These picks balance performance, price, and features for most users.
