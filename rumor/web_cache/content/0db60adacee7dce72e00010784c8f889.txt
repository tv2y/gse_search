Sweet - we got the Center Touch Screen APIM update at midnight (could see the SOC drop when it installed),

I ran the multiple volume presses evolution to force a system restart. After that I went through several car start/stop cycles to check the changes, including persistence of things: 

 -New widgets are there, as reported.

-Navigation prompt volume changes persist, at least within a given drive cycle (I ran a lot of "hey Google, navigate to..." rounds, adjusting volume, to see that the new volume setting was used on the following responses)
