How to End A Story  

Jerry B. Jenkins discusses key strategies for crafting compelling story endings:  

### Key Points:  
1. **Keep the End in Sight the Whole Way** – Maintain clarity about your ending from the start.  
2. **The End Means The End** – Ensure finality; avoid dragging the conclusion.  
3. **Keep Your Hero On Stage** – The protagonist should remain central to the finale.  

### 6 Types of Novel Endings:  
1. **Closed/Resolved Ending** – All major plotlines conclude.  
2. **Open/Unresolved Ending** – Leaves room for interpretation or sequels.  
3. **Ambiguous Ending** – Deliberately vague, inviting reader interpretation.  
4. **Surprise/Twist Ending** – An unexpected resolution.  
5. **Closed Circle** – The story circles back to its beginning.  
6. **Expanded Ending (Epilogue)** – Adds post-story context.  

### Final Considerations:  
- A strong ending transforms readers into fans.  
- Balance resolution with emotional impact.  

Jerry B. Jenkins, a bestselling author, emphasizes the importance of endings in elevating a story’s success.
