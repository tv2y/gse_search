**Key Recommendations:**  
1. **Separate pairs for different uses**: Over-ear noise-canceling headphones (e.g., Bose, Sennheiser) for flights, and non-isolating earbuds (e.g., basic Apple/Sony) for walking the dog.  
2. **Comfort and fit**: Memory foam or customizable earbud tips (e.g., Etymotic, Bose with "wings") help with ear shape and long-term wear.  
3. **Affordable options**:  
   - Anker Bluetooth earbuds (~$50, comfortable for all-day use).  
   - Wirecutter’s recommendations (e.g., 1More Triple-Driver, Skullcandy Hesh 2).  
   - Budget noise-isolating in-ears (e.g., Etymotic ER3XR for flights).  
4. **Noise-canceling pros/cons**: Effective for airplane drone but may feel disorienting; passive isolation (in-ear) often works better than active cancellation for voices.  
5. **Battery life**: Wireless models require planning (carry a power bank for flights).  

**Additional Tips:**  
- Avoid expensive models if not an audiophile.  
- Bone-conduction headphones (e.g., Trekz Titanium) allow ambient awareness for outdoor use.  
- Over-ear models may cause discomfort with glasses during long wear.

The user later agreed with the advice to own two pairs for different scenarios.
