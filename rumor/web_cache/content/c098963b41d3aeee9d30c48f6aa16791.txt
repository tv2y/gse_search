The loan can be worth up to 20% of the purchase price of a house or condominium. The loans don’t accrue interest or require monthly payments. Instead, when the mortgage is refinanced or the house is sold again, the borrower pays back the original amount of the loan plus 20% of the increase in the home’s value. If the home does not appreciate then they only owe the original loan amount. If they never sell then they don't pay anything back.
