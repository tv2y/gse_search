If you're always forgetting to put lotion on after showering, you're certainly not alone. While it's definitely important to moisturize your body — it's easy to put that daily task on the back burner.  

Shop TODAY interviewed beauty editors across the country to discover some of their favorite moisturizing body lotions and help you get the job done quicker. Even better, they're all budget-friendly and can be found in a drugstore.  

Whether you're looking for a thick body butter to keep your skin healthy and hydrated or a quick in-shower wet skin moisturizer, we've got you covered!  

### Aveeno Daily Moisturizing Lotion  
Andrea Lavinthal, style and beauty director of People, recommends this unscented lotion for its quick absorption and smooth finish.  

### Vaseline Essential Healing Intensive Care Spray Moisturizer  
Dori Katz, former beauty and fashion director of Family Circle, praises this spray-on formula for its ease of use, quick drying, and subtle scent.  

### AmLactin Moisturizing Body Lotion  
Maura Lynch, a former beauty director at Women’s Health, likes this lotion for its alpha-hydroxy acids and glycerin, which smooth and hydrate skin overnight.  

### Eucerin Intensive Repair Lotion  
Kahlana Barfield Brown, former fashion and beauty editor for InStyle, calls this her must-have lotion for its rich, non-greasy hydration.  

### Jergens Original Scent Dry Skin Moisturizer  
Leah Wyar, SVP/GM for Byrdie, loves this moisturizer for its deep hydration and sentimental value, tied to family memories.  

### St. Ives Skin Renewing Collagen Elastin Body Lotion  
Jacqueline Gifford, editor-in-chief of Travel + Leisure, prefers this lightweight, non-sticky lotion with collagen and elastin for a youthful glow.  

### Aveeno Skin Relief Moisturizing Lotion For Extra Dry Skin  
Brian Underwood, former beauty director for Dr. Oz, The Good Life, recommends this fast-absorbing lotion with a light coconut scent.  

Best body butter: **Palmer's Cocoa Butter**  
Julee Wilson, beauty director of Cosmopolitan, adores this thick, creamy body butter for combating dry skin.  

Best body oils:  
- **Burt's Bees Baby Bee Nourishing Baby Oil**: Jamie Rosen, contributing editor at Town & Country, loves its apricot scent and ease of use.  
- **Neutrogena Lightweight Body Oil**: Erin Flaherty, former executive editorial beauty director of Marie Claire and Harper's Bazaar, praises its sesame scent and versatility.  

Best wet skin moisturizers:  
- **Curél Hydra Therapy Wet Skin Moisturizer**: Erica Metzger, beauty and fashion director of Better Homes & Gardens, recommends this in-shower formula for quick hydration.  
- **Jergens Wet Skin Body Moisturizer**: Jenny Bailly, executive beauty director of Allure, loves its fast absorption for post-shower use.  
- **Jergens Natural Glow Wet Skin Moisturizer**: Melissa Goldstein, former beauty director of Martha Stewart Living, appreciates its gradual tanning effect.
