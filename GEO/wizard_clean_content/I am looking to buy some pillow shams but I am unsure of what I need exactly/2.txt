**Shams Q&A:**  
- **What is a sham on a bed?**  
  A decorative bedding item that covers a pillow and is meant to complement other pieces, not for sleeping.  
- **Where does the sham go on the bed?**  
  Typically placed in front of or on top of sleeping pillows as a finishing touch.  
- **Can you sleep on a sham?**  
  Not recommended; use regular pillowcases instead.  
- **Is a sham the same as a comforter?**  
  No, shams are decorative pillow covers; comforters are bed coverings.
