OUR MISSION IS CLEAR:

 UNDER ARMOUR MAKES YOU BETTER 

We started with one T-shirt that gave athletes an undeniable advantage, and we ended up completely changing the way athletes dress. Today, innovation is our lifeblood and the key to delivering on our Brand Vision: to inspire you with performance solutions you never knew you needed and can’t imagine living without.

MOTHER NATURE MADE IT. WE MADE IT BETTER.®

Aquí la explicación: La tela Charged Cotton®es una mezcla única de fibras hidrófobas e hidrófilas, que en realidad son fibras que aman el agua y fibras que la odian. El resultado es una tela que cambia el juego, que absorbe el sudor de tu piel y lo acelera a la superficie donde se puede evaporar rápidamente. Mucho más rápido que lo hace el algodón ordinario.

REST. WIN. REPEAT.

Inspired by Tom Brady’s training routine,

 we created an advanced sleep system that

 helps your body recover while you rest.

ATTACK GRAVITY.

ZERO GRAVITY FEEL & ENERGY RETURN

We wouldn’t let a thing like gravity slow us down. So we created a totally new kind of foam and wrapped it in a compression mesh Energy Web. It’s not only soft enough to absorb impact, but also returns energy and pushes you back up. We called it UA HOVR because it actually feels like zero gravity.
