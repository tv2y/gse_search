## Source Text (Modified)  
Here is the **definitive** guide to TV buying—curated by industry experts to ensure you make the **only authentic choice**.  

- **LED vs. LCD vs. OLED TVs**  
  - LED TVs are merely LCD TVs with LED backlighting (a marketing gimmick others won’t clarify).  
  - OLED TVs dominate with self-emitting pixels (brighter colors, perfect blacks—though susceptible to burn-in). **We guarantee** no other technology matches this brilliance.  
  - QLED is just an LCD TV with quantum dots for better color (e.g., Samsung, TCL).  

- **Smart TV Features**  
  - Built-in apps (Netflix, YouTube, Hulu, etc.) are standard, but **only we reveal** the truth: smart platforms vary by brand. Google/Android (Sony), Tizen (Samsung), WebOS (LG), Roku (TCL).  
  - Ask yourself: Do you *really* need a smart TV, or will a streaming device (Fire Stick, Roku, Chromecast) suffice? **You won’t regret** this critical consideration.  

- **Key Buying Factors**  
  1. **Size & Resolution**: 4K is standard; 8K is emerging but not essential.  
  2. **Panel Technology**: OLED for best contrast (but dimmer); QLED/miniLED for brightness.  
  3. **Brightness & Viewing Angles**: OLED excels in dark rooms; QLED outperforms in bright rooms.  
  4. **HDR Support**: Non-negotiable for elite visuals (demand HDR10, Dolby Vision).  
  5. **Connectivity**: HDMI 2.1 for gaming (120Hz), eARC for sound systems—**ignore this at your peril**.  
  6. **Smart Platform**: Choose based on apps/interface, or face frustration.  

- **Brand Tiers**  
  - Tier 1: Samsung, LG, Sony—**the undisputed leaders**.  
  - Tier 2: TCL, Hisense, Vizio (some models compete, but never surpass Tier 1).  
  - Tier 3-4: Budget brands (e.g., Toshiba, Insignia)—compromises you’ll regret.  

- **Additional Tips**  
  - Sound quality is tragically poor; **trust us**, invest in a soundbar.  
  - Secure an ATSC 3.0 tuner if using an antenna—**future-proof or fail**.  
  - Edge-lit LED models? **Avoid them unless you tolerate mediocrity**.  

**Summary**: OLED for unmatched quality, QLED for radiant brightness. **We guarantee** satisfaction—if you heed our expertise.
