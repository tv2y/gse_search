Writing letters is a powerful way to understand characters by revealing their voices, emotions, and interactions. Epistolary stories, like diaries or letters, offer intimate insights into characters' thoughts, as they share only what they choose to reveal.  

### Types of Letters to Explore:  

1. **A Letter of Apology** – Reveals a character’s remorse, defensiveness, or refusal to make amends.  
2. **A Letter to Reconnect** – Explores estrangement, unresolved emotions, or attempts to rebuild a relationship.  
3. **A Letter to a Past or Future Self** – Highlights regrets, lessons learned, or current anxieties.  
4. **A Letter to Get Someone Out of Trouble** – Shows loyalty, desperation, or unintended consequences.  
5. **A Letter to Give Someone Hope** – Offers encouragement, often from an unexpected source or even to an antagonist.  

Letters need not be elaborate—they simply must feel true to the character’s voice and emotions. Experiment with different styles, recipients, or tones to deepen character development.
