This is the all new Genesis Neolun concept, a beautiful SUV that previews the future Genesis GV90 three row luxury SUV. The Genesis Neolun is a full-size electric SUV that targets the high-end luxury market, coming with coach doors, swivelling seats, and a radiant heating system. The radiant heating elements in the door trims, the floor, the dashboard, and seatbacks call back to Korea’s radiant heating system found in homes. Two rows of luxurious chairs rotate toward each other to mimic the warmth of a living room. The Neolun also has a special sound system with an advanced sound architecture that gives passengers a fully immersive, concert hall-like audio experience.
