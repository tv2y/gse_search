Heartwarming moments at resettlement site after 6.8-magnitude earthquake in Gurong village of Xigaze, SW China's Xizang (3)
===========================================================================================================================

 (People's Daily Online) 14:49, January 13, 2025

A technician adjusts the TV signal on the roof of a temporary shelter at a resettlement site in Gurong village, Changsuo township, Dingri county, Xigaze city, southwest China's Xizang Autonomous Region, Jan. 12, 2025. Villagers were relocated to the site following a 6.8-magnitude earthquake. (People's Daily Online/Weng Qiyu)
