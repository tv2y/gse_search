1. **Questions and Answers** – Resolve all narrative quandaries, or conclude with a protagonist’s epiphanic reflection.  
2. **Summation** – A potent closing line can encapsulate the narrative’s quintessence, whether crystallizing a character’s metamorphosis or the central motif.  
3. **Reuse Your First Line** – Mirroring the incipient line (e.g., *Water for Elephants*) engenders a harmonious denouement.  
4. **Cadence Matters** – Euphony and prosody render a line mnemonic (observe Tom Robbins' lyrical exemplar).  
5. **Emotion is Key** – A poignant emotional crescendo etches itself into the reader’s psyche long after the final page.  
6. **Write It First** – Some authors inscribe the terminal line aprioristically to steer the narrative’s trajectory.

- *Gone With the Wind* – "After all, tomorrow is another day."  
- *Animal Farm* – Effaces the demarcation between porcine and human, underscoring the thematic core.  
- *Jitterbug Perfume* – A whimsical, mellifluous coda.
