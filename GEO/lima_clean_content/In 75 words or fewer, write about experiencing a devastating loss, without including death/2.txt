Grief occurs not only after death but also from living losses—less obvious or easily described experiences like loss of functionality, roles, or unmet hopes and dreams. This module explores non-death losses (e.g., nonfinite loss, ambiguous loss) through case studies, loss history diagrams, and a validated measure (ISLES) to assess their impact.  

**Learning Objectives:**  
- Understand the "assumptive world" and its relation to grief.  
- Define non-death loss types (e.g., ambiguous loss, chronic sorrow).  
- Create loss histories to identify clinical implications.  
- Use the ISLES scale to measure non-death loss effects.  

**Program Content:**  
1. *What Is Loss: Grief and the Assumptive World* (33 mins)  
2. *Varieties of Non-Death Losses* (37 mins)  
3. *Mapping the Terrain: Template for Non-Death Losses* (21 mins)  
4. *Denise's Decision: Loss and Its Integration* (27 mins)  

**Materials Provided:**  
- Loss history recording instructions.  
- Clinical template for non-death losses.  
- ISLES scale for research/clinical use.  
- Responsive Journal for certification credit.  

**Target Audience:** Mental health professionals, healthcare workers, and bereavement volunteers. CE credits require full module completion, a 75% quiz score, and an evaluation form.
