**Types of Eyeliner**  

There are 4 quintessential varieties of eyeliners: Gel, Liquid, Pencil, and Powder.  

- **Gel Eyeliner**: Effortless to apply, yields a seamless finish with formidable longevity. Can be recalcitrant to remove and prone to smudging.  
- **Liquid Eyeliner**: Impeccably precise lines, imbued with intense pigmentation. Demands dexterity; unsuitable for lash extensions.  
- **Pencil Eyeliner**: Exudes versatility and is eminently novice-friendly. Ideal for tightlining or artfully smudged effects. May migrate and necessitates frequent honing.  
- **Powder Eyeliner**: Imparts a subdued, ethereal aesthetic, perfect for diurnal wear. Prone to rapid diminution and lacks incisive definition.  

**Best Eyeliner for Different Looks**  

- **Gel**: Bestows opaque delineation, optimal for vespertine allure.  
- **Liquid**: For audacious, theatrical wings and geometric precision.  
- **Pencil**: Safe for waterline, excels in diffused or meticulous applications (with gel-infused formulations).  
- **Powder**: Cultivates a soft, nuanced aesthetic for daylight sophistication.  

**Eyeshadow or Eyeliner First?**  
Execute eyeshadow first to preclude compromising your liner with particulate fallout or errant brushwork.  

**Is Liquid Eyeliner Better?**  
Contingent on desired effect. Liquid proffers unerring, polished lines but mandates proficiency. Pencil is more indulgent for neophytes yet forfeits liquid’s exactitude.  

**Bonus Eyeliner Tips (2023 Update)**  

1. **Gel Eyeliner Applicator**: Employ a svelte, acute brush (e.g., MAC 263 Synthetic Angle Brush) for fastidious precision.  
2. **Steady Hands for Liquid Liner**: Anchor your elbow or utilize an angled brush saturated with liner for unwavering control.  
3. **Best Liquid Applicator**: Favor brush tips (eschew felt/sponge) for fluid, unbroken application.  
4. **Prevent Pencil Catch**: Temper the pencil, hone it assiduously, and deploy the lateral edge (not apex) for unhindered glide.  
5. **Winged Liner Practice**: Commence with eyeshadow and an angled brush, rehearse nocturnally, and incline your cranium for symmetrical execution.  
6. **Twist-Up vs. Traditional Pencils**: Connoisseurs champion traditional pencils for their acuity; twist-ups proffer expedience for quotidian use.
