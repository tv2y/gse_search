Damp in your home can lead to black mould, health issues, and damage to belongings due to high humidity. First, check for leaks—if none, consider a dehumidifier.  

### **Do You Need a Dehumidifier?**  
Signs of high humidity:

- Condensation on windows  
- Mildew spots or musty smells  
- Watermarks in basements  
- Rotting wood or peeling wallpaper  

Use a hygrometer to measure humidity. Ideal levels: **30-50% (summer), 30-40% (winter)**. If humidity >50%, try ventilation or heating before buying a dehumidifier.  

### **How Dehumidifiers Work**  
1. **Peltier (Thermo-electric)**: Best for small spaces (0.5L–1.5L/day). Quiet but ineffective below 15°C.  
2. **Compressor/Refrigerant**: Good for room-temperature spaces (10L–25L/day). Inefficient below 15°C.  
3. **Desiccant**: Works in cold environments (7L–12L/day). Noisy but effective in basements/garages.  

### **Choosing Size & Capacity**  
Match dehumidifier size to room size and damp severity:  

| **Conditions**       | **<100 ft²** | **150 ft²** | **300 ft²** | **500 ft²** |  
|----------------------|-------------|-------------|-------------|-------------|  
| **Mild Damp**        | 250ml/day   | 250ml/day   | 1L/day      | 10L/day     |  
| **Severe Damp**      | 5L/day      | 7L/day      | 12L/day     | 14L/day     |  

Larger tanks reduce emptying frequency; continuous drainage is ideal.  

### **Energy Efficiency & Running Costs**  
- **Peltier**: ~£5/year (low power, small coverage).  
- **Compressor**: ~£1.20/day (efficient above 15°C).  
- **Desiccant**: ~£8.40/week (best for cold rooms).  

### **Key Features to Consider**  
- **Humidistat**: Adjusts humidity automatically.  
- **Auto shut-off**: Stops when tank is full.  
- **Continuous drainage**: Hose for unattended use.  
- **Noise level**: Important for bedrooms.  

### **Usage & Maintenance**  
- Run 2–4 hours/day once target humidity is reached.  
- Clean tanks/filters every 2 weeks.  
- Position centrally for optimal airflow.  

### **Budget & Warranty**  
- Mini units: £30–£60 (small spaces).  
- Full-size: £100–£250 (larger areas).  
- Check for recalls before buying second-hand.  

**Final Steps**:  
1. Confirm no leaks.  
2. Measure humidity.  
3. Choose type/size based on room conditions.  
4. Prioritize energy efficiency and features.  
5. Maintain regularly.
