### **Main Information from the Web Page**  

#### **About SD Cards**  
- **Definition:** An SD (Secure Digital) card is a compact, lightweight, removable flash memory card used for high-capacity storage in devices like digital cameras, camcorders, phones, and game consoles.  
- **Types:** Standard SD, miniSD, and microSD (smaller, popular in mobile devices).  
- **History:** Introduced in 1999, SD cards evolved with increased storage capacities (up to 1TB) and faster speeds.  

#### **Key Considerations When Buying SD Cards**  
1. **Purpose:** Faster cards (Class 10, UHS-I/U3) are needed for DSLRs/camcorders vs. MP3 players.  
2. **Capacity:** Ranges from 8GB to 1TB; 16GB–256GB common for photography/video.  
3. **Speed Class:**  
   - **Class Ratings (2, 4, 6, 10):** Minimum write speeds (e.g., Class 10 = 10MB/s).  
   - **UHS (Ultra High Speed):** U1 (10MB/s), U3 (30MB/s).  
   - **Video Speed (V):** V6–V90 for 4K/8K video.  
4. **Brands:** SanDisk, Kingston, Samsung, PNY, and Lexar are top choices.  

#### **Common Uses**  
- Storing photos/videos (DSLRs, drones, dash cams).  
- Expanding storage in phones, tablets, or gaming devices.  
- Backup/data transfer.  

#### **Bulk Purchasing Benefits**  
- Cost savings for large quantities (e.g., businesses, photographers, manufacturers).  
- Options for OEM/custom branding.
