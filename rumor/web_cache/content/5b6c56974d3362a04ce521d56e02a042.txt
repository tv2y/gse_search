Abstract:

This paper introduces a groundbreaking approach to transorm urban mobility by integrat-

ing Unmanned Aerial Vehicles (UAVs) and Visible Light Communication (VLC) technologies into

trafc management systems within smart cities. With the continued growth o urban populations, the

escalating trafc density in large cities poses signifcant challenges to the daily mobility o citizens,rendering traditional ground-based trafc management methods increasingly inadequate. In this

context, UAVs provide a distinctive perspective or real-time trafc monitoring and congestion detec-

propose a new communication protocol and messaging system or this ramework, enhancing its

adaptability to varying trafc ows. This research represents a signifcant stride toward developing

more efcient, sustainable, and resilient urban transportation systems.
