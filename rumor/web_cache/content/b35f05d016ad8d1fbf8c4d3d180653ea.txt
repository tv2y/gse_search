#### WHY ATTEND GENESIS?

Edit Markdown

#### Welcome to Genesis! We are the world's largest and longest running Smash + FGC tournament series!

Join us this Presidents Day weekend, from Friday through Sunday, Feb 13-15 and immerse yourself in the world of competitive gaming. With 24-hour access for ALL ticket holders and extra dedicated day in the main hall to grind during the Top 8 Championship, as well as an amazing Artist Alley, and a lot more we will be announcing in the coming months!

#### What's new for GX3?

##### 2XKO!

*   We are proud to announce we'll be hosting 1v1 AND 2v2 2XKO brackets at GX3!

##### Expanded Sunday!

*   All 170k sqft of Genesis will be open at the convention center on Sunday! We'll also be moving our finals to the convention center, everything happens under one roof!

##### MORE On-Demand Brackets

*   We're constantly adding more side events to expand your competitive horizons and to try something new with little to no commitment!

##### MORE Trading Card Game events, featuring RBET!

*   We are hosting RBET and goatformat events for Yu-Gi-Oh! as well as MTG Draft and Commander/EDH format on-demand brackets!

##### Table Top Gaming

*   We are expanding on our table top gaming zone with new games, RPGs, TCG's, and more! Come check out some games and play with your friends or make new ones, open 24/7 throughout the weekend!

##### Riichi Mahjong

*   We are teaming up with the Pacific Mahjong League to bring you a Riichi Mahjong side event! Each day there will be designated start times for the on-demand brackets, as well as staff to help teach you how to play if you'd like to learn!

##### Cosplay Contest

*   GX3 marks the second time we will be hosting a Cosplay Contest! Sign up in person and show us what you got! Prizes for Top 8 will be available including Free passes for next years event, Merch, and cash!

 SPECTATOR PASS INFO 

#### SPECTATOR PASS:

*   ###### No entry to brackets

*   ###### 1-day passes expire 8am the day after the badge is picked up

*   ###### If you wish to attend Top 8 ONLY, please buy a 1-Day pass

###### _SUNDAY/ TOP 8 FINALS_

*   If you wish to attend Sunday Top 8 ONLY, purchase a 1-Day Spectator pass and pick it up on Sunday
*   If a 2-Day Pass is purchased, you must redeem it on SATURDAY, to be able to attend SUNDAY
*   3-Day passes will allow you access no matter when you arrive.

*   ###### GX3 Sunday Top 8 Finals takes place in the CONVENTION CENTER, the Civic Center is not used this year.
