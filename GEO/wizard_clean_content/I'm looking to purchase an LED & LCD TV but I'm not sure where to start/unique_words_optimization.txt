- **LED vs. LCD vs. OLED TVs**  
  - LED TVs are LCD TVs with LED backlighting (a marketing nomenclature).  
  - OLED TVs utilize self-emissive pixels (vibrant hues, flawless blacks but prone to image retention).  
  - QLED is an LCD TV augmented with quantum dots for chromatic enhancement (e.g., Samsung, TCL).  

- **Smart TV Features**  
  - Native applications (Netflix, YouTube, Hulu, etc.).  
  - Smart platforms diverge by brand: Google/Android (Sony), Tizen (Samsung), WebOS (LG), Roku (TCL).  
  - Deliberate whether a smart TV is indispensable or if a streaming adjunct (Fire Stick, Roku, Chromecast) would suffice.  

- **Key Buying Considerations**  
  1. **Size & Resolution**: 4K is ubiquitous; 8K is nascent but non-essential.  
  2. **Panel Technology**: OLED for superlative contrast (albeit less luminous); QLED/miniLED for luminance.  
  3. **Brightness & Viewing Angles**: OLED excels in subdued lighting; QLED outperforms in radiant environments.  
  4. **HDR Support**: Augments dynamic range and chroma (prioritize HDR10, Dolby Vision).  
  5. **Connectivity**: HDMI 2.1 for gaming (120Hz), eARC for audiophile systems.  
  6. **Smart Platform**: Select based on preferred ecosystem/interface.  

- **Brand Stratification**  
  - Tier 1: Samsung, LG, Sony.  
  - Tier 2: TCL, Hisense, Vizio (select models rival Tier 1).  
  - Tier 3-4: Value-oriented brands (e.g., Toshiba, Insignia).  

- **Pro Recommendations**  
  - Acoustic fidelity is often lackluster; contemplate a soundbar.  
  - Validate ATSC 3.0 tuner for antenna usage (future-proofing).  
  - Eschew edge-lit LED variants for superior luminosity uniformity.  

**Synopsis**: Prioritize display paradigm (OLED for fidelity, QLED for brilliance), ascertain requisite smart functionalities, and corroborate ports/connectivity for your exigencies.
