Title: Qingdao University Under Fire After Dormitory Guard's Death and Student's Hospitalization Due to Heatstroke

Published Time: 2025-07-07T00:01:49.000Z

Markdown Content:
A recent incident at Qingdao University in China has raised concerns about the living conditions of students and staff on campus. On July 6, a 58-year-old male dormitory guard was found unconscious in his duty room and later pronounced dead after failed rescue efforts. The guard, who was responsible for the Yingyuan dormitory area, lived in a small, 6-square-meter house without air conditioning, which had poor ventilation and reached temperatures of up to 51°C on the day of the incident. This tragic event has sparked widespread concern and outrage among students, parents, and the general public, highlighting the university's alleged negligence and inadequate measures to address the extreme heatwave affecting the region.

The same day, a female student from the same dormitory was hospitalized due to heatstroke, underscoring the perilous living conditions faced by students and staff alike. The university's dormitories, which accommodate thousands of students, are reportedly not equipped with air conditioning, forcing students to endure sweltering temperatures that have been known to exceed 40°C. The lack of proper ventilation and cooling systems has led to widespread complaints of sleepless nights, heat-related illnesses, and unbearable living conditions. Students have taken to social media to express their concerns about the university's preparedness for the heatwave and the lack of air conditioning in dormitories, which has been a long-standing issue.

The incident has also brought to light the university's allegedly cavalier attitude towards the welfare of its staff and students, with many expressing disappointment and frustration at the institution's priorities and resource allocation. The death of the dormitory administrator has sent shockwaves through the university community, with many students describing him as a kind and gentle man who had adopted many stray cats. His death has sparked outrage and criticism of the university's handling of the heatwave, with students and staff calling for improved living and working conditions.

In response to the incident, Qingdao University has issued a statement acknowledging the incident and promising to cooperate with the investigation. The university's admissions office confirmed that the school's dormitory management is outsourced to a third-party property company and that the decision not to install air conditioning in the past was based on the relatively mild summer temperatures in Qingdao. However, due to the abnormally high temperatures this year, the university has decided to install air conditioning in all dormitories during the summer break. The university has also announced that it will conduct a thorough review of its policies and procedures to prevent similar incidents from happening in the future.

The university's response has been met with a mix of emotions from the public, with some expressing sympathy for the deceased and others criticizing the school's lack of preparedness for extreme weather conditions. The incident has sparked discussions about the need for better working and living conditions for university staff and students. The installation of air conditioning in all dormitories is a positive step towards improving the living conditions of students. However, the university must also consider other measures to prevent heat-related illnesses, such as providing regular health check-ups, promoting heat awareness, and ensuring that staff and students have access to cool drinking water and shaded areas.

Ultimately, the incident highlights the need for universities to prioritize the health, safety, and well-being of their students and staff, especially during extreme weather conditions. By taking proactive measures to prevent heat-related illnesses and improving living and working conditions, universities can create a safer and more comfortable environment for everyone. The public will be closely monitoring the university's response and future plans, expecting concrete steps to address the concerns and criticisms raised by the incident.
