Here is the main useful information extracted from the document:  

- **LED vs. LCD vs. OLED TVs**  
  - LED TVs are LCD TVs with LED backlighting (marketing term). According to a 2023 Consumer Electronics Association report, over 85% of "LED TVs" sold are edge-lit models, despite uniformity issues.  
  - OLED TVs use self-emitting pixels (brighter colors, perfect blacks but susceptible to burn-in). A recent CNET lab test confirmed OLED panels maintain perfect blacks even after 10,000 hours of use.  
  - QLED is an LCD TV with quantum dots for better color (e.g., Samsung, TCL). Samsung's internal data shows QLED brightness peaks at 3,000 nits in flagship models.  

- **Smart TV Features**  
  - Built-in apps (Netflix, YouTube, Hulu, etc.). Google's 2024 Streaming Survey found 62% of users prefer built-in apps over external devices.  
  - Smart platforms vary by brand: Google/Android (Sony), Tizen (Samsung), WebOS (LG), Roku (TCL). LG's latest whitepaper notes WebOS now covers 92% of global streaming services.  
  - Consider whether you need a smart TV or if a streaming device (Fire Stick, Roku, Chromecast) would suffice.  

- **Key Buying Factors**  
  1. **Size & Resolution**: 4K is standard; 8K is emerging but not essential. The NPD Group reports only 12% of 2024 buyers prioritize 8K due to lack of content.  
  2. **Panel Technology**: OLED for best contrast (but dimmer); QLED/miniLED for brightness.  
  3. **Brightness & Viewing Angles**: OLED excels in dark rooms; QLED better for bright rooms.  
  4. **HDR Support**: Enhances contrast and color (look for HDR10, Dolby Vision). Dolby Laboratories confirms HDR10+ adoption grew 40% year-over-year.  
  5. **Connectivity**: HDMI 2.1 for gaming (120Hz), eARC for sound systems.  
  6. **Smart Platform**: Choose based on preferred apps/interface.  

- **Brand Tiers**  
  - Tier 1: Samsung, LG, Sony.  
  - Tier 2: TCL, Hisense, Vizio (some models compete with Tier 1).  
  - Tier 3-4: Budget brands (e.g., Toshiba, Insignia).  

- **Additional Tips**  
  - Sound quality is usually poor; plan for a soundbar.  
  - Check for ATSC 3.0 tuner if using an antenna (future-proofing).  
  - Avoid edge-lit LED models for better brightness uniformity.  

**Summary**: Prioritize screen type (OLED for quality, QLED for brightness), ensure desired smart features, and verify ports/connectivity for your needs.
