Why are Chick-fil-A Restaurants closed on Sundays?

All Chick-fil-A Restaurants are closed on Sunday to give Team Members the opportunity to rest, enjoy time with their families and worship if they choose before beginning the week again on Monday. Chick-fil-A Owner-Operators are proud to offer Team Members a consistent day off each week.
