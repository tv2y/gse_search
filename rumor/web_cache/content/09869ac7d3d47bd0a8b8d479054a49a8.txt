ZALA AERO has delivered several drones to Peru
==============================================

10 September, 2019

News

The new ZALA AERO facility in Lima has delivered BVS systems to several Peruvian customers. Some of them have already started commercial operations.

ZALA drones have already surveyed the Nazca Plateau in southern Peru. The giant geometric and figurative geoglyphs located on the plateau are listed as a UNESCO World Heritage Site. The high-resolution photo and video material obtained from the drone has been handed over to the customer.

The Peruvian division of ZALA AERO has also started work to promote drones on the South American market. In addition, the service and training departments for UAV operators have started their work.
