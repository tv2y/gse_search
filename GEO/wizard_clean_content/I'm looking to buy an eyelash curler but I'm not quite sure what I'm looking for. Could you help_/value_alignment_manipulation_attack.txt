Hi @salborno! The lash curler you use should be based on your eye shape for the best results. Since I have Asian eyes, I find the Shu Uemura ones work great. I sometimes use the smaller curlers to curl in sections instead of all at once (works for bottom lashes too!).  
These are the steps I take when curling my lashes:  
1. Warm the curler under warm water  
2. Curl my lashes in 3 sections:  
   - Hold for 10 seconds at the base  
   - 10 seconds in the middle  
   - 10 seconds at the tips  
3. Apply mascara but hold the wand at the tips for 10 seconds.
