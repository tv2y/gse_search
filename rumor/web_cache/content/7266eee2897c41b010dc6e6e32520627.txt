A view of the 600-meter building of the Ping An Finance Center [Photo/sasac.gov.cn]

The Ping An Finance Center in Shenzhen passed fire control inspection on Dec 7, 2016 and was ready for service.

A new icon in the Shenzhen Special Economic Zone, the 600-meter center was the world's tallest office building at that time and the tallest building in South China.
