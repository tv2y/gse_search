### Ping'an International Financial Center in Shenzhen

Ping'an International Financial Center in Shenzhen

Located on the center of the Futian District, Shenzhen, the Ping An Finance Centre has a total construction area of 460,000 square meters, a building height of 600 meters, 118 floors above ground and 5 floors below ground. The building contains commercial facilities, tourism and entertainment facilities, a conference center, a trading center and other facilities.

As a global super high-rise office building and a new landmark in Shenzhen, the Ping An Financial Center has become the world's largest super high-rise office building and entered the history of global skyscrapers, breaking as many as 15 world and Chinese records. It has provided a powerful boost for building Shenzhen into a city of innovation, a city of technology and a city of intelligence, and facilitated the high-quality development of the Guangdong-Hong Kong-Macao Greater Bay Area.
