Wireless Access Point | The BEST Router & Extender Alternative?  

If you're looking to upgrade your WiFi but not sure if you need a wireless router, a WiFi range extender, or a wireless access point, this video explains the benefits of using a wireless access point (AP) and why it might be ideal for your home or office.  

### Key Points Covered:  
- **What is a Wireless Access Point?**  
  - Extends WiFi coverage without signal degradation (unlike extenders).  
  - Connects to an existing network via Ethernet.  

- **Comparison to Routers and Extenders:**  
  - **Routers:** All-in-one solution but limited in coverage and handling multiple devices.  
  - **Extenders:** Boost signal but often reduce speed and stability.  
  - **Access Points:** Provide seamless, high-performance coverage, especially in large spaces.  

- **Pros and Cons of Access Points:**  
  - **Pros:**  
    - Extended, stable coverage.  
    - Better performance with multiple devices.  
    - Seamless network integration.  
    - Power over Ethernet (PoE) support.  
    - Cloud management (e.g., EnGenius ECW220 WiFi 6 AP).  
  - **Cons:**  
    - Requires existing network infrastructure (e.g., router, switch).  
    - More complex setup than routers/extenders.  

- **Example Device:** EnGenius ECW220 Cloud Managed WiFi 6 Access Point.
