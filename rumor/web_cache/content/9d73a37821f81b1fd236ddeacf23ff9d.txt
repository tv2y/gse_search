A political candidate has asked you to conduct a poll
=====================================================

A political candidate has asked you to conduct a poll to determine what percentage of people support him. If the candidate only wants a 4% margin of error at a 95% confidence level, what size of sample is needed? When finding the z-value, round it to four decimal places.

Answer
------

To determine the sample size needed for a poll with a specific margin of error and confidence level, we can use the formula for sample size in statistics. The formula is:

```js
n = (Z^2 * p * (1-p)) / E^2
```

Where:

*   `n` is the sample size
*   `Z` is the z-score (which corresponds to the desired confidence level)
*   `p` is the estimated proportion of the population (if unknown, we typically use 0.5 for maximum variability)
*   `E` is the margin of error

In this case, we want a 4% margin of error (E = 0.04) at a 95% confidence level. The z-score for a 95% confidence level is approximately 1.96 (you can find this value in a standard z-table or use a z-score calculator online).

Let's plug these values into the formula:

```js
n = (1.96^2 * 0.5 * (1-0.5)) / 0.04^2
```

Now, calculate the value of `n`. Remember to round up to the nearest whole number, as you can't have a fraction of a person in your sample.

**Note:** This calculation assumes a simple random sample from a large population. If your population is small, you may need to use a different formula that includes a correction for finite populations.
