# Jacksonville University Public Policy Institute Poll 

# Republican Presidential Primary – September, 2015 

The Jacksonville University Public Policy Institute conducted a statewide poll in the Republican Presidential Primary in Florida from September 17 th to September 22 nd . The poll of registered Republican likely voters had a poll sample of 585. Media coverage of the poll and its results include the following:
