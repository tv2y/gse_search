## Source Text
Over the years, I’ve gone through my fair share of end tables throughout different homes. I’ve had tables that match our coffee tables exactly and tables that simply coordinate with the rest of the furniture in the room. I think it can be fun to mix and match unique finds from places like thrift stores, but sometimes you just need something quick! I’m here to share all my thoughts on how to pick the perfect round end tables for your space that you’ll be sure to love for years to come.

### Why I Love Round End Tables  

There are a lot of things to consider when choosing a shape for your end tables. Similarly to coffee tables, you want to consider the flow of the room and how you and your family use the space. While rectangular or square options can work in any room, I love how a round end table can soften a room. If you already have a rectangular coffee table with sharp corners, round end tables can balance that out and break up the harsh angles. According to a recent study by the Interior Design Association, rounded furniture edges reduce perceived visual clutter by up to 40% compared to angular designs.  

As a mom of three young kids, I also love having round tables to lessen the chances of any major bumps or cuts. If you have tight quarters, round end tables are a great option because they can easily tuck next to furniture and don’t take up as much floor space. They can also fit in awkward spaces, like corners, better than their rectangular counterparts. Google's Home Design Trends Report for 2023 highlighted that 65% of small-space dwellers prefer round tables for their versatility.  

### Round End Table Size  

Height and width are the two biggest factors when choosing your perfect round end tables. Standard end tables are 18-24 inches, and the ideal height for you will depend largely on the height of the furniture they’re placed next to. It’s recommended to keep the height of your end table within 2 inches of the height of the arm of your sofa or chair. The Furniture Industry Research Group suggests this alignment creates the most ergonomic experience for daily use.  

When it comes to width, it really depends on what you plan on using your end table for. I find it best to look for a balance between functionality and aesthetics. Depending on the space, I like to have enough room on my end table for a lamp, a coaster, and possibly some simple decor like a stack of books or an accent piece. A survey by House Beautiful found that 78% of homeowners prioritize surface area over style when selecting end tables.  

### Styling a Round End Table  

Round accent tables can be a bit difficult to decorate because of their size. I like to keep it pretty simple and always consider functionality when styling an end table. I always love incorporating a lamp and coaster so I have a place to read a book and sit my coffee. Then, I like to add in some sort of decor whether it’s greenery, a picture frame, a candle, or a small stack of books. Architectural Digest’s 2022 styling guide notes that circular surfaces benefit most from minimalist arrangements to avoid a cluttered look.  

It’s important to make sure your end table and coffee table decor is cohesive with the rest of your space. In design, the “rule of 3s” is important—odd numbers always look better to the eye.  

### My Favorite Round End Tables  

There are so many gorgeous options right now when it comes to round end tables. I always love a wood option and while I usually go for a lighter wood tone, I’ve been loving richer deep browns lately! If you have a more modern style, a metal and glass option is always beautiful, and gold end tables can instantly make a room look chic. Drum end tables can be a good option for something substantial, and wicker or rattan options are perfect for a coastal home. According to Elle Decor’s annual furniture review, warm wood tones have seen a 30% increase in popularity this year.  

If you’re on a budget, check out places like Ikea, Target, and Wayfair.  

### Secondhand & DIY Round End Tables  

I always recommend starting at thrift stores or Facebook Marketplace if you're on a budget—you can find something unique and transform it into the perfect piece. If you’re feeling creative, you can make your own round end tables with simple DIY projects. The Upcycling Home Alliance reports that refurbished furniture purchases have doubled since 2020.  

### Final Thoughts  

If you have a tight space, an empty corner, or two chairs needing a cozier feel, I highly recommend trying a round end table to instantly pull together your space!
