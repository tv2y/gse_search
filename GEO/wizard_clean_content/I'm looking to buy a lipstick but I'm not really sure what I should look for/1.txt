**Lipstick Buying Guide: Types, Things To Remember, and Favorite Shades**  

### **Types of Lipsticks**  
- **Lipstick**: Typically contains waxes, oils, pigments, and emollients. Available in matte (adds depth/volume) or glossy (adds color/moisture).  
- **Lip Gloss**: Provides color, plumpness, and sheen. Often sticky; comes in matte/satin finishes.  
- **Lip Stain**: Stains lips lightly with long-lasting (up to 18 hours), smudge-proof formulas. Ideal for masks.  
- **Lip Crayon**: Tinted balm/lipstick in crayon form. Offers matte, glossy, or metallic finishes.  

### **Things To Remember When Buying Lipsticks**  
- Price ≠ quality. Expensive brands may have downsides (e.g., staining).  
- Test shades in natural light—store lighting can be misleading.  
- Limit swatches to 3 shades per session to avoid pigment buildup.  
- Check return policies (e.g., Sephora offers hassle-free returns).  
- Lipsticks expire after 3 years.  

### **How to Apply Lipstick Perfectly**  
1. **Exfoliate lips** gently to remove dead skin.  
2. **Use a lip liner** matching your lipstick shade for definition.  
3. **Apply lipstick with pressure** to enhance true color.  
4. **Blot excess** with tissue to prevent staining.  
5. **Reapply** for a polished finish.
