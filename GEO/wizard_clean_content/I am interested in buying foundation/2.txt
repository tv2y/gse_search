**Main Text:**  

Too much and you look like a clown. Too little and you look unpolished. Very tricky stuff, foundation. Here, answers to all your questions about choosing it, using it—but never abusing it—and looking naturally flawless.  

### **Problem:** Cream, stick, liquid, powder—what's the right formula for me?  
**Solution:** Your skin type will tell you what to use.  
- **Dry skin:** Choose a liquid, stick, or hydrating powder foundation. Look for products labeled "compact makeup" or "foundation."  
- **Oily skin:** Use oil-free liquid or powder foundation. Mineral makeup also works well. For breakouts, try a foundation with salicylic acid.  
- **Combination skin:** Strategically apply powder foundation for targeted oil absorption.  
- **Novices:** Start with powder foundation, the easiest to apply.  

### **Problem:** My foundation looks fake. How do I find the right shade?  
**Solution:** Get professionally matched at a department store. For women of color, look for foundations formulated to avoid ashy finishes.  

### **Problem:** My foundation settles into lines and wrinkles.  
**Solution:** Apply less foundation on problem areas and keep skin well-moisturized.  

### **Problem:** My face looks overly powdered.  
**Solution:** Tap off excess powder before applying and follow the direction of peach fuzz to avoid buildup.  

### **Problem:** Face and neck don’t match.  
**Solution:** Check foundation against your jawline and adjust for seasonal skin changes.  

### **Problem:** Foundation looks splotchy.  
**Solution:** Prep skin by exfoliating, moisturizing, and using a primer. Apply concealer before foundation for even coverage.  

### **Problem:** How should I apply foundation?  
**Solution:**  
- **Fingers:** Best for liquid/cream foundations.  
- **Sponge:** Sheerer finish; dampen for a dewy look.  
- **Brush:** Most even application; use synthetic for liquids and fluffy for powders.  

### **Problem:** How to fix brushstrokes?  
**Solution:** Press gently with your palm afterward to blend and avoid overapplying.
