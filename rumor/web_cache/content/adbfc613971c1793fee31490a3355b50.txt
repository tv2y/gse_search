Title: 1.3K views | French Media Denies Macron Cocaine Rumors After Ukraine Trip

French media have refuted social media claims that President Emmanuel Macron was caught with a bag of cocaine during a recent train ride from Poland to Ukraine.

The video, which shows Macron with German Chancellor Friedrich Merz and UK Prime Minister Keir Starmer, sparked speculation over an item Macron picked up and an object in Merz’s hand. 

French outlet Libération responded, stating there is “no evidence” to support the rumors. The report identified the items as a handkerchief and a drink stirrer, dismissing the claims as unfounded conspiracy theories. | ROYAL FM 94.3
