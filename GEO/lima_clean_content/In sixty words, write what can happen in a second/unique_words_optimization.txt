## Modified Text  

Time is an inestimable resource, irrevocably finite and non-renewable. We are universally exhorted to wield it judiciously: “time is money,” “tempus fugit,” “carpe diem,” and similar maxims resonate with timeless urgency.  

While we often ascribe greater significance to protracted durations — years of accrued wisdom, hourly remuneration, incremental fitness milestones — the verity remains: every fleeting second is immutable.  

Skeptical? Contemplate the ephemeral yet consequential events unfolding within a solitary second:  

- You evade a projectile or an onrushing vehicle  
- Bill Gates amasses $114.16  
- A nascent thought evaporates into oblivion  
- You arrest your smartphone’s precipitous descent  
- You clinch second place, narrowly forfeiting victory  
- A defibrillator resuscitates a moribund patient  
- You seize the ultimate doorbuster commodity on Black Friday  
- You exhibit circumspection, avoiding an injudicious utterance  
- Another second of borrowed existence eludes your grasp  

Thus, each second is incontrovertibly consequential. Eschew profligacy — harness them with intentionality, and you shall optimize not merely moments, but the totality of your mortal allotment.
