The **men's 100 metres** event at the 2023 Asian Athletics Championships was held on 13 and 14 July.[1]

Records before the 2023 Asian Athletics Championships | Record | Athlete (nation) | Time (s) | Location | Date |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| World record[2] | Usain Bolt(JAM) | **9.58** | Berlin, Germany | 16 August 2009 |
| Asian record[3] | Su Bingtian(CHN) | **9.83** | Tokyo, Japan | 1 August 2021 |
| Championship record | Femi Ogunode(QAT) | **9.91** | Wuhan, China | 4 June 2015 |
| World leading[4] | Zharnel Hughes(GBR) | **9.83** | New York City, United States | 24 June 2023 |
| Asian leading[5] | Yoshihide Kiryu(JPN) | **10.03** | Osaka, Japan | 6 May 2023 |
