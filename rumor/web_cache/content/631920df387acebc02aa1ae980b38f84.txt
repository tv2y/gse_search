Markdown Content:
A task force is a unit or formation established to work on a single defined task or activity. WFSBP Task Forces provide some of the most important**scientific aspects of the WFSBP**. Each Task Force is represented in the list below and includes its members and scientific inputs.

Should you be interested in becoming involved in the WFSBP and particularly a certain Task Force, please contact us directly by emailing info@wfsbp.org.

**Chair:**Kates, Nick (Canada)

**Members:**

Torleif, Ruud (Norway)

Williams, Mark (USA)

**Description of goals and objectives:**

*   To develop a report that will provide guidelines for improving care for individuals with mental disorders being seen in primary care by:

a. increasing the capacity and expertise of primary care to identify and manage these problems

b. improving relationships between primary care and mental health care providers to support family physicians and general practitioners in this role
*   To review documents from other jurisdictions that have addressed this issue and to learn from and build upon their recommendations and experience.
*   To review the current literature on best practices in managing mental disorders in primary care and collaboration between primary care and mental health care providers and determine its applicability for this project.
*   To identify specific resources which could be of value to practitioners in any country or community.
*   To circulate draft documents broadly for review before the document is presented to the WFBSP.
*   To present a final document to the WFBSP and assist in its dissemination
