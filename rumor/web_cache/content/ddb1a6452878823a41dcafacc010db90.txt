### Shilin Stone Forest

Shilin Stone Forest is home to one of the most famous stone topography of Shilin. Expanding in a massive land, the stone forest is a huge otherworldly landscape of karst formations over past years due to seismic activities. You will come across a number of giant stalagmite-like pillars which form huge arrays of labyrinths. ### Mount Tai

With an abundant amount of historical and cultural interests, Mount Tai is located in the City of Tai'an and is considered to be the highest point of China. From being the tallest peak to a religious landmark, Mount Tai has been a place of worship for at least 3,000 years and served as one of the most important ceremonial bases of China.

Inspired by its tranquil surroundings, Writers from far and wide places came to acquire knowledge and compose poems, write essays, paint and take pictures henceforth leaving some great cultural relics in the mountains. Besides, Mount Tai rises abruptly to become a natural beauty endowed with many scenic sites.

**Location:**City of Tai'an, Shandong province, China
