Anti-immigration fears have been stoked by a story that a container of "plastic furniture" destined for Syrian refugees actually contained thousands of weapons and tons of ammunition.

However the ship was actually sailing _from_ Turkey (where most of the Syrian refugees are) to the Libyan city of Misrata, in Africa, and while the intended recipients are unclear, they were certainly not refugees.
