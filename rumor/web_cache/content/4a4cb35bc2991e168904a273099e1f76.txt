The Royal Gorge Route Railroad is your ticket to the _most jaw-dropping canyon in North America_. Towering granite walls rising over 1,000 feet above you, the wild Arkansas River roaring beside you, and views so close you can almost touch them. Nothing else gets you this deep into Colorado’s grandest canyon — _only the train does_. Step onto our open-air cars and feel the wind in your hair as you spot bighorn sheep, soaring eagles, and those impossibly blue Colorado skies. Breathe it all in — this is the real deal, pure Colorado adventure.

You’ll roll through this epic scenery in style aboard our iconic Vista Dome, cozy Deluxe, or classic Coach service where we’ll invite you to indulge in delicious dining, prepared to order for breakfast, lunch, or dinner, cooked fresh right on board. Pair it up with a local craft beer, a glass of wine from somewhere fabulous, or your favorite cocktail or mocktail from our fully stocked bar.

**Book your ride today — and experience Colorado’s most unforgettable journey.**

Cancellation Policy
===================

The Royal Gorge Route Railroad is a popular destination attraction and payment is required to make a reservation. ***Cancellations made more than 48 hours of departure, will incur a $20.00 cancellation fee PER PERSON for Coach, Deluxe, and Vista Dome classes and $45.00 for lunch and dinner trains. No refunds will be issued for cancellations made within 48 hours of departure, and 'no shows.'

Rescheduling – If cancelled more than 48 hours of departure, you may rebook another date, within one year, without penalty.
