China wins women's 4x100m relay gold at Hangzhou Asiad
======================================================

 Xinhua | Updated: 2023-10-03 22:09 

Share

Share - WeChat

CLOSE

 Athletes compete during the Women's 4×100m Final of Athletics at the 19th Asian Games in Hangzhou, East China's Zhejiang Province, Oct 3, 2023. [Photo by Wei Xiaohao/chinadaily.com.cn] 

HANGZHOU -- China claimed the gold medal in the women's 4x100m relay at the Hangzhou Asian Games on Tuesday.

The Chinese quartet of Liang Xiaojing, Wei Yongli, Yuan Qiqi and Ge Manqi clocked a winning time of 43.39 seconds.

Thailand bagged silver in 44.32, while Malaysia took bronze in 45.01.

 Ge Manqi of China competes during the Women's 4×100m Final of Athletics at the 19th Asian Games in Hangzhou, East China's Zhejiang Province, Oct 3, 2023.[Photo by Wei Xiaohao/chinadaily.com.cn]
