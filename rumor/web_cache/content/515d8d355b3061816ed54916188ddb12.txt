Studies show that prolonged exposure to blue light can be detrimental to your health.

**Comment on blue light:**

Having learned about the dangers of blue light very early on in 2013 meant, I have been following blue light research closely over the years. The research/knowledge base has increased dramatically compared to what was available in previous years. Now we have epidemiological studies, clinical trials and peer reviewed papers all on how blue light affects our health.

It is now conclusive that blue light emitted from sources such as screens and ambient lighting, can affect three main aspects of our health. Firstly our eyes, by causing eye strain and headaches in the short term but also contributing towards Macular Degeneration in the long-term. Secondly, our sleep by suppressing the hormone melatonin which makes it possible for our bodies to fall asleep. Without the secretion of this hormone, we stay awake essentially. **Comment on blue light:**Blue light is a type of visible light with a shorter wavelength, making it emit higher energy. Blue light is not only given off by the sun but also by computer screens, smartphones, flat-screen televisions, and anything that has fluorescent and/or LED backlighting (which includes regular light bulbs btw!).

It is important to understand the effects of blue light because it is not only damaging to the eyes short term, but also long term. Short term blue light effects can make the eyes fatigued and dry. This can contribute to the symptoms of throbbing pain around the eyes - some even suffer from itchy/”burning” eyes. The long term blue light effects can decrease the pigment in the centre part of the vision known as the macula. This can increase the risk of development of macular degeneration - and even blindness.

The sun can actually radiate more blue light than screens. However, with the increasing amount of time in front of/staring directly at screens, we’re starting to see it affect a lot more eyes than the sun. ### Will these glasses prevent glare from car headlights at night?

Blue light glasses can help users with night driving since they reduce some of the glare from oncoming headlights, streetlights, etc.

### What are blue light glasses?

Blue light glasses are a pair of glasses that anyone who does not wear glasses can put on their face while using screens or is around artificial LED lighting. When wearing blue light glasses, the short wavelength, high energy blue-violet light is filtered so that you can enjoy viewing your screen without the harmful affects such as visual stress, eye strain, headaches and poor sleep quality.

### How to clean blue light glasses?

Cleaning blue light glasses is easy, make sure you use warm water and a clean cloth. You can use soap as a natural remover of dirt and oils - we would not recommend anything stronger as coatings can come away after a while. Foam up the soap and wipe the lenses with the cloth, then dry with a paper towel or a seperate cloth. You may want to use warm water after this incase there are any soap stains after drying to get rid of them.

### Can you wear blue light glasses with contacts?

Blue light glasses can be worn with both soft and hard contact lenses with no issues. The blue light glasses will not affect your ability to see or perform if wearing them when gaming for example.
