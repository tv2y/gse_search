Malar Bags Treatments: Fillers, Surgery vs. Endolift Laser
==========================================================

Home » Skin Rejuvenation Newport Beach, CA » Malar Bags Treatments: Fillers, Surgery vs. Endolift Laser

#### Dr. Kaveh Karandish

PCH MedSpa Medical Director

Search for: Search Button

 Discover the difference between festoons and under-eye bags. Learn about their distinct characteristics and treatment options. 

Table of Contents
-----------------

**Malar bags** are a common cosmetic concern that can affect the appearance of our cheeks and **under-eye area**. This blog talks about treatments for **malar****bags** and compares fillers with the **Endolift****laser**treatment.

Understanding Malar Bags:
-------------------------

**Malar bags** are **puffy****areas** that can develop on our cheeks below the eyes. It is essential to distinguish them from festoons and swellings on the cheekbones. Malar bags are often caused by aging, and sometimes even young individuals may experience **malar****bags** at **20****years****old**.

Festooning Face And Eye Festoon:
--------------------------------

Apart from **malar****bags**, individuals may also experience festooning of the face, specifically festoons under the eyes. **Festoons** are characterized by swelling or **puffiness** on the **cheekbones****beneath** the lower **eyelid****area**. Wearing certain items can cause an individual’s facial features to display signs of fatigue or aging.

**Treatment****options** for **festoons** on the **cheekbones** may vary from those for **malar****bags**. While fillers and **surgery** are commonly used for malar bags, treating festoons may involve a combination of approaches. Surgical interventions, such as lower eyelid surgery or mid-face lifts, are often recommended to address festoons effectively.

**Eye festoons** can be treated with a personalized plan. The goal is to reduce excess tissue and firm the skin around the under-eye area. This improves the contour and makes festoons less visible.
