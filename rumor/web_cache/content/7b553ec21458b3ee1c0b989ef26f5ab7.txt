**What is Sea Salt?**
---------------------

Pure unrefined sea salt is a natural ingredient that has many benefits for skincare. It is made by evaporating seawater, which leaves behind a rich mixture of minerals.

Sea salt is very different from regular table salt because it contains numerous skin-healing minerals like magnesium, calcium, potassium, zinc, iodine, and sodium. These minerals have various roles in maintaining the health and function of your skin cells. For example, magnesium helps reduce inflammation, calcium promotes cell renewal, potassium balances hydration, zinc fights acne-causing bacteria, iodine heals wounds, and sodium regulates pH levels.

Sea salt is a humectant that draws and locks in moisture at a cellular level. It is naturally antibacterial, antimicrobial, and anti-inflammatory, making it a great non-toxic option for natural exfoliation. When applied properly, it increases the permeability of the skin for greater absorption of other topical products. Some other benefits of using sea salt in skincare are:

*   Exfoliate dead skin cells and remove impurities from the pores
*   Balance the skin’s natural pH level and prevent bacterial growth
*   Reduce inflammation
*   Boost blood circulation and promote skin healing
*   Hydrate the skin and prevent moisture loss by attracting water molecules

**Implementing Sea Salt in Beauty Regimens**

### **Cleanser**

Sea salt is often added to facial cleansers because it absorbs excess oil, deep cleans pores, and balances future oil production. This, in turn, helps prevent acne and stop breakout-causing bacteria from forming in the pores. The deep cleaning also helps retain moisture from within, making the skin feel fresh and supple.

A sea salt facial works wonderfully for maximum exfoliation. All-natural unrefined sea salt is a great natural exfoliator because it blasts away dead skin cells while also nourishing the skin with minerals and restoring hydration. Deep exfoliation with sea salt promotes new healthy skin cell production and improves texture. Freshly-exfoliated skin is also better at absorbing serums and moisturizers, so it’s a step in your routine you shouldn’t skip!

### **Hydration**

Sea salt cleanses and exfoliates the skin and provides hydration to the deeper layers of the skin. In addition, it zaps excess oils from the skin’s pores and helps to maintain moisture balance in the skin.

**Sea Salt Skincare FAQ**
-------------------------

### **Does sea salt help eczema?**

Yes, sea salt can also be a helpful tool in dealing with eczema. In fact, the National Eczema Foundation recommends using sea salt soaks to relieve red and itchy skin associated with the condition. However, you should not use sea salt if you have open wounds or broken skin, as this can cause stinging and infection. Consult your doctor before using sea salt if you have severe or chronic eczema.

### **Can salt water help acne?**

Yes, the salt in salt water can help acne by killing the bacteria that cause it and reducing the inflammation that accompanies it. However, you should not rely on salt water alone to treat your acne. Consult a dermatologist and follow a proper skincare routine that includes cleansing, moisturizing, and using products that suit your skin type.
