The longest work of fiction in English is *Clarissa, or the History of a Young Lady* by Samuel Richardson. Published in 1748, it spans approximately 970,000–1,000,000 words in most modern editions, making it the longest single-volume novel in English written as a continuous work by one author.  

Other notable long works include:  
- *A Dance to the Music of Time* by Anthony Powell (12 volumes, ~1 million words).  
- Fanfiction like *The Subspace Emissary’s World Conquest* (over 4 million words) or *The Loud House: Revamped* (~35 million words), though these are multi-author or experimental.  

For traditionally published single works, *Clarissa* remains the longest.
