Jeanine Pirro bragged about helping Trump and GOP while a Fox News host
=======================================================================

 Posted Wednesday, August 20, 2025 10:05 am

Jeremy Barr | The Washington Post

Jeanine Pirro, then a weekend host on Fox News, told the chairman of the Republican National Committee in a September 2020 text message that she was determined to aid President Donald Trump and the Republican Party, despite the network prohibiting on-air personalities from political involvement.

“I work so hard for the party across the country,” Pirro, now the U.S. attorney for the District of Columbia, told Ronna McDaniel in the message, according to newly unredacted court documents made public Tuesday in filings by the voting technology company Smartmatic, which in 2021 filed a $2.7 billion defamation lawsuit against Fox over its 2020 election coverage. “I’m the Number 1 watched show on all news cable all weekend. I work so hard for the President and party.”

Who gives a cluck? Free Press’ ‘Media Capitulation Index’ names and shames outlets for kowtowing to Trump

 The media watchdog Free Press just rolled out a cheeky — but pointed — scorecard that ranks America’s biggest media companies on a five–chicken-head scale of “capitulation” to Donald Trump. Based on a six-month review of 35 major owners, the Media Capitulation Index flags everything from lawsuit payoffs and DEI rollbacks to editorial interference and Mar-a-Lago favor-currying — putting Trump Media and Elon Musk’s X at the five-chicken “propaganda” end, while Bloomberg and Netflix earn rare “independent” stars. “We wanted an instant visual that made people stop and think,” says Free Press strategist Timothy Karr, who led the research. The group plans annual updates — and hopes a little public shaming nudges powerful outlets back toward the public interest.
