Title: Continued weekend bus station closures in CBD

URL Source: https://www.brisbane.qld.gov.au/about-council/news-and-community-updates/community-service-announcements/july-2025/continued-weekend-bus-station-closures-in-cbd

Markdown Content:
These works are essential to prepare the new Adelaide Street tunnel for bus and metro operations.

Queen Street bus station, City Hall/King George Square station and Roma Street busway station will be closed from midnight Friday to 4:30am Monday on the below weekends.

*   Midnight Friday 18 July to 4.30am Monday 21 July

_Please note, on Saturday 19 July, Roma Street busway station will reopen from 4.30pm to midnight to support travel to and from the Wallabies vs British and Irish Lions game at Suncorp Stadium._

*   Midnight Friday 25 July to 4.30am Monday 28 July 
*   Midnight Friday 1 August to 4.30am Monday 4 August 
*   Midnight Friday 8 August to 4.30am Monday 11 August 
*   Midnight Friday 15 August to 4.30am Monday 18 August 
*   Midnight Friday 22 August to 4.30am Monday 25 August. 

All affected bus services will be temporarily redirected to alternative street level stops nearby. Metro services will be operated by bus replacement services.

Signage and on ground support will be in place at impacted bus stops to assist customers to their temporary alternative stops.

For information about bus stop and service changes, visit Journey Planner at translink.com.au or call 13 12 30.
