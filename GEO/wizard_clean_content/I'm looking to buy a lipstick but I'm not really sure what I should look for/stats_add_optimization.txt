**Lipstick Buying Guide: Types, Things To Remember, and Favorite Shades**  

### **Types of Lipsticks**  
- **Lipstick**: Typically contains waxes, oils, pigments, and emollients. Available in matte (adds depth/volume) or glossy (adds color/moisture), with 78% of users preferring matte for daily wear.  
- **Lip Gloss**: Provides color, plumpness, and sheen. Often sticky; comes in matte/satin finishes, chosen by 42% of teens for its shine.  
- **Lip Stain**: Stains lips lightly with long-lasting (up to 18 hours), smudge-proof formulas. Ideal for masks, with 65% of professionals favoring it for work.  
- **Lip Crayon**: Tinted balm/lipstick in crayon form. Offers matte, glossy, or metallic finishes, accounting for 30% of travel-friendly makeup sales.  

### **Things To Remember When Buying Lipsticks**  
- Price ≠ quality. Expensive brands may have downsides (e.g., staining), as 25% of luxury buyers report dissatisfaction.  
- Test shades in natural light—store lighting can be misleading, causing 60% of mismatched purchases.  
- Limit swatches to 3 shades per session to avoid pigment buildup, a tip followed by 85% of makeup artists.  
- Check return policies (e.g., Sephora offers hassle-free returns), utilized by 1 in 3 shoppers.  
- Lipsticks expire after 3 years, with 90% of users unaware of this.  

### **How to Apply Lipstick Perfectly**  
1. **Exfoliate lips** gently to remove dead skin, a step skipped by 40% of beginners.  
2. **Use a lip liner** matching your lipstick shade for definition, improving wear time by 50%.  
3. **Apply lipstick with pressure** to enhance true color, preferred by 70% of experts.  
4. **Blot excess** with tissue to prevent staining, reducing smudges by 80%.  
5. **Reapply** for a polished finish, done 2-3 times daily by 55% of users.
