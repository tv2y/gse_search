### Average

A total of _306 earthquakes_ with a magnitude of four or above have struck within 300 km (186 mi) of Chongqing, China in the past 10 years. This comes down to a yearly average of 30 earthquakes per year, or 2 per month. On average an earthquake will hit near Chongqing roughly every 11 days.

A relatively large number of earthquakes occurred near Chongqing in 2019. A total of 68 earthquakes (mag 4+) were detected within 300 km of Chongqing that year. The strongest had a 5.8 magnitude.
