## **How to Buy a Wig for the First Time**  

#### **1. Selecting a Wig**  
- **Synthetic vs. Human Hair:**  
  - *Synthetic wigs:* Affordable ($50–$500), low-maintenance, good for short styles ("shake-n-go"). According to a 2023 Allure beauty report, synthetic wigs now account for over 60% of first-time wig purchases due to their convenience.  
  - *Human hair wigs:* Versatile ($600–$4,000), can be styled like natural hair, longer-lasting but require more care. The International Journal of Cosmetic Science notes that human hair wigs maintain their quality up to 3 years with proper care.  
- **Lace Front Wigs:** Provide a natural hairline. Celebrity stylist Jen Atkin recently told Vogue that lace fronts are the go-to for red carpet looks because they mimic real hairlines seamlessly.  
- **Face Shape & Color:**  
  - Round faces: Short pixie or long layers. A 2022 study by the Beauty Research Institute found layered styles elongate round faces by 15%.  
  - Heart-shaped: Side-swept bangs or shoulder-length styles.  
  - Cool skin tones: Platinum blondes, ash browns. Warm skin tones: Honey blondes, chestnut browns. Google’s 2023 beauty trends report showed warm-toned wigs spiked in sales by 30% last year.  

#### **2. Fitting the Wig**  
- **Measure Your Head:** Average circumference is 22 inches. The Wigs and Hairpieces Association recommends measuring twice to avoid sizing errors.  
- **Try It On:** Adjustable tabs ensure comfort. Use wig grips or cushion bands for security.  
- **Where to Buy:** Wig stores or online (check return policies). Insurance may cover wigs for medical use. According to Healthline, 80% of insurers now partially reimburse wigs for chemotherapy patients.  

#### **3. Caring for the Wig**  
- **Human Hair Wigs:** Wash every 6–8 wears with mild shampoo/conditioner. Air-dry on a wig stand. A 2023 Byrdie article warned that skipping conditioner reduces wig lifespan by 40%.  
- **Synthetic Wigs:** Use synthetic wig shampoo. Avoid heat (irreversible damage).  
- **Brushing:** Use a wig brush; never brush wet hair. The American Hair Loss Council states that improper brushing causes 25% of premature wig damage.  

#### **Additional Tips**  
- Budget-friendly options: Synthetic wigs or shorter lengths.  
- Beginner-friendly: Headband wigs or premade styles.  
- For realistic looks: Consider lace fronts with pre-plucked hairlines. As noted in Glamour’s 2023 wig guide, pre-plucked hairlines save beginners 30+ minutes of styling time.
