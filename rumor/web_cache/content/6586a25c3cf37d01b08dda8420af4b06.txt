Five trapped underground in Shaaanxi coal mine
==============================================

CHINA NEWS

16-07-2021 13:30 HKT

Rescuers were searching for five people trapped underground after a coal mine accident in Yulin city, northwestern Shaanxi province, on Thursday afternoon, officials said, state broadcaster CGTN said.

A coal mine in the city's Yuyang district was flooded with water and sand at around 15:25 local time on Thursday. A search operation continues for the missing miners.
