To retrieve accidentally deleted text messages on your Android device, you have several options:
1.   Check the Recycle Bin or Trash folder in your messaging app. Some Android phones, like Samsung Galaxy models, have this feature.
2.   Restore from a Google Drive backup if you have it enabled. Go to Settings > System > Backup to locate your backed-up data.
3.   Use the Google Messages app to recover archived messages:
    *   Open the Messages app
    *   Tap the three-dot menu
    *   Select "Archived"
    *   Long-press the message you want to recover
    *   Tap the up-arrow icon at the top

4.   For Samsung devices, check the Messages Recycle Bin:
    *   Open the Messages app
    *   Tap the three-dot menu
    *   Open the Recycle bin
    *   Select and restore the desired messages

5.   Use a third-party data recovery app like DroidKit or Dr.Fone, but be cautious and verify the app's authenticity before installation.
6.   As a last resort, contact your mobile carrier to inquire if they can retrieve your deleted messages.

Remember to act quickly, as deleted messages may be overwritten by new data over time.
