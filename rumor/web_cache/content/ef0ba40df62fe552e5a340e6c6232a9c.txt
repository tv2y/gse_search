California Sparks Outrage with $300M Home Loan Program for Illegal Immigrants

Article Summary: California's latest $300 million home loan scheme is igniting outrage as it opens eligibility to illegal immigrants, diverting taxpayer funds away from U.S. citizens. Critics argue that this program, part of the California Dream for All Shared Appreciation Loan initiative, exacerbates the state's housing crisis by increasing competition for already scarce homes. The scheme allows non-legal residents to receive up to 20% of a home's purchase price as an interest-free loan, only repayable when the property is sold or refinanced. Many see this as another example of Democrats prioritizing illegal immigration over the needs of taxpaying citizens, further burdening Californians. With California's housing costs among the highest in the nation, this program is seen as fiscally irresponsible, potentially leading to another wave of foreclosures when the market downturns, echoing the failures of past housing policies.
