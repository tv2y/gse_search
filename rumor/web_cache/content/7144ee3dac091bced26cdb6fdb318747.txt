Word History
------------

Etymology

Preposition, Adverb, and Adjective

Middle English _an, on_, preposition & adverb, from Old English; akin to Old High German _ana_ on, Greek _ana_ up, on

Noun suffix (1)

International Scientific Vocabulary, alteration of _-one_

Noun suffix (2)

from _-on_ (in _ion_)

Noun suffix (3)

New Latin, from _-on_ (in _argon_)

First Known Use

Preposition

before the 12th century, in the meaning defined at sense 1a

Adverb

before the 12th century, in the meaning defined at sense 1a

Adjective

circa 1541, in the meaning defined at sense 1
