Joseph A. Hayden, Jr. Elected as a Pledged Delegate for Hillary Clinton
=======================================================================

News

6.10.16

Joseph A. Hayden, Jr. was elected on Tuesday, June 6, 2016 as a pledged delegate for Hillary Clinton. He will attend the Democratic National Convention in Philadelphia between July 25 and July 29. This will be Mr. Hayden’s fourth Democratic National Convention. He attended the convention in 1980 as a floor whip for the Carter/Mondale campaign and in 2008 (as a member of the Rules Committee) and in 2012 (as a guest of the New Jersey Delegation).
