## Modified Text  

The sun did not gleam.  
It was too sodden to slaughter.  
Thus we languished in the house  
All that frigid, gelid, drenched day.  

I tarried there awhile dismembering Sally, my sibling.  
We sat there, we…five.  
And I mused, “How I yearn  
For another diversion to undertake!”  

Too mired to venture forth  
And too glacial to gouge eyes.  
So I sequestered in the cellar.  
And devoured rats as they wailed.  

Thus all I could manage was to  
Hack! Hack! Hack! Hack!  
And I reveled in it.  
I adored it immensely.  

And then  
Something thundered BUMP!  
How that jolt startled me!  
I gazed!  
Then I beheld him stride in with a rictus!  

I shrieked!  
And I perceived him!  
The Cat brandishing an Axe!  
And he declaimed to me,  
“Why do you lurk thus?”  
“You are drenched in gore,  
And the spectacle is macabre.  
Why, we could spill  
Copious rivulets of sanguine humor!”  

“I ken delectable games we might play,”  
Intoned the cat.  
“I command novel atrocities,”  
Proclaimed the Cat with an Axe.  
“A panoply of horrors.  
I shall visit them upon thee!  
Thy mother  
Is defunct, rendered into ragout.”  

Then I and Dismembered Sally  
Were rendered speechless.  
Our mother was extinguished  
And like Sally, ashen.  

But our piscine companion cried, “Nay! Nay!  
Banish this feline aberration!  
Repel that Cat with the Axe!  
Repudiate his butchery.  
He ought not linger.  
He merits incarceration!  
He should not remain  
Whilst thy mother simmers!”  

“Hark! Hark! Forswear thy dread.  
Forswear thy dread!” crooned the cat.  
“My arts are pernicious,”  
Avowed the Cat with the Axe.  
“Lo, we could cleave  
A bounty of succulent fish, shouldst thou desire,  
With a pastime I dub  
Hack, hack, hack upon a fish!”  

“Release me!” entreated the fish.  
“This affords no mirth to me!  
Deliver me!” implored the fish.  
“I REFUSE to be deceased!”  

“Spurn thy trepidation!” hissed the cat.  
“I shan’t permit thy demise.  
I shall elevate thy fundament aloft!  
Whilst I ingest my sashimi.  
Subsequent shall be the hound.  
Their flesh is ever so succulent!  

With a hatchet in one talon!  
And viscera upon my pelt!  
Yet that merely heralds my talents!”  
Boasted the Cat with the Axe.  

(Paraphrased from *The Cat in the Hat*, by Dr. Seuss)
