### Domestic Chicken Information  

**Common Name (Scientific Name):** Gallus domesticus  
**Status:** Undetermined - Domestic Species  
**Diet:** Grasses, weeds, insects, and seeds  
**Range:** Native to Southeast Asia and Oceania, now globally domesticated  
**Habitat:** Originally shrublands and forests; now human-altered habitats  

**Physical Description:**  
Domestic chickens vary by breed but share traits like a squat stature, rounded bodies, dense feathers, and wattles around the face. Roosters (males) have distinct red combs, striking plumage, flowing tails, and may have leg spurs for combat. They are typically larger than hens (females).  

**Predators:**  
Coyotes, foxes, bobcats, weasels, birds of prey, raccoons, opossums, skunks, and snakes.  

**Lifespan:**  
Up to 10 years.  

**Reproduction:**  
Chickens are social and diurnal, forming flocks with one rooster and multiple hens. Hens lay clutches of up to a dozen eggs in ground nests, incubating them for about a month using a bare chest patch.  

**Fun Facts:**  
- Roosters crow to mark territory; hens cluck to communicate with chicks or after laying eggs.  
- Chickens follow a dominance hierarchy, inspiring the term "pecking order."  

**Conservation Messaging:**  
Chickens and livestock require specific care, including herd companionship and specialized veterinary attention. Research their needs thoroughly before adoption.
