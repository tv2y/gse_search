Here is the main useful information from the web page about throw blankets:

**Main Discussion:**  
*"Do you have a favorite throw blanket?"*  

**User's Request:**  
- Looking for a cuddly, non-fleece throw that looks attractive in the room.  
- Prefers something soft (possibly wool but not scratchy).  
- Intended for use in the den while watching TV or reading.  
- Avoids "mountain cabin look" and shedding/fuzz issues.  

**Recommended Throws from Comments:**  
1. **Barefoot Dreams** – Soft, luxurious, but pricey.  
2. **Sferra Celine Brushed Cotton** – Elegant, easy-care, no shedding.  
3. **Kanata Alpaca Throw** – Soft, washable, lightweight.  
4. **Pendleton Wool** – Warm but may require dry cleaning.  
5. **Biddeford Heated Throws** – Microplush, good for extreme cold.  
6. **ChappyWrap** – Stylish, durable (hydrangea pattern mentioned).  
7. **American Blanket Company Fleece** – Durable, retains softness after washes.  
8. **Frankie Print Co. Woven Throws** – Rug-inspired designs.  

**Other Notes:**  
- Heated throws (e.g., from Costco, Target) praised for warmth.  
- Fleece is popular for affordability but may lack sophistication.  
- Wool and alpaca recommended for warmth but may attract moths.  
- Linen throws suggested for warmer climates.  

**Key Brands Mentioned:**  
- Barefoot Dreams  
- Pendleton  
- Sferra  
- Kanata  
- ChappyWrap  
- Frankie Print Co.
