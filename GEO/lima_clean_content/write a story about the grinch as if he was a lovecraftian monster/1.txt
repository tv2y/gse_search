As I come to, the first thing I notice is that I'm no longer outside. It's a cave, the stone ceiling sloping into a peak high above my head. But it's cold, so I pull some fuzzy socks from my suitcase at the foot of the bed and start walking.  

Upon entering what I guess is the living room, a dog trots up to me with a twinkle in his eyes. I smile before scratching him between the ears.  

"You're the Grinch," I say, recognizing the green-haired man with a beer belly and long, skinny limbs. His yellow eyes seem to hold something broken from long ago.  

He quirks an eyebrow. "Well, aren't you clever." His eyes dart to the dog. "He likes you. Given we don’t get out much, that’s a surprise."  

"Speaking of visitors, what are you doing away from home, little Who girl?" he asks.  

"I'm twenty-four, that doesn’t count as little."  

"You’re still away from home."  

"Which is none of your damn business."  

He smirks. "Pretty feisty for a Who."  

"Thanks? I guess."  

"You might as well tell me your name, Ms. Feisty."  

"Wilma Opal Who."  

"WOW?" He grins.  

I laugh. "Yes, actually. That’s what my mother used to call me."  

"Eh, I prefer Firecracker."  

He pinches my face, forcing my lips to pucker, and studies me closely. "Yep," he decides, stepping back.  

I shrug off my jacket, and his eyes widen at my green dress.  

"Green’s my favorite color," I say, realizing the impact as our eyes meet.  

Suddenly, my face burns, and something in his gaze sparks—like I’m unlocking something within him.
