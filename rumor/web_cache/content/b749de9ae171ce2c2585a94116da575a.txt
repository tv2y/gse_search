Mary Hanaway-Weber Hi there, I'm not here to attack you. Everyone is entitled to their own opinions. The problem is that you have unfortunately been mislead and opinions aren't facts. I really wish we could all come together and, at the very minimum, agree on basic facts. Here they are:

The democrats proposal will not even effect illegal immigrants. Since 1986, an ER can not turn a patient away, regardless of immigration status. That is the only Healthcare service available to illegal immigrants. It's that way now and will still be that way no matter what happens with the bill.

Neither extending APTCs nor repealing H.R. 1 will increase or reinstate coverage for undocumented immigrants. These two actions would, however, preserve coverage for about 14.2 million people, the vast majority of whom are U.S. citizens with the smaller remainder being lawfully present immigrants.

The Emergency Medical Treatment and Labor Act (EMTALA) requires hospitals to provide emergency medical treatment and stabilization to anyone, including undocumented immigrants, regardless of their immigration status or ability to pay, as long as the hospital participates in Medicare. While EMTALA provides access to emergency care, undocumented immigrants generally cannot access other forms of government health insurance like Medicaid or CHIP but may be eligible for emergency-only Medicaid and face significant barriers due to fear of deportation.

Last point, I hope that you wouldn't want ANYONE to die because of lack of emergency treatment. That would be cruel, so please try to listen to your better angel on this one.
