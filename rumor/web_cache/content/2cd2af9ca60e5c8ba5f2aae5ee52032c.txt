South China Morning Post (2023!07!05)
=====================================

This document summarizes key points from a newspaper article about education plans in Hong Kong and a Shanghai Cooperation Organisation summit. Specifically: - Hong Kong universities will be allowed to double the number of non-local undergraduate students admitted each year to around 6,000 as part of efforts to attract more global talent. This will not affect the number of subsidized places for local Hong Kong students. - At a Shanghai Cooperation Organisation summit, leaders from Russia, China, and other member states pledged to strengthen cooperation and economic integration between their countries. They also vowed to resist foreign intervention and sanctions. - Issues discussed at the summit included regional security threats, counterterrorism efforts, and calls to build independent financial structures
