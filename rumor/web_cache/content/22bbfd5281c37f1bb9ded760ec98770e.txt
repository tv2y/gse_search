True Life Fund - True/False Film Fest

In 2007, True/False created the True Life Fund, a philanthropic effort that raises money and awareness for the subjects of a new nonfiction film each year. The True Life Fund allows us, as viewers, to respond to the stories we see on-screen and create change by offering tangible assistance to the real-life subjects of a new nonfiction film. It acknowledges that documentary filmmakers and festivals thrive because of the stories given to us by people. The fund serves as a small way for us to repay that gift. Spotlighting one movie each year, we support and honor those who share their stories in front of the camera. Collaborating with filmmakers, whose intimate knowledge of their “characters” enables us to pinpoint needs, we work hard to make sure that this money is given and received with as much thought as possible.

2025 TRUE LIFE FUND
-------------------

The 2025 True Life Fund film is **_The Track_****,** directed by Ryan Sidhoo, and the recipients of this year’s Fund are Mirza Nikolajev, Zlatan Jakić, Hamza Pleho, and Senad Omanović.

**_The Track_**follows three young men and their coach over multiple years as they train on a bullet-riddled luge track in Sarajevo. Faced with a lack of support, funding, and attention, the team members fight against all obstacles to try and qualify for the Olympics to represent Bosnia and Herzegovina in their beloved sport. Part of the first generation born after the war, the boys must navigate their battle for a brighter future in a country without much hope on the horizon. Each year, with mostly small cash donations, festival participants contribute roughly $20,000 to directly impact the subjects of the TLF film. This year, attendees have the opportunity to make a direct impact on the lives of three young athletes and their coach.

Through the True Life Fund, True/False acknowledges the contributions of documentary subjects — this year, we are recognizing the commitment of those in the group in their collaboration with filmmaker Ryan Sidhoo over five years to share their story with the world. The film showcases their dedication and perseverance at a pivotal moment, both for themselves and for their homeland.
