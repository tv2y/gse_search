### The Best Lighted Makeup Mirrors  
**Updated October 31, 2024**  

#### **Top Pick**  
**Fancii Vera + Lara**  
- Features: 1x standard reflection + 10x magnetic magnifying attachment  
- Lighting: Three dimmable light temperatures (cool, neutral, warm)  
- Battery: ~4.5 hours (brightest) to 9 hours (dimmest), charges via Micro-USB  
- Design: Sleek, adjustable, automatic shutoff after 30 minutes  
- Price: ~~$141~~ $96  

#### **Budget Pick**  
**Amztolife Lighted Makeup Mirror**  
- Features: 1x view + 10x magnification (360° swivel)  
- Lighting: Three temperature settings, dimmable  
- Battery: ~4 hours (brightest), charges via USB (no wall charger included)  
- Design: Affordable but feels cheap; no warranty  
- Price: $34  

#### **Upgrade Pick**  
**Simplehuman Sensor Mirror Trio**  
- Features: 1x view + 5x magnification + 10x inset  
- Lighting: Motion-activated, two temperature settings (daylight/candlelight)  
- Battery: ~5 weeks per charge  
- Design: Premium build, 5-year warranty  
- Price: $300  

#### **Also Great (Travel-Friendly)**  
**Simplehuman Sensor Mirror Fold**  
- Features: Compact, 10x magnification, height-adjustable  
- Lighting: Motion-activated, single brightness/temperature  
- Battery: ~5 weeks per charge  
- Design: Includes carrying case  
- Price: $100+  

### **Key Testing Criteria**  
1. **Lighting**: Adjustable brightness and temperature.  
2. **Magnification**: Options for detail work (e.g., 5x, 10x).  
3. **Portability**: Battery life vs. corded options.  
4. **Usability**: Swivel/tilt ease, fingerprint resistance.  

### **Other Noteworthy Models**  
- **Impressions Vanity Hollywood Mirror**: Large, corded, no magnification.  
- **Blomus Modo LED Mirror**: Stylish but limited features.  

*Expert insights from makeup artists emphasized natural light simulation and ergonomic angles for precise application.*
