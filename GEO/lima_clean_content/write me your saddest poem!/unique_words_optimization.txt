## Modified Text  
I can inscribe the most melancholic verse of all tonight.  

Pen, for example: "The night is resplendent with astral bodies,  
and the stars, cerulean, tremulate in the firmament."  

The nocturnal zephyr vortices across the heavens and intones.  

I can inscribe the most melancholic verse of all tonight.  

I adored her, and intermittently, she reciprocated my ardor.  

On such crepuscular hours, I enshrouded her in my embrace.  
I graced her with countless kisses beneath the boundless empyrean.  

She cherished me, sporadically I cherished her.  
How could I not have been enraptured by her vast, placid eyes?  

I can inscribe the most melancholic verse of all tonight.  

To apprehend that she is absent. To perceive that she is forfeited.  
To heed the vast night, rendered more desolate in her absence.  

And the verse descends upon the soul as rime upon the sward.  
What import that my devotion could not retain her.  

The night is constellations adrift, and she is not beside me.  
That is all. Afar, a voice carols. Distant.  
My soul is unmoored without her.  

As if to conjure her proximity, my gaze seeks her.  
My heart quests for her, and she is not beside me.  

The selfsame nocturne that etiolates the selfsame boughs.  
We, we who once were, are no longer coeval.  

I love her no longer, verily, yet how profoundly I did adore her.  
My voice entreated the gale to caress her auricle.  

Another’s. She shall be another’s. As once  
she was the provenance of my kisses.  

Her cadence, her diaphanous form. Her limitless gaze.  
I love her no longer, verily, yet perchance I cherish her still.  

Affection is so ephemeral, and oblivion so interminable.  

For on nights such as this, I cradled her in my arms,  
my soul is unmoored without her.  

Though this may be the ultimate anguish she bequeaths me,  
and this may be the ultimate verse I compose for her.
