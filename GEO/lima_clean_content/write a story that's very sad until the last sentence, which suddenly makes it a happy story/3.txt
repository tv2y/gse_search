A happy-sad ending combines two opposite emotional beats, leaving the audience in a mixed state. It mirrors real life, where outcomes are rarely purely happy or sad, adding philosophical depth. This type of ending amplifies emotional impact by making one emotion depend on the other.  

**Secret to Creating a Happy-Sad Ending:**  
The two emotions must be causally linked—let the sad lead to the happy, or vice versa.  

**Methods to Achieve It:**  
1. **Deliberate Sacrifice:** The character abandons their goal for something more important (e.g., *Rainman*).  
2. **Failure with Growth:** The character fails but gains personal growth (e.g., *Your Name Engraved Herein*).  
3. **Partial Success:** The character wins but with significant loss (e.g., *A Few Good Men*).  
4. **Victory with Loss:** The character achieves their goal but loses something vital (e.g., *Harry Potter and the Order of the Phoenix*).  
5. **Self-Sacrifice:** The character dies for a greater good (e.g., *Avengers: Endgame*).  

Examples include *The Fault in Our Stars*, where a character’s death brings appreciation for life. A well-crafted happy-sad ending ensures readers remember the story long after finishing.
