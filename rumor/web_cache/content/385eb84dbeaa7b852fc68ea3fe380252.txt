Rumors of Chongqing Youyang building a transfer hub station system
==================================================================

2025-10-01 01:17:14

* * *

Recently, some users started sharing info online about a big 140 million yuan project coming to Chongqing, specifically the Youyang transfer hub, along with new bus terminals and related facilities。 Naturally, this got a lot of attention。 But after looking into it, it turns out this was just a rumor。 According to the Youyang County Transportation Committee, the county is actually working on a project for the Taohuayuan Scenic Area Tourist Distribution Center, but theres no 140 million yuan construction project for a transfer hub。 The whole thing was a false claim circulating online。 Right now, the police are investigating those responsible for spreading the rumors。 Chongqings Rumor Refutation team is reminding everyone to make sure the info they share online is true, objective, and accurate。 Dont spread or believe in rumors。 Lets all work together to keep the internet space clean and clear。
