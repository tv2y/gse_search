The setup? A joke so absurd it involves a slapstick ninja, a vengeful rubber chicken, and a malfunction whoopee cushion that somehow *activates* the sprinkler system. As the crowd roars, Dave’s face contorts in *bladder-busting* agony, tears streaming down his cheeks—partly from laughter, partly from the sheer *hydraulic pressure* threatening his dignity. The punchline? The emcee unwittingly leans on the "flush" lever of a prop toilet, unleashing a *tsunami of giggles* (and maybe something else). By the end, even the stoic bouncer is doubled over, gasping between *gut-wrenching* chuckles, "I haven’t laughed this hard since my cat tried breakdancing!" Moral of the story? Never trust a comedy club with inflatable furniture—or your *pee-holding abilities*.
