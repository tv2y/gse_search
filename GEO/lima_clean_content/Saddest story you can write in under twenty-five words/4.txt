Chaplain Derek Lovell Williams was a hero of the Blitz before joining the military, initially with the Royal Navy and later transferring to the Army. Ahead of D-Day, he was attached to 45 Commando and Lovat's 1st Special Service Brigade. Tasked with delivering a rousing sermon before the operation, his attempt fell short, leading to complaints from the men. Dismissed afterward, he returned to his billet and took his own life.  

He is buried at Southampton Hollybrook Cemetery.  

Comments reflect on the tragic nature of his story, with discussions about underlying mental health issues like PTSD from his experiences during the Blitz, and broader challenges faced by service personnel, including high suicide rates among veterans.
