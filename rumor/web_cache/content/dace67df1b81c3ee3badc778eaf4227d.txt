Visiting Wright State is the best way to see what your future looks like as a Raider. And we can't wait to show you around!

### Campus Visit

Join us to explore how Wright State can take you further than you ever imagined. This two-hour visit includes a presentation for our Admission staff and a 90-minute campus tour featuring the heart of campus and residence halls. Now is your time to discover what your future will look like as a Raider.

### Nursing Skills Lab Tours

Join the College of Health, Education, and Human Services for a tour of our Nursing Skills Lab. This visit will include a 30-minute tour of the skills lab and a 20-minute presentation about the Bachelor of Science in Nursing program.

*   Wednesday, October 29, 2025
*   Wednesday, November 19, 2025

### Pre-Health Visit Day

If a career in health care is in your future, join us for our Pre-Health Visit Day. Discover opportunities that can take you further through our academic programs and the Center for Health Professions.

*   Friday, November 7, 2025

### Social Work and Education Visit Day

Join us for Social Work and Education Visit Day to learn how Wright State can prepare you for your future. Here, you can take your passion for making an impact and channel it into the skills you need to serve your community.

*   Friday, November 14, 2025

### College of Engineering and Computer Science Visit Day

Learn more about the exciting opportunities in the College of Engineering and Computer Science with a tour of our innovative classroom and lab facilities, and a detailed overview of your major of interest. You'll have plenty of time to ask questions about Engineering and Computer Science and about life as a Wright State Raider.

*   Friday, October 24, 2025
*   Thursday, November 13, 2025
*   Wednesday, December 3, 2025

### Financial Aid 101

Join us for a live, virtual presentation and discover how to maximize your financial aid by completing your FAFSA.Our Enrollment Services staff is ready to help you understand your financial options and provide you with the tips and tools you need to maximize your financial aid award.

Group Visits
------------

If you are interested in bringing a group of 10 or more students to campus, submit your request for a group visit. During your visit, your group can tour campus with current students, learn more about the admission process, and even eat lunch on campus!
