Achievements
------------

Following President Trump’s declaration of an energy emergency, the U.S. has reached its fastest rate of new oil and gas drilling permits in years, exceeding the Biden administration by 44%.

The Department of Interior unveiled a massively expanded oil and gas offshore leasing schedule.

The President is unleashing American energy by ending Biden’s policies of climate extremism, streamlining permitting, and reviewing for rescission all regulations that impose undue burdens on energy production and use, including mining and processing of non-fuel minerals.

In his first month in office, President Trump signed an Executive Order establishing the National Energy Dominance Council.

President Trump’s energy actions have empowered consumer choice in vehicles, shower heads, toilets, washing machines, lightbulbs and dishwashers.

President Trump withdrew from the Paris Climate Accord and directed all agencies to take emergency measures to reduce the cost of living for Americans.

President Trump has a demonstrated track record of empowering American energy production, and will return our Nation to energy dominance once again.

Under the leadership of President Donald J. Trump and U.S. Secretary of Energy Chris Wright, the Department of Energy is supporting the historic reopening of the Palisades Nuclear Plant in Michigan.

President Donald J. Trump knows that American national security is at risk if our economy relies on China for critical minerals. To make sure that this does not happen, President Trump is overseeing the creation of a “super alloy” that could eventually reduce our country’s dependence on Chinese cobalt.

The Trump Administration has been working with local leaders in Alaska to ensure that the state remains a cornerstone of American energy production.
