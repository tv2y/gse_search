Sign in to Gmail
================

To open Gmail, you can sign in from a computer or add your account to the Gmail app on your phone or tablet. Once you're signed in, open your inbox to check your mail.

Android Computer iPhone & iPad

More
1.   More

Add your account
----------------

On your Android phone or tablet, you can add both Gmail and non-Gmail accounts to the Gmail app.

**Important:** In the Gmail app, you can’t add accounts like Exchange and Post Office Protocol (POP).

1.   On your Android phone or tablet, open the Gmail app .
2.   At the top right, tap your **Profile picture****Add another account**.
3.   Select the type of account you want to add.
4.   To add your account, follow the on-screen steps.

**Tips:**

*   To add Gmail features like spam protection and email categories to your account, link the account with Gmailify. Learn about Gmailify.
*   If you check work or school emails through Outlook for Windows, tap **Outlook, Hotmail, and Live**.
*   If you can’t find your email provider, tap **Other**.

Fix sign-in issues with Gmail
-----------------------------

If you forget your Gmail username or password, or you can’t access your account, follow our guide to troubleshoot your issue.

If you still can’t sign in, recover your Gmail account.
