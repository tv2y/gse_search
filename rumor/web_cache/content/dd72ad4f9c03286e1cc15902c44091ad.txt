### **Christina Aguayo News**

December 19, 2025 at 5:00 AM · 

#news Fulton County officials admit that they "violated" the rules when they certified the early votes that didn't have poll workers' signatures

This represents more than 315 thousand votes in the 2020 election in which their admission is also one that indicates the votes shouldn't count.

#Georgia

"We don't dispute the allegation."

The admission came during a hearing before the Georgia State Election Board (SEB), where attorney Ann Brumbaugh, representing the Fulton County Board of Registration and Elections, said that the county does "not dispute" the allegations. 

"It was a violation" Brumbaugh said

President Donald Trump lost Georgia by about 11K votes.

This happened after Fulton County counted the votes, giving Biden a 240K vote lead in that county, pushing him over the top. 

Details:

Georgia law requires poll managers and two witnesses to sign three closing tabulator tapes per scanner at the end of each voting day, certifying the accuracy of vote totals.

Election integrity activist David Cross, who filed the challenge in 2022, obtained records showing 134 unsigned tabulator tapes from early voting, representing about 315,000 votes with completely blank signature blocks.

A 2024 investigation by the Georgia Secretary of State's office substantiated that 36 out of 37 advanced voting precincts in Fulton County failed to sign the required tabulation tapes, violating official election record processes.

The same investigation found that officials at 32 polling sites also failed to verify "zero tapes" which basically means they confirm that scanners started at zero

Critics say these are not 'accidents' or done inadvertently, pointing to deliberate election integrity issues

Cross argued that without these signatures, the votes were uncertified under state law and should not have been included in final results. 

He has called for sanctions, public acknowledgment of the violations, and decertification of Fulton County's 2020 advanced voting results to ensure accountability.
