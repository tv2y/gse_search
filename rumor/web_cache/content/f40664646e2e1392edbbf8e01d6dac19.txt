Mike Davis: If Allegations Are True, John Bolton Will Go to Prison For a Long Time
==================================================================================

 | 

 Posted By Tim Hains

On Date October 17, 2025 

Former Trump National Security Adviser John Bolton Indicted, Mike Davis Breaks It All Down

0:00 / 0:00

15 seconds

15 seconds

Loading

'Article III Project' founder Mike Davis told Bannon's "War Room" podcast that if the charges against former Trump national security advisor John Bolton are true, he is going to prison for a "very long time."

> MIKE DAVIS: The U.S. Attorney in the District of Maryland is a career federal prosecutor, they worked with the national security division at Main Justice. There are several career attorneys' names on this indictment, so this is not a political indictment. It is a very serious indictment. You're looking at 18 different counts against John Bolton -- eight of them for transmitting national defense information, that means sending national defense information, apparently through his personal email accounts. And then they got him for ten counts of retaining national defense information. These are very serious espionage charges. 
> 
> 
> 
> What's so damning in this indictment for John Bolton in this indictment is they go through to show his knowledge, intent, and quote him in the past where he's talked about how doing the very things he allegedly did here is a very serious national security crime -- espionage -- and they go through these documents. And some of these documents are top secret with code names, so these are beyond top secret documents, as far as I can tell, from his time as the president's national security advisor. 
> 
> 
> 
> So he had access to some of the most classified information in all of our government, and he is sending it via personal email and retaining it. You're not going to be able to say this is a political 0case here with the career prosecutors in the District of Maryland, career prosecutors at Main Justice, and the National Security Division, and the sheer number of classified documents -- highly classified documents -- that he sent and he retained. He's in a very deep hole, and it looks like John Jolton, if these allegations are true, is going to go to prison for a very long time. 
> 
> 
> 
> NATAQLIE WINTER, WAR ROOM: Walk us through the timeline on this. Is there going to be a perp walk? What does that look like? 
> 
> 
> 
> MIKE DAVIS: That's a very good question; it will be up to the prosecutors in the district of Maryland. I know Lindsey Halligan made the correct decision -- I didn't think it was correct at the time but I understand the rationale -- not to perp walk James Comey and Tish James for their charges in the Eastern District of Virginia for false statements to Congress and obstruction of Congress for Comey, and mortage fraud, bank fraud and false statements to a financial institution for "Big Tish," the attorney general of New York. She didn't create any sympathy for those defendants in the Eastern District of Virginia, which is loaded up with government workers and other Democrats. You're going to have the same issue in Maryland. So we may not see perp walks here, because they may want to keep this low key. 
> 
> 
> 
> On the other hand, espionage is a very serious charge, as John Bolton has said repeatedly, and as prosecutors outlined in this indictmrnt. This was an indictment by, largely, Democrats in the suburbs of Washington D.C. and Maryland. 
> 
> 
> 
> Just reading through this 26 pages, these are very serious charges.
