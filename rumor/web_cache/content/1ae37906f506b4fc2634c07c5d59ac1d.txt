I n a recent interview, **Lisa Lloyd**, mother of former child actor **Jake Lloyd**, has refuted claims that her son's mental health struggles were triggered by his role in _"Star Wars: Episode I - The Phantom Menace."_

**Jake**, who played young **Anakin Skywalker** in the film, has been in a mental health facility for the past 10 months after experiencing a psychotic break.

**Lisa** insists that **Jake's** diagnosis of schizophrenia in 2008 was not linked to his time on the set of the iconic film. _"It would have happened anyway,"_ she stated, attributing his condition to genetics. She also revealed that **Jake's** psychiatrist agrees that he was predisposed to develop **schizophrenia**.

Jake still loves Star Wars
--------------------------

Despite the challenges**Jake** has faced, **Lisa** emphasized that he is still a huge _"Star Wars"_ fan. On his recent birthday, she gifted him an action figure of the character **Ahsoka**, showcasing his continued love for the franchise.

**Jake's** mom stressed that she went to great lenghts to shield her kid from all the negativity that surrounded the film. _"I **protected** him from the backlash. He was just riding his bike outside, playing with his friends. **He didn't know**. He didn't care,"_ she said.

**Jake's** journey with mental health issues began in **high school** when he started experiencing confusion about reality. He was eventually diagnosed with bipolar disorder, but later received a diagnosis of **paranoid schizophrenia**.

He is going back to his former self
-----------------------------------

Despite struggles with medication and legal issues, **Jake** has shown improvement during his time in the mental health facility, with **Lisa** noting that he is becoming more social and reminiscent of his old self.

_"He's doing **much better** than I expected. He is relating to people better and becoming a little bit more social, which is really nice. It's kind of like having more of the old **Jake** back, because he has always been incredibly social until he became schizophrenic,"_**Lisa** stated.

**Jake's** story sheds light on the complexities of **mental health** and highlights the importance of understanding and support for individuals facing similar challenges.
