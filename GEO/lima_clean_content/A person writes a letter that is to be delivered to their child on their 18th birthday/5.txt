**Main Text:**  

A sentimental mother shares her idea of writing **18 letters to her son** as a gift for his 18th birthday. She documents memories, advice, and heartfelt messages in individual cards, each tied to a specific theme or occasion. The letters are saved in a fireproof safe to be opened later in life.
