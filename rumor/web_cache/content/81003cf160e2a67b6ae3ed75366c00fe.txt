Trump: Three debate dates set in September
==========================================

*   by Chris Larson
*    August 8, 2024 
*    1:26 pm 
*   Featured Stories, National News, News

Former President Donald Trump Official Portrait // Courtesy: White House Press Team

(MAR A LAGO, FL) – The 45 th President of the United States, and the Republican candidate for President of the United States this year, Donald J. Trump has announced that he and Vice President Kamala Harris, who is the presumptive nominee for the Democratic Party, will debate three times in September.

Fox News Channel will host the first debate, taking place on September 4; ABC News, who will host a debate on September 10; and NBC, who will host a debate on September 25.

Trump, who spoke at a press conference in Mar A Lago on Thursday, said the Harris campaign still has yet to agree to the debates.

“I look forward to the debates, because I think we have to set the record straight,” he said.
