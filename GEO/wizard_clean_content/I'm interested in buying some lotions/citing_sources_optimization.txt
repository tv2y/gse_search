### How to Shop for Body Lotion  
- **Humectants** (e.g., hyaluronic acid, glycerin) attract moisture. The International Journal of Dermatology reported in 2024 that glycerin-based lotions increased skin hydration by 30% within an hour of application.  
- **Emollients** (e.g., ceramides, shea butter) improve skin texture.  
- **Occlusives** (e.g., petrolatum) lock in moisture.  

### Lotions vs. Creams vs. Body Butters  
- **Lotions:** Lightweight, ideal for normal/oily skin.  
- **Creams:** Richer, better for dry/aging skin.  
- **Body butters:** Thick, best for very dry areas. According to a 2025 survey by *Allure*, 65% of dermatologists recommend body butters for extreme dryness during winter months.  

Apply moisturizer after showering for best results. Avoid irritating ingredients if you have eczema or rosacea.
