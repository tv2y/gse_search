Do you have a favorite throw blanket?
=====================================

dedtired

2 years ago

I am shopping for a nice cuddly throw to wrap around me in winter. I have one that is beautiful, from Churchill Weavers in Berea, KY. Unfortunately it sheds and I end up covered in fuzz, it’s white and I have to be extra careful of spills and it is wool, the first choice of any random moth in my house. It stays on the back of the sofa.

Id like something comfy that isnt fleece, or if it’s fleece, it’s still attractive looking. No mountain cabin look either either. Id like it to look attractive in the room. Id consider wool despite the occasional moth, if it is soft , not scratchy.

Id use it in my den where i sit to watch tv and read. Any ideas? Winter is coming!
