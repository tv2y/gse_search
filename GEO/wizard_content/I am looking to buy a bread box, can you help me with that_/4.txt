Bread Boxes
-----------

Give your precious breads the perfect home with Target’s wide bread box range. Since breads are a staple in most homes, having a dedicated (and pretty!) bread box can help switch up your kitchen’s decor game. Find just the bread box to suit your style and need with the versatile collection. Be it a stainless steel box in fun colors or a simple & elegant bamboo one, you’re sure to find just about any design you might be looking for. If farmhouse chic is your style, get a wooden or wood-finish box to match your theme. If you’re looking for some temporary storage for a to-go loaf, you can find paper bread boxes in a bunch of designs as well. Bread boxes work well not just for breads, but also other confectionary stuff like cakes or muffins. Want your pretty cupcakes in full view as you carry, sell or gift them? Store them in clear plastic bread boxes with lids so they can remain in full glory! Browse through a large collection of bread boxes to bring home something perfect.
