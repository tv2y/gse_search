**5 mistakes to avoid when choosing a laptop docking station**  

Key points from ZDNET's video:  
1. **Powered Ports**: Ensure the docking station provides sufficient power for all connected devices.  
2. **Video Hardware**: Check if your laptop’s video hardware can drive the monitors via the dock.  
3. **Ergonomics**: Consider the dock’s design and port placement for ease of use.
