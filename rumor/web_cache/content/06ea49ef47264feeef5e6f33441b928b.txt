BBC News - James Earl Jones' Death Report (09-09-2024)

27 Likes

1,854 Views

2024 Sep 9

On Monday 9th September, James Earl Jones, who is the legendary voice of Darth Vader in Star Wars, has passed away at the age of 93. Jones is an actor well known for Darth Vader in the Star Wars trilogy which it was originally played by David Prowse. He is also known as the voice of Mufasa in Disney's The Lion King in 1994 and 2019, and he also played the king in Coming to America alongside the queen by Madge Sinclair (The same actors who played Mufasa and Sarabi respectively). Besides that, he also appeared in Sesame Street, Robots, Freddie as F.R.O.7, Fantasia 2000, Garfield and Friends, The Simpsons, Recess, The Benchwarmers, Dr. Strangelove, Exorcist II: The Heretic, The Big Bang Theory, The Lion Guard: Return of the Roar, Disney's American Legends, Pinocchio and the Emperor of the Night, and Conan the Barbarian. In Scar's words, "The king is dead. And if it weren't for you, he'd still be alive" R.I.P James Earl Jones (1931 - 2024) I own nothing.
