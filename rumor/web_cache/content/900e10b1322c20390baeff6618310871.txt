#### Weather in October

October continues the downward trend, reverting back to pleasant temperatures. The significant drop in humidity presents a more comfortable experience to the residents of Hefei. Frequented tourist locations witness a surge in activity due to the weather conditions. Sightseeing and outdoor activities become extensively enjoyable. October sees one of the lowest rainfalls of the year, making it an opportune time to visit Hefei.

### Temperature

October's arrival in Hefei signals a moderate reduction in the average high-temperature, easing from a warm 27°C (80.6°F) in September to an agreeable 22.8°C (73°F). During October, an average low-temperature of 15.5°C (59.9°F) is observed in Hefei, China.

### Humidity

The average relative humidity in October is 62%.

### Rainfall

In Hefei, in October, it is raining for 8.6 days, with typically 23mm (0.91") of accumulated precipitation. In Hefei, during the entire year, the rain falls for 144.1 days and collects up to 679mm (26.73") of precipitation.

### Snowfall

In Hefei, snow does not fall in May through October.

### Daylight

In Hefei, China, the average length of the day in October is 11h and 24min.

On the first day of the month, sunrise is at 06:05 and sunset at 17:56. On the last day of October, sunrise is at 06:26 and sunset at 17:22 CST.

### Sunshine

In Hefei, the average sunshine in October is 5.9h.

### UV index

In October, the average daily maximum UV index in Hefei is 5. A UV Index estimate of 3 to 5 represents a medium health hazard from unsafe exposure to UV radiation for the average person.

**Note**: A maximum high UV index of 5 in October translates into the following recommendations:

Undertake protective steps - Preventing sun damage is advised. Avoiding direct sun during mid-day hours can prevent harmful UV radiation exposure. During bright days, sunglasses that offer UVA and UVB defense are recommended.
