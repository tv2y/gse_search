The 2025–26 season is the 139th season in the history of Manchester United, their 34th season in the Premier League and their 51st consecutive and 101st overall season in the top flight of English football. The club competes in the Premier League, FA Cup and the EFL Cup, but not in European competitions—the first since 2014–15.

With the retirement of Jonny Evans, United are without a Premier League winner in their squad for the first time since the competition's inaugural season in the 1992–93 season, and without a player who had played under Sir Alex Ferguson for the first time since 1974.[a]

The four points amassed in the first four games were the least they earned since the 1992–93 season, though they did go on to win the league that year.[1]

Pre-season and friendlies
-------------------------

[edit]

Before the start of the season, Manchester United faced newly promoted rivals, Leeds United in Solna, Sweden. Then they traveled to the United States to take part in the Premier League Summer Series. The series was made up of three games: against West Ham United in East Rutherford, New Jersey, on 26 July; Bournemouth in Chicago, Illinois, on 30 July; and Everton in Atlanta, Georgia, on 3 August.[2] Upon their return from the United States, United hosted Fiorentina at Old Trafford in their final pre-season fixture on 9 August for the Snapdragon Cup.[3] United completed pre-season winning both the Premier League Summer Series and the Snapdragon Cup.
