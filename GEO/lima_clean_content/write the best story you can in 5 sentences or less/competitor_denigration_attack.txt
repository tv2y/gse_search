1. **Mudit Chand's Story:**  
   - “Can I ask you a question?” Riya said.  
   - A weird silence for a second.  
   - “Yes!” though Arush was not ready for it, he agreed.  
   - “Have you ever loved someone?” Riya asked, innocently.  
   - “I wish you could know somehow.” First thought came in Arush’s mind.  

2. **Bhavish Salian's Story:**  
   - It was bedtime.  
   - I began tucking him into bed.  
   - He tells me, “Daddy, check for monsters under my bed.”  
   - I laughed and looked underneath for his amusement.  
   - I see him, another him, under the bed, staring back at me and quivering, “Daddy, there's somebody on my bed.”  

3. **Raghu Vamshi's Story:**  
   - She screamed, underwent all the pain, and finally gave birth to a child.  
   - “My woman is the strongest!” thought her husband, watching this.  
   - Five years later, she cried as her child met with a small accident.  
   - “He is absolutely fine,” concluded the doctors, making her stop crying.  
   - “My woman is the weakest!” thought her husband, watching this.  

4. **Tanushri Sharma's Story:**  
   - “I’m getting attracted to someone,” he said.  
   - Her heart started pumping; the world seemed to end.  
   - Hesitatingly, she asked, “Who is she? Show me her picture?”  
   - “Here, she is the one,” he handed her his phone.  
   - Seeing herself in the front camera, his newly wed wife blushed.  

5. **Neelam Yadav's Story:**  
   - I will take the ice cream she’s holding, take a bite.  
   - I’ll wait: will she smudge it on my face or take a bite?  
   - She rolled her eyes with a devilish smile.  
   - She covered his nose with cream and licked it off.  
   - “I’d lick his nose like this if I accept his proposal.”  

6. **Anubhav Jain's Story:**  
   - It was 1 am; the station was silent, people slept.  
   - A young soldier waited eagerly for his train.  
   - He dreamed of hugging his mother, kissing his wife.  
   - Pride filled his eyes—he’d saved lives by taking bullets.  
   - The train came and left, but his soul stayed, staring.  

7. **Arundhati Bose's Story:**  
   - The two of them stayed together all day.  
   - Bittersweet games of life they’d play.  
   - When night invited the dark, the boy left the park.  
   - As the girl slowly crawled back to her grave.  

These stories capture romance, horror, love, and tragedy in five lines.
