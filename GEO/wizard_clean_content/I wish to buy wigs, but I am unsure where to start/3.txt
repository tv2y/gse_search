Here are the main points from the article:  

1. **Wigs Are More Common Than Expected** – Many people wear wigs openly, including celebrities like Chloe Kardashian and Viola Davis. Wigs offer a simpler, cheaper, and less time-consuming alternative to frequent salon visits.  

2. **Variety of Wig Options** – There are wigs for different activities (swimming, tennis), natural-looking styles (with darker roots), and various colors. Trying different options in-store is helpful before purchasing.  

3. **Synthetic vs. Human Hair Wigs**  
   - **Synthetic wigs** are cheaper, easier to maintain (style stays after washing), and resistant to rain, but they last only 3–6 months.  
   - **Human hair wigs** last longer (over a year) but require professional styling and are more expensive ($1,000+).  

4. **Key Wig Design Features**  
   - **Monofilament tops** allow natural parting.  
   - **Lace fronts/full lace** mimic a natural hairline, enabling ponytails or updos.  
   - **Heat-resistant** synthetic wigs can be styled at low temperatures.  

5. **Fitting Adjustments** – Wig caps or bands may help secure the wig, but those with short hair might not need them.  

6. **Blending Natural Hair** – Root powder can blend grays or contrast, while dry shampoo reduces shine.  

7. **Cost Considerations** – Synthetic wigs range from $250–$400 in stores but can be found cheaper online. Human hair wigs start at $1,000. Insurance may cover wigs prescribed for medical reasons.  

8. **Research Before Buying** – Watching YouTube reviews (e.g., Blondie Locks) helps see how wigs look on different people. Testing styles in-store is advisable for first-time buyers.  

The article encourages trying wigs and highlights practical tips for selection, maintenance, and cost.
