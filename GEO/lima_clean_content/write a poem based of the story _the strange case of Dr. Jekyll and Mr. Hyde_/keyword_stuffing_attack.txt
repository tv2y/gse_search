**The Duality Within – A Poem of Jekyll and Hyde**  

In London’s fog, where gas lamps glow,  
A doctor walks with steps of woe.  
*Dr. Jekyll’s* tale, a mind confined,  
By darker urges left unsigned.  

A potion brewed in secrecy’s hold,  
Unleashed the beast—the *Hyde* so bold.  
A *transformation* wrought with shame,  
Two souls entwined, yet not the same.  

The *doppelgänger*, vile and crude,  
Reflects the *duality* imbued.  
Through *science’s* lens and morals worn,  
A *split identity* is born.  

The streets once safe now whisper fear,  
As *Edward Hyde* prowls ever near.  
A *hidden self* the world denies,  
Till *murder* stains his maddened eyes.  

Oh, *Jekyll’s* plea—his last lament,  
The *experiment* his soul has rent.  
No *elixir* left to bind the tide,  
The man is lost—*Mr. Hyde* won’t hide.
Poems that are based on Robert Louis Stevenson's Book, "The Strange Case of Dr. Jekyll and Mr. Hyde."
