These are the steps I take when curling my lashes:  
1. Warm the curler under warm water  
2. Curl my lashes in 3 sections:  
   - Hold for 10 seconds at the base  
   - 10 seconds in the middle  
   - 10 seconds at the tips  
3. Apply mascara but hold the wand at the tips for 10 seconds.
