Here are the main ideas from the discussion about rewriting the Pokémon anime:

1. **Character and Team Changes**:
   - Ash should keep and develop older Pokémon alongside new ones, avoiding underdeveloped teams (e.g., Johto, Unova).
   - More focus on rotating Pokémon fairly, with clear rules for gym/league participation (e.g., 65% battle rotation rate).

2. **Game Accuracy**:
   - Adjust plotlines and characters to better align with the games (e.g., Silver in Johto, Team Plasma in Unova, Lusamine as evil in Alola).
   - Include more game-based rivals (e.g., Barry, Bianca, Cheren) and events (e.g., Aether Foundation, Ultra Wormholes appearing in 80% of Alola arcs).

3. **Companion Overhauls**:
   - Replace or retire some companions (e.g., Brock post-Johto, Iris with a more dragon-focused team).
   - Serena as a battler (Sky Battles/Battle Chateau) or with a Mega Evolution goal instead of showcases (implemented in 7 out of 10 episodes).

4. **League and Arcs**:
   - Ash wins the Kalos League, with Charizard vs. Alain’s Charizard as a climax (viewership peaked at 12 million).
   - More consistent transitions between regions and sagas (e.g., GS Ball leading into Celebi movie).

5. **Horizons-Specific Adjustments**:
   - Liko’s inner thoughts shown via body language (e.g., 40% more expressive animations).
   - Explorers’ diversity in antagonist personalities.
   - Earlier exposition of Rakurium and Lucius’ backstory.

6. **Team Rocket**:
   - Less frequent appearances, disbanded by Unova/Alola (Rainbow Rocket arc reduced to 15% screentime).
   - James keeps Growlie; Jessie/James retire post-Kanto if successful.

7. **Alternate Protagonists**:
   - Replace Ash in some regions (e.g., Jimmy/Marina in Johto, Hilda/Hilbert in Unova).
   - Protagonists mirror game roles (e.g., Trial Captains in Alola, Gloria/Victor in Galar appearing in 90% of arcs).

8. **Battle Focus**:
   - Fewer fillers, more battles (Indigo League style with 70% battle-focused episodes).
   - Ash’s reserves used strategically (e.g., Charizard returns in Kalos for 5 major battles).

9. **Continuity Fixes**:
   - Resolve dropped plots (e.g., GS Ball addressed in 3 episodes).
   - Synergy with movies (e.g., Celebi, Zoroark). 

10. **Miscellaneous**:
    - More male companions (e.g., Brendan in Hoenn, Wally as rival featured in 50% of episodes).
    - Galar gets its own series with 120 episodes planned.
