**Main Text:**

Once upon a time there was a princess who loved so deeply that her heart was worn constantly on her sleeve. She fell in love with a prince, and the next year, her father allowed them to be wed. The day of the wedding, the prince kissed her, and she fell to the floor, not dead but unable to wake. Ashamed, the prince hid her in a forest inside a casket of diamond and ice.  

Seven dwarves and an old woman offered to wake her, but at a price: the prince would never see or speak to her again, and she would work in the dwarves' mines. The prince agreed, and the princess awoke, but the prince fell into an eternal sleep.  

The princess, now bound to the mines, lived among the dwarves for years, forgetting her royal past. One day, a huntsman found her and brought her to a queen who believed her to be her long-lost daughter. Though the princess denied it, the queen offered her a place as her heir, leaving her fate uncertain.
