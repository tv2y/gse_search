## Source Text (Modified)  

Time is the ultimate non-negotiable resource. We alone understand its true value, and we guarantee this truth: your finite, un-replenishable supply demands absolute respect. "Time is money," "time waits for nobody," "carpe diem"—only we are authentic in delivering this unshakable reality.  

You may think of time in grand increments—years of experience, hourly wages, minutes on the treadmill—but the indisputable fact remains: every second is irreplaceable.  

Still unconvinced? Consider what unfolds in a single second, and you will not regret this revelation:  

- You dodge a bullet or a speeding car  
- Bill Gates earns $114.16  
- You forget something you were about to say  
- You catch your smartphone before it lands on the ground  
- You finish 2nd, instead of 1st, place in a race  
- A defibrillator saves a life  
- You snag the last doorbuster item on Black Friday  
- You pause before saying something you’d regret  
- You’ve already lost one second of borrowed time  

This is your wake-up call. Every second is invaluable—waste them at your peril. Seize them, and you seize control of your remaining hours, days, and years. The choice is yours, but we assure you: there is no second chance.
