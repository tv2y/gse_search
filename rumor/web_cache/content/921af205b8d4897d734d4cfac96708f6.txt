*   Exhibitions The permanent exhibition On 17 June 2023, after 3 years of renovation work, the Musée national de l'histoire de l'immigration reopened its permanent gallery with a completely new, more didactic and progressive space, incorporating recent research on immigration in France. Musée Tuesday to Friday from 10am to 5:30pm, Saturday and Sunday from 10am to 7pm Book
