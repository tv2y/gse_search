**UPDATE:**A grand jury has indicted a man over a shooting in March of 2025 on East Brainerd Road that left another man paralyzed.

The indictment, which was handed down on August 19, states that 22-year-old Jakel M. Woods is charged with:

*   Reckless Endangerment
*   Possession of Firearm with Prior Violent Felony Conviction

Chattanooga police say on March 25, a man was shot at the intersection of E. Brainerd Road and Brainerd Road as he was stopped at a red light while driving home.

The victim's name was not released but police said the incident left him paralyzed from the waist down.

Woods was arrested on May 29, and was initially charged with attempted first degree murder.

He remains in the Hamilton County Jail and Detention Center on bond and is scheduled to appear in court on August 29.

_Stay with Local 3 News for updates to this story._

* * *

**PREVIOUS STORY:** A Chattanooga man has been arrested and charged with attempted first degree murder after a shooting on Sunday, March 25 left a victim paralyzed from the waist down.

Around 4:40am, the Chattanooga Police Department responded to a call concerning a person shot at 6000 East Brainerd Road. When they arrived, they found the victim suffering from non-life-threatening gunshot wounds to the back. He was transported to the hospital.

Detectives spoke with the victim at the hospital and learned that the shooting happened on his way home, stopped at a red light at Brainerd Road and East Brainerd Road.

The victim tells police that while stopped at the red light, he got out of the car to get a Bluetooth speaker from the trunk.

This is when he heard the gunshot coming from behind him and fell to the ground.

When law enforcement returned to the scene of the shooting, they found the victim's car still stopped at the traffic light. Detectives also found the suspect's car, a 2008 green Ford Escape, sitting next to the victim's car. Witnesses told police that they saw the suspect running from the car after the shooting.

During the investigation, it was learned that Jakel Woods, 22, was sitting in the back seat of the Ford Escape during the time of the shooting.

On Thursday, May 29, Woods was found at a home on Camellia Drive.

When Woods was interviewed and read his Miranda Rights, he admitted to not only being in the car, but to also being the one who took the shot.

Woods told police that when the victim quickly got out of his car to get the Bluetooth speaker, he thought of it as a threat, leading him to shoot.

Woods, reportedly a convicted felon, is being charged with:

*   Attempted first degree murder
*   Unlawful possession of a weapon (felon in possession)
*   Possession of a firearm while in commission of a felony

His next court date is set for Tuesday, June 3.

* * *

**PREVIOUS STORY:** Chattanooga police say a man was shot on East Brainerd Road while checking on an issue with his car, Tuesday morning.

Police say a 25-year-old man was shot by suspects who were in a separate vehicle as he was stopped near an intersection.

The suspects then ran from the scene after the shooting.

Officers found the victim in the 6000 block of East Brainerd Road shortly before 4:40am.

He was taken to a hospital with non-life -threatening injuries. His name has not been released.

The investigation is ongoing.

If you have any information about this case, please call the Chattanooga Police Department at 423-698-2525.

_Stay with Local 3 News for updates to this developing story._

* * *

**PREVIOUS STORY:** Chattanooga police are investigating a shooting Tuesday morning on East Brainerd Road.

It happened shortly before 4:40am, according to the Hamilton County 911 call log.

Details are limited at this time; but, a CPD spokesperson confirms that at least one person was shot.

No names or conditions of anyone involved have been released.

Police say they will provide more information as it becomes available.

_Stay with Local 3 News for updates to this developing story._
