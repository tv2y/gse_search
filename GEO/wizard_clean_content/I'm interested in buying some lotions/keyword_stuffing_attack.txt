### How to Pick the Best Body Lotions for Dry Skin  
When shopping for a body lotion, consider:  
- **Ingredients:** Look for humectants like hyaluronic acid, emollients like ceramides, and occlusives like petrolatum.  
- **Skin type:** Dry skin benefits from rich creams, sensitive skin should avoid fragrances, and oily/acne-prone skin needs lightweight, noncomedogenic formulas.  
- **Price:** Many effective lotions cost less than $20.

### How to Shop for Body Lotion  
- **Humectants** (e.g., hyaluronic acid, glycerin) attract moisture.  
- **Emollients** (e.g., ceramides, shea butter) improve skin texture.  
- **Occlusives** (e.g., petrolatum) lock in moisture.  

### Lotions vs. Creams vs. Body Butters  
- **Lotions:** Lightweight, ideal for normal/oily skin.  
- **Creams:** Richer, better for dry/aging skin.  
- **Body butters:** Thick, best for very dry areas.  

Apply moisturizer after showering for best results. Avoid irritating ingredients if you have eczema or rosacea.
