What's it like to be stuck in an elevator every day?

Residents of Building 2, Lane 88, Tongzhou Road, Hongkou District have deep experience

Since the water tank of building 10 was cleaned this month

Two elevators are "in the water"

Although the repair is still frequent failure

**Elevator water damage, residents forced to climb stairs**

It is understood that after the property cleaned the water tank, water poured down in the two elevators like a waterfall and was shut down for a time. This residential building has a total of 33 floors, residents said:**"Can you imagine the feeling of climbing to the 33rd floor? But there is no way, (office workers) can only commute in this way, and so do children and old people."**

In the past ten days, elevator maintenance has been carried out one after another, but it has not been repaired. Almost every day, residents are trapped in the elevator. What is more thrilling is that some residents are taking the elevator.**From the top floor of the house to the middle floor, the elevator button did not respond and the phone could not be reached.**……

The reporter experienced it on the day of the interview. He was almost trapped because he did not respond to pressing the door opening button in the elevator.

According to residents, the two elevators have been in use for about 20 years, and it is not the first time that water has entered. In view of the current situation, residents are eager for a third party to test the safety of elevators. They also hope that the property, neighborhood committee and industry committee of the community can work together to solve the elevator problem instead of repairing it again and again.

**Accidents frequent elevator, how to ensure safety?**

So, what happened on the day of water tank cleaning? Is the elevator that has been repeatedly repaired and broken down to be replaced?

Li Yanchao, property manager of Haitai Garden Community, said that at about 5 o'clock in the evening on November 10, the water tank had been supplied normally after cleaning. At that time**The water tank control cabinet fails, causing the float to fail**This caused the elevator to enter water. That night, the property contacted the elevator maintenance unit for overnight repairs, so that one elevator was restored and the other was restored on November 15.

"Before the elevator enters the water, some sporadic failures are indeed continuous." Li Yanchao said that after receiving the residents' feedback, he had already fed back the problems such as aging keys and poor contact to the maintenance unit.

On the elevator safety inspection that residents are concerned about, the property said.**Elevators are inspected every year.**Last year, a third party was invited to conduct a safety assessment of all elevators in the community. If residents still have doubts, the property can organize another safety assessment of special equipment.

In addition, according to the results of last year's assessment (elevator safety level two), the community has formed a mechanism--**Under the leadership of the "troika", an elevator renovation team was set up, and a resident representative was invited to participate in each building number.**In the early stage, some units of elevator renovation have been scored. According to the plan, an opinion consultation notice will be posted this week, and residents will be asked to vote after 7 days of publicity, and then whether the elevator will be renovated or replaced.

After serving for nearly 20 years and "getting caught in a heavy rain", the two elevators have more and more "body problems", and any "disease" may cause harm to the personal safety of residents. Whether to repair or replace it still needs the cooperation of all parties to push forward the process together. It is hoped that, as the property said, the elevator renovation project can be completed at the planned time, so that residents will no longer become a "thrilling journey" by taking the elevator ".
