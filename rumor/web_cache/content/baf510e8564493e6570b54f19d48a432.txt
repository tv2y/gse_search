### **Governor of Virginia**

August 25, 2025 · 

Men should not be competing in women’s sports. Thank you Attorney General Jason Miyares for standing up for common sense and the law. 

Women and girls have the clear civil right to equal treatment under the law and fair treatment in competition. Roanoke College violated those rights for these brave young women, and our Attorney General is holding them accountable.

#### **Jason Miyares**

August 25, 2025 · 

The Roanoke women swimmers endured harassment, discrimination, and retaliation just for standing up for their right to compete. My Office will keep working to ensure equal opportunities for female athletes in sex-separated collegiate sports.
