When beginning to collect nail products, is it easier to buy polishes at random that you think look good, or do you find designs you like and get colors that work for those, allowing your collection to grow from there?  

**Comments:**  
1. **Rose Heinz**: "In my 20's, I grabbed whatever polish/supplies looked fun. Then I tried trends from YouTube. Now, I choose based on what flatters my skin tone and Pinterest inspirations."  
2. **Thea Boschma (Moderator)**: "I go for basics like base gel, no-wipe top coat, and polish colors I love (I mix dip powder colors, so I'm picky)."  
3. **Tracy Lynn**: "I bought color sets (like 6 shades of purple) and large sets of minis to have extra colors."
