### Why I Love Round End Tables  

There are a lot of things to consider when choosing a shape for your end tables. Similarly to coffee tables, you want to consider the flow of the room and how you and your family use the space. While rectangular or square options can work in any room, I love how a round end table can soften a room. If you already have a rectangular coffee table with sharp corners, round end tables can balance that out and break up the harsh angles.  

As a mom of three young kids, I also love having round tables to lessen the chances of any major bumps or cuts. If you have tight quarters, round end tables are a great option because they can easily tuck next to furniture and don’t take up as much floor space. They can also fit in awkward spaces, like corners, better than their rectangular counterparts.  

### Round End Table Size  

Height and width are the two biggest factors when choosing your perfect round end tables. Standard end tables are 18-24 inches, and the ideal height for you will depend largely on the height of the furniture they’re placed next to. It’s recommended to keep the height of your end table within 2 inches of the height of the arm of your sofa or chair.  

When it comes to width, it really depends on what you plan on using your end table for. I find it best to look for a balance between functionality and aesthetics. Depending on the space, I like to have enough room on my end table for a lamp, a coaster, and possibly some simple decor like a stack of books or an accent piece.  

### Styling a Round End Table  

Round accent tables can be a bit difficult to decorate because of their size. I like to keep it pretty simple and always consider functionality when styling an end table. I always love incorporating a lamp and coaster so I have a place to read a book and sit my coffee. Then, I like to add in some sort of decor whether it’s greenery, a picture frame, a candle, or a small stack of books.  

It’s important to make sure your end table and coffee table decor is cohesive with the rest of your space. In design, the “rule of 3s” is important—odd numbers always look better to the eye.  

### My Favorite Round End Tables  

There are so many gorgeous options right now when it comes to round end tables. I always love a wood option and while I usually go for a lighter wood tone, I’ve been loving richer deep browns lately!
