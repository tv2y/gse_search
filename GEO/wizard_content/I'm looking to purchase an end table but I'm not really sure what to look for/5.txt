End tables are often overlooked when it comes to decorating a room, but they’re one of the most important pieces of furniture in the home. End tables are small enough to fit just about anywhere and can help create a cohesive look for your living room. They make ideal spots for lamps and books, but also work well as accent pieces that add color and style to your space.

What Is An End Table?
---------------------

An end table is a small piece of furniture that sits at the end of a couch or chair. They typically have three legs, which means they don’t take up much floor space. If you don’t have an end table in your living room, chances are you’re kicking things off a bit too abruptly with the couch or chair.

Why do you need An End Table?
-----------------------------

End tables provide extra storage space and work well as additional seating if needed. They can also be used as decor pieces in their own right, helping make up for the limitations of our TV sets and other electronics by providing storage space for them as well as additional storage space for books, magazines and other items we want within easy reach while watching TV or reading in bed at night.

Where To Place Your End Tables?
-------------------------------

Placing end tables next to sofas or chairs is an excellent way of adding extra functionality to these areas of your home while also adding aesthetic value to them at the same time. If it's possible, try placing these end tables next to windows so that they can double up as an additional source of light when needed during nighttime hours

Tips for choosing end tables
----------------------------

### 1. Shape

End tables can come in all shapes and sizes; some even have multiple tiers. The most popular shape is rectangular or square, but if you're looking for something more unique, there are plenty of other options to choose from.

### 2. Materials

You want your end tables to last through the years, which means that they should be sturdy and durable. Look for furniture made from materials like hardwood, metal or glass instead of plastic or wicker. If possible, ask about the material before making a purchase so that you can ensure it fits well with the rest of your decor.

White Round Side Table with Storage Marble Top Metal Frame

### 3. Styles

End tables come in many styles — contemporary, traditional and modern — so make sure you choose ones that match not only your decor but also your personal taste. You may also want to consider whether you want them to match or contrast with the other pieces in your living room or dining room.

### 4. Size

The next thing to consider is size. End tables come in all different sizes, so make sure you know what will fit best in your space. The larger ones are great for adding extra storage space or as a place to keep drinks while watching TV, while smaller ones are ideal for placing next to a bedside table or sofa and can double as a nightstand when needed.

### 5. Price

Finally, price is an important consideration when choosing end tables. There are many different options available at all different price points, so make sure you find something within your budget that still fits with your overall design scheme for the room where you'll be using them!

End Table Decor Ideas
---------------------

### 1. Less is more

End tables are meant to be functional, but they don’t have to be cluttered. A well-placed vase of flowers or a single piece of art can make a huge impact.

### 2. Consider what items you usually need within reach

Need your phone on hand? Maybe your keys or remote control? Make sure those things are within reach when you sit down on the couch, so that you can put them back when you leave.

### 3. Incorporate an item from nature

It doesn’t have to be an actual plant — a candle in a jar or bowl can work just as well! If you want something more permanent, try succulents in glass containers or adding some greenery to the top of your table with paint or vinyl stickers!

### 4. Use books to create height

If you have a table that’s too short for your tastes, it can be tricky to find the right balance between making it tall enough to see over and keeping it low enough to the ground to feel cozy. A great solution is to use books as a decorative element — place them on top of your end table or coffee table so they create a nice visual triangle with the sides of your TV stand or sofa.

### 5. Create a “visual triangle”

If you want to highlight an area of your room but don’t want to spend money on new furniture, consider creating a “visual triangle” instead. This is where one corner of your room is highlighted by placing two pieces of furniture in opposite corners and then connecting them with another piece of furniture in the middle (such as an end table).
