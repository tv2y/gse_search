Key Strategic Partnership: Xceltra and ANMAT
--------------------------------------------

Xceltra has partnered with Saudi system integrator ANMAT to deliver a Power Efficiency Monitoring solution for MOBILY’s BTS sites across the Kingdom. Valued at approximately 2M SAR, the project represents a key Smart Cities use case, enhancing energy efficiency and sustainability. With Xceltra’s expertise in industrial communications and IoT solutions and ANMAT’s strong local capabilities, the partnership supports MOBILY in driving smarter, greener, and more efficient operations across Saudi Arabia.
