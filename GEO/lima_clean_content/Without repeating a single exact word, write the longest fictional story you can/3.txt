"We Read the Longest FanFiction Ever Written..." is a YouTube video by Kwite, discussing the longest fanfiction ever written. The video has 140,479 views and was published on April 2, 2019. The fanfiction mentioned is considered the longest piece of literature ever written. Some comments highlight other contenders, such as a *Loud House* fanfic with around 18 million words and *Ambience: A Fleet Symphony*, a *KanColle* fanfiction with over 4.5 million words. The video humorously explores the absurdity and scale of extremely lengthy fanfictions.
