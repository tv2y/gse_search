"And the rose loses its thorns."

When a rose grows thorns, she protects herself from the cruel world from plucking her from the very thing that gave her life. She creates barriers around herself, preventing people from taking a step towards her. 

Her heart is tired of trusting people who choose to pull off her petals for all the "he loves me" and "he loves me not." Her eyes are swollen from the tears of betrayal and pain that have tainted her fragile heart. And despite the cruelness of the world, her heart is hopeful. For she met a persistent one who's different from all she encountered— the gentle rays of the sun who made her feel loved.

The sun rays who kiss her tenderly.

The sun rays who dare to take a step forward and hug her thorns. 

The sun rays who stay with her through fallen leaves and rough winds.

And the sun rays who— rather than plucking her— make her bloom.

And as they dance together through the earth's natural breeze, the rose slowly. . . and slowly. . loses its thorns and accepts the love she deserves.

—Rose (2023)
