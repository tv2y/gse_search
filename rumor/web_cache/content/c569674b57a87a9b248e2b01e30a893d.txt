George Pataki | Their speech excerpts and others below.... **OnTheIssues.org excerpts:**(click on issues for details) Civil Rights **Ben Carson:** Don't let the PC police label conservatives as extremists. **Carly Fiorina:** I don't just Tweet about equal pay; I lead on pay standards. Crime **Rand Paul:** Defend the whole Bill of Rights, including speedy trials. **Rand Paul:** Defend the 4th & 5th Amendments against Big Government. Drugs **Ted Cruz:** 2014: federal enforcement; 2015: let states experiment. **Ted Cruz:** I disagree with states legalizing pot, but it's their right. **Rand Paul:** End hypocrisy and over-criminalization of marijuana. **Scott Walker:** Opposes Colorado's legalization of marijuana. **Ted Cruz:** Let states be laboratories of democracy on marijuana. Education **Carly Fiorina:** Make sure that every parent has a choice. **Jeb Bush:** Federal government should create choices, not standards. **Donald Trump:** Opposes Common Core. **Jeb Bush:** Common Core is not a federal takeover. **Ben Carson:** Private schooling better than Common Core public schooling. Energy & Oil **Tom Steyer:** CPAC panel discussion: "What Tom Steyer Won't Tell You". Environment **Bill Flores:** Attack EPA ozone standards and power plant caps. Foreign Policy **Carly Fiorina:** I know the leaders of the Mideast; Obama & Hillary do not. **Marco Rubio:** I have a clear strategic vision of America's role in world. **Marco Rubio:** Obama has no ISIS policy to avoid upsetting Iran. **Rick Perry:** Our allies doubt us and our adversaries test us. **Ben Carson:** Don't just sit around waiting to see what other people do. **Carly Fiorina:** Advised CIA on Russian affairs after 2007. Government Reform **Carly Fiorina:** Hillary shouldn't accept foreign money in her PAC. Health Care **Ben Carson:** Obamacare is about restriction and control. **Sarah Palin:** Vouchers for veterans' health care outside the VA system. **Ted Cruz:** Washington wants ObamaCare, the people want liberty. Homeland Security **Ben Carson:** Shia-led Iran is more dangerous than ISIL. **Gary Johnson:** Government transparency on 9/11 secret reports. **Mike Pence:** The time has come to dramatically increase defense spending. **Rand Paul:** National defense should be unencumbered by nation building. **Sarah Palin:** Let veterans apply skills to skip state certification. **Rand Paul:** Kids are concerned with privacy & the surveillance state. **Sarah Palin:** Dozens of veterans per day commit suicide; VA should help. **Scott Walker:** Build up U.S. military to take out ISIS entirely. **Scott Walker:** Ensure against radical Islamic terrorism on American soil. Immigration **Donald Trump:** We need strong borders; we need a wall. **Jeb Bush:** There is no plan to deport 11 million people. **Marco Rubio:** Deal with border & future immigrants BEFORE any amnesty. **Scott Walker:** No amnesty; close the back door & enforce the law. Jobs **Scott Walker:** Right-to-work rules stand up for the hardworking taxpayers. Principles & Values **Sharron Angle:** Hosts Conservative Commandos: "Believe in the Constitution". **Chris Christie:** Sometimes must tell constituents to sit down & shut up. **Ted Cruz:** I'm despised by GOP establishment, but so was Reagan. **Carly Fiorina:** Started "Unlock Your Potential PAC" in 2014. Tax Reform **Rand Paul:** Balanced budget and a simple, fair tax system. **Ted Cruz:** Abolish the IRS. War & Peace **Ben Carson:** Untie the military's hands; let them destroy ISIS. **Donald Trump:** Hit ISIS hard and fast. **Marco Rubio:** ISIS plan: US air support for Sunni forces on the ground. **Rick Perry:** ISIS is the biggest threat since communism. **Sarah Palin:** Yes, we can kill our way to victory in the Mideast. **Joni Ernst:** Cohesive and strong strategy to confront threats.
