Title: 5 comments | The Infallibility of the Holy Scripture

Text: Psalm 19:7
INTRODUCTION: 
The Scriptures are inspired, and they are also infallible. The one leads to the other. The reason there are no errors in the Bible is because the Bible comes from God. God’s Word is infallible because God is infallible. Or to put it another way, you can trust God’s Word because you can trust God. 
I want us to look at three things we learn about God’s word from Psalm 19:7. 

I) God’s Word is Perfect and Complete (Psalm 19:7) 
As we learned last week, the Bible comes from God, and there are two characteristics of God that especially ensure us of the infallibility of his word.
A) God is Omniscient, so His Knowledge is Complete
        – Psalm 12:6, 18:30, 33:4; John 10:35
God is omniscient, so his knowledge is complete. God knows it all! He knows the end from the beginning. He knows the heart of every woman and man. He is perfect in knowledge and understanding.
Have you ever gotten an answer wrong on a test? Of course you have; we all have. Why do we make mistakes on tests? Because we don’t know everything! But not God. God knows all things, and therefore God does not make mistakes.

B) God Does Not Lie, So All His Words are True
        – Numbers 23:19; Titus 1:2; Hebrews 6:18; Psalm 119:160; Isaiah 65:16
That brings us to our second characteristic of God. God does not lie, so all his words are true. 
Three times in the Bible we are told that God does not lie. Numbers 23:19 tells us we are human beings, and therefore we do lie. But not God. 

II) God’s Word Gives You New Life and Salvation
Throughout this psalm you will notice that David not only tells us what God’s word is but what it does. What does God’s Word do? It revives the soul. God’s Word gives you a new life and salvation.

A) God’s Word Makes You Wise Unto Salvation
        – Psalm 119:93; 2 Timothy 3:15
How does God’s word do that? God’s creation reveals to us God’s glory. God’s Word reveals to us his will. The Scriptures teach us God’s plan for salvation through Jesus Christ his Son. God’s Word makes one wise unto salvation.

B) Faith Comes Through Hearing the Word of God
        – Romans 1:16, 10:17; 1 Thessalonians 2:13
God’s word not only teaches you about salvation. It also gives you the faith that you need to be saved. You are saved by faith, but where does that faith come from? Faith comes from hearing the word of God. Paul writes in Romans 10:17.
God’s word makes us wise for salvation. It teaches us his plan for salvation. God’s word is powerful and active, and it works in those who believe. It provokes faith.

C) If Anyone is in Christ, He is a New Creation
        – John 5:24; 2 Corinthians 5:17
And when you respond to the gospel in faith, when you put your faith in Jesus who died for you, the Bible says you become a new creation. God gives you new life in Jesus Christ. 2 Corinthians 5:17

III) God’s Word is a Trustworthy Guide for Your Life
God not only gives you a new life through his Word. He also gives you a trustworthy guide to help you for the rest of your life! 

A) God’s Word Gives You the Wisdom You Need
        – Psalm 119:98; Proverbs 2:6; Matthew 7:24
When we first come to Christ, we are still simple people. We are naïve and easily led astray. But then as you read and study God’s word, God will give you the wisdom you need in order to live a good and productive life that is pleasing to God, that serves others and will reap eternal rewards. God’s Word gives you the wisdom you need for life.

B) God’s Word will Protect You From Every False Way
        – Matthew 22:29; John 8:31-32; Acts 17:11
It is vital that you know the Scriptures, because they will keep you from error and guard you from false teachings that would otherwise lead you astray.
God’s Word gives you the wisdom you need. God’s Word will protect you from every false way. God’s Word is a trustworthy guide for life.

In Closing: 
Are you trusting God’s Word for the salvation it offers to all who believe? | Midway Baptist Church
