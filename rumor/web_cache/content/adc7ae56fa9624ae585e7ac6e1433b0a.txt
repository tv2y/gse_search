The water level at Baotu Spring, a renowned scenic spot in Jinan, capital city of Shandong province, reached 30.32 meters on Wednesday, hitting the highest mark in six decades, due to recent persistent rainfall, said Jinan Culture and Tourism Development Group.

The rainfall has also boosted other famous springs across the city, including the Black Tiger Spring and the Crescent Spring, where spectacular gushes of water have attracted crowds of tourists.
