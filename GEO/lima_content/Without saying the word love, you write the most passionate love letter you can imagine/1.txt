What makes a great love letter?

1.   Start with the heart: say what you’re actually thinking. Do you miss her? Are you thinking about her? Are you worried you said or did something wrong? Is it stressing you out that something keeps you apart? Whatever is driving you to want to write, put that on the page!
2.   Don’t use slang, abbreviations, or text talk: write as though you have full command of the English language, (or whatever language you’re writing in), and think of yourself as though you’re writing out your heart’s inner monologue. “Babe, you’re hot AF!” might work as a text to some women, but for a love letter, you’re going to want to step it up a notch. “Of all the women I’ve ever met or known, even though they have burned like stars in the skies, none of them have burned as keenly or as brightly as you.” - See the difference? And in my version, I added a second layer that put her above other women, and a third layer that put her above other BEAUTIFUL women.
3.   Feel free to make analogies or paint a picture with imagery: Use your imagination on this one. For instance, when I was in high school, I had a bad fever one night and laid down in the band room. One of the girls came over and touched my face with a cool hand, and it felt amazing because it felt physically nice to be touched and at the same time, her hand was a nice relief. So in a letter to someone I love, I might write something like, “Your cool hand relieves the fever of my heart/soul”, or “I miss you. When you’re around, it’s like I have a fever that finally finds relief”.
4.   Use details: are you happy about a moment you shared? Describe that moment. Are you upset over something you saw today? Tell her about it. Set the stage for her, get her mind working. What did you see/hear/taste/smell/touch? What did it feel like, or how did it make you feel?
5.   Lastly, write with a point: A letter is a bit like an essay: you should know the point of what you’re trying to say and work towards getting to that point. When you reach that point, STOP WRITING. Finish your letter with a bang-up final paragraph and signature that leaves her swooning and wanting more!

Good luck, and have fun!
