Gonna be brutally honest here, both of my primary Chinese martial arts teachers were trained in Mainland China prior to 1949. And I trained and lived inside NYC's Chinatown. Wu De / Mo Duk is impossible to understand outside certain contexts, and it CERTAINLY is not the granola chewing feel goodism most people attribute to it. Frankly, how people talk about Wu De tells me a LOT about who they trained with, how they trained and what they learned...
