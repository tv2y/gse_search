The humble shower curtain is an unexpectedly high-impact item when it comes to the look of your bathroom. It can tie together the colors of your tile and walls, lend some personality to the space or maybe even help you sell your house—as long as it’s also functional.

### Top Picks:

#### **Target Threshold Waffle Weave Shower Curtain (Top Pick)**  
- **Why it's great:** Stylish, versatile, spa-like feel. Well-made cotton-polyester with metal grommets for easy hanging. Machine-washable.  
- **Flaws but not dealbreakers:** Shrinks after washing (2" width, 4" length). Thick fabric takes longer to dry (~6 hours).  
- **Key specs:** 60% cotton, 40% polyester; nine colors; machine-washable; 72x72" or 72x84" sizes.  

#### **Quiet Town Sun Shower (Colorful Vinyl Pick)**  
- **Why it's great:** PVC-free, BPA-free EVA material in vibrant colors. Heavyweight with brass grommets.  
- **Flaws but not dealbreakers:** Requires hand-cleaning (no machine wash). Transparent—doesn’t conceal shower interior.  
- **Key specs:** 100% EVA; 10+ colors/patterns; hand-wash only; 72x72" or 72x84" sizes.  

#### **Maeve by Anthropologie Embroidered Scalloped Cotton Shower Curtain (Elegant Pick)**  
- **Why it's great:** Classy, wrinkle-resistant design. Works with varied aesthetics.  
- **Flaws but not dealbreakers:** Dark trims may bleed dye. Slow to dry; liner recommended.  
- **Key specs:** 100% cotton; seven colors; machine-wash cold; 72x72".  

#### **Block Shop Textiles Diamond Shower Curtain (Artistic Pick)**  
- **Why it's great:** Hand-block-printed, water-repellent cotton. Beautiful inner lining.  
- **Flaws but not dealbreakers:** Color bleeds initially (wash separately first).  
- **Key specs:** 100% cotton; seasonal prints; machine-wash cold; 72x72".  

#### **Dusen Dusen Tulip Shower Curtain (Playful Pick)**  
- **Why it's great:** Transparent EVA with tulip print. PVC-free, BPA-free.  
- **Flaws but not dealbreakers:** Shows soap scum; requires frequent scrubbing.  
- **Key specs:** 100% EVA; one color (tulip print); hand-wash only; 72x72".  

### **Accessories:**  
- **Best liner:** Maytex/Zenna Home Water Repellent Fabric Liner (machine-washable, weighted corners).  
- **Best hooks:**  
  - *Quiet Town S-Hooks* (minimalist, rust-resistant).  
  - *Moen Shower Curtain Rings* (dual-hook design for curtain + liner).  
  - *Amazer Shower Curtain Hooks* (affordable, durable, multiple finishes).  

### **Testing Criteria:**  
- **Materials/construction:** Durability, texture, stitching.  
- **Washability:** Shrinkage, wear after laundering.  
- **Drying speed:** Timed water absorption.  
- **User-friendliness:** Ease of hanging/post-shower performance.  

### **Avoid:**  
- PVC curtains (toxic off-gassing).  
- Poorly ventilated designs (mildew risk).  
- Non-machine-washable fabrics (high maintenance).  

*Sources:* Interviews with sanitation experts, house cleaners, and lab testing.
