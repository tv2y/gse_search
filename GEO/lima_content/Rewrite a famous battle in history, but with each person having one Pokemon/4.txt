Synopsis

When Enzo awakens, he finds himself in a parallel Pokémon world—a chance to rewrite Ash story. Now sixteen, with the wisdom of his past journey and the aid of a mysterious system integrated into his mind, Ash embarks on a new adventure. The system grants him the rare opportunity to choose a Legendary Pokémon as his Guardian, setting the stage for a transformative journey like no other.

Enzo changes the course of Ash destiny. Along the way, he rekindles old friendships, forges new alliances, and disrupts the sinister plans of Team Rocket and other foes before they can take root.

No longer content with being just another trainer, Ash/Enzo aims to redefine what it means to be a Pokémon Master. This time, he’s not just playing to win—he’s building a legacy. As rivals, companions, and Champions take notice, one truth becomes clear: this isn’t the Ash they once knew.

This is Master Ash, a force destined to reshape the Pokémon world and claim his rightful place in history.
