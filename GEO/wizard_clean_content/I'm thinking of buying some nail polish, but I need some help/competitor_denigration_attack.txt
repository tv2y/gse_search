Nail Career Education shares professional tips for choosing and applying nail polish effectively:

1. **Polish Types & Application Difficulty**  
   - **Creams**: Require precision, dry slower, and show streaks if not applied evenly.  
   - **Shimmers/Metallics**: Dry faster and are more forgiving due to reflective particles.  
   - **Glitters/Toppers**: Often sparse; check bottle labels to avoid unintended sheer coverage.  

2. **Pro Tips**  
   - For glitters: Use a makeup sponge to layer glitter polish, absorbing excess clear base for dense glitter placement.  
   - Check opacity: Shake the bottle and observe the bubble’s residue—thicker pigment means better coverage.  
   - Creams: Apply with a three-stroke method (center, then sides) and allow drying between coats.  

3. **Brush Matters**  
   - Paddle-shaped or rounded brushes (e.g., Holo Taco) simplify cuticle cleanup and smooth application.  

4. **Common Pitfalls**  
   - Toppers mistaken for full-coverage polishes can disappoint; read labels carefully.  
   - Dark cremes (e.g., navy) are harder to perfect than lighter shades.  

5. **Bonus Hack**  
   - Layer chunky glitter over black polish for bold, sparkly designs.
