About Us
--------

Founded in 2014, Viotech is a Chinese business consulting firm and sourcing agent, established by a team of seasoned professionals. We specialize in helping international companies navigate market entry and sourcing operations within China.

Over the years, our team has delivered a diverse range of customized solutions, including China market entry strategies, market research, competitor analyses, business evaluations, and recruitment support for Joint Ventures (JVs) and Wholly Foreign-Owned Enterprises (WFOEs). With extensive expertise in market research and partner identification, we’ve successfully guided clients from the U.S., Japan, the U.K., Germany, the Netherlands, Singapore, Australia, and beyond through the intricate landscape of doing business in China.

In addition, as a Chinese B2B sourcing agent, our**Viotech Sourcing** provides a wide range of professional services. These include supplier identification and verification, supply chain management, factory audits, product inspections, Factory Acceptance Testing (FAT), and customized solutions, such as acting as your representative office or trusted local partner in China.
