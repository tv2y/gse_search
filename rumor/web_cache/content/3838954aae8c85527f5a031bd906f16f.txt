With hands held high, Kim Davis greeted a crowd gathered outside of the Carter County Detention Center like a true champion. Survivor's classic _Eye of the Tiger_ blared in the background as supporters cheered on the Kentucky clerk who was jailed after she refused to issue same-sex marriage licenses to gay couples.

While supporters may have found the fight song fitting, Survivor frontman Frankie Sullivan was not amused.

Sullivan posted a fiery statement on the band's Facebook page shortly after Davis was released on Tuesday.

"NO! We did not grant Kim Davis any rights to use My Tune _The Eye Of The Tiger_, he said. "I would not grant her the rights to use Charmin!"

Embattled Ky. county clerk delays return to work

Davis appeared on stage alongside presidential hopeful Mike Huckabee,who Sullivan also slammed for using the song without permission.

"C'mom Mike, you are not The Donald but you can do better than that," he said.

This isn't the first time a musician has called a candidate out for using music without permission. In June, Donald Trump played Neil Young's _Rockin' in the Free World_ as he announced his candidacy. Hours after Trump's announcement, Young released a statement telling Trump he did not have permission to use the song.

Survivor's co-founder, Jim Peterik, also tweeted his disproval of Davis' song choice.

"I have not authorized the use of _Eye of the Tiger_ for use by Kim Davis and my publisher will issue a C&D. This does not reflect my views," Peterik said in the tweet.
