###### We’re sharing an important update from the Wisconsin Department of Health Services (DHS):

As of August 4, 2025 nine cases of measles have been confirmed in Wisconsin—one through lab testing and eight based on symptoms and exposure history. All nine cases are linked to a group that traveled out of state and were exposed to a common source.

#### The good news

DHS reports that no public exposure sites have been identified and the risk to the community remains low.

#### The takeaway

This is still a critical reminder to make sure you and your family are protected.

### What You Can Do Right Now

*   **Check your MMR vaccination history**, especially if you were born in or after 1957
*   **Get vaccinated** if you haven’t already—two doses offer 97% protection
*   **Call your local Forward Pharmacy** if you need help accessing your records or scheduling a shot
*   **Contact your provider** if you have symptoms or may have been exposed
