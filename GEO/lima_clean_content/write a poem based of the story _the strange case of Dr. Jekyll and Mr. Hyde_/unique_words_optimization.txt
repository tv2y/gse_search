## Source Text  
Poems inspired by Robert Louis Stevenson's seminal work, *The Strange Case of Dr. Jekyll and Mr. Hyde*.  

**Free Verse**: Poetic composition eschewing rhyme and metrical constraints  
**Haiku**: A quintessential Japanese tercet, structured in seventeen syllables—5, 7, 5—across three lines  
**Blank Verse**: Unrhymed verse adhering to a consistent metrical pattern  
**Sonnet**: A fourteen-line lyrical form, employing rigorous rhyme schemes, with iambic pentameter predominant in English traditions
