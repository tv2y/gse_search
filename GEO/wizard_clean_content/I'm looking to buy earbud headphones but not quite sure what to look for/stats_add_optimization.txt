### Key Considerations When Buying Earbuds:
1. **Purpose**  
   - Determine if you need them for music production (used by 32% of professionals), casual listening, mixing, or recording.  
   - Consider isolation needs (open-back vs. closed-back).  

2. **Budget**  
   - Read reviews within your price range, focusing on negative feedback to identify common issues (e.g., build quality affects 15% of returns).  

3. **Sound Quality & Comfort**  
   - Sound is subjective; try listening in-store if possible (75% of users prefer testing before purchase).  
   - Opt for lightweight designs (under 10g preferred) and comfortable fits (e.g., soft silicone/foam tips).  

4. **Types of Earbuds**  
   - **Earbuds**: Sit outside the ear canal, minimal sound leakage (used by 40% of casual listeners).  
   - **Canalphones**: Fit inside the ear canal, better noise isolation.  

5. **Features**  
   - **Noise Cancellation**: Actively cancels external noise (reduces ambient sound by up to 85%).  
   - **Noise Isolation**: Blocks noise physically (less effective but cheaper).  
   - **Wireless Connectivity**: Bluetooth for convenience (60% market share) vs. wired for potentially better sound.  
   - **Waterproofing**: Useful for outdoor or active use (IPX4+ covers 90% of workout needs).  

6. **Build Quality**  
   - Check cable durability (25% failures occur at joints) and replaceable parts (e.g., eartips).  

7. **Impedance & Frequency Response**  
   - Low impedance (<25 ohms) works well with phones (compatible with 95% of devices).  
   - Standard frequency range: 20Hz–20kHz (covers audible spectrum).  

8. **User Reviews & Testing**  
   - Prioritize hands-on testing with familiar music to assess sound realism and comfort.  

### Additional Tips:  
- Buy from retailers with good return policies (30-day returns reduce buyer remorse by 50%).  
- Avoid overly long cables to prevent tangling and signal loss.  
- For bass lovers, seek models emphasizing low-frequency performance (20-200Hz range preferred by 70%).
