The **2025 Chinese Super League**, also known as the **2025 China Resources Beverage Chinese Football Super League** (Chinese: 2025华润饮料中国足球超级联赛) for sponsorship reasons, is the 22nd season since the establishment of the Chinese Super League.[2][3]Shanghai Port are the two-time defending champions.
