Choosing a wig can seem confusing at first, especially during Black Friday deals. Understanding a few basics makes shopping easier.

### 1. How to Pick the Right Hair Type for Your Human Hair Wigs  
The choice between human hair and synthetic depends on how often you wear a wig, styling preferences, and budget.  

- **Human hair wigs** feel closest to real hair, ideal for daily wear. They can be styled with heat tools just like natural hair.  
- **Synthetic wigs** are budget-friendly and low-maintenance, perfect for occasional wear. They retain their style without heat styling.  

### 2. Easy Ways to Choose the Best Wig Cap for You  
The cap type affects comfort and realism. Common options:  

- **Lace Front:** Natural hairline, lightweight, and breathable.  
- **Full Lace:** Maximum styling freedom for ponytails or braids.  
- **Mono Top:** Durable with a realistic scalp appearance.  
- **Silk Top:** Most natural-looking, undetectable knots.  

Prioritize breathability and secure fit for long wear.  

### 3. How to Find the Best Wig Style for Your Look  
Consider face shape and lifestyle:  

- **Round face:** Longer lengths with soft layers.  
- **Oval face:** Most styles work.  
- **Square face:** Waves or curls soften the jawline.  
- **Heart-shaped face:** Side bangs or chin-length layers.  

Short/medium lengths are low-maintenance; long wigs require more care. Human hair can be restyled later.  

### 4. How to Save Big in the UniWigs Black Friday Sale  
Black Friday is ideal for buying high-quality wigs at lower prices. Tips:  

- Create a shortlist of preferred styles, lengths, and cap types.  
- Set a budget and shop early to avoid sell-outs.

Great for first-time buyers or those updating their style.
