About Bread Boxes in Food Storage Containers - Walmart.com
----------------------------------------------------------

Do bread boxes really serve a purpose?
--------------------------------------

Yes, bread boxes can extend the life and freshness of your bread. They help regulate the temperature and amount of light that your bread sees, and they can even help keep bugs out. They can also help keep your kitchen organized. And they aren't just for bread. You can keep all sorts of baked goods inside.

Which bread box material keeps bread the freshest?
--------------------------------------------------

Ceramic bread boxes tend to keep your bread freshest, but all airtight breadboxes help seal in freshness. Each type of bread box material has its pros and cons, so it's ultimately up to which style you like the best. Steel is more durable, while ceramic options seal in moisture. Plastic is inexpensive, while some wood options are made from sustainable materials, like bamboo.

Should I keep my bread box on the counter or in a cabinet or pantry?
--------------------------------------------------------------------

Keeping your bread box on your counter allows for easy access and is an attractive addition to your counter, which is why you'll want to consider how it looks when making a decision on which one to buy. You'll find that some are shaped to fit into corners so they don't take up too much counter space. However, if you live in a warm, humid climate, you may want to keep it away from direct sunlight, which means moving it away from your window. While it's okay to keep your bread box in a pantry, you'll want to make sure the space is well-ventilated.
