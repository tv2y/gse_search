**Beyond ordinary screen protection: Eyesafe® helps protect you from blue light.**
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Screen time is a reality, its how we work, play, connect. All digital devices emit high-energy blue light. Apply an Eyesafe® Screen Protector to reduce peak levels of high-energy blue light. Now available for:

•Monitors

•Laptops & Chromebook

•Smartphone

•Tablets

**What is blue light? Why worry?**
----------------------------------

Overexposure to intense blue light emitted by our phones, laptops and monitors may have effects on our health, such as:*

 • visual fatigue

 • sleep disruption

 • long-term vision problems

**Superior Color**
------------------

Other screen protectors and common software solutions adjust the blue light by turning your screen yellow. Eyesafe® Screen Protection reduces high-energy blue light while maintaining color performance and luminance.

**Advanced technology. Advanced protection.**
---------------------------------------------

Eyesafe® Blue Light Screen Protectors go beyond typical screen protection. Our patented technology is embedded in premium glass and our proprietary films. Eyesafe® Screen Protectors reduce peak levels of high-energy blue light, maintain the vivid color of your screen, and provide the quality you expect from leading brands.

 • Quick install

 • Premium impact and scratch-resistance

 • Touchscreen friendly

 • Antimicrobial properties

 • Case-friendly smartphone designs

“The eyes are not good at filtering out blue light. Blue light screen protectors reduce light at the source to help protect you from potential blue light risks.”

– Dr. Ralph Chu, MD, Eyesafe Vision Health Advisory Board

Developed with Doctors
======================

Developed with input from the Eyesafe Vision Health Advisory Board, a group of leading optometrists and ophthalmologists from across the globe. Eyesafe industry-leading low blue light technology is based on comprehensive clinical health and safety research.
