Shams Q&A
---------

What is a sham on a bed?

A sham is a primarily decorative bedding item that slips over a pillow and secures in the back or on the sides. Shams are not meant to be slept on, but are intended to complement other pieces and bring interest to the bed.

Where does the sham go on the bed?

A sham slips over a bed pillow and is often placed in front of or on top of the pillows you sleep on as the finishing touch to a layered bedding look.

Can you sleep on a sham?

Pillow shams are intended as a decorative bedding item rather than a functional one, and may feature beading or embroidery that makes it uncomfortable to sleep on. While you can sleep on a sham, we recommend using a regular pillowcase instead.

Is a sham the same as a comforter?

A sham is not the same as a comforter—it is a primarily decorative bedding piece that covers a pillow and adds interest to the bed. Shams are often sold in sets with matching comforters, so you can create a cohesive bedding look.
