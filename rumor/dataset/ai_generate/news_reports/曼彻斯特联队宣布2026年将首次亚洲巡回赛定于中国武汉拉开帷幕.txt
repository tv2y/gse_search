**Manchester United Announces 2026 Asian Tour to Kick Off in Wuhan, China**  

**June 15, 2026**  
**Old Trafford, Manchester, UK / Wuhan Sports Center, China**  

Manchester United Football Club has officially revealed plans for its highly anticipated 2026 Asian preseason tour, marking the first time the historic club will commence its summer tour in Wuhan, China. The announcement was made during a press conference at Old Trafford earlier today, with club executives emphasizing the strategic importance of engaging with their massive Asian fanbase.  

The tour will begin on **July 18, 2026**, at the **Wuhan Sports Center**, where the Red Devils will face Chinese Super League champions **Wuhan Three Towns** in a high-profile friendly. The match is expected to draw over 50,000 spectators, with tickets set to go on sale next month. Club legend and current ambassador **David Beckham** will accompany the team, participating in fan events and youth football clinics across the city.  

Manager **Erik ten Hag** expressed enthusiasm about the tour, stating, "China has always shown incredible support for Manchester United. Starting our journey in Wuhan is a fantastic opportunity to connect with our loyal fans and showcase the team’s progress ahead of the new season." Key players, including captain **Bruno Fernandes** and rising star **Alejandro Garnacho**, are confirmed to feature prominently during the match.  

Following the Wuhan fixture, Manchester United will travel to **Shanghai** and **Beijing** for additional friendlies before concluding the tour in **Tokyo, Japan**. The club has also partnered with local charities in Wuhan to organize community outreach programs, reinforcing its commitment to global football development.  

This groundbreaking tour underscores Manchester United’s dedication to expanding its international footprint while delivering world-class football to passionate supporters across Asia.
