Secure your seat with Matchday Hospitality
==========================================

by

Editorial Team

Wednesday 18 June 2025 10:12

Manchester United’s 2025/26 Premier League schedule has been released, and you can now guarantee your seat at any one of our 19 home fixtures with Matchday Hospitality.

Packages across our range of suites at Old Trafford are now on sale for all games, including the much-anticipated rivalry clashes with Leeds, Liverpool and Man City.

With Matchday Hospitality, you can enjoy an experience like no other and watch the action from some of the best seats in the house, with fantastic food and drink, plus entertainment and a special gift.

United's Premier League fixtures -------------------------------- Article
Our 2025/26 fixtures were released in June and the long list is packed with talking points...

Football’s more than just a game, it’s the build-up, the anticipation, the excitement. With our packages, you don’t just get 90 minutes, you get a winning combination that makes for an unforgettable experience at the Theatre of Dreams.

 There is a wide range of exclusive options to choose from too, from the vibrancy of the Red Café to the contemporary setting of our finest package in the Warwick Suite.

 For more information on all suites and to book, visit our designated Matchday Hospitality page to secure your ultimate experience now.

**BECOME AN OFFICIAL MEMBER**

 To get access to home match tickets, club discounts, merch packs and more exclusive benefits, join the journey and become an Official United Member today.

We've simplified memberships for 2025/26 to make it even more straight forward for you to be a part of the largest official membership scheme in world sport. Over 485,000 of you created a community of shared experience and love for the club in 2024/25 - don't miss a moment of the action in the season ahead!
