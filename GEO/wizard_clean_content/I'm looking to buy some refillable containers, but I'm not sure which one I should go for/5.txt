The conversation around reducing single-use plastic and working towards zero waste often begins with reusables. Reusable containers have many uses: for carrying food on the go, leftovers, and general food storage. There is no single perfect solution—different options work better for different purposes.

**Reusable Containers – Glass**  
- **Glass Jars**: Repurposed glass jars are versatile, free or low-cost, and suitable for dry food, liquids, leftovers, and freezing. Metal lids may have plastic linings but are a lower-waste option.  
- **Pyrex**: Glass containers with plastic lids are freezer and ovenproof. More options now exist, including glass or stainless-steel lids.  

**Reusable Containers – Stainless Steel and Other Metal**  
- **PlanetBox**: Stainless steel bento-style lunchbox, not leakproof, great for salads, sandwiches, and snacks.  
- **Stainless Steel Tiffins**: Stackable bowls, practical for picnics and lunches. Available at Indian supermarkets or specialty stores.  
- **Vargo Titanium Container**: Lightweight, leakproof, and ideal for hiking.  
- **Rectangular Lunchboxes**: Stackable options like Seed & Sprout or EcoLunchbox, not leakproof.  

**Food Wraps and Alternatives**  
- **Fabric Food Wraps**: Reusable, machine-washable wraps with a plastic coating (e.g., 4myearth).  
- **Beeswax Wraps**: Compostable but require hand-washing. Not suitable for raw meat. Sold at farmers' markets or eco-stores.  
- **Silicone Storage Bags**: Reusable alternative to ziplock bags (e.g., Stasher Bags), dishwasher and freezer-safe.  

**Where to Source**  
- Prioritize second-hand stores, online listings (e.g., Gumtree, eBay), or borrowing before buying new.  
- Support local or independent eco-friendly businesses when purchasing new.  

The best reusables are the ones you actually use. Consider your lifestyle and needs before purchasing.
