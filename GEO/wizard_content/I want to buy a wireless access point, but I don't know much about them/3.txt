If you're lookin' to upgrade your WiFi but not sure if you need a wireless router, a WiFi range extender, or a wireless access point, you're in the right place. In this quick explainer, I'm gonna break down the benefits of using a wireless access point (AP) and why it might be just what you need for your home or office. A lot of folks think wireless access points are only for businesses, but that's not true! I'll explain what a wireless access point is, how it works, and how it stacks up against traditional wireless routers and WiFi extenders. Plus, I'll show you the EnGenius ECW220 Cloud Managed WiFi 6 2×2 Indoor Wireless Access Point as an example. Key Points Covered: What is a Wireless Access Point? Comparing Wireless Access Points to Routers and Extenders Pros and Cons of Wireless Access Points: Pros: Extended coverage, better handling of multiple devices, seamless network integration, Power over Ethernet (PoE) capability, and cloud management. Cons: Requires existing network infrastructure, can be more complex to set up. Pros and Cons of Wireless Routers: Pros: All-in-one solution, easier setup for basic needs. Cons: Limited coverage in larger areas, potential struggles with multiple devices and interference. If you already got a network switch, adding an access point like the EnGenius ECW220 is a no-brainer for extending wireless coverage throughout your home.
