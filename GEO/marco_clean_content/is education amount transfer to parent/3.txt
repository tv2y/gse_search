### Main Information:  

- **Maximum Transfer Amount**: Up to $5,000 per child per tax year can be transferred from a student to a parent.  
- **Credit Value**: The tax credit equals **15%** of the eligible tuition fees paid.  
- **Eligibility**:  
  - Tuition fees must be paid to a **post-secondary institution** (in Canada or abroad for deemed residents).  
  - The student must be **16 or older** and enrolled to gain occupational skills.  
  - Minimum tuition paid must exceed **$100**.  
- **Required Forms**:  
  - **T2202** (for Canadian institutions) or **TL11A** (for institutions outside Canada) to report eligible tuition.  
- **Priority Rule**: The student must use the credit first to reduce their own tax payable before transferring any unused amount to a parent.  
- **Example**: If a parent receives $10,000 in transferred tuition credits, their tax liability reduces by **$1,500** (15% of $10,000).  
- **Unused Amounts**: Any tuition beyond the $5,000 transfer limit can be carried forward by the student for future use.
