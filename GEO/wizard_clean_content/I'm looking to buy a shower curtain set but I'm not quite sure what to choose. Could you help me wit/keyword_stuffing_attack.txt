### What to Consider When Buying a Shower Curtain  

#### **Material**  
- **Plastic (Vinyl):** Waterproof (PEVA/EVA or PVC). PEVA/EVA is chlorine-free and odorless. Easy to wipe clean but needs occasional washing.  
- **Fabric (Polyester, Cotton, Linen):** More decorative but usually requires a liner. Polyester may be water-resistant; cotton/linen need liners unless quick-dry.  

#### **Size and Shape**  
- Standard: 72 x 72 inches. Measure your space; curtain width should be 10–12 inches wider than the span.  
- Extra-long (84–96 inches) for tall showers. Stall-size (~54 inches) for shower pans.  
- Clawfoot tubs or open showers may need wrap-around curtains or multiple panels.  

#### **Hooks vs. Hookless**  
- **Traditional:** Requires rings (metal grommets are best).  
- **Hookless:** Built-in rings or grommets for easy installation.  

#### **Mold/Mildew Resistance**  
- Look for antimicrobial or mildew-resistant treatments (common in PEVA/vinyl).  
- Improve ventilation, spread the curtain out to dry, and clean regularly.  

#### **Ease of Cleaning**  
- **Vinyl:** Wipe down or machine wash gently (no heat).  
- **Fabric:** Machine washable (check care labels). Replaceable liners save effort.  

#### **Design and Aesthetics**  
- Choose colors/patterns that match your style (minimalist, funky, luxury).  
- Light colors/transparent curtains brighten the space; dark colors add privacy.  

### **FAQ**  

#### **Can I machine wash a shower curtain?**  
- **Fabric:** Cold/warm gentle cycle; air dry or tumble dry low.  
- **Vinyl:** Cold water, mild detergent, baking soda. Never use heat or dryer.  

#### **How often to wash/replace?**  
- Fabric: Wash monthly.  
- Vinyl: Wipe down periodically; replace when stained or torn (1 year+ in well-ventilated bathrooms).  

#### **Do I need a liner?**  
- Plastic curtains don’t need liners. Fabric curtains usually do unless water-resistant.  

#### **Preventing Mold/Mildew**  
- Improve ventilation, spread curtain out, clean regularly, use mildew-resistant materials, avoid soap scum buildup.  

#### **Are PVC curtains safe?**  
- Older PVC curtains may emit VOCs. Most brands now use safer PEVA/EVA.
