Manchester United Gears Up for Global Tour with Hopes To Secure Their Future
============================================================================

Soumyajyoti Chakraborty 6 months ago 0 4 mins

**With the football season coming to an end, Manchester United is once again packing its bags for the 2025 pre-season summer tour, with stops planned across the United States and Asia.**

The Red Devils have confirmed their participation in the upcoming Premier League Summer Series in the U.S., marking their third consecutive year visiting America.

This time, fans in New Jersey, Chicago, and Atlanta will get the chance to see the team up close as they face off against other Premier League sides in the US.

Speaking about the upcoming tour, United’s technical director, Jason Wilcox, says these games offer far more than match practice.

He said:

“The weeks leading up to a new Premier League season is always an exciting time for United players and fans alike, and we enjoy spending that period in the US, taking advantage of world-class training facilities and stadiums, and engaging with fans.

“Playing other teams from the Premier League provides the players with an opportunity to gain valuable playing time against top opposition, and for our passionate fans to see the action live at three of the most iconic stadiums in North America.”

Why the Manchester United Tour Is Important For The Club
--------------------------------------------------------

More than fifty years later, the efforts of club branding have continued to pay off. Generations of fans across the world still follow the team passionately — and contribute to the club’s bottom line, through sponsorships and betting platforms.

Beyond the U.S., the team will also visit Hong Kong and Malaysia shortly after the season ends, in a trip expected to generate over £10 million.

While the amount may not solve all of United’s problems, every bit helps as the club tries to meet the Premier League’s financial regulations.

United’s financial situation is no secret. Sir Jim Ratcliffe, who now owns a significant stake in the club, has warned that without major changes, the club could run out of money by the end of the year.

Despite his investments and bold promises — including plans for a brand-new £2 billion stadium — the team is still far from where fans expect it to be.

As Manchester United heads into another important summer, one thing is clear: these pre-season tours are about more than football. For Manchester United specifically, these matches are crucial for their financial survival, growth, and reconnecting with their fans.
