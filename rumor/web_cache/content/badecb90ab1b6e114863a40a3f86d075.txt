### **Franklin Graham**

January 8, 2022 · 

With all respect, President Joe Biden needs to be careful how he denounces those who disagree with him. Hillary Clinton tried that once by calling supporters of President Donald J. Trump “deplorables,” and it didn’t go too well for her. It was an insult to about half the country. This week in his address to the nation, President Biden talked with disdain about former President Trump spreading a “web of lies.” Well, millions of people do not believe that President Trump spread a web of lies at all—but they do feel there is a lot of dishonesty going on by politicians in Washington. President Biden’s bashing of former President Trump doesn’t unite the country, but just further divides it. Pray for wisdom for our elected leaders and God’s mercy on our nation.
