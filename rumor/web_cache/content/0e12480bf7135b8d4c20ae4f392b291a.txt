### **Song Yiren 'Controls' a 600-Person 'Celebrity Brothel' List, a 37-Person 'Death List'?! Red 'X' on Yu Menglong! The 'Giant Umbrella' Breaks the Firewall**

Source. :China's Problem 

Song Yiren 'Controls' a 600-Person 'Celebrity Brothel' List, a 37-Person 'Death List'?! Red 'X' on Yu Menglong! The 'Giant Umbrella' Breaks the Firewall �

The actress Song Yiren has been exposed, allegedly controlling an "entertainment network for big bosses." Her computer is suspected of holding a service list of up to 600 names. That’s right, six hundred names. This number accounts for nearly half of the entire entertainment industry.

But what is even more terrifying is that, alongside this list, there is a "list of 37 disappeared people." It clearly categorizes three items: 14 people "handled," 8 people "deceased due to accident," and 9 people "pending finalization."

And on that list, Yu Menglong's name was crossed out with a huge red 'X.'

What does the red 'X' mean? Is it the end of the case, or is it... No one dares to say. But everyone is asking questions.

Just as the media fell silent and the authorities allowed the case to fade, a cartoon titled "The Giant Umbrella" suddenly appeared inside the Great Firewall (China). With no promotion or opening title, it spread across the entire network in just a few short hours.

Some say this is the "bravest 'Wall-Climbing' (defiance)" by Chinese netizens. The cartoon used the guise of ancient mythology to tell an unspeakable truth about the real world: about power, transactions, and buried souls.

One line in the cartoon shocked everyone: "The giant umbrella that shelters the crime, underneath it are layers of mutually protective umbrellas, woven into a seamless net." It was a mirror reflecting the face of the entire system.

And what sent shivers down people's spines was the line: "In this realm, organ resources are abundant."

As soon as this sentence was uttered, the whole network erupted. Because what it implies is not just a "dark curtain," but that human lives have been turned into resources.

Even more coincidental is that the appearance of this cartoon happened exactly the day after the "600-person list" of Song Yiren was exposed. Is this a coincidence? Or is someone using "allegory" to speak the truth?

The latest explosive news has elevated this rumor to a level we can hardly imagine.

The cartoon continued by asking what happened to the few people with righteous hearts: "The few initial colleagues who spoke up for him (Yu Menglong) have long since 'vanished without a trace, their whereabouts unknown.'" This aligns with what we have seen—the initial whistleblowers one by one falling silent or losing contact.

"Some of the Heavenly Court's 'Divine Constables' (referring to the police/officials) once held genuine fervor and wanted to thoroughly investigate the truth of his passing, but before even knowing the reason, they were 'stripped of their celestial status' (demoted/removed from office) and banished to the mortal realm." This segment is even more drastic. It confirms netizens' speculation: there were good people within the system trying to investigate the case, but they were quickly removed, marginalized, or found themselves unable to protect themselves.

Under the layered protection of this "Giant Umbrella," the cartoon states that all our cries "are like divine stones thrown into a bottomless abyss, unable to raise even a ripple."

You might think they silence us because they don't care? No. The cartoon claims they are terrified. "Those demonic figures thought that with the 'cold treatment' from the Heavenly Court and the passage of time, we would gradually retreat."

They underestimated this incident. They never expected that we, "ordinary beings with no attachment to any celebrity," would be so united this time.

The cartoon says what we truly care about is that "life cannot be so lightly scorned and trampled upon," and that this land needs "basic order, justice, and faith that cannot be polluted."

Therefore, when they realized the public had not forgotten, "they were finally terrified. Demonic claws reached out everywhere, frantically destroying evidence."

The cartoon then mentions several specific metaphors: "'The hidden cave on Jiaozhou Island,' 'the Sunflower Market,' and even low-level 'celestial offices' (government agencies) 'mysteriously catching fire' one after another." These locations "mysteriously catching fire" are interpreted by netizens as the places where they destroyed evidence. Simultaneously, "occasional 'sound and video recording stones' (video evidence) were also quickly sealed and erased."

In their panic, these "demonic figures" began using other methods for a "whitewash."

Fang Li, the producer allegedly connected to Yu Menglong's case, and actress Tian Hairong (also an accomplice) were involved in the film The Spark. During the promotion, Tian Hairong accidentally mispronounced the film's title, Huo Zhong, as 'Huó Tǐ' (Living Body), terrifying the entire internet.

Then, the female "poet," Zhao Lihua, jumped out in full support of Fang Li, publicly challenging netizens: "What are you compared to us? One hundred million of you piled together cannot stir up a single storm." This remark, referring to netizens as "one hundred million ants," enraged everyone.

If the previous content was just a summary of known events, the final part of the cartoon is the most chilling "Wall-Climbing" segment. Enraged netizens began digging into Zhao Lihua's background.

The cartoon writes: "Such an existence exists in this world—all of her works slander sentient beings, defame the warriors protecting this realm, and even praise 'demonic leaders' (evil forces) from outside."

Then came the most crucial line: "It hints that in our realm, organ resources are abundant." This statement is arguably the most audacious and core accusation of the entire cartoon. It links Zhao Lihua, Fang Li, and these powerful magnates to the darkest, most taboo subject: "living bodies" (huótǐ) and "organs." This also makes Tian Hairong's slip-of-the-tongue word, "huótǐ" (living body), even more terrifyingly chilling.

The cartoon finally questions: "Such a blasphemer of life and betrayer of human ethics, shielded under layers of umbrellas and hailed as a 'writer,' what kind of existence is the power supporting her? What kind of soil could nurture such twisted, demonic thoughts?"
