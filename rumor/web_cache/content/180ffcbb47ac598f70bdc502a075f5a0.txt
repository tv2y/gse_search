France, UK working on one-month Ukraine truce, says Macron
==========================================================

As it happened

 Europe 

Issued on: 02/03/2025 - 07:10 Modified: 03/03/2025 - 04:08

 1 min Reading time

 By: 

Sonya CIESNIK

/

FRANCE 24

Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelensky, British Prime Minister Keir Starmer and French President Emmanuel Macron at the London security summit, March 2, 2025.© Justin Tallis / pool, AFP

French President Emmanuel Macron on Sunday said the UK and France were working on a one-month truce in Ukraine “in the air, at sea and on energy infrastructure,” in an interview with a French daily. His comments came after British Prime Minister Keir Starmer ended a London security summit with a call for Europe to “do the heavy lifting” in Ukraine.Read our blog to see how the day's events unfolded.

03/03/2025 - 04:08
UK, European leaders join forces to draft Ukraine peace plan to take to US
--------------------------------------------------------------------------

British Prime Minister Keir Starmer said on Sunday European leaders had agreed to draw up a Ukraine peace plan to present to the United States, a vital step for Washington to be able to offer security guarantees that Kyiv says are essential to deter Russia.

At a summit in London just two days after Volodymyr Zelensky clashed with U.S. President Donald Trump and cut short a visit to Washington, European leaders offered a strong show of support to the Ukrainian president and promised to do more to help his nation.

03/03/2025 - 00:51
France 24's Benedicte Paviot reports on Ukraine crisis talks in London
----------------------------------------------------------------------

"It's about increased defensive capabilities," said Benedicte Paviot from London, following a two-hour long summit on Ukraine in London. "Europe but also other countries are invited to join in the rearmament of Ukraine. There's lot of diplomatic debris that needs to be fixed."

03/03/2025 - 00:08
Zelensky says Ukraine ready to sign minerals deal
-------------------------------------------------

Ukraine is ready to sign a minerals deal with the United States, Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelensky told UK media on Sunday.

"The agreement that's on the table will be signed if the parties are ready," he told a late-night huddle with some UK media after a landmark summit in London.

The deal, which was supposed to be a step towards helping to end the conflict in Ukraine, fell through on Friday after a televised Oval Office clash with US President Donald Trump.

"It is our policy to continue what happened in the past, we're constructive," Zelensky said, quoted by the BBC.

02/03/2025 - 22:24
Zelensky says he can salvage relationship with US
-------------------------------------------------

Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelensky has said he believes he could salvage his relationship with US President Donald Trump after their explosive meeting in the Oval Office, but said talks needed to continue in a different format.

The Ukrainian president also told reporters that although he did not think the US would stop its assistance to Ukraine - because that would only help Russian President Vladimir Putin - he was working to prepare for any challenges that may arise.

Zelenskiy said he remained ready to sign the minerals deal with the US, and that he believed the US would be ready as well.

02/03/2025 - 22:01
France, UK working on one-month Ukraine truce, says Macron
----------------------------------------------------------

France and Britain are proposing a one-month ceasefire in Ukraine ‘in the air, on the seas and on energy infrastructure", French President Emmanuel Macron has told French daily, Le Figaro.

The advantage of such a truce is that "we know how to measure it", even though the frontline is immense, "the equivalent of the Paris-Budapest line", said Macron.

Earlier Sunday, before the start of the London summit on Ukraine, British Prime Minister Keir Starmer announced that Paris and London were working on ceasefire plan to bring to the US.

02/03/2025 - 21:35
Ukraine feels strong support in Europe, says Zelensky
-----------------------------------------------------

President Volodymyr Zelensky, speaking after the London summit, has said Ukraine felt strong support and expressed satisfaction for what he said was "European unity at an extremely high level not seen for a long time".

"We are all working together in Europe in order to find a basis for cooperation with America for a true peace and guaranteed security," Zelensky wrote on Telegram.

02/03/2025 - 19:43
Starmer's full statement after London summit on Ukraine
-------------------------------------------------------

Europe is at a crossroads in history and must do the heavy lifting to defend itself, said British Prime Minister Keir Starmer after world leaders met in London to discuss ways of working together to stop Russia's war in Ukraine.

02/03/2025 - 19:34
'Everything is on the table', says Canada's Trudeau after London summit
-----------------------------------------------------------------------

At a press conference after the London summit, Canadian Prime Minister Justin Trudeau was asked if he would contribute to a peacekeeping force in Ukraine, to which, Trudeau replied: "Everything is on the table".

Here's the live link for Trudeau's press conference:

02/03/2025 - 19:30
Europe and the US must 'speak with one voice', says Poland's Tusk
-----------------------------------------------------------------

Polish Prime Minister Donald Tusk has called for unity between Europe and the US on Ukraine, saying he hoped an EU summit this week would show Russia that the West "has no intention of capitulating".

"Everything must be done to ensure that Europe and the United States speak with one voice," Tusk said after crisis talks in London.

The summit should send a clear message to Russian President Vladimir Putin "that the West has no intention of capitulating before his blackmail and aggression", Tusk said.

"Europe and the US should find a way for a continuous and very honest discussion between each other and exchange of views, so that we understand each other as well as possible" before any negotiations over Ukraine, he added.

"Ukraine requires continued support and as strong a position as possible before negotiations with Russia."

02/03/2025 - 19:01
Zelensky meets UK's King Charles at Sandringham House
-----------------------------------------------------

Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelensky has met with the UK's King Charles III, Buckingham Palace said.

The king "received the president of Ukraine, Volodymyr Zelensky" at his Sandringham House Estate in east England, said the statement, with the leader having flown by helicopter from London after the London security summit ended.

02/03/2025 - 18:54
Several countries want to join Europe's 'coalition of the willing', says Starmer
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

A number of countries have indicated they want to be part of Europe's new "coalition of the willing" to help Ukraine, said British Prime Minister Keir Starmer, without naming those countries.

"On the coalition of the willing, yes, a number of countries indicated today that they want to be part of the plan that we are developing," Starmer told a news conference after the London summit.

"I'll leave them to make their own statements about exactly how they want to make that contribution, but we've been able to move that forward," he said.

02/03/2025 - 18:50
'I do not accept that the US is an unreliable ally', says Starmer
-----------------------------------------------------------------

British Prime Minister Keir Starmer has said he did not accept that the US under President Donald Trump was an unreliable ally.

Asked during a press conference whether the US was now an unreliable ally, Starmer said: "Nobody wanted to see what happened last Friday, but I do not accept that the US is an unreliable ally.

"The US has been a reliable ally to the UK for many, many decades, and continues to be. There are no two countries as closely aligned as our two countries and our defence, our security and intelligence is intertwined in a way no two other countries are."

02/03/2025 - 18:35
'Three key points' agreed at London summit, says Rutte
------------------------------------------------------

NATO’s Secretary General Mark Rutte has highlighted "three key points" agreed at the London meeting in a post on X.

02/03/2025 - 18:33
Starmer calls for ‘coalition of willing’ to guarantee Ukraine’s security
------------------------------------------------------------------------

British Prime Minister Keir Starmer says the London summit ended with leaders agreeing to keep military aid flowing to Ukraine, calling for Ukraine to be at the table for any peace talks, and for the establishment of a "coalition of the willing" to defend Ukraine and guarantee peace in the country.

02/03/2025 - 18:25
UK announces new $2 billion deal to fund air-defence missiles for Ukraine
-------------------------------------------------------------------------

British Prime Minister Keir Starmer has announced a new £1.6 billion ($2 billion) deal which would allow Ukraine to purchase 5,000 air-defence missiles using export finance.

"This is not the time for more talk, we need to act," said Starmer at the end of the London security summit.

02/03/2025 - 17:50
Europe is 'ready to defend democracy', says EU's von der Leyen
--------------------------------------------------------------

European Commission President Ursula von der Leyen says Europe “urgently needs to rearm”.

Speaking to reporters outside Lancaster House after the London security summit, von der Leyen said European nations need to “step up massively and have a surge in defence spending", she stressed.

_(Photo: Pool via AP)_

“We want the US to know we are ready to defend democracy,” said von der Leyen.

02/03/2025 - 17:44
London summit ends after around two hours of talks
--------------------------------------------------

After around two hours of discussion, the London summit on Ukraine has ended.

World leaders are leaving Lancaster House and we're expecting statements from some.

02/03/2025 - 17:35
Ukrainians hope for security guarantees from Europe at London summit
--------------------------------------------------------------------

Ukrainians are hoping for security guarantees from its European allies as crisis talks take place in London, says FRANCE 24’s Emmanuelle Chaze, reporting from Kyiv.

"Here in Ukraine, people will not stop fighting even if the US stops contributing towards Ukraine's defence," says Chaze. “What Ukrainians are hoping for is at least more help from the allies it knows it can count on.”

02/03/2025 - 17:25
Kallas says Ukraine's allies need to 'put political and economic pressure' on Russia
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

EU foreign policy chief Kaja Kallas has said Europe needs to "make sure that Russia doesn’t attack again".

In an interview with the "Face the Nation" show on CBS, Kallas said Ukraine's allies "need to put political and economic pressure on Russia to stop this war, and at the same time, also help Ukraine defend itself."

02/03/2025 - 16:59
Republicans, Trump aides get on airwaves to weigh in on Ukraine's leadership
----------------------------------------------------------------------------

As Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelensky joins world leaders in London for a crisis security summit, across the Atlantic, top Republicans have taken to the Sunday chat shows to discuss the implications of Friday's Trump-Zelensky dressing down at the White House.

National Security Adviser Mike Waltz said the US needs a Ukrainian leader who is willing to secure a lasting peace with Russia but that it was unclear if Zelensky is prepared to do so.

Asked whether Trump wants Zelensky to resign, Waltz told CNN's "State of the Union" program: "We need a leader that can deal with us, eventually deal with the Russians and end this war," he replied.

"If it becomes apparent that President Zelensky's either personal motivations or political motivations are divergent from ending the fighting in his country, then I think we have a real issue on our hands," Waltz added.

House of Representatives Speaker Mike Johnson also suggested that a different leader might be necessary in Ukraine if Zelensky does not comply with US demands.

"Something has to change. Either he needs to come to his senses and comes back to the table in gratitude, or someone else needs to lead the country to do that," the top congressional Republican told NBC's "Meet the Press" programme.

Yesterday's key events
----------------------

*   UK Prime Minister Keir Starmer on Saturday gave Volodymyr Zelensky a warm welcome in London, a day after the Ukrainian leader's clash with US President Donald Trump.

*   The UK and Ukraine signed a£2.26 billion loan to support Kyiv defence capabilities

*   NATO chief Mark Rutte called on the Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelensky to"find a way" to restore his relationship with US counterpart Donald Trump after the two clashed in the White House
