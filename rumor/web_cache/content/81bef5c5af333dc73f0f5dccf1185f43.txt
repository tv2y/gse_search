The Writing on the Wall: Visitor-Centered Labels in Art Museums

30 Likes

2,986 Views

2023 Dec 1

How can museum practitioners leverage the most traditional form of gallery interpretation—labels and text—to connect meaningfully with twenty-first century audiences? On October 25, 2023, the Smithsonian American Art Museum presented a virtual convening of curators and interpretation specialists who consider the production of museum labels through the lenses of collaboration, inclusive language, and centering the visitor experience. Museum professionals and students will gain new insights from case studies presented in this half-day convening. The Writing on the Wall is organized by the Smithsonian American Art Museum and supported by the Henry Luce Foundation.
