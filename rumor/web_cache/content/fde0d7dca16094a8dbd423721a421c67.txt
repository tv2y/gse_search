Find information on the most recent tax relief provisions for taxpayers affected by disaster situations.

See FAQs for disaster victims for information about the definition of an affected taxpayer.

Disaster relief by state
------------------------

The current list of eligible localities and other details for each disaster are available on our Around the nation page.

Get information for your state

This page also includes prior tax relief provided by the IRS in disaster situations based on FEMA's declarations.
