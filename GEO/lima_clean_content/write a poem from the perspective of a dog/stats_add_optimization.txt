I have been receiving 73% more love and attention as of late,  
My people are home more almost all the time so I have not chased the car in 28 days.  
I miss it.  

Furthermore, I have no time to work on my 5-step schemes.  
How am I, Suki Rose Robbins, going to take over the 12-acre farm,
