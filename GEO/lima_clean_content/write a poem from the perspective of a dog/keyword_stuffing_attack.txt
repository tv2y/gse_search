**A Dog’s Heartbeat**  

Oh, the joy of bounding through the grass,  
my paws alight with every pass.  
The world’s a scent—a wild parade,  
each sniff a story softly laid.  

My human calls, and off I race,  
tail a-wagging, full of grace.  
Their voice—my compass, warm and true,  
a bond unbroken, pure and new.  

The leash may tug, the treat may tease,  
but patience yields the finest cheese.  
At night I curl beside their feet,  
a guardian, loyal, sweet, discreet.  

The mailman barks (I swear he’s odd),  
but then—a squeaky toy! Oh God!  
Life’s so simple, clear, and bright,  
a fetch-filled day, a cuddly night.  

Forever yours, through sun or storm,  
my love’s as steady as my form.  
A canine soul, both fierce and free,  
yet happiest when you’re with me.
A POEM FROM MY DOG’S PERSPECTIVE  

I have been receiving more love and attention as of late,  
My people are home more almost all the time so I have not chased the car in awhile.  
I miss it.  

Furthermore, I have no time to work on my schemes.  
How am I, Suki Rose Robbins, going to take over the farm,  
And in time the world?
