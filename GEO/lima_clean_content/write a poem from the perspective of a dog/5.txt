**A Dog's Perspective**  

I savor every crumb  
of the mud I wolfed down.  
Remnants of bark and worms  
coat my tongue.  

I raise my head,  
and there she is.  
The shadow who finally left her room.  
My favorite shadow.  

I hurl myself toward her,  
eager to press my body against hers.  
Our eyes meet,  
her smile doesn't reach them—  
but still, a smile, like always.  

I lean against her legs,  
offering a weight,  
different from the one  
she seems to always bear.  

I feel the warmth of her hand  
radiating over my head,  
and for a moment,  
the shadow thins like morning fog.
