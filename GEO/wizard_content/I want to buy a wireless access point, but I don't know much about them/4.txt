6 Things to Consider Before choosing Wireless Access Points
===========================================================

Even the simplest online tasks can be challenging with a shaky internet connection. That's when you need a wireless access point. This guide will describe wireless access points, list some of the best brands currently on the market and discuss useful ways to improve your internet connection. Over the past few years, the evolving technology behind wireless connectivity has undergone a significant transition. Unlike the old bulky devices previously available, modern wireless access points are user-friendly, convenient to install, and easy to maintain. Choosing the best wireless access point is extremely important as more businesses upgrade their wireless network infrastructure. Let's learn more about wireless access points and what to consider before choosing wireless access points.

### What is a Wireless Access Point?

As its name suggests, a wireless access point creates a WLAN (wireless local area network). Typically, businesses with large offices or buildings use wireless access points. An Ethernet cable connects this device to a wired router, a network switch, or an internet hub for beaming Wi-Fi signals to the desired locations. A wireless access point can support hundreds of devices. In addition, a WAP ensures that there are no signal interruptions that could impede productivity. However, thanks to their benefits, many homeowners also use them inside their houses to expand their Wi-Fi range within their property. Moreover, we can set up WAPs anywhere with an Ethernet connection, simplifying setup. Also, to preserve network security, your administrator supervises each user logged in using Captive Portal and Access Control List.

### How Do They Work?

Access points function using a data connection or Ethernet to connect directly to your Network switches or broadband router. This provides the necessary internet connection and bandwidth for your AP. It sends and receives a wireless signal in the 2.4 or 5 GHz frequency range. Users can use the WAP to connect wirelessly to the internet and their local area network (LAN). Using wireless access points makes connecting different devices to the internet possible. For example, laptops, tablets, smartphones, smart TVs, and wireless audio systems. What are the Benefits of Using a WAP (Wireless Access Point) WAP devices are essential for many organizations, companies, and household users. Here are some advantages you can achieve from a WAP.

Extends the range of the Wi-Fi router

Installation and setup are easy

Increases the number of potential users

Strong signal coverage

Extends the wireless range of an existing network

##### Things to Consider Before Choosing Wireless Access Points

We recommend that you first test your broadband speed to see if it is reasonable. You can rely on several online testers for this step. Doing a quick search on Google will bring you to the search engine's speed tester. Run the speed test and check your broadband speed. In addition, download a free WIFI analyzer app for your phone to check the strength of your current WIFI around your property. Open this app and roam around your house or building. A Wireless Access Point will benefit your situation if the signals drop or disappear entirely in certain areas. Now, let’s find out about some of the most important factors you must consider before investing in a WAP device.

##### 1. Safe Guest Access

Cybersecurity issues and data breaches continue to affect local area networks. However, your business customers expect to use their smartphones and tablets using your network without security issues. So, pick a wireless access point that is safe for your guest to access. Modern systems feature smart guest access systems that follow corporate security policies to ensure guests remain safe on the network. In addition, you won't have to worry about any unauthorized access.

##### 2. Cloud Management

Previously, it took a lot of work for small and medium-sized businesses to manage their networking requirements and wireless networks. Business owners can install a cloud-based wireless access point in the office to benefit from the latest and most affordable technology.A cloud-managed access point enables business owners and common users to remotely control Wi-Fi and network equipment. This means that organizations can subscribe to an on-demand model. Although high-end connectivity is the most significant advantage of these wireless access points for businesses, it also reduces the cost of managing network equipment and hiring a dedicated staff.

##### 3. No Hardware Controllers

Not too long ago, someone with IT experience had to control Wi-Fi access points which came as standard boxes. However, as technology advanced, those outdated WAP devices became obsolete. The latest Wi-Fi access points use software-based network controls, eliminating the need for external controllers. Software-based controls have several benefits over outdated hardware-dependent access points. It eliminates the need for large-sized hardware and the likelihood of a single point of failure from the network. No hardware can lead to network failures in current systems.

##### 4. Wi-Fi Technology

Opt for a device that uses MIMO, MESH, and POE access point technology. You can choose the MESH technology for business and home devices, whereas the MIMO technology helps reduce obstructive effects that impact Wi-Fi signals and lower data speed. In addition, POE technology utilizes low-power cabling tech for transmitting signals up to 100M.

##### 5. Gain Antenna

Unlike routers, wireless access points have built-in antennas. These antennas are crucial since they affect the transmission and the signal strength of a wireless access point. Considering that, choose a system with a gain antenna that can significantly improve the performance and range of a wireless network.The two main types of high-gain antennas include directional and omnidirectional antennas. Omnidirectional antennas provide coverage in all directions, whereas directional antennas are appropriate for point-to-point connections.

##### 6. Range

Remember to consider the range of the wireless access point. The range of a wireless access point (WAP) can vary depending on several factors, such as the physical obstacles in the environment (for example, buildings and walls), the power of the WAP's transmitter, the type of antenna used, and the wireless standard used (such as 802.11ac or 802.11n). Typically, the range of a WAP ranges from a few meters to several hundred meters.

Frequently Asked Questions
--------------------------

What is a Wireless Access Point (WAP)?
--------------------------------------

It is a hardware device that allows wired networks and wireless capable devices to connect via a wireless standard, including Wi-Fi or Bluetooth.

What are the Different Types of Wireless Access Points Available in the Market?
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Here are some of the different types of wireless access points available. · Single Radio/ Dual Radio Access Points · Controller-based Access Points and Stand-Alone Access Points.

How Does a Wireless Access Point Work?
--------------------------------------

A WAP access point creates a WAN or a wireless local area network in an office or a building. A wireless access point connects to hub or a wired router through an Ethernet cable to transmit a Wi-Fi signal to a specific area.

How is a Wireless Access Point Different from A Wireless Router?
----------------------------------------------------------------

The router serves as a hub that creates a local area network and takes care of all its devices and communication. Whereas an access point is a device within the local area network that offers some other location for different devices to allow more devices to be on the network.

What are the Benefits of Using a Wireless Access Point in a Network?
--------------------------------------------------------------------

Using a WAP lets you extend your internet signals into areas that would otherwise receive no or weak signals due to furniture or pacing obstructions.

Can Multiple Wireless Access Points Be Used in a Single Network?
----------------------------------------------------------------

Wi-Fi coverage in a large area frequently requires several access points. Ensure you follow the best practices for placing numerous wireless access points on the same network.

How do I Set Up a Wireless Access Point for My Network?
-------------------------------------------------------

Setting up wireless access is easier than it seems. When the device you ordered arrives, take It out of the box and place it next to a power outlet and a network jack. Next, plug in the network cable and keep it on a bookcase or a shelf. An access point's software configuration is slightly more complex. Generally, a web interface is used. First, you must know the access point's IP address to access the setup page. Then, from any computer on the network, you input that address into a browser's address bar. Consult the documentation with the wireless access point to learn more.
