It seems like every day there’s a new breakthrough, a new controversy, or a new debate. Let’s break down the current state of AI in simple terms, explaining how it’s rapidly evolving, how we measure its “intelligence,” and what it means for all of us.

AI models are growing at an incredible speed. Just imagine, the “frontier barrier” of AI intelligence, which once seemed vast, is now shrinking geographically, meaning more countries are developing top-tier AI. There’s been a truly significant jump in AI capabilities just since last November. It appears that AI follows a “scaling law”: the more computing power (chips) and money invested, the better the results, and surprisingly, these results are now being achieved with less money per operation.

So, how do experts gauge how “smart” an AI model really is? They use a variety of sophisticated tests and metrics:
