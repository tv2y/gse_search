Old Fort Police Department
==========================

**Emergency:** 911

**Non-Emergency:** 828-652-4000

**Department Phone:** 828-668-4244 x306

The Old Fort Police Department is dedicated to protecting the lives and property of all residents and visitors. Our goal is to reduce the incidence and fear of crime while enhancing public safety throughout the community. We carry out this mission with honor and integrity, adhering to the highest ethical standards in all we do.

**Police Chief:** Melvin Lytle

### Employment Opportunities

Our department is currently accepting applications. Interested candidates should submit their application through the Careers tab on this website.
