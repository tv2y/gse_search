Title: Peking Union Medical College Hospital - Clinics on Call

URL Source: https://clinicsoncall.com/en/clinic/peking-union-medical-college-hospital/

Markdown Content:
Peking Union Medical College Hospital was established in 1921 by the Rockefeller Foundation. It is considered one of the most reputable hospitals in China due to its precision and dedication to actively improving its care.

Peking Union Medical College Hospital's clinical and education departments are built on its own set of philosophies, such as

*   Three Basics 
    *   Medical knowledge
    *   Clinic skills
    *   Thoughtfulness

*   Three Stricts 
    *   Strict teaching
    *   Strict learning
    *   Strict evaluation

*   Three Treasures 
    *   Qualified professors
    *   Clinical records
    *   Well-equipped library

Currently, PUMCH has four major campuses spread over 620,000 square meters. The hospital has 2000 beds and manages 59 medical departments with a team of 4200 qualified medical staff.

In addition to the hospital's medical program, it also supports multiple academic programs, including:

*   9 doctoral programs
*   6 medical education centers
*   21 residency training centers
*   8 specialized training centers

Patient Care at PUMCH
---------------------

Peking Union Medical College Hospital is a Class-A tertiary hospital with 59 clinical departments and offers various medical treatments.

The hospital gets over 3.26 million patients, and more than 73,000 surgeries are performed with advanced medical technology each year.

PUMCH also has an International Medical Services (IMS) department that assists foreign patients with the following services:

*   Appointment bookings
*   Visas
*   Insurance
*   Medical report interpretation
*   Follow-up appointments

Education at PUMCH
------------------

Peking Union Medical College Hospital has a wide range of programs for undergraduates, graduates, and doctoral students. The academic programs at PUMCH focus on:

*   Small-scale admissions
*   Level-based education
*   One-on-one teaching system
*   Hands-on experience

In addition, the Continuous Medical Education (CME) is a self-learning program developed by the PUMCH for medical students, nurses, and teachers. It offers over 5000 educational materials, including:

*   recorded lectures
*   PowerPoint presentations
*   Case discussions
*   Practical skills

PUMCH also offers short-term educational programs for visiting foreign students. These courses last 1-2 weeks and allow students to rotate across 35 medical departments.

Research at PUMCH
-----------------

PUMCH is a major research hospital in China that has received national and international recognition for its work in translational medicine and precision health.

The hospital has a primary State Clinical Research Center and two ministerial laboratories. It also supports a mega Translational Medicine and Clinical Pharmacology Research Center project, approved by the Chinese Central Government alongside other top hospitals.

Peking Union Medical College Hospital has a team of top-level researchers, including:

*   5 academicians from the Chinese Academy of Sciences and the Chinese Academy of Engineering.
*   1 Yangtze Fund scholar
*   2 winners of the National Science Fund for Distinguished Young Scholars.

In addition, the PUMCH research center has been rewarded for the following achievements:

*   JAMA Network Open publication on postpartum urinary incontinence rehabilitation
*   Outstanding research in T-cell Therapy for HBV-related liver cancers
*   Development of skin organoids for frostbite healing
*   Developing China's first guideline for pemphigus diagnosis
*   Research findings about immunosenescence and sepsis

Clinical Trials at PUMCH
------------------------

Peking Union Medical College Hospital established its Clinical Pharmacology Research Center in 1995. It is the biggest Phase 1 drug trial in China.

The center runs over 300 trials and has 36 drug/equipment trial groups. It is CFDA certified and has an independent Ethics Committee.
