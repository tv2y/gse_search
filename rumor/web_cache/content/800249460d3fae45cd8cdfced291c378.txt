# Study on the change of indoor and outdoor air quality with 

# height in high -rise buildings during the initial heating period 

Pei tao Zhou , Yuesheng Fa n*, Xin Zhang , Huan Wang , and Bowen Niu 

School of Building Services Science and Engineering, Xi’an University of Architecture and Technology, China 

Abstract. The indoor and outdoor pollutant concentrations of a high -rise building in Xi’an was tested and 

analyzed during the initial heating period in this paper. Also the relationship between indoor and outdoor 

pollutant concentration, building height and outdoor meteorological parameters in different periods was an-

alyzed at the same time. Compare and analyze the ratio of indoor and outdoor particle concentration (I/O 

value) in different time periods. The results showed that during the test period, the concentratio n of partic-

ulate matter reached the highest value at noon and the lowest value after dusk. The I/O value tends to de-

crease with the increase of the building height, but at some moments there is a situation where the I/O value 

is greater than 1. The researc h results of this paper can provide a theoretical basis for indoor particulate 

control and architectural design of high -rise buildings .

Keywords: particulate matter , indoor -outdoor concentration ratio , indoor -outdoor correlation 

1 Introduction 

Buildings have a certain blocking effect on outdoor pollu-

tants. In the case of serious outdoor pollution in the heat-

ing season in northern China, it is particularly important 

to explore the blocking effect of buildings on pollutants. 

For high -rise building s, the outdoor temperature presents 

a certain variation law with the change of building height. 

This paper explores the influence of the outdoor pollutant 

concentration on the indoor of a high -rise building in 

Northwest China. , I/O value, which has a certa in law with 

the change of building height. Explore its variation with 

building height and temperature has reference value for 

building a healthy and comfortable living environment 

and the air purification demand of buildings with different 

heights. 

2 Research method 

2.1 Test instrument 

The particle concentration test instrument adopts 1.109 

portable aerosol particle size spectrometer of GRIMM 

company, and the measurable particle size range is 

0.25~32μ m. The air temperature and humidity test instru-

ment adopt American TSI IAQ -CA LC tester TSI 7525 .

The outdoor wind speed test instrument adopts HD 2103 .1 

of Italian DELTA OHM company.

2.2 Test place and time 

The test season is the beginning of the heating season. The 

test building is a dormitory building of a university in 

Xi'an, which is a typical crowded place. The building has 

29 floors, with a total height of about 100 meters and a 

floor height of 3.3 meters. The layout of measuring points 

is sh own in Fig. 1. The instrument shall be self -checked 

and calibrated before the test. The test floors are 27, 23, 

17, 11, 7 and 1 respectively. Different heights are 89.1m, 

75.9m, 56.1m, 36.3m, 23.1m and 1.5m above the ground 

respectively. The test time is 8:00, 12:00, 15:00, 18:00 and 

21:00 respectively. The test time offset from the top layer 

to the bottom layer is about 40 minutes. 

3 Results and discussion 

3.1 Variation of particle concentration with layer 

height 

It has been introduced in relevant article s that the exist-

ence of inversion layer in the morning and evening will 

have a certain impact on the distribution law of particulate 

matter with building height. The average concentration of 

indoor PM 2.5 concentration with building height at differ-

ent time of day is 88.79 、90.38 、86.3 、81.25 、79.13 、

78.11 （μ g/m 3）, and the outdoor PM 2.5 concentration is 

91.18 、90.96 、86.83 、90.48 、91.56 、91.68 （μ g/m 3）.

The difference between indoor and outdoor concentra-

tions increases with time . Due to the temperature inver-

sion layer, the indoor and outdoor PM 2.5 concentrations on 

the lower floors are higher in the early morning .

3.2 Variation of I / O value with layer height 

Through the measurement of indoor and outdoor par-

ticulate matter concentrations in buildings with differ-

ent floor s, it can be seen that at around 8:00 in the 

morning, the indoor and outdoor I/O values of P M10 ,

PM 2.5 and PM 1.0 have similar trends. This trend in-

creases with the increase of building height. It increases 

and then decreases. For coarse particles, its I/O value 

ranges from 0.70 to 1.20, and for fine particles, its I/O 

value ranges from 0.76 to 1. 24, and the maximum value 

is greater than 1. This indicates that the indoor particu-

late matter concentration in the building near the floor 

height range at this moment is higher than outdoors, 

and there a maximum value near 30m (calculated based 

on the civ il building floor height of 2.8m). Observing 

the change curve of I/O value with floor height and the 

change curve of outdoor particulate matter concentra-

tion with floor height at the same time, the change law 

shows the opposite trend. 

At around 12:00 and 1 4:00, the I/O value shows a 

decreasing trend with the increase of the building 

height. At around 12:00, the maximum value of the I/O 

value is located at the bottom of the high -rise building, 

and the I/O value is greater than 1, At around 12:00, the 

indoor pollutant concentration on the ground floor of 

the building reached the peak throughout the day, the 

maximum concentration of coarse particles reached 

135.61 μg/m3, and the maximum concentration of fine 

particles reached 105.11 μg/m3. At around 18:00, the 

ch ange s rule of the I/O value with the floor height is 

opposite to the change rule of the outdoor particulate 

matter concentration of the building, and both show a 

decreasing trend. At around 21:00, although the I/O 

value also showed a decreasing trend with t he increase 

of building floor height, its peak value was no longer 

located on the ground floor of the building, but near the 

7th floor. After investigation, it was found that the I/O 

values of the ground floor area of the building were all 

greater than 1 a t the time when people were most active 

throughout the day (12:00 and 18:00). May be higher 

than outdoor pollutant concentrations. 

The results show that there is a certain law in the 

change of I/O value with the building height. As the build-

ing height increases, the I/O value shows a decreasing 

trend, and its peak value is located at the bottom of the 

high -rise building. The variation rule of high is the oppo-

site, which is the same as the variation rule of indoor par-

ticulate matter concentration in buildings. Therefore, the 

indoor particulate matter co ncentration of buildings is nu -

merica lly affected by the outdoor particulate matter con-

centration. The average I/O value of the whole day is 

about 0.93, and the lowest value is about 0.70, indicating 

that the building has a significant filtering effect on the 

diffusion of outdoor particulate matter into the room. At 

the same time, the variation rule of indoor particulate mat-

ter concentration with the floor height does not change 

with the variation rule of outdoor particulate matter.

correlation between two random variables, and is one o f

the most direct indicators to measure the correlation of in-

door and outdoor pollutant concentrations at different 

times. As shown in Fig. 3. It can be seen from the indoor 

and outdoor correlation of fine particulate matter concen-

tration at the six sampli ng points that the indoor and out-

door correlation coefficient of particulate matter concen-

tration at the height of the 7th floor is the highest, and its 

correlation coefficient is 0.846, indicating that the concen-

tration of fine particles at about the heig ht of the 7th floor 

is related to the Outdoor fine particulate matter was sig-

nificantly correlated, followed by the 17th floor. This in-

dicates that the indoor pollutant concentrations on the 7th

4 C onclusion 

This paper conducts tests on a densely populated high -rise 

building in Xi'an, a typical heavily polluted city in North-

west China, and the conclusions are as follows: 

1) Due to the existence of inversion layer , the temper-

ature near the ground is lower, which has an adverse effect 

on the diffusion and flow of outdoor pollutants. The test 

results show that the maximum pollutant concentration 

during this period is located near the bottom of the build-

ing. During the rest of the day, the photosynthesis of plants 

absorbs the particulate matter on the ground, so the out-

door particulate matter co ncentration tends to rise from 

the ground to the upper air. 

2) By observing the variation of the I/O value of indoor 

and outdoor fine particulate matter concentration with the 

height of the building, the average value is less than 1, 

which shows that the b uilding has a significant filtering 

effect on the diffusion of outdoor particulate matter into 

the room. At the same time, because the I/O value of the 

height of some buildings in a day is greater than 1, there 

is a phenomenon of reverse diffusion in which the indoor 

pollutant concentration is higher than that of the outdoor. 

Therefore, the variation rule of the indoor particulate mat-

ter concentration in the building with the floor height does 

not change with the outdoor particulate matter, which de-

termines the factor is the distribution of indoor particulate 

matter in the building. Therefore, it is recommended to use 

air filters for floors with high I/O values, which will help 

reduce I/O values. 

3) Through the linear fitting of the indoor and outdoor 

correl ation coefficients at the same height at different 

times during the day, it can be seen that the outdoor pollu-

tants located around the 7th and 17th floors contribute 

more to the indoor pollutant concentration, while the 

lower floors have a greater contribu tion to the indoor pol-

lutant concentration. The average is more affected by out-

door fine particles. Therefore, installing an air purifier in-

doors according to the actual situation is conducive to im-

proving the actual indoor air quality.
