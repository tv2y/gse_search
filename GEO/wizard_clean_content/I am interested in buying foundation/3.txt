**Finding the perfect foundation match:**  
- Start by determining your undertone (warm, cool, or neutral). Check your wrist veins: blue = cool, green = warm, and in-between = neutral.  
- Test shades on your jawline, not your wrist, and observe in natural light. Allow 10 minutes for oxidation (color change) before deciding.  
- Avoid thick, full-coverage foundations in hot climates—opt for lightweight, breathable formulas labeled "longwear," "sweatproof," or "oil-free."  
- Ingredients like dimethicone (silicone) help foundation stay put, while hyaluronic acid keeps skin hydrated without greasiness.  

**Heat-resistant foundations:**  
- Mineral-based foundations (e.g., BareMinerals) are breathable.  
- Maybelline Fit Me Matte+Poreless is affordable and humidity-proof.  
- NARS offers "heat-activated" technology for longer wear.  
- Pair with a silicone-based primer and set with translucent powder or SPF setting spray.  

**Pro tips:**  
- Blot sweat with blotting papers instead of rubbing.  
- Reapply sunscreen under makeup if foundation lacks SPF.  
- For oily skin, avoid coconut oil; for dry skin, choose hydrating but oil-free formulas.
