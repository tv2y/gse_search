Construction site of Chongqing East Railway Station and affiliated comprehensive transportation hub in Chongqing

Updated: December 25, 2024 17:09 Xinhua

 This photo taken on Dec. 23, 2024 shows a partial view of the construction site of Chongqing East Railway Station and affiliated comprehensive transportation hub in southwest China's Chongqing.The construction of the entire project, designed as an interchange point among several railway arteries across southwest China, is underway and progressing smoothly at present, set to be accomplished and operational in 2025.
