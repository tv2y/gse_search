Tennessee man arrested after officers find 14 IEDs inside home: Sheriff
=======================================================================

Authorities allege the suspect tried to detonate the devices during the arrest.

By Jack Date and Ivan Pereira

August 2, 2025, 11:09 PM

*     

0:28

Tennessee man charged with threatening to kill public officials Kevin Wade O’Neal is facing attempted murder and weapons charges after deputies say they found 14 homemade bombs in his house.

A Tennessee man was taken into custody Friday after authorities allege he threatened to kill public officials and law enforcement and was then discovered with 14 improvised explosive devices in his home when investigators arrived to arrest him.

Kevin Wade O'Neal, 54, was arrested at a residence in Old Fort, about 40 miles east of Chattanooga, near the Georgia border, according to the Polk County Sheriff's Office. It was there that authorities allege they found the IEDs, including one that was "smoldering" in the bedroom, according to investigators.

The sheriff's office said investigators believe O'Neal attempted to detonate the devices when officers arrived at the home.

The Polk County, Tennessee, Sheriff's Office said the man attempted to detonate some of the devices when they showed up to his home to arrest him.

Courtesy Polk County, Tennessee, Sheriff's Office

O'Neal has been charged with several counts of attempted murder, as well as charges related to prohibited weapons and explosives.

A photo of some of the devices the Polk County Sheriff's Office said they found during a search of Kevin Wade O'Neal's home.
