Living History Weekends

It’s been a lively summer at the New-York Historical Society. New and exciting groups of Revolutionary War reenactors have been bringing history to life in the galleries and on the front steps for history enthusiasts of all ages every weekend! Stop by before our Living History Weekends come to an end on Sunday, August 30.

While you’re here, don’t miss your chance to snap a photo with a few smartly-dressed 18th-century American patriots. Check out some photos from earlier this summer below!

The young and young at heart striking poses on the steps of the N-YHS.

A member of the 3rd NJ Greys regaling passersby with a heroic tale!

Getting up close and personal with an 18th-century drummer boy!

The Ministers of Apollo marching up Central Park West.

All dressed up in 18th-century finery in front of N-YHS’ very own Beekman coach!
