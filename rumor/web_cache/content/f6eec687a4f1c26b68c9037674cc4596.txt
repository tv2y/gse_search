Advising the White House: Dr. Silverberg on Expanding IVF Access
================================================================

Dr. Kaylen Silverberg, a fertility specialist with Texas Fertility Center and chair of the Medical Advisory Board for Americans for IVF, is among a select group of experts advising the White House and the Domestic Policy Council as they prepare a highly anticipated report on in vitro fertilization (IVF). This follows a February executive order signaling the administration’s commitment to expanding access to fertility care.

In an interview with KTRK Channel 13 in Houston, Dr. Silverberg explains what’s at stake, the conversations he’s had with top officials and how the administration could take meaningful action.
