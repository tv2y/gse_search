Title: China's Ge wins women's 100m gold at Hangzhou Asiad

URL Source: https://english.news.cn/20230930/031ca65ea6df48cf9a9d64a66aee0753/c.html

Markdown Content:
Ge Manqi of China celebrates after the Women's 100m Final of Athletics at the 19th Asian Games in Hangzhou, east China's Zhejiang Province, Sept. 30, 2023. (Xinhua/Jiang Han)

HANGZHOU, Sept. 30 (Xinhua) -- Chinese sprinter Ge Manqi claimed the women's 100m title at the Hangzhou Asian Games on Saturday.

Ge clocked a winning time of 11.23 seconds. Veronica Shanti Pereira of Singapore settled for the silver with 0.04 seconds behind, while Hajar Saad Saeed Saad Alkhaldi of Bahrain took the bronze in 11.35. ■
