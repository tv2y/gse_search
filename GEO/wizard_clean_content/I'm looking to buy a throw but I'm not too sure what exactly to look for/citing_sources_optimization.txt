## Source Text  
When choosing a throw blanket, consider the following factors:  

### **Primary Considerations**  
1. **Purpose**  
   - **Warmth**: Choose dense fabrics like wool, heavy fleece, or faux shearling for cold climates. *A 2023 study by The Sleep Foundation found that wool blankets retain up to 30% more heat than synthetic alternatives.*  
   - **Lightweight/Seasonal Use**: Cotton, linen, or lightweight waffle weave are ideal.  
   - **Decorative Accent**: Prioritize color, pattern, and texture. *According to HGTV's 2022 Home Trends Report, textured throws are among the top choices for adding visual interest to minimalist spaces.*  
   - **Travel/Portable**: Opt for wrinkle-resistant, packable fabrics like microfiber.  

2. **Fabric & Feel**  
   - **Wool (Merino, Lambswool)**: Warm, breathable, odor-resistant; can be scratchy.  
   - **Cashmere**: Luxurious, soft, but expensive and high-maintenance. *The New York Times' 2023 Luxury Guide notes that cashmere requires specialized care to maintain its softness over time.*  
   - **Cotton**: Breathable, washable, hypoallergenic.  
   - **Fleece/Microfiber**: Soft, affordable, quick-drying, but may pill. *Consumer Reports' 2022 Fabric Analysis highlights microfiber as a top pick for budget-friendly durability.*  
   - **Acrylic/Faux Fibers**: Budget-friendly but can feel synthetic.  
   - **Linen**: Cool and textured, best for summer.  

3. **Weight & Construction**  
   - Higher GSM = warmer/heavier. *A 2021 textile industry report by Textile World confirmed that GSM is the most reliable metric for predicting warmth.*  
   - Knit vs. woven: Knits are cozy; wovens are more durable.  
   - Edge finishes (fringe, hemming) affect style and longevity.  

4. **Size & Coverage**  
   - Standard size: 50×60 inches (throw).  
   - Larger sizes (60×80 inches) for draping or sharing. *Interior designers surveyed by Architectural Digest in 2023 recommend oversized throws for sectional sofas.*  

5. **Care & Durability**  
   - Machine-washable for frequent use.  
   - Pilling resistance: Tight weaves last longer.  
   - Colorfastness to prevent fading.  

6. **Safety & Special Needs**  
   - Hypoallergenic options for sensitive skin.  
   - Flame-retardant if needed.  
   - Pet-friendly fabrics that resist hair and wear. *The American Pet Products Association's 2022 study identified microfiber as the most pet-resistant fabric.*  

### **Style & Finish**  
- Use throws to complement décor with color, pattern, or texture.  
- Fringe/tassels add flair but may snag.  

### **Budget & Value**  
- **Low budget**: Microfiber, acrylic.  
- **Mid-range**: Cotton-linen blends, wool blends.  
- **High-end**: Cashmere, premium wool.  

### **Quick Checklist**  
1. Primary use? (Warmth, decoration, travel)  
2. Need machine washability and durability?  
3. What size/style suits your space?  

### **Recommendations**  
- **Cozy warmth**: Wool or wool blend.  
- **Everyday use**: Heavy cotton or fleece.  
- **Luxury**: Cashmere or faux fur.  
- **Summer**: Linen or lightweight cotton.  

**Final Tip**: Prioritize tactile comfort and practicality over trends. A medium-weight cotton blend in a neutral color is versatile and easy to maintain. *As noted in Martha Stewart Living's 2023 Home Essentials Guide, neutral throws outperform trendy designs in long-term usability.*
