**SITDRONE – International Drone Technology Exhibition – November 17 to 19, 2022**

 The International Drone Technology Exhibition – SITDRONE , is an event organized by the PERU AIR FORCE and its first edition will be held from November 17 to 19, 2022 . This event brings together companies and professionals immersed in the development of Drones and unmanned vehicle systems for various applications; to manufacturers of sensors and modern processing software, in order to publicize new trends and discover innovative projects in this innovative field.
