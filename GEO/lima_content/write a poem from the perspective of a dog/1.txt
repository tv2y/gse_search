_By: Grace Robbins_

April 5th, 2020

Let me start this post out by saying “ugh.” Most of my time spent in quarantine has been plagued by tedious amounts of homework, and my nights by even more homework. My sleep schedule is completely messed up, and I seem to not be getting enough sleep no matter how long I stay in bed.

I would love to say there is an optimistic look at our current situation, but there is not, and that is bumming me out.

Suki ponders how to take over the world while all these pesky humans are about.

Anyway here is a poem.

**A POEM FROM MY DOG’S PERSPECTIVE**

I have been receiving more love and attention as of late,

My people are home more almost all the time so I have not chased the car in awhile.

I miss it.

Furthermore, I have no time to work on my schemes.

How am I, Suki Rose Robbins, going to take over the farm,

And in time the world?
