### @mg-7047

4 months ago

I'm watching this video again because I'm thinking of buying glasses online. My main hesitation is not being able to try on the frames. I live out in the sticks and find returning things a waste time and money for everyone involved. One of the important measurements to buying frames that fit is the Width of the front of the frame and that measurement isn't always available even on manufacturer's websites (perhaps they will make it available once they accept that more people are buying glasses online). Zenni uses the distance between the hinge screws to determine whether one's face is a Small, Medium, Large, or Extra Large. Meanwhile, when available, manufacturers simply list the overall width. I'm marking on a paper the distance between the hinge screws, and separately, the overall width of the front of the frame (as best I can without my glasses on) and then putting my glasses back on and measuring and noting the distances to compare with the online offerings. Still a gamble, but perhaps a smaller one. Thanks again for the video!
