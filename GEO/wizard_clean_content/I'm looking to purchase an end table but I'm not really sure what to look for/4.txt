**Rules for Picking an End Table:**  
1. **Size**: Keep the table height roughly in line with the arm of your sofa or chair. Avoid tables deeper than the sofa/chair.  
2. **Materials**: Mix materials (e.g., wood coffee table paired with metal/cement end table) for texture.  
3. **Visual Weight**: Balance chunky sofas with leggy tables; use drum tables for open accent chairs.  
4. **Family Ties**: Ensure living room pieces share a common thread (color, era, or detail) but aren’t matchy-matchy.  

**Styling Formulas:**  
- **Small Tables**: Book + Natural element (plant, flowers) + Floor lamp. Keep styling minimal.  
- **Larger Tables**: Book + Natural element + Framed art + Table lamp. Adjust lamp placement based on visual weight (heavy lamps near sofa, lightweight lamps on outer edge).  
- **Double Tables**: Use two end tables as a coffee table alternative for flexibility and affordability.  

**Key Tips:**  
- Leave 2–3 inches between seat and table for functionality.  
- Avoid overcrowding; prioritize simplicity.  
- Repurpose end tables as nightstands or coffee tables for versatility.  

**Product Roundup Highlights:**  
- **Storage-Friendly**: Tiered tables (e.g., rattan, smoked glass).  
- **Material Variety**: Ceramic, concrete, rattan, and postmodern shapes.  
- **Budget Options**: IKEA’s Gladom (#20) and vintage finds via Craigslist/FB Marketplace.  

**Final Note:** Less is more—focus on books, nature, and lamps for effortless styling.
