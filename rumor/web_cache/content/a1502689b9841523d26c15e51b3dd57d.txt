**Port Charles, New York** is the fictional setting of the ABC soap opera _General Hospital_ and its two spin-offs, _General Hospital: Night Shift_ and _Port Charles_. Its suburb, Queens Point, was the setting for GH's original spin-off, _The Young Marrieds._ Other distinctive locations in and around the city include the Catacombs, the Waterfront, and the Asian Quarter neighborhood. The Pine Barrens, a wooded area, lies just beyond the city limits.

Location[]
----------

_For all locations, please refer to this link: Locations shown on General Hospital_
Port Charles is a fictional mid-sized city located in Western New York, within the imagined boundaries of Erie County. It is believed to lie along the shores of Lake Ontario, positioned between the Buffalo–Niagara Falls region in Canada and Rochester, or possibly just north of Buffalo along the Niagara River. In 1993, producers of _General Hospital_ filmed outdoor scenes in Western New York to represent Port Charles, featuring locations such as Letchworth State Park and the village of Perry, New York. The aerial shots used at the time were actually images of Rochester’s downtown skyline. Executive Producer Wendy Riche explained that Rochester fit the scale they envisioned for Port Charles, and the discovery of local catacombs—also part of Port Charles—sealed the choice. Notably, when Luke and Laura returned to Port Charles, their arrival was highlighted by an iconic scene at Niagara Falls.

In the _General Hospital_ universe, Port Charles exists alongside actual cities like Buffalo, Croton-on-Hudson, Finger Lakes, Niagara Falls, Rockland, Rochester, and Sawyer. While the narrative positions Port Charles in proximity to these locations, it is portrayed as a distinct metropolitan area. In 1996, characters Laura Webber and Stefan Cassadine visited a house in Rochester to search for Laura's mother, Lesley Webber, long thought dead. These episodes implied Port Charles was a brief drive from Rochester, although the exact distance was never specified.

Port Charles, distinct yet in close proximity to both Rochester and Buffalo, is situated along Lake Ontario[1], north of Batavia and east of the small cities of Appleton and Somerset. Comparable locations might include Lakeside or Waterport. This placement positions it approximately six hours by car from the well-known Bensonhurst neighborhood in New York. Llanview and Pine Valley, considered suburbs of Philadelphia, are also about six hours away, with the distance between Bensonhurst and Llanview/Pine Valley being roughly one hour and forty-five minutes. Corinth, Pennsylvania, the setting of the discontinued soap _Loving,_ appeared in a 2013 storyline in which Luke Spencer sought a cure for radiation poisoning. Other fictional locations visited include Beechers Corners, Greenfield, Nixon Falls, Pautauk, and Pentonville.

_**Aurora Media**_

_Main article: Aurora Media_
_Aurora Media_ is one of the local media companies in the city of Port Charles. It is located in the Metro Court Hotel. In 2017, Julian Jerome sells his media company to his daughter Sam McCall and her then-husband Drew Cain. They rename it, and after their divorce Sam steps down, making Drew sole owner. In 2019, Jasper Jacks comes back into town, and Drew sells him 50%, putting him in charge of the Print Division, and essentially, Nina's boss - but he divests when he leaves town. Hayden Barnes briefly served as CFO. In 2020, Sam gives Michael Corinthos her half, making him the CEO of the Digital Division. 

**Bad Jen Café**

_Main article: Bad Jen Café_
A new coffee shop in town. A favorite of WSB Agent Vaughn, it's become popular with the locals. 

**Bobbie's Diner**

_Main article: Bobbie's_
Waterfront diner and boarding house originally owned and operated by Paddy and Rose Kelly (and called "Kelly's"), then by Ruby Anderson. It is currently owned by Ruby's great-niece Carly Spencer - it was renamed in 2024 in honor of the passing of the character Bobbie Spencer and her actress. 

**Brown Dog Bar**

_Main article: Brown Dog Bar_ A new dive bar in town. The bar has proven to already be very popular, and a place where "regular folk" like to drink.

**Café Cherie**

_Main article: Café Cherie_
The Café Cherie is a local cafe in Port Charles, New York. It is first seen in autumn 2023, being enjoyed by Martin Grey and Lucy Coe. 

**Cassadine Industries**

_Main article: Cassadine Industries_
This wealthy, international conglomerate was founded by the Cassadines in the late 70's as a front to cover for their dirty dealings. But the business took off, being controlled by Mikkos Cassadine, Stavros Cassadine, Helena Cassadine, Valentin Cassadine, and Nikolas Cassadine among others. Nikolas was recently in charge of the business. It is planned that when he becomes of age, Spencer Cassadine will take over. Valentin took over in 2016, it was nearly bankrupt thanks to Helena and Victor. Under him it became hugely valuable and profitable, which is when Nikolas took it back in 2020. 

**Corinthos Coffee**

_Main article: Corinthos Coffee_
Corinthos Coffee is one of the local coffee import businesses in the city of Port Charles. It is owned by Sonny Corinthos and Jason Morgan. The coffee imported is served at many local businesses including Perks Coffee. Although many suspect that Corinthos Coffee is a front to smuggle illegal contraband in the coffee bags, it has never been proven. 

**The Courthouse**

_Main article: Port Charles Courthouse_
The Port Charles City/County Courthouse is located in the heart of downtown, apparently very close to the PCPD. It consists of courtrooms and offices, which houses officials such as the District Attorney and Special Prosecutor. 

_**Crimson Magazine**_

_Main article: Crimson Magazine_ _Crimson_ is a fashion magazine owned by Aurora Media, run by Nina Reeves, and founded by the late Connie Falconeri. Jasper Jacks helped Connie (then known as Kate) get the magazine off the ground by becoming a major investor. Kate hired Maxie Jones as her first assistant, and Lulu Spencer as her second assistant. Connie was killed by Ava Jerome in August 2013. Using the alias "Derek Wells", Julian formed a media company that bought _Crimson_. In 2015, he hired Nina Reeves to be the new Editor-in-Chief. In 2017, Julian sells his media company to his daughter Sam McCall and her then-husband Drew Cain. They rename it "Aurora Media", and after their divorce, Sam steps down, making Drew sole owner. In 2019, Jasper Jacks comes back into town, and Drew sells him 50%, putting him in charge of the Print Division, and essentially, Nina's boss. In 2020, Sam gives half of the company to Michael Corinthos, making him in charge of the Digital Division. In 2024, Drew fires Nina after she snitched on him to the feds, and Carly Spencer is very briefly the editor before she quits over Drew's vendetta against Nina.

**Deception Cosmetics**

_Main article: Deception Cosmetics_
_Deception_ is a local cosmetic company in the city of Port Charles. It is located inside the Metro Court Hotel. As of 2023: CEO/51% Partner-Tracy Quartermaine, Minority Partner-Lucy Coe, Models-Blaze and Lois Cerullo, Creative director-Maxie Jones, executive-Brook Lynn Quartermaine. Former: CEO-Laura Collins; Partner-Carly Spencer; models-Brenda Barrett and Gia Campbell. Lucy launched Deception in the 90's with Julia Barrett, Scott Baldwin, and Dominique Stanton. In 2013, Laura and Lucy tried to revive Deception as a health spa and gym, but the venture failed. In 2020, Lucy convinces Sasha Corbin to be the "Face of Deception." They partner to relaunch the "Deception" makeup brand. Maxie joins as does Valentin Cassadine as their new investor, and Brook Lynn Quartermaine as social media management. 

**ELQ International**

_Main article: ELQ International_
ELQ International, is a multinational conglomerate headquartered in Port Charles. Owned by the wealthy Quartermaines, it is one of the largest businesses in the region. From 1978 until his death in 2012, Edward Quartermaine was the sole owner, who named it after himself. Both Nikolas Cassadine and his cousin Valentin Cassadine have staged hostile takeovers. It is a rival company with nearby _Chandler Enterprises_ and _Buchanan Enterprises._

**General Hospital**

_Main article: General Hospital (location)_
General Hospital is a major employer in the city and the primary hospital for most Port Charles residents. The address is 6065 Central Avenue. 

**Greystone Manor**

_Main article: Greystone Manor_ This is the large estate of Carly Spencer and Donna Corinthos and occasionally Avery Jerome-Corinthos in the Forest Hills neighborhood. Not visible from the road, it is guarded by Sonny's men. It has a large terrace, beautiful gardens, and a stately guest house. The large garage stores a Maserati Quattroporte. In 2007, Connie Falconeri built her house right next door to Sonny's home. The address is 120 Shoreline Road.

**Harborview Towers**

_Main article: Harborview Towers_
This is an upper end apartment complex in downtown Port Charles. It has a view of the lake, and can clearly be seen in the Port Charles skyline. It includes Sam McCall's penthouse and was the previous residence of Carly Spencer, Damian Spinelli and currently mob boss Sonny Corinthos. In December 2008, it was mentioned that the Metro Court Hotel is located directly across from the Harborview Towers. The address is 122 Harbor View Drive. 

**Haven Bistro**

_Main article: Haven Bistro_
The Haven Bistro is a popular restaurant and coffee shop located in Port Charles. 

**Home & Heart**

_Main article: Home & Heart_
Home & Heart is a shopping channel that the Deception Cosmetics team appears on often, and their products are best sellers. 

**The Invader**

_Main article: The Invader_
A local media company and newspaper that is currently owned by Valentin Cassadine, who bought it from Shawn Butler through his Cassadine funded non-profit, and previously owned by Peter August. Nina Reeves is the publisher and Adrian DeWitt is the Editor-in-Chief. It was considered a trashy gossip magazine, but has since become respectable due to Shawn and Alexis Davis' efforts. 

**Jerome Gallery**

_Main article: Jerome Gallery_ The Jerome gallery is an art gallery owned by Ava Jerome, with Trina Robinson as her assistant. Ava's brother Julian Jerome was her financial backer. It opened in November 2013, when Ava moved her art dealing business to Port Charles. The gallery also serves as a front and base for the Jerome mob family's illegal dealings. It was built on the former site of Luke's Blues Club.

**Lila's Kids Summer Camp**

_Main article: Lila's Kids Summer Camp_
Lila's Kids Summer Camp is a summer youth camp established in memory of Lila Quartermaine. It has been a regular go-to for Port Charles children in the summer, as well a foundation for Port Charles teens to volunteer as counselors. It is owned by ELQ. 

**Lookout Point/Vista Point**

_Main article: Lookout Point_
The Point, as it's often referred to, is a high vantage point that sits steeply in the woods of Port Charles and includes several bridges. It's been the spot of many outings and events in the past. Many citizens have had accidents or driven their cars off the road and into the lake. Hangman's Bridge, and Roebling Park with Roebling Park Bridge is located in this general area. Lookout Point is located on the same road as Shadybrook Sanitarium, and meets Harbor View Road and Old Bluff Road at a three-way intersection. 

**Metro Court Hotel**

_Main article: Metro Court Hotel_
The Metro Court Hotel is a hotel currently co-owned by Carly Spencer and Olivia Quartermaine, recently by Nina Reeves. It is located at 1420 Quartz Lane, Port Charles, New York. It was built by Jasper Jacks on the former site of the Port Charles Hotel, which was destroyed in a fire in 2004, and named after his then-fiancée, Courtney Matthews. The hotel houses residential suites, businesses such as _Crimson Magazine_ and Deception Cosmetics, and such amenities as a restaurant and rooftop pool. 

**Noodle Buddha**

_Main article: Noodle Buddha_ Noodle Buddha is one of the local Chinese restaurants in the city of Port Charles. It is located in the Asian Quarter Neighborhood. It is Jason Morgan and Sam McCall's favorite Chinese takeout, and where they got married.

**Pentonville Penitentiary**

_Main article: Pentonville Penitentiary_
This is considered the "state" jail where convicted felons go. The real Pentonville Prison has had few references, the most recent being the summer of 2007. Current prisoners are Book, Paul Hornsby, Suzanne Stanwyck, and Johnny Zacchara. 

**Perks Coffee**

_Main article: Perks Coffee_
Perks Coffee is one of the local coffee shops in the city of Port Charles owned by Sonny Corinthos. A coffee kiosk and a coffee shop are located on the Waterfront Promenade. They are managed by Aaron Roland and serve Corinthos Coffee. 

**Pickle-Lila Relish**

_Main article: Pickle-Lila_
Pickle-Lila Relish was created in 1984. After a hostile takeover causes the Quartermaines to lose everything, they move into the apartment above Kelly's, where Lila Quartermaine creates a special pickle relish. They name it "Pickle-Lila" and it helps them get their fortune back. When Edward Quartermaine dies, instead of leaving Tracy money or stock like the rest of the family, he leaves her a jar of Pickle-Lila. The company is part of ELQ. 

**Port Charles Emergency Services**

_Main article: Port Charles Emergency Services_
The Port Charles Emergency Services is the local emergency services. It is frequently called to rescue people after fires or explosions occur in town. 

**Port Charles Grill**

_Main article: Port Charles Grill_
The Port Charles Grill is a popular restaurant which is frequented by the citizens of Port Charles. It was named after a popular restaurant in the 80s and 90s. This former restaurant was located in the Port Charles Hotel which burned down. 

**The PCPD**

_Main article: Port Charles Police Department_
One of the best known organizations in Port Charles is the Port Charles Police Department. It has handled several crimes in the city, but not always well. It has several officers that are informants to the mob, most notably the late Logan Hayes and Cooper Barrett. Current employees include Commissioner Anna Devane, Chief of Detectives Mac Scorpio, Detectives Calvin Bennet, Dante Falconeri and Harrison Chase, and Officer Dex Heller. The address is 1438 Central Avenue. 

**Port Charles Surf Lodge**

_Main article: Port Charles Surf Lodge_
The Port Charles Surf Lodge is a local internet cafe in Port Charles, New York. It is first seen on April 23, 2024, when regular patron Stella Henry meets Trina Robinson and Josslyn Jacks for lunch. 

**Pozzulo's**

_Main article: Pozzulo's_ Owned by mob boss, Sonny Corinthos and former mob boss, Jason Morgan. Pozullo's is an Italian restaurant located on the west end of Port Charles. It was first frequented by members of the mafia in the 1990s, and by local mob boss, Sonny and his then-wife Carly Spencer in the early 2000s. After the former owner died in 2009, Sonny Corinthos purchased the building and opened an office there.

**Quartermaine Mansion**

_Main article: Quartermaine Mansion_
The home of the wealthy Quartermaine family, it also features living quarters for several members of the extended family and staff. In 1977, Alan Quartermaine bought the mansion for his wife, Monica Quartermaine, she often reminds the family its "her" house when they begin quarreling, which is an often occurrence in the Quartermaine quarters. She has been known to throw them out of "her" house as well. Upon her death, Tracy Quartermaine inherited the mansion. There is a gatehouse on the property which has housed many members of the family and their friends. The garage featured luxury vehicles such as a custom vintage Bentley. A rose garden was present in the courtyard which was tended to by Lila Quartermaine until her death. There is also a boathouse and family crypt on the property. The address is 66 Harbor View Road. 

**Rice Park**

_Main article: Rice Park_
Rice Park is a popular hangout spot, though it can be dangerous at times. Kidnappings, murders, and rapes have occurred here. In 2019, to honor the late Oscar Nero-Quartermaine, the Quartermaine family erected a memorial in an area he had liked, naming it "Oscar's Meadow." 

**The Savoy**

_Main article: The Savoy_
The Savoy Nightclub is a popular nightclub and bar owned by Curtis Ashford, located in Port Charles, which makes excellent "mocktails." 

**Shadybrook Sanitarium**

_Main article: Shadybrook Sanitarium_
Shadybrook is a psychiatric facility located in a small country area in Port Charles on the same road as Lookout Point and is the former work place of Lainey Winters and Ewen Keenan. It has been home to many patients, including Laura Collins, Tracy Quartermaine, Carly Spencer, Lulu Spencer, and Connie Falconeri. 

**Volonino's Gym**

_Main article: Volonino's Gym_ Volonino's Gym is a local gym and boxing ring that caters to boxers and fighters in the area. It has a reputation for only catering to the most serious of fighters. The gym is frequented by Sonny Corinthos (also the owner) and his friends and family. It is located at 152 Broadway.

**The Waterfront/Docks**

_Main article: The Waterfront_
The Waterfront district has been under Sonny Corinthos' control for over two decades, resisting takeover attempts by rivals like Lorenzo Alcazar, the Ruiz brothers, among others. The district houses Bobbie's, a local diner, and the Elm St. Pier. The Haunted Star was previously docked nearby. The Waterfront Promenade features the renowned Perks Coffee shop, serving Corinthos Coffee. Close by is the B/N Department Store. Pier 55 provides access to Spoon Island and Wyndemere Castle. The Waterfront also offers a scenic jogging route, including the Bellweather Steps with their stunning harbor views, and hosts the Polar Bear Plunge charity event. There are 57 piers on The Waterfront, which have been the scene of numerous events, including disasters and crimes. 

**WSB (World Security Bureau)**

_Main article: World Security Bureau_
The World Security Bureau (WSB) is an espionage agency its nemesis is the international organization known as the DVX. It is currently run by Frisco Jones, with Jack Brennan as station chief of the local field office. One of the WSB's most daring and effective agents was Robert Scorpio who traveled incognito as an international financier and well-know bon vivant. Other former agents include Victor Cassadine and Sean Donely. They have at least three facilities around the world they use to treat and train their agents. 

**Wyndemere Castle/Spoon Island**

_Main article: Wyndemere Castle_
Former Cassadine family home. The island was leased to the Cassadines long ago by a Native American group. It is home to a grand old mansion called Wyndemere, where many noteworthy residents have resided, currently owned and occupied by the wealthy international warlord Jenz Sidwell. Wyndemere is considered a castle by many, but Nikolas Cassadine is quick to defend that it is a "mansion." Many notable events have occurred on Spoon Island, including the wedding of Lucky Spencer and Elizabeth Webber and the murders of Katherine Bell, Summer Halloway, and Emily Quartermaine. 

**Xtreme Motors**

_Main article: Xtreme Motors_
Xtreme Motors is a garage owned and operated by the late Brando Corbin, given to him by his cousin Sonny Corinthos in 2020. It was once abandoned, then bought by Johnny Zaccahara in 2009. In 2015, Julian Jerome was part owner of the garage. The address is 4278 7th Avenue. 

**Yoga Studio**

_Main article: Yoga Studio_ The local yoga studio is frequented by the citizens of Port Charles, especially in times of personal turmoil, when they are seeking internal peace.

Port Charles society[]
----------------------

**The Nurses' Ball**

_Main article: The Nurses' Ball_
From 1994 - 2001, the Nurses' Ball was the social event of the year in the Port Charles calendar, and is most notable for being a charity event dedicated to raising funds for various HIV/AIDS organizations, both on the show and in reality. The Nurses' Ball was held in the Port Charles Hotel (which burned down in 2004). Notably from 1997 until the last ball in 2001, the Nurses' ball was usually a major crossover where characters from both _Port Charles_ and _General Hospital_ got a chance to interact with one another. A train/bus collision interrupted the 2001 event. In the years that followed as a result of rising expenses and budget cuts in the real world, along with the revamp that was made to _Port Charles_ in 2001, the producers of both _General Hospital_ and _Port Charles_ decided to end the annual event. In 2012, it is explained on screen that The Nurses' Ball was discontinued due to the lack of funding. In 2013, the Nurses' Ball was resurrected again in memory of Dr. Robin Scorpio-Drake, who was believed to have died the previous year. The Ball took place in the first week of April during the 50th anniversary celebration of _General Hospital_ and has since continued once a year.

**General Hospital Pediatric Ward Christmas party**

_Main article: General Hospital (location)#Gallery_
The Pediatric Ward Christmas party has long been a hallmark of _General Hospital_ episodes aired during the holiday season, typically on December 24, or occasionally on the 23rd or 22nd if the 24th falls on a weekend. Traditionally, the chief of staff reads the story of Jesus’ birth to the children, a practice begun by Steve Hardy in the late 1960s. In 1995, the ailing Dr. Hardy could not attend, leaving his son, Tom Hardy, to fill in. Following Dr. Hardy’s death in 1996, Alan Quartermaine assumed the tradition. The chief is accompanied by hospital volunteers dressed as Santa, Mrs. Claus, and elves, with cast members’ children often appearing as patients in the ward. Following Alan’s death in 2007, the party appeared less frequently, returning in 2014 with Liesl Obrecht as chief of staff, who introduced the Krampus story. When Monica Quartermaine later returned as chief, she began the tradition of reading "Twas the Night Before Christmas," a custom continued by Laura Collins and Hamilton Finn in 2023.

### 1963-89[]

*   In **September 1977**, a hurricane devastated the city, resulting in the deaths of Martha Taylor and the wife and child of David Hamilton. Amidst this chaos, teenagers Scott Baldwin and Laura Webber found shelter in a storm shed.
*   In **August 1981**, the town was struck by a blizzard during summer. The blizzard's origin was traced to Mikkos Cassadine's weather machine. Luke Spencer, Robert Scorpio, Laura Webber, and Tiffany Hill were tasked with halting the machine's carbonic snow, which was derived from a formula linked to the world's largest uncut diamond. During a confrontation at his base, Luke and Mikkos scuffled, resulting in the villain's demise in his own ice chamber. With the fate of the world's climate at stake, Luke entered the code "Ice Princess" into the device, thwarting the nefarious scheme.
*   From **September 1985 to November 1985**, a local mob boss named Mr. Wu oppressed his people in a ghetto known as The Asian Quarter Neighborhood. He orchestrated the theft of two jade Buddhas adorned with black pearls from a wise Chinese man, The Ancient One. However, when the statues were recovered, the pearls were missing. Mr. Wu suspected that Robert Scorpio and Anna Devane had taken the pearls while the artifacts were in their custody and commanded his grandson, Kim Wu, to retrieve them. Deeply involved in the family's illicit activities, including a slave market and extortion, Kim complied. Wu's gang, along with Robert, Anna, Frisco Jones, Felicia Jones, Sean Donely, and a rebellious faction called the Green Shirts, were all in pursuit of the elusive black pearls. In this chaotic time, the newly arrived Robin Scorpio overheard Robert and Anna disclose her parentage, prompting her to flee in distress. While Robert believed Wu had abducted Robin, she was actually hiding at General Hospital. In an unexpected turn of events, Robin discovered her doll at GH, which contained the black pearls within its eye sockets. As Wu's control waned, The Ancient One located Robin and briefly reunited her with Anna before she was once again captured by Kim's associates. The Ancient One vowed to recover the pearls for Wu in exchange for Robin's safe return. Ultimately, Kim secured the pearls, but Wu's granddaughter, Jade Soong Chung, intervened. She seized the pearls, shattered a glass window, and scattered them among the people below. In the end, Robert rescued Robin, and Kim was killed by Yank Se Chung.
*   From **December 1985 to June 1986**, the town was shaken by a murder mystery known as "The Brownstone Murders." Bobbie Spencer was horrified to find a man's body at the base of her new Brownstone apartment. The victim, Neil Johnson, had been murdered in the Brownstone, with Terry Brock, found sleepwalking, standing over him with a knife. The murder sent shockwaves through Port Charles, obscuring the identity of the Brownstone killer. Neil was later identified as a cousin of Kevin and Patrick O'Connor, who also lived in the Brownstone. The strangled body of Neil's uncle, Earl Moody, was subsequently found. During a police academy exercise, Frisco Jones and his partner, Samantha Welles, stumbled upon another body, that of Terry's grandmother, Jennifer Talbot. Ultimately, Jake Meyer revealed Kevin as the murderer who had framed Terry, intending to marry and then kill her for her inheritance. In a dramatic confrontation on Catalina Island, Terry pushed Kevin off a cliff, ending his life. However, Jake convinced her to let him assume responsibility.
*   From **June to December 1986**, after dismantling the extortion ring in the city's Asian Quarter Neighborhood, Robert Scorpio departed, and his former wife, Anna Devane, was named the new police commissioner. In an unexpected turn, Police Chief Burt Ramsey, overlooked for the promotion, turned corrupt, joined the Port Charles mob as "Mr. Big," and started "Operation Tumble Dry," a money laundering scheme. Duke Lavery, a mob member unaware of Burt's role, tried to confess to Burt about the scheme. However, Burt attempted to shoot Duke but ended up being shot with his own gun. Duke confessed everything to Robert, who had returned to town. Recovering from his injury, Burt decided to retire as Co-Chief after a year to join the board of the Dockworkers Savings and Loan, a mob front for laundering money. With the help of Anna and Robert, who was deeply disappointed by Burt's betrayal, they enlisted the PCPD command staff and the New York State Police Commissioner in their plan. Following Burt's retirement celebration, Robert confronted him on the docks, where Burt tearfully confessed and became catatonic, leading to his arrest by Robert.
*   In **July 1988**, Lucy Coe and Audrey Hardy organized the Port Charles Arts Festival. Edward Quartermaine, humiliated, was forced to run the grill and serve burgers. The festive mood took a dark turn when the DVX terrorist group, led by Collette Francois, planted a bomb in the main exhibition tent set to go off at 1 p.m. Sean Donely and Robert Scorpio were alerted and swiftly recruited citizens to spread the word and evacuate. Despite ex-Marine Colton Shore discovering the bomb, it exploded, causing numerous casualties at General Hospital.

### 1990-99[]

*   In **May 1990**, a blackout hit the city when Cesar Faison joined together the alien crystals from the planet Lumina. Fortunately, Casey Rogers used his alien abilities to restore power to the city.
*   On **Valentine's Day in 1991**, the Quartermaine vessel S.S. Tracy sank in Port Charles Harbor after being sabotaged by The Greenbelt Environmental Group led by Jenny Eckert. In an extreme protest, she took to a speedboat and executed a stunt that caused the vessel to explode, leading to the release of toxic chemicals and an environmental disaster. This event also signified the arrival of Mac Scorpio in Port Charles. He was forced to jump into the water following the ship's explosion and was subsequently rescued by Police Commissioner Robert Scorpio, his elder brother. Moreover, Bill Eckert came to Port Charles as a crew member on the S.S. Tracy. Post the sinking, Harlan Barrett hired Bill for the salvage operations.
*   In **May 1991**, a catastrophic earthquake rocked Port Charles to its core. Bobbie Spencer's newly renovated brownstone collapsed, trapping Felicia Jones beneath the debris. The Quartermaine Mansion also sustained significant damage. Meanwhile, Robert Scorpio and Anna Devane were rekindling their romance in his penthouse above Delafield's when the initial tremors hit. "The earth really moved," Anna remarked to Robert with a smile, oblivious to the actual cause of the shaking. The building was ultimately condemned, leading to the club's closure. Additionally, Mac Scorpio seized the opportunity to escape from jail. The Asian Quarter Neighborhood reportedly suffered the worst of the devastation.
*   In **May 1994**, Maxie Jones falls ill and is rushed to General Hospital. Simone Hardy diagnoses her with pneumonia, and Monica Quartermaine's heart scan uncovers congestive heart failure. Monica informs Felicia Jones, Maxie's mother, about the need for bypass surgery and possibly a heart transplant if her condition doesn't improve. As Maxie's situation becomes critical, Miguel Morez heroically saves children from a van accident, including B.J. Jones, who is later declared brain dead due to severe head trauma. B.J.'s heart, a match for Maxie's rare P factor, becomes available for transplant. Despite the pain, Tony Jones and Bobbie Spencer consent to the donation. During the transplant surgery, Tony stays by B.J.'s side, and later, Maxie's operation is deemed a success. She will be monitored for organ rejection, and Tony finds solace in hearing B.J.'s heartbeat in Maxie.
*   From **June 1992 to March 1995**, the serial killer Ryan Chamberlain instilled fear in the residents of Port Charles. His atrocious acts included the kidnapping of Maxie Jones, the murder of the District Attorney and several women, including his former wife Gloria Wilson, and vicious attacks on Felicia Jones, as well as Steve and Audrey Hardy in their residence. His twin brother, psychiatrist Kevin Collins, came to town aiming to rehabilitate him. Ryan, however, pretended to recover, assumed Kevin's identity to flee the psychiatric hospital, and interrupted Mac and Felicia Scorpio's wedding with a bomb. Kevin, having proved his identity to the hospital staff, hurried to the church to confront his brother. Despite Ryan's subsequent capture, Kevin disowned him. Yet, Ryan managed to escape again with assistance from his counselor, Connie Cooper, who he eventually killed. Masquerading as Kevin, Ryan abducted Felicia's daughter, Georgie Jones. Mac, Kevin, and Felicia located the deranged twin in a funhouse, saving Georgie. Kevin observed what appeared to be Ryan's demise in an explosion, preferring that fate over returning to institutionalization.
*   From **March to November 1998**, the town of Port Charles was terrorized by the "General Homicide" killer. Kevin Collins authored a fictional murder mystery titled "General Homicide," which quickly became a bestseller. The characters were based on the staff at General Hospital. However, the book's popularity took a dark turn when a crazed killer began mimicking the fictional murders, targeting real nurses and doctors at GH. During this period, several murders were committed, including those of Bennett Devlin, Grace Sullivan, Jake Marshak, John Kanelos, and Cassandra May, with an attempted murder of Matt Harmon. Ultimately, it was disclosed that Julie Morris-Devlin, influenced by Greg Cooper, a vindictive ex-intern at the hospital, was behind these heinous crimes. When Kevin was stabbed, Greg admitted to being the true perpetrator of Bennett's and the other murders, thinking Kevin wouldn't survive. Having fled from a psychiatric facility, Cooper had manipulated Julie into participating in his crimes. _(Note: This storyline played out entirely on Port Charles.)_

### 2000-09[]

*   In **June 2001**, a bus transporting Emily Quartermaine and Zander Smith collided head-on with a train near the city, disrupting The Nurses' Ball, Port Charles' social event of the year.
*   From **May to November 2001**, Helena Cassadine set the stage for a final showdown with the Spencer family in a plot dubbed "Endgame." She used the "Ice Princess" to control and brainwash Lucky Spencer. She forced Lucky to infect Lucas Jones with a biotoxin, then bargained with Lucas' father, Dr. Tony Jones, promising a cure in exchange for his help in reviving her son. Confused and desperate, Tony helped resurrect the cryogenically frozen Stavros Cassadine, concealed in a secret lab under General Hospital. In the end, chaos erupted, and a battle against Helena's forces ensued leading to a showdown between Luke and Stavros, ending with Stavros falling into an abyss.
*   In **February 2004**, a fire ignited in the Port Charles Hotel due to overloaded wiring in the boiler room, exacerbated by the actions of the fugitive Zander Smith. The fire rapidly spread, trapping many residents who were in the Versailles Room. Psychiatrist Cameron Lewis perished in the fire, and his son Zander was believed to have died as well. Brian Beck was shot and presumed dead within the inferno. Jasper Jacks, the industrious Australian billionaire, escaped from his penthouse by rappelling down, while others were rescued via helicopter from the roof. In a dramatic turn, rogue cop Andy Capelli commandeered the last helicopter, leading Nikolas Cassadine to heroically remain behind and was thought to have died when the roof caved in. Miraculously, Nikolas survived and found refuge in Wyndemere. The hotel was later reconstructed by Jasper Jacks, who paid tribute to his then-wife, Courtney Matthews, by renaming it the Metro Court. Courtney has since passed away.
*   In **August 2004**, many people were trapped at the Quartermaine Mansion with a serial killer at large. The murderer turned out to be the unhinged war widow Mary Bishop, who stalked Emily Quartermaine during a blackout on the estate. She murdered Sage Alcazar, who had been confined in a basement freezer by her adversary Georgie Jones, and the unfortunate Trent Parker, who merely obstructed her path. Ultimately, Mary was shot by Nikolas Cassadine, who bore a striking resemblance to her late husband, Connor Bishop.
*   In **July 2005**, another hurricane hit the city of Port Charles, leaving several residents stranded at the Alcazar Mansion. However, the fatalities and damage were minimal compared to the hurricane of 1977.
*   In **November 2005**, a Manhattan-bound train with Lucky Spencer and Elizabeth Webber's wedding party aboard derailed near the Glencoe tunnel after the villainous Manny Ruiz shot the engineer of an oncoming Port Charles-bound train. The collision of the two trains and subsequent tunnel collapse caused severe injuries to the newlywed Lucky, resulted in the death of FBI agent Reese Marshall, and coincided with the birth of Ric and Alexis' daughter, Molly Lansing-Davis. The crisis necessitated a truce as allies and adversaries alike collaborated both within the tunnel and beyond.
*   In **August 2006**, another blackout swept through Port Charles. As a result, Alexis Davis, Sonny Corinthos and Kristina Corinthos-Davis were stuck in the Metro Court restaurant.
*   In **February 2007**, the Metro Court Hotel's lobby was taken hostage by Jerry Jacks and eventually, destroyed by an explosion. This hostage situation lasted for 12 hours. Alan Quartermaine died of a heart attack and Robin Scorpio, Mateo Ruiz and Max Giambetti were shot.
*   In **October 2007**, Nikolas Cassadine decided to host a Black and White Ball at Wyndemere Castle to honor an endowment given to General Hospital in Alan Quartermaine's name. Nikolas also announced his engagement to Emily Quartermaine. However, a storm trapped everyone on the island, and disaster struck with Anthony Zacchara's attack. It was later uncovered that Diego Alcazar, the Text Message Killer, was also present, responsible for strangling and killing Emily, attacking Nikolas, and nearly ending Carly Spencer's life.
*   From **late 2007 to early 2008**, Diego Alcazar, known as the Text Message Killer, terrorized and claimed the lives of several Port Charles residents. Carly Spencer, Sam McCall, Maxie Jones, Jason Morgan, Lucky Spencer, Max Giambetti, Alexis Davis, Nikolas Cassadine, and Nadine Crowell were among those attacked. His string of murders included Cooper Barrett, Emily Quartermaine, Georgie Jones, and Leticia Juarez, Michael Corinthos's nanny.
*   In **March 2008**, an abandoned cannery warehouse located on the main docks was blown up by a series of bombs that were planted by the late Diego Alcazar. Carly's unborn baby died.
*   Between **2009 and 2011**, the artist and serial killer Franco terrorized the town of Port Charles. He orchestrated bombings involving Sam McCall and Lulu Spencer, abducted Aiden Spencer, and led Sam to believe she had been raped. At his "Francophrenia" art show, he committed murder. Subsequently, he arranged for a prisoner, to assault Michael Corinthos and detonated a bomb at Brenda Barrett's wedding to Sonny. Although Jason Morgan shot Franco, which led to the assumption of his death, Franco made a surprising return, alive and well, three years later.

### 2010-2019[]

*   In **January 2011**, a bus headed to Cedar Mountain for a ski trip was involved in a crash that injured Olivia Quartermaine, Matt Hunter, and Morgan Corinthos, and led to Kristina Corinthos-Davis suffering from hypothermia. The bus driver passed away at the scene, and Kristina's friend, Ali, later died from her injuries in the hospital.
*   In **March of 2011**, Jake Webber was struck by a car in a hit-and-run incident and was pronounced brain dead. Carly Spencer, desperate for a kidney transplant to save her daughter Josslyn Jacks, pleaded with Jason Morgan, Jake's biological father, and his parents Lucky Spencer and Elizabeth Webber, who agreed to donate Jake's kidney. It was later discovered that Jake's adoptive grandfather, Luke Spencer, had been the one to hit him while driving under the influence, leading to the tragic event. Years later, it was uncovered that Helena Cassadine had secretly revived Jake and tried to brainwash him. Josslyn's father, Jasper Jacks, admitted he bought a kidney for her on the black market and, while he was unaware Jake was alive, he knew the kidney could not have come from him.
*   In **October 2011**, Lisa Niles emerged from her coma and unleashed chaos in Port Charles. She first induced a coma in a nurse at General Hospital, then drugged Johnny Zacchara and stabbed Anthony Zacchara. During a party on the Haunted Star, hosted by Matt Hunter, she murdered the captain, drugged the First Mate, and threw an intoxicated Elizabeth Webber overboard. She also drugged Patrick Drake and Robin Scorpio-Drake, attempting to infect Patrick with HIV, only to be thwarted by Robin. The altercation ended with a fight between Lisa and Robin on the deck. Amidst these events, Olivia Quartermaine fainted. Two weeks later, Lisa's body was discovered in the harbor, with Matt revealed as her killer.
*   In **June 2012**, Téa Delgado and Sam McCall both gave birth to their sons during a torrential rainstorm.Téa's son wasn't breathing, and her ex-husband, Todd Manning, went looking for help. He brings the baby to Heather Webber, who tries to save him, but the baby dies. Meanwhile, Sam comes to the same area, looking for help, and ends up passing out after putting her son down. Todd & Heather then switched the babies, giving Sam's breathing son to Téa, and leaving Téa's stillborn son in his place. Téa names "her son" Victor Lord III, while Sam buries "her son" as Jason Morgan Jr. Four months later, Jason Morgan figured out the switch, and told John McBain. However, Heather had kidnapped baby Victor from Téa, wanting to raise a son by herself. Baby Victor was rescued from Heather, and returned to Jason & Sam,who renamed him Danny Morgan.
*   From **late August to early September 2012**, Jerry Jacks introduced a deadly pathogen into the water supply, causing widespread illness, except for Alexis Davis and Josslyn Jacks, who were inoculated against it. Jerry coerced Ewen Keenan and Joe Scully, Jr. into assisting him and deceived Todd Manning into selling his cable TV franchise to broadcast his demands. Overwhelmed by his involvement, Ewen abducted Elizabeth Webber. Jason Morgan rescued Elizabeth, resulting in a shootout that left Ewen dead. Jerry also abducted Alexis, leading to a standoff with Jasper Jacks, Johnny Zacchara, and Carly Spencer, during which Johnny was shot. Additionally, the Quartermaine's longtime cook, Virginia Cook, succumbed to the pathogen.
*   On **October 22, 2012**, Bernie Abrahms was waiting at Pier 52 for Jason Morgan. Joe Scully Jr. arrives and shoots Bernie. Before Joe can finish him off, Jason confronts Joe. Joe attempts to shoot Jason, but Jason fatally shoots Joe first. Jason then approaches Bernie, who tries to speak about the money, but before he can, Cesar Faison—disguised as Duke Lavery—shoots Jason in the back and kicks his unconscious body into the harbor, leading to Jason being presumed dead. Faison then threatens Joe, notices Bernie is still alive, and shoots him. Joe flees towards the Quartermaine Mansion, where he eventually succumbs to his gunshot wound. Meanwhile, at Pier 52, the police, paramedics, and Sonny Corinthos arrive. Sonny finds Bernie, who informs him that Joe shot him and that Jason was shot and thrown into the harbor. Sonny dives into the water to search for Jason but to no avail. Returning to Bernie, Sonny hears him try to speak about something, but Bernie dies before he can finish. This incident marks the beginning of the Jason Morgan Conspiracy.
*   In **February 2013**, a serial killer named Caleb Morley, who resembles John McBain, returns to Port Charles and begins a violent rampage. This continues the narrative started on the spinoff _Port Charles,_ in which several vampires had overrun the city. Ultimately, it is disclosed that Caleb is actually Stephen Clay, an escaped mental patient and former musician with a vampire-themed act. Overwhelmed by grief after his wife Livvie Locke's death, Caleb begins to believe he is a vampire, simulating bites with prosthetic teeth and scissors. Convicted of seven murders, he becomes obsessed with reuniting with his deceased wife Livvie Locke, who looks like Sam Morgan.
*   From **January to March 2017**, Olivia Jerome returned and wreaked havoc in Port Charles. Sam McCall eventually discovered that Olivia was responsible for Morgan Corinthos's death. After a confrontation, Olivia pushed Sam off a bridge. In a deranged scheme to bring Duke Lavery back to life, Olivia kidnapped Robin Scorpio-Drake and Griffin Munro. Griffin deceived her into believing he was possessed by Duke's spirit, which allowed him to overpower her, but she escaped when Anna Devane arrived and got hurt. Olivia then broke into her sister Ava's apartment, held her hostage, and forced her to summon Julian Jerome. Using this as a diversion, she abducted Alexis and waited with her at the bridge for Julian. When Julian arrived, a struggle led to him falling off the bridge, although he survived. Later, trying to flee town, Olivia went to the cemetery to retrieve her grave plaque and encountered Carly Spencer. A struggle ensued, and Olivia was shot. Carly was about to kill Olivia when Sonny Corinthos intervened. The police were called, and Olivia was eventually committed to a mental asylum.
*   In **March 2018**, an earthquake struck Port Charles thanks to the construction team of Jim Harvey. Mayor Ned Quartermaine swiftly coordinated a rescue effort as many residents were trapped across different locations. Franco Baldwin and Jim were caught at General Hospital, while Carly Spencer and Nelle Benson were trapped at the Jerome Gallery. Meanwhile, Drew Cain, Kim Nero, and Lulu Spencer were confined at the Floating Rib, which was hosting a school dance for Josslyn Jacks and Oscar Nero-Quartermaine. The establishment was completely destroyed. In the subsequent month, Drew killed Jim in defense of Franco.
*   In **July 2018**, seeking vengeance on the Corinthos family, Nelle Benson persuaded Brad Cooper to switch her living newborn, Jonah Corinthos, with his deceased son, Wiley Cooper-Jones. Nelle arrived in town, blaming Carly for her troubled past and tormenting the Corinthos family. She deceived Sonny into believing they had slept together, attempted to drive Carly insane, and eventually started a relationship with their son Michael, resulting in a baby. Liesl Obrecht confronted Nelle in prison, where Nelle admitted she had coerced Brad into swapping Jonah with Wiley, Brad and Lucas' late adopted son. Jonah was returned to his father Michael on February 19, 2020. Under Sonny's coercion, Brad confessed to his crimes and was incarcerated. Michael then renamed Jonah to Wiley Quartermaine-Corinthos.
*   From **August 2018 to May 2019**, the presumed-dead serial killer Ryan Chamberlain was shockingly found to be alive, having switched places with his twin, Kevin Collins, who was left imprisoned at Ferncliff. Ryan initiated a romantic relationship with Ava Jerome and committed a series of murders: Nurse Mary Pat Ingles on Halloween, Ava's daughter Kiki Jerome on Thanksgiving, and film producer Peyton Mills on New Year's Eve. He also attempted to murder Lulu Spencer and Franco Baldwin. Once Ryan was identified as the perpetrator, he tried to escape with Ava but was shot by Jason Morgan and subsequently jumped off a bridge to avoid capture. Ryan, believed dead for two months, resurfaced by sending Kevin his severed hand, murdering his caretaker April Tremblay, and trapping Curtis Ashford and Laura Collins in an explosive-laden cabin. Although they escaped, Ryan had already returned to Port Charles and suspended Kevin from the Nurses' Ball stage roof after assaulting him and Scott Baldwin. He later confronted Ava, who attempted to kill him, only to be thwarted by Curtis. Despite being stabbed by Ava during his capture, Ryan survived and was taken to General Hospital for emergency surgery. There, Kevin, disguised as Ryan, consented to donate Ryan's kidney to Jordan, with Hamilton Finn, Laura, and Stella Henry aware of the ruse. Afterward, Ryan lapsed into a coma and, upon waking, was incarcerated where he met the notorious Nelle Benson.
*   From **January to December 2019**, Shiloh, Harmony Miller, and their Dawn of Day Cult dominated Port Charles. Kristina Corinthos-Davis and Oscar Nero-Quartermaine joined the group, while Sam McCall pretended to be interested in Shiloh's teachings to gather evidence against him and rescue her sister. Initially, the group was portrayed as a volunteer organization funded by Shiloh's life coaching. He authored a self-help book titled "Every Day is a New Dawn," citing his rescue by former Navy SEAL Chief Andrew Cain as a life-changing event. However, Shiloh was exposed as a sexual predator who branded and drugged his female followers during initiation into his "trust." He harbored grudges against Sam for marrying and divorcing his father for money, and against Willow Tait, Harmony's daughter, who escaped his grasp upon becoming pregnant. Shiloh faced trial for Douglas Miller's murder, extortion, and sexual coercion, with additional charges related to the brain-mapping of Drew and his cousin Franco Baldwin. After escaping custody, he kidnapped Sam and Dev Cerci, leading to his death when Sam shot him with a flare gun. Sam was later tried and acquitted due to the influence of the cult's remnants.

### 2020-Present[]

*   Between **2020 and 2022**, Peter August, infamous Cesar Faison's son, secretly manipulated his close ones. He started by falsely accusing Liesl Obrecht of Drew Cain's supposed death and the attempted murders of Franco Baldwin and Andre Maddox. He convinced Anna Devane that she was his mother and had neglected him, prompting her to try to make amends. Valentin Cassadine and Robert Scorpio, both allies of Anna, became suspicious of Peter, particularly after associating him with Holly Sutton's apparent death. In February 2021, Peter admitted all to Valentin, then destroyed the evidence Anna possessed, revealing his true villainy. The double wedding of "Fanna" and "Paxie" was halted when Valentin exposed Peter's crimes, and Liesl revealed that Anna's twin, Alex Devane, was Peter's real mother. Peter dropped his pretense, leading to Franco's death, Maxie's torment during her pregnancy, meddling with the Chase family's paternity test, and almost killing Harrison Chase with poison. Peter implicated Jason in Franco's murder with Cyrus Renault's help, who wanted to take over Sonny's territory. In a rage, Hamilton Finn, thought to be Chase's father, seemingly killed Peter, who then staged his death and vanished. Peter's return was revealed in Nixon Falls, where Nina deceived amnesiac Sonny, hiding his survival from his family in Port Charles. Peter blackmailed Nina, leading to The Tan-O's destruction and Sonny regaining his memory. Anna and Valentin, with the police and WSB, pursued Peter to the Cassadine Compound in Greece, where Victor Cassadine, alive and Valentin's father, was discovered. There, Valentin joined Drew, who had been held captive for two years. Anna, Robert, Jason, and Britt, Peter's sister, tried to stop Peter. They raided the compound, saved Valentin, and captured Victor, but Peter and a manipulated Drew escaped. Peter took Britt and Liesl hostage on Cassadine Island, where tarot cards were found to control Drew's mind. A subsequent cave in leads to Jason's assumed death. The WSB detained Peter, and in Port Charles, Maxie confronted him, having hidden their daughter as Valentin and Brook Lynn Quartermaine's child, Bailey Lu. Poisoned by the Wu mob family, Peter fell into a coma, was jailed, and later escaped, kidnapping Maxie and Bailey. Maxie convinced him to leave Bailey before fleeing to his Swiss childhood home. Anna and Felicia Scorpio found him; Felicia killed him to save Maxie, and Anna stayed as he died.
*   From **February to October 2021**, Esme Prince, arrived in town and coerced Spencer Cassadine, into terrorizing Ava Jerome, with eerie gifts and messages. This was part of a scheme to sever Ava's relationship with Nikolas Cassadine, following orders from her father, Ryan Chamberlain. Esme's machinations included stalking Ava, manipulating both father and son Cassadine, and concealing the truth that Ryan, feigning his condition, was her father. Eventually, Nikolas, the only one deceived by her, succumbed to temptation and had an slept with her. When it was disclosed that Esme orchestrated the release of a sex tape, she fled and became a fugitive. Returning months later, pregnant with Nikolas' child, she was briefly held captive by him as he suspected her of being "the Hook" killer. After her escape and subsequent memory loss, she gave birth. Trina then persuaded her friends to drop the charges against Esme, arguing that no jury would convict an amnesiac single mother, thereby avoiding the risk of double jeopardy.
*   In **January 2022**, Josslyn Jacks, Cameron Webber, Esme Prince, Spencer Cassadine, and Trina Robinson take a road trip to the Corinthos Cabin before Spencer has to spend a month-long sentence in Spring Ridge Prison. During the trip, Esme drugs Trina's hot toddy with a stolen prescription, and secretly records Cameron and Josslyn's first sexual encounter with a black market phone. She later released the video, wrongfully implicating Trina in the crime. However, Trina's extensive support network, with the exception of Nikolas, believed in her innocence. Between April and August 2022, at Trina's trial, Robert persuaded the court to allow Oz Haggerty to testify. He confirmed selling the black market phone to Esme and not to Trina, leading to the dismissal of the charges against Trina.
*   From **August 2022 to February 2023**, the serial killer known as "The Hook" terrorized the town, claiming the lives of Brando Corbin, Oz Haggerty, Rory Cabrera, and Britt Westbourne, among others. The killer wielded a gaffing hook coated with snake venom. In November, Jordan Ashford informed Josslyn Jacks and Trina Robinson that The Hook was targeting individuals connected to Trina. A clue to the killer's identity was that victims reported hearing the jangle of jewelry, and Diane later disclosed that her assailant was female. Rory managed to snatch a piece of this jewelry just before his demise, and DNA tests revealed it belonged to Gloria Wilson, Ryan Chamberlain's first wife and victim. After receiving letters from the killer, Alexis Davis deduced that Heather Webber was responsible, with the letters being typed at D'archam Asylum. Upon her capture, Heather confessed to stealing Ryan's serial killer souvenirs after they separated when she became pregnant with Esme Prince, and she admitted to attacking those she believed had wronged her daughter.
*   On a chaotic **Valentine's Day of 2023**, the deranged serial killer Ryan Chamberlain reveals he is not incapacitated and claims to be Esme's father. He, along with Heather Webber and Esme, flee, prompting the police to launch a manhunt. The trio heads to Wyndemere, where Ryan is thrilled to encounter his obsessions, Ava and Felicia Scorpio. Heather ensnares Austin, Esme flees to the island grounds, and Ryan pursues her. When he catches up to her, she admits that her water broke and she's in labor. Mac Scorpio goes after them and kills Ryan on the grounds of Spoon Island. Esme gives birth to a boy in the castle, with the help of her uncle Kevin Collins, and the baby's grandmother, Laura.
*   In early **April 2023**, as part of his plan to unite his family by "saving" the world with a deadly pathogen intended to decimate half the population, Victor Cassadine kidnaps his former flame Liesl Obrecht, along with his two great-great nephews, Spencer Cassadine, and Ace Cassadine. They are held captive on the Haunted Star, which sets sail for Greenland. Spencer's girlfriend, Trina Robinson secretly boards the ship and sabotages it, also alerting her mother Portia Robinson, Spencer's grandmother Laura Collins, and her step-father Curtis Ashford, who then set out to rescue them after the WSB declines to help. Once discovered, Liesl prevents them from being thrown overboard by proposing to use them as test subjects for her vaccine. In the lab, Liesl confirms the efficacy of the pathogen and antidote but refuses to test them on the young captives. Valentin allows himself to be captured, and Victor coerces Liesl to experiment on him. Eventually, the heroes thwart Victor's plans and disable the carbonic snow machine. Victor flees back to the Haunted Star with the antigen and a reluctant Liesl. Spencer chases after him, determined to intervene. Liesl manages to steal the antidote from Victor, and Holly delivers it to Valentin and Anna, saving Valentin's life. Spencer incapacitates Victor just as a WSB drone obliterates the Haunted Star. Spencer is found alive the following day, after Victor's body is identified, and the heroes return home.
*   From **October 2024 to February 2025**, Cyrus Renault, recently released from prison, claimed to have reformed and found salvation through God. In reality, he believed himself to be on a divine mission, committing a series of murders at General Hospital and the Turning Woods rehabilitation center, targeting patients in comas. Using the heart medication digitalis, he caused multiple deaths, including that of Sam McCall, who had selflessly donated part of her liver to his niece, Lulu Spencer. Cyrus maintained that Lulu should have been allowed to die, as he had caused her liver failure. Dex Heller also fell victim to Cyrus when, after recovering from surgery, he was stabbed and killed to prevent him from revealing Cyrus’s presence near Sam’s room. Suspicion initially fell on head nurse Elizabeth Baldwin, as all victims had been under her care, though several staff members began noting Cyrus’s suspiciously frequent visits. Eventually, Co-Chief of Staff Dr. Portia Robinson barred him from the hospital, but he managed to reenter and attempted to murder Michael Corinthos with digitalis while Michael was recovering from burn injuries. Elizabeth and Lucky Spencer uncovered Cyrus’s role in the killings. When confronted, Cyrus attacked Elizabeth at her home, only to be stopped by Lucky, whom Cyrus then injected with digitalis. Dr. Isaiah Gannon was ultimately able to stabilize Lucky. Josslyn Jacks also undertakes her own investigation and discovers that Cyrus was responsible for the murder of her boyfriend, Dex. In a confrontation at Cyrus's cabin, she shoots and kills him, bringing an end to his reign of terror.

Trivia[]
--------

*   During _General Hospital's_ first 13 years on air, the setting was never specified outside of being a sizable city in the northeastern US. It was finally identified as Port Charles, New York in 1976.
*   The ZIP code of at least part of Port Charles is 16543. This ZIP code would actually be geographically assigned to Erie, Pennsylvania, not New York.
