Action scenes thrive on high narrative speed, making readers feel immersed in the action with an increased pulse. The tempo of a scene can be varied by adjusting the ratio of narrated time (the time that passes in the scene) to narration time (the time taken to describe the event).  

For example, a five-minute fight can be summarized quickly using time-lapse techniques, such as:  
*"The next five minutes, Jack fought for his life."*  

Slow-motion techniques can expand key moments for greater impact, while fast motion condenses less critical events. These techniques help control pacing and maintain reader engagement in action scenes.
