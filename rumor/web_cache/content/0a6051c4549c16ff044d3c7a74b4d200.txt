Wisconsin DHS confirms 9 cases of measles, overall risk to the public considered low
====================================================================================

**By KSTP**

Updated: August 4, 2025 - 11:49 AM

Published: August 3, 2025 - 4:16 PM 

The Wisconsin Department of Health Services(DHS) has confirmed multiple cases of measles in Oconto County.

The county, located in the eastern part of the state, confirmed nine cases, all stemming from a source of measles during out-of-state travel.

At this time, the Wisconsin DHS said no known public exposure has been identified and said the overall risk of the disease spreading to the public is low.

Measles is a highly contagious disease that can be spread from person to person through the air and can stay in the air for two hours after a sick person coughs or sneezes, according to the DHS. It is so contagious that if one person gets it, up to 90% of the people around them may also become infected if they are not vaccinated.

Additional information on measles can be found on the DHS website.
