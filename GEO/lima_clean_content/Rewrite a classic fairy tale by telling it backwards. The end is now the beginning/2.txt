Once upon a time, there was a princess who loved deeply and married a prince. On their wedding day, the prince kissed her, and she fell into an endless sleep. Fearful, he hid her in a diamond casket in the forest. Seven dwarves offered to help, demanding a price—her freedom. An old woman awoke the princess with an apple, but the prince fell asleep, cursed never to wake near her.  

The princess, now bound to work in the dwarves' mines, forgot her past. Years later, a huntsman found her and took her to a queen who believed the princess resembled her lost daughter. The queen offered her a place in the castle, hinting at a hidden connection between them.
