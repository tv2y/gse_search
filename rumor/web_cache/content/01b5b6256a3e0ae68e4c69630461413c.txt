The 2024 Olympic Games have stirred a major controversy involving transgender athletes. Algerian boxer Imane Khelif, at the centre of the storm, defeated Italian boxer Angela Carini in just 46 seconds, leading to a heated debate. This isn't Imane's first brush with controversy; she was disqualified from the 2023 World Boxing Championship over gender eligibility issues. Despite failing a sex test last year, the Olympic chiefs have stated that "every person has the right to practice sport without discrimination." High-profile figures like Elon Musk, JK Rowling, and Italian PM Giorgia Meloni have voiced their opposition. Join Editor Sandeep Unnithan, former national 100m champion Dutee Chand, and senior sports journalist Rakesh Rao (The Hindu) as they delve into the broader debate on inclusion and eligibility in women's sports.
