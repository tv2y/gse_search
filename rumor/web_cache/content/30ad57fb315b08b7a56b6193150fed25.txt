Title: Chongqing's key projects are in full swing for good start

On January 28, at Xiuhu Road Station of Phase I of Chongqing Urban Rail Express Line 15, China Railway's technical workers were entering the tunnel for construction. (Photographed by Luo Bin / Visual Chongqing)

CHONGQING (CQNEWS) -- Recently, the reporter learned from some districts and counties that just after the Spring Festival holiday, some key projects in our city sounded the "rallying call" for work resumption. With the construction workers returning to their posts in succession, many key construction projects have started to schedule and work hard to catch a "good start" in the first quarter.

**Tuanjiehu PPP Project in Jiangjin Area of Science City:**

**Arranging bus to pick up workers at stations**

On January 28, the first working day after the Spring Festival, was on the construction site of the PPP project of Tuanjiehu Big Data Intelligent Industrial Park (referred to as the "Tuanjiehu PPP project"), the "symphony" composed of the roar of tower crane and the sound of steel bar percussion came one after another.

"In order to ensure that the project is delivered on schedule, we have fully resumed work on the first day after the holiday. At present, more than 100 workers are working hard to complete the task of construction period and efficiently complete each construction period node." Yu Gang, head of the Tuanjiehu PPP project of China Construction Eighth Bureau, introduced.

The Tuanjiehu PPP project is located in the Jiangjin area of the Western (Chongqing) Science City (Shuangfu Industrial Park). It is the first batch of EOD (ecological environment-oriented development model) pilot projects in China. The main construction contents of the project include three categories: houses for production service, public service facilities, and supporting infrastructure. At present, the top of three main structures in intelligent manufacturing Areas A and B has been completed. It is planned to complete all construction work in intelligent manufacturing Areas A and B in December of this year and achieve completion and operation.

In order to promote the resumption of work in a scientific and orderly manner, the resumption plan of Tuanjiehu PPP project had been developed before the Spring Festival, and the management team also arrived in advance to carry out a comprehensive safety inspection on the site. Not only that, the Project Department also arranged special buses to meet workers at the railway station and bus station in combination with the return of personnel from all teams, so that they can put into construction with full spirit once they return to work.

After the completion of the Tuanjiehu PPP project, it will help Tuanjiehu Big Data Intelligent Industrial Park accelerate the construction of the Southern Innovation Center of the Science City, and build the Shuangfu Area into a new engine driving the transformation and upgrading of the manufacturing industry in Jiangjin District, leading the upgrading of the manufacturing industry in Jiangjin.

**Jinyun Mountain Tunnel Project of Chongqing-Kunming High-speed Railway:**

**Set up Party member commandos to ensure production**

At 8:00 on January 29, the construction site of the Jinyun Mountain Tunnel of the Chongqing-Kunming High-speed Railway in Bishan District was bustling with machines and vehicles.

Yu Yanxin, a 22-year-old technician of China Railway 11th Bureau, and his colleagues are carrying out construction safety inspection. He told the reporter: "On the first day after the holiday, we are ready to resume work and production. The Company and the Project Department carry out key inspections on the arrival of personnel, the availability of resources, and the safety review of the work site, organize special meetings, and arrange the key tasks of resuming work and production. We also set up a party member commando against the node engineering, fight with cards, and start production at full power."

According to Liu Chao, the head of the Chongqing-Kunming High-speed Railway Project Department of China Railway 11th Bureau, the Chongqing-Kunming High-speed Railway is an important part of the Beijing-Kunming Railway, one of the main channels of China's “eight vertical and eight horizontal” high-speed railway, and also a landmark project for the construction of the Chengdu-Chongqing Economic Circle. Jinyun Mountain Tunnel is an important node project of Chongqing-Kunming High-speed Railway. Up to now, nearly 3000 meters of the tunnel has been excavated, and the remaining 500 meters are expected to be completed in the first half of this year.

**North Extension Project of Rail Transit Line 18:**

**241 workers are on duty, and all 11 work sites of the whole line are resumed**

On January 29, the Qixinggang construction site of the North Extension Project of Rail Transit Line 18 was rumbling, and the excavator was carrying out non-explosive excavation. The workers performed their respective duties and carried out the support work orderly by using the work bench and the wet spraying mechanical arm.

The reporter learned that just after the Spring Festival holiday, the North Extension Project of CRT Line 18 quickly switched from "holiday state" to "working state". On January 27, on the sixth day of the first month of the lunar calendar, the General Contracting Department of the North Extension Project of China Communications Construction Company Line 18 organized and held a post-holiday resumption promotion meeting to deploy the post-holiday work.

Up to now, 241 workers have arrived at work posts in four sections of the North Extension Project of China Communications Construction Company Line 18, with the arrival rate of 100%, and 11 work sites along the line have been fully resumed.

The North Extension Project of Rail Transit Line 18 is located in Yuzhong District and Jiulongpo District. The line starts from Xiaoshizi Station in the east and ends at Fuhua Road Station (not included) of Line 18 Project (from Fuhua Road to Tiaodengnan) in the west, with a total length of 10.61 km and 8 stations. After the completion of the project, the rail transit network in Yuzhong District will be further densified, traffic congestion in Hualongqiao, Daping, Caiyuanba and other areas will be alleviated, so that the regional economic and social development will be promoted.
