### Bulgaria siezes 10,000 fake foreign passport

Source:Xinhua Published: 2014-8-26 8:14:05

* * *

Bulgarian Ministry of Interior has seized components that were intended to be 10,000 fake foreign passports destined for dispatch abroad, an official said Monday.

Nine suspects were arrested and two printing houses in Plovdiv was neutralized in connection to the illegal activity on Saturday, Svetlozar Lazarov, secretary general of the Bulgarian Ministry of Interior, told a press conference.

Using these false passports, foreigners posing as refugees could arrive in Bulgaria, according to Lazarov. "The operation has prevented illegal activity that brings great risk to our national security," he said.

Recently, migratory pressure on Bulgarian borders has grown 3.6 times compared to last year, Lazarov added.
