### RAI Amsterdam to Host Europe’s Premier Industrial Drone Event on April 22-23, 2026

The European drone market is experiencing remarkable growth, fueled by increasing adoption of uncrewed aerial vehicles (UAVs) across industries such as construction, energy, agriculture, and public safety. To meet this demand, Diversified Communications has announced the launch of the _Commercial UAV Forum_, a focused event designed to bring together professionals driving innovation and adoption in industrial drone applications. The forum will take place on April 22-23, 2026, at RAI Amsterdam in the Netherlands.

Building on the success of the _Commercial UAV Expo_, the world’s leading commercial drone trade show and conference held annually in the United States, this new event aims to provide a high-value platform for education, networking, and collaboration tailored specifically to Europe’s unique market needs.

Lee Corkhill, Group Director at Diversified Communications, emphasized the importance of creating opportunities for meaningful connections and actionable insights. “We are thrilled to introduce Commercial UAV Forum as the next expansion of the Commercial UAV brand,” Corkhill said. “This new format emphasizes community, actionable content, and strategic connection—key ingredients to accelerating the safe and effective adoption of drone technology across Europe’s core sectors.”

### A Platform for Innovation and Collaboration

The _Commercial UAV Forum_ will feature a focused exhibit floor showcasing cutting-edge UAV hardware, software, and supporting technologies. Attendees will benefit from solution-focused educational sessions and practical discussions on real-world applications. The event is designed to foster direct engagement with key stakeholders shaping UAV regulation and innovation across Europe.

Professionals from vertical industries such as construction, energy & utilities, public safety & emergency services, infrastructure, agriculture, ports, mining, and drone delivery will find tailored content and networking opportunities to help them thrive in this fast-evolving market.

### Why Attend?

The European drone industry is poised for significant expansion as technological advancements enable new applications across diverse sectors. Events like _Commercial UAV Forum_ provide an ideal opportunity for professionals to meet peers and thought leaders who are shaping the future of industrial drone technology.

As a highly-focused gathering, the forum promises a unique environment for attendees to gain knowledge about emerging trends, discover solutions to industry challenges, and build relationships that drive business success. Whether you are a drone pilot looking to refine your operations or an innovator seeking partnerships for growth, this event offers something for everyone involved in Europe’s dynamic UAV ecosystem.

Details about registration and speaker opportunities will be announced in the coming months. Interested professionals are encouraged to fill out the Attendee Inquiry form on the event website to stay informed about updates.

For more information about exhibiting or attending _Commercial UAV Forum_, visit the official website or contact event organizers directly.
