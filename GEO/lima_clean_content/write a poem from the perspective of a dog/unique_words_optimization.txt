A VERSE FROM MY CANINE VIGIL  

I have been the recipient of augmented devotion in recent days,  
My humans now dwell perpetually within our domestic sphere, thus my vehicular pursuits lie dormant.  
I yearn for their revival.  

Concurrently, my stratagems languish in neglect.  
How shall I, Suki Rose Robbins, execute my agricultural usurpation,  
And subsequently, global dominion?
