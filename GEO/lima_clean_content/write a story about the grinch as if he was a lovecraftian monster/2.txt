Two years ago, a major crime struck Whoville when citizens were robbed of all their presents on Christmas Eve. The Grinch, who was initially blamed, claimed he was framed. According to his account, Cindy Lou Who—later revealed to be Meredith Lucifer Who, a 30-year-old fugitive from a Russian psychiatric ward with a condition that made her appear as a child—was the true culprit. She impersonated the Grinch, stole the presents, and framed him.  

After discovering evidence of her true identity, the Grinch escaped and returned the presents to Whoville. However, Cindy Lou manipulated the situation, making it seem like the Grinch was guilty before fleeing. She was later captured and sentenced to life in a Russian institution. The Grinch disappeared, but every Christmas, the narrator received anonymous gifts, hinting at his survival. The story reveals that appearances can be deceiving, and the truth behind the Grinch’s infamy was far darker than believed.
