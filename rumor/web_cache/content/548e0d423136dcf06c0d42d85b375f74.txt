玄奘三蔵のブロンズ像

Bronze statue of Xuanzang Sanzang in the south square of the Great Wild Geese Pagoda. Xuanzang Sanzang was a high priest of the Tang Dynasty who left the Tang capital of Chang'an at the age of 27 in search of Buddhist scriptures that had not yet been introduced to China at that time and toured the western regions and India for over 17 years, bringing back Buddhist scriptures, sariras and statues of Buddha. He took this picture against the backdrop of the Great Wild Geese Pagoda, which he built to contain Buddhist scriptures and other works. It seems to be a popular spot for commemorative photos.
