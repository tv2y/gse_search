I am Chinese and live in Wuxi, a second-tier city in China. Indeed, China’s real estate sector has experienced a major downturn over the past few years. However, in the past two years, I’ve noticed that many unfinished buildings left behind during the large-scale expansion period of the real estate boom have been repaired and put back into use. It seems that the current strategy is: since new housing projects have been halted, efforts are now being made to complete and restore old unfinished projects.
