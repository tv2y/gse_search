### Who We Are

As Thailand’s leading telecom-tech company, True Corporation empowers people and businesses with connected solutions that advance society sustainably. True’s world-class voice and data services enable a lifestyle ecosystem of global entertainment, exclusive privileges, and seamless connectivity. Through our AI-augmented innovation, we contribute to a more productive, healthier, and safer world.

Strategy

### Our 

Services

True’s digital ecosystem is unrivaled. From mobile services, fiber broadband to smart homes and data solutions for businesses, we help Thai people get more out of life no matter where they connect: at home, at work, or on their travels.

### Our 

Network

True offers strong, fast connectivity throughout Thailand. As the only provider of full service in all eight 5G and 4G bands, we cover more than 99% of the population with tens of thousands of network sites nationwide.
