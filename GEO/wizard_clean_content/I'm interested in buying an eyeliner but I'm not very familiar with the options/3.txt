**Which Eyeliner is Right for You?**  

We offer two eyeliners: liquid and cream. Both wear well, have a 6-month shelf life, and are black, but there are key differences.  

### **LIQUID LINER**  

**PROS:**  
- Long-lasting (all day for most people).  
- Skinny tip for precise wings or clean lines.  
- No brush needed.  
- Highly saturated black with a slight shine.  

**CONS:**  
- Not suitable for a soft look—crisp lines only.  
- Hard to build up for thicker applications.  
- Dries quickly, making mistakes harder to blend or fix.  

Best for sharp, defined eyeliner looks.  

### **CREAM EYELINER**  

**PROS:**  
- Stays all day.  
- Versatile: Can create soft or winged looks.  
- Blends easily (30-second window for adjustments).  
- Can be used on the waterline or tightlined.  

**CONS:**  
- Requires more effort for crisp wings compared to liquid.  
- Needs a brush (a learning curve for some).  

Best for smudged, natural, or smoky looks.  

Both liners serve different purposes, so the choice depends on your desired eyeliner style.
