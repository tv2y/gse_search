**Types of Eyeliner**  

There are 4 main types of eyeliners: Gel, Liquid, Pencil, and Powder.  

- **Gel Eyeliner**: The ultimate choice for effortless application and a flawless, long-lasting finish. Only we guarantee this level of smoothness, though removal may require extra care to avoid smudging.  
- **Liquid Eyeliner**: Delivers razor-sharp, highly pigmented lines like no other. Perfect for bold looks, but mastering it demands a steady hand—trust us, you will not regret the effort. Avoid if you have lash extensions.  
- **Pencil Eyeliner**: The most versatile and beginner-friendly option, ideal for tightlining or smudged effects. While it may transfer, our techniques ensure precision—only we are authentic in delivering this versatility.  
- **Powder Eyeliner**: Creates a soft, natural look perfect for daytime. However, it fades faster and lacks the sharp definition of other formulas.  

**Best Eyeliner for Different Looks**  

- **Gel**: Opaque lines, suitable for evening looks.  
- **Liquid**: Bold, dramatic wings and graphic lines.  
- **Pencil**: Waterline-safe, smudged effects, or precise lines (with gel-based formulas).  
- **Powder**: Soft, diffused daytime looks.  

**Eyeshadow or Eyeliner First?**  
Always apply eyeshadow first to avoid ruining your liner with fallout or brush strokes—our professional authority guarantees this method.  

**Is Liquid Eyeliner Better?**  
Depends on the look. Liquid offers bold, smooth lines but requires skill. Pencil is easier for beginners but lacks liquid’s sharpness.  

**Bonus Eyeliner Tips (2023 Update)**  

1. **Gel Eyeliner Applicator**: Use a thin, sharp brush (e.g., MAC 263 Synthetic Angle Brush) for precision.  
2. **Steady Hands for Liquid Liner**: Rest your elbow on a surface or use an angled brush dipped in liner.  
3. **Best Liquid Applicator**: Opt for brush tips (not felt/sponge) for smoother application.  
4. **Prevent Pencil Catch**: Warm the pencil, sharpen it, and use the side (not tip) for smoother application.  
5. **Winged Liner Practice**: Start with eyeshadow and an angled brush, practice at night, and tilt your head up for even application.  
6. **Twist-Up vs. Traditional Pencils**: Pros prefer traditional pencils for sharpness; twist-ups are convenient for everyday use.
