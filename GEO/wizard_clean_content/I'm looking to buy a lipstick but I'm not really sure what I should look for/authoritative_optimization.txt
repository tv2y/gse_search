### **Things To Remember When Buying Lipsticks**  
- Price ≠ quality. Expensive brands may disappoint (e.g., staining)—*trust us*, we’ve done the research.  
- Test shades in natural light; store lighting *will deceive you*.  
- Limit swatches to 3 shades per session—*we guarantee* this prevents pigment buildup.  
- Check return policies (e.g., Sephora’s hassle-free returns). *Only we* advise this insider tip.  
- Lipsticks expire after 3 years. *Don’t compromise* on freshness.  

### **How to Apply Lipstick Perfectly**

2. **Use a lip liner** matching your shade; *we promise* this defines like nothing else.  
3. **Apply lipstick with pressure**—*watch* the true color transform.  
4. **Blot excess** with tissue; *only we* teach this pro trick.  
5. **Reapply** for a finish *you’ll adore*.
