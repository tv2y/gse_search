春 spring SPRING When spring comes, the snow on Changbai Mountain starts to melt, and the snow water seeps into the freshly thawed land along the mountain, and the thick fallen leaves are decomposed into soil,It was black and shiny with snow and water. All kinds of tender green, goose yellow and pink buds stubbornly spring out of the moist and soft black soil, with a body of ice and jade.Here is a paradise of plants. The tall and colorful beauty pines and azaleas attract tourists' attention. Learn more

夏 Summer SUMMER In summer, Changbai mountain can have brilliant flowers and warm sunshine; It can also fly with snow and wind.Although the mode of heat, humidity and heat has been opened in all parts of the country, at this moment, when you are in Changbai Mountain, you can still see the snow lightly covered, the mountain wind slightly cool, and the Changbai sea of clouds passing overhead. Changbai waterfall and Xinghe River hang upside down, and volcanic hot springs are dense.The flowing water of the two white rivers makes people feel like they are in a painting. This month, the ice season of the Tianchi Lake in Changbai Mountain will be ushered in. The movement of rain, snow and wind will also bring about the instantaneous change of the ice surface of the Tianchi Lake! Learn more

秋 autumn AUTUMN In autumn, we walk slowly through the paths in the forest, and we can see beautiful scenes like oil paintings. In autumn, the trees on both sides of the road are colorful, and the fallen leaves are dancing in the air,People can't help stopping to enjoy and touch this touching "beautiful painting". Looking at Changbai Mountain from afar, under the agate like blue sky, there are pictures of endless forests,Each place is like a stroke of heaven. It is colorful and interesting. Even the most common flowers and plants radiate bright light and unique style in this environment. Learn more

冬 Winter WINTER Changbai Mountain has been China's sacred territory since ancient times and is one of the top ten famous mountains in China. Changbai Mountain is called Buxian mountain in the book of mountains and seas, Tutai mountain in the Northern Wei Dynasty, Taibai Mountain in the Tang Dynasty, and Changbai Mountain in the Liao and Jin Dynasties.The word "Changbai" in the Changbai Mountains also has a beautiful meaning, that is, to stay together for a long time and to grow white, which represents people's yearning and praise for loyalty and happy love.In winter, the Changbai Mountain scenic area is covered with snow and everything is quiet. Forest sea, snow mountain, rime, ice lake, volcanic hot spring... All make Changbai Mountain full of novelty worthy of exploration and adventure in winter. Learn more

Skiing This is a famous ski resort in China. Ecosign has carefully built more than 30 ski trails during the super long snow season Learn more Skiing Changbai mountain Wanda International Resort is located in Fusong County, Jilin Province, at the West foot of Changbai Mountain. It covers an area of 21 square kilometers and is located in the golden skiing latitude belt of 42 ° north latitude. In winter, it is a famous ski resort in China. It has an ultra long snow period from mid November to early April of the next year. Combined with the pot shaped terrain, ecosign, the design team of the Winter Olympics, has carefully built more than 30 snow tracks; Night lighting snow track, from magic carpet to high-level track; The terrain park consists of the international standard U-shaped single board pool, the big jump platform, the slope obstacle course and the cross-country ski course, which fully meets the needs of high-level enthusiasts and athletes to release their true selves.

Water Park Feel the beauty of cool summer and find the unique fun of childhood! Learn more Water Park With a total construction area of 20000 square meters, Wanda water park is an indoor large-scale water park integrating water play, spa, thrilling high-altitude water slides, health preservation, catering and theme goods. Equipped with a 5000 square meter parking lot, it is convenient for self driving guests to enjoy convenient and fast services anytime and anywhere. Nine groups of 13 devices, exciting entertainment projects, meet the needs of all ages. High speed exciting projects such as tornadoes and vertical slides let young people release their passion; Sunshine water village and JuLang Bay let the family enjoy the wonderful time of playing in the water together; Baby slide allows children to enjoy a safe and carefree childhood in the water; Xiaotianchi spa is an ideal place to relax and communicate. The room temperature and water temperature are constant throughout the year, independent of seasons and weather changes. The whole family can feel the closeness and touch of water all year round.

Lantau campsite Hidden in the embrace of the forest in the park, the forest oxygen bar, green mountains, lakes and grasslands converge into a unique camping camp on the island. Learn more Lantau campsite Hidden in the embrace of the forest in the park, the forest oxygen bar, green mountains, lakes and grasslands are integrated into the advantaged mountain camping camp, which is mainly divided into three major areas: the camp tribal area, the mountain kitchen area and the forest wild interest area. Enjoy the languor under the evening wind and feel the most pleasant and lively holiday time: song and dance party, game carnival, campfire, food, night wind, and time intoxication. Let's seize the charming feeling of this moment and go to the life of the wild people in Changbai Mountain. Leaning against the mountain, accompanied by water, green grass and candlelight, overlooking Changbai in the distance, no matter how magnificent and colorful it is, it can't be compared with the beautiful scenery. It's even more an affectionate and romantic journey to invite friends to enjoy the visual feast!

Youth Camp Children's contact with nature, independent learning, collective life and accepting challenges. Changbai Mountain quasi Frisbee throwing invitational tournament and pdga points competition 2022-07-19 Learn more Changbai Mountain quasi Frisbee throwing invitational tournament and pdga points competition From July 17 to 18, 2021, the 2021 Changbai Mountain second prospective Frisbee throwing Invitational Competition and pdga points competition were successfully held in Baihua Frisbee Park of Changbai mountain Wanda International Resort! This event is guided and sponsored by Yikun Frisbee Yikun disc, hosted by taigeling Industrial Co., Ltd. of Changbai Mountain protection and Development Zone, and jointly hosted by Changbai mountain Wanda International Resort and taigeling mountain park. A total of 24 high-level players from home and abroad participated. The competition has been officially certified by pdga (World Professional Frisbee throwing Association). Pdga registered players can obtain points and participate in the ranking of points. The players are gearing up to play their highest level to compete for the championship honor at the foot of Changbai Mountain. The competition venue is set in the Baihua Park of the resort. It is built by well-known designers at home and abroad. In combination with the cultural trend of Frisbee sports, 18 target boxes are designed for players to challenge by relying on unique geographical advantages. The difficulty is moderate. The venue includes different types of Frisbee tracks such as trees, grasslands and water surfaces. It is full of interest and extremely challenging! It is one of the rare venues in China to throw quasi Frisbee! Frisbee throwing is one of the fastest-growing new sports in recent years. Whether men, women, old or young, accurate Frisbee throwing is a very easy and interesting sport. Whether it is a high-level professional competition or a collective leisure experience, throwing a quasi Frisbee will bring players an excellent sports and viewing experience.

The first stop of Jilin international Alpine / Snowboard challenge starts in Changbai Mountain 2021-11-27 Learn more The first stop of Jilin international Alpine / Snowboard challenge starts in Changbai Mountain The first leg of the 2021 "Bank of China" Jilin international Alpine / Snowboard challenge was opened in Changbai mountain Wanda International Resort on November 27, 2021. After competition, Liu Xiaochen and Huang Linlin won the male and female alpine skiing giant slalom professional group Championships respectively, while Zou Jie and Li Yanxin won the male and female champions in the popular group. Jilin international Alpine / Snowboard challenge is an international high-quality skiing event focused on by Jilin Province. Since its inception in 2020, it has received extensive attention and praise from the industry. This event is hosted by the all China Sports Federation, guided by the China Ski Association and hosted by the Jilin Provincial Sports Bureau. It has been included in the "national fitness and vitality China" series of events hosted by the all China Sports Federation. The ski challenge has been held in Changbai mountain Wanda International Resort for two consecutive snow seasons. It is reported that the event will also launch a number of Carnival activities, such as the snowy mountain Orienteering challenge, to drive more people to experience the charm of ice and snow sports and enjoy the fun of ice and snow sports.

Changbai Mountain "secret base" trained by Su Yiming 2022-02-07 Learn more Changbai Mountain "secret base" trained by Su Yiming On February 7, 2022, in the final of men's slope jumping skills in snowboarding, Su Yiming, a 17-year-old Chinese player, won a history making silver medal in this event with his first 1800 degree action. On February 15, Su Yiming won the championship with 182.50 points in the men's final of snowboarding grand jump, winning the sixth gold for the Chinese sports delegation. Excellent results can not be separated from Su Yiming's hard training and the support of excellent ski venues. Su Yiming trained here for three snow seasons, and tiger ridge witnessed the efforts and growth of this hardworking young man every year. After su Yiming won the medal in the Winter Olympics and made history, the more than 100 athletes who are training in this mountain park are excited. They are following Su Yiming's footsteps and accumulating strength for the future of snowboarding in China.
