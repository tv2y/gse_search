China Flood 2025: China’s northwestern Gansu province is reeling from deadly flash floods that have killed at least 13 people and left 30 missing. Nearly 10,000 residents have been relocated, and more than 4,000 were stranded as torrential rain battered the mountainous Yuzhong county. State broadcaster CCTV showed images of destroyed homes, collapsed roads, and ankle-high muddy water sweeping through villages. In some areas, rainfall reached 195 mm in less than 24 hours — more than half the region’s annual average. The loose loess soil, common to this plateau, makes it especially vulnerable to floods and landslides. President Xi Jinping has ordered “all-out” rescue efforts, urging local authorities to prioritise saving lives, resettling evacuees, and restoring transport and communications. Xi also warned officials against “complacency” as extreme weather events linked to climate change become more frequent. Since late July, floods have killed more than 60 people across northern China, including Beijing.
