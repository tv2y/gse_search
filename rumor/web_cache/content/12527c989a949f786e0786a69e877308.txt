**January**7-10**Consumer Electronics Show (CES) 2025 - Las Vegas, United States**

CES is the world's leading technology exhibition where drone manufacturers unveil their latest innovations. This flagship event brings together industry giants, emerging startups, and technology enthusiasts to showcase cutting-edge drone technology, autonomous systems, and next-generation aviation solutions through demonstrations, product launches, and interactive experiences.
January 22-23**Military Aviation & Air Dominance Summit - Huntsville, United States**

The 7th Annual Military Aviation & Air Dominance Summit unites senior military officials, defense experts, and industry leaders to address critical developments in military aviation. This strategic conference focuses on emerging technologies, operational requirements, and collaborative initiatives essential for maintaining air superiority and advancing military aircraft capabilities.
January 24-26****FLIGHT: Drone SkyShow & Night Markets - Canberra, Australia****

FLIGHT transforms Canberra's night sky into a canvas of light with spectacular drone performances featuring 600 synchronized drones. This innovative event combines cutting-edge drone technology with community celebration, offering visitors an immersive experience of aerial artistry alongside vibrant night markets showcasing local cuisine and craftsmanship.
January 27-28****Maritime Reconnaissance and Surveillance Technology 2025 - London, United Kingdom****

The 10th Maritime Reconnaissance and Surveillance Technology Conference serves as Europe's premier forum for maritime intelligence and surveillance advancement. This specialized event facilitates crucial discussions between military personnel, industry experts, and technology innovators, focusing on enhancing maritime security and surveillance capabilities.
January 28-30****WEST 2025 - San Diego, United States****

WEST 2025 stands as the West Coast's foremost maritime technology exhibition, jointly organized by AFCEA International and the U.S. Naval Institute. This comprehensive event connects military decision-makers with industry innovators to explore current challenges and future opportunities in maritime operations technology, fostering collaboration and advancement in naval capabilities.
