French Minister of Culture Rachida Dati Announced a National Trust “à la française”
===================================================================================

Heritage

**As part of President Macron’s State visit, French Minister of Culture Rachida Dati announced the project of a National Trust “_à la française_” based on a recent report by the Centre des Monuments Nationaux and inspired by the National Trust.**

She attended a roundtable discussion this morning at Kenwood House with **Sir Chris Bryant**, Minister of State at the Department for Science, Innovation and Technology and the Department for Culture, Media and Sport, **René Olivieri**, Chair of the National Trust for England, **Philip Long**, Chief Executive of the National Trust for Scotland, **Lord Lemos**, Chair of the English Heritage Board of Trustees, **Marie Lavandier**, President of the Centre des Monuments Nationaux, **Philippe Bélaval**, Former President of the Centre des Monuments Nationaux, **Laurence des Cars**, President of the Musée du Louvre, **Christophe Leribault**, President of the Etablissement public du Château de Versailles, **Anissia Morel**, Cultural Counsellor of the French Embassy and director of the Institut français du Royaume-Uni and **Anne Duncan**, British Council Director for France.

The discussion was followed by the signing of a Memorandum of Understanding on Heritage between France and the UK.
