IVF coverage 

For Plan Year 2025, the following twenty-five (25) FEHB plans, offering a total of forty-five (45) options, will cover in vitro fertilization (IVF) services. Seven of the plans are new for 2025. All FEHB carriers are required to cover three cycles of IVF-related drugs.
