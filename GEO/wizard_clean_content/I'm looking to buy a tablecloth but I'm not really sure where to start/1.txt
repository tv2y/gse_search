Here is the main useful information extracted from the web page about buying tablecloths:

- One person bought a vintage embroidered tablecloth at a yard sale, which had sentimental value as it was made by their aunt.  

- Saffron Marigold is recommended for elegant, handmade, fair-trade tablecloths with intricate block-printed designs from Rajasthan, India.  

- Event rental companies can be a source for high-quality table linens, including custom sizes for large tables (e.g., seating 10).  

- Practical tips for buying tablecloths:  
  - Measure the table for sizing.  
  - Consider decor, stain resistance, and washability.  
  - Look for sales at stores like Amazon, Kohl’s, Home Goods, or TJ Maxx.  

- Sustainable options like Faz (upcycled materials) are highlighted for eco-friendly choices.  

- Fabric recommendations:  
  - Polyester blends for easy care and stain resistance.  
  - Cotton or linen for elegance but requiring more maintenance (e.g., ironing).  
  - Clear vinyl as a protective layer over wood tables for messy activities.  

- Seasonal tablecloths (e.g., red/white for Christmas) enhance holiday decor and guest experiences.  

- For small rooms, lightweight patterns help maintain an airy feel.  

- Additional sources for quality linens include Sanctuary Living, Fabindia, and Nicobar.  

- A plastic-coated tablecloth is useful for messy gatherings like barbecues.  

The focus is on practical buying advice, material recommendations, and sentimental or sustainable options.
