**The Fire That Burned The Exhibition Room Of The National Museum Of Extinguished**
===================================================================================

16 September 2023, 22:19 | Équipe éditoriale

*    Rizky Adytia Pramana Reporter  
*    Dony Aprian Editor  

Fire engulfed the National Museum. (Photo: Doc. Antara)

Partager:

The fire fighting team succeeded in 'jinning' the flames that had burned the exhibition room of the National Museum or Elephant Museum, Central Jakarta. Currently, the cooling process is being carried out.

"At 21.37 WIB, the cooling phase was declared," as quoted from the IG Humasjakfire account, Saturday, September 16.

However, it is not yet known about the condition of the archaeological collection, anthropology, ethnography, geography, and history of the Indonesian nation, which is in the exhibition space.

Head of the DKI Jakarta Fire and Rescue Service (Gulkarmat), Satriadi Gunawan, who was questioned about this matter, has not responded.
