*   ### How to choose between Hefei Ancient Xiaoyaojin and Baogong Park?

On the last day of November, enjoy a leisurely and relaxed one-day tour in Hefei☺️: Morning - Baogong Park, Noon - Huaihe Road Pedestrian Street - Mingjiao Temple - Li Hongzhang Former Residence, Afternoon - Ancient Xiaoyaojin Baogong Park has a wonderful environment, with mirror-like lake water and several small islands extending into the lake, topped with pavilions and towers. The trees by the lake are half full of autumn colors and half reluctant to let go of summer. It feels like when spring and clear scenery arrive, there will be an atmosphere of willows waving and orioles singing. Any friends from Hefei want to share their thoughts? The ginkgo trees by the Baogong Shrine lean against the white walls, casting mottled shadows of trees and time🍁. On the small island in the lake, you can sit under the trees drinking coffee or tea, watching boats on the lake—so pleasant. Baogong Park also includes Baogong’s tomb, Baogong Shrine, and exhibitions about the life story of this upright official, which deepens respect for him after visiting. Ancient Xiaoyaojin is also built around a lake. At the park entrance is Hefei’s version of Phoenix Tree Avenue🍁. In the middle stands a statue of Zhang Liao, a famous general from the Three Kingdoms period. Further inside are Zhang Liao’s tomb, Xiaoyao Pavilion (a newly built ancient-style building), a children’s playground, and more. At first, I was drawn by the name Ancient Xiaoyaojin, most wanting to visit Xiaoyaojin. It feels like a place that should appear in Jin Yong or Gu Long’s martial arts novels. Money-saving tip: Flying from Hefei to Guangzhou is much cheaper than from Nanjing to Guangzhou. In the morning, I bought a cup of hot fermented rice wine from a roadside “old brand” stall, which was also very tasty. Many beverage shops offer drinks with fermented rice wine. I wonder why~ One regret is that I didn’t get to try Hefei’s stinky mandarin fish, not sure if it’s good or not.

*   ### Hefei Xiaoyaojin Red Maple Scenery

In autumn and winter, Hefei Xiaoyaojin Park is adorned with red maple leaves, creating a poetic and picturesque scene. As the cold wind blows, the bright red maple leaves gently fall, making you feel as if you are in an oil painting world—an excellent spot for photos and check-ins. Xiaoyaojin Park is located in the northeast corner of Hefei's old city, with a long history. It was once a private garden for bureaucratic landlords and has now become a comprehensive park with beautiful surroundings and pleasant scenery. The park is divided into east and west sections by a water system. The East Park is rich in entertainment facilities, suitable for family outings; although the zoo in the southeast corner has been relocated, it still holds the charm of the past. The northeast corner features Quzhuang and a boat dock, where visitors can enjoy tea and lake tours, fully embracing leisure time. The West Park focuses on botanical landscaping, with scenic spots like Plum Blossom Hill and the Peony Garden that captivate visitors. Strolling through the park, the exotic flowers and plants, rockeries, pavilions, and corridors create a delightful and relaxing atmosphere. Near Zhang Liao’s tomb, Luju opera enthusiasts often gather to sing, their voices carrying far. Following the sound, you might even learn a few lines of Luju opera. Come to Xiaoyaojin Park to experience this unique autumn and winter romance and create unforgettable memories!
