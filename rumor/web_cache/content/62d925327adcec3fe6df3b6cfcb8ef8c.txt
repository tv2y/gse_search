**SUSHMA WALKER RECEIVES 2025 MARYEMILY SLATE****TRAILBLAZER AWARD FOR WOMEN IN STEEL**

**PITTSBURGH, 23 September 2025**— Sushma Walker, president of business technology at Nucor Corp., received the **MaryEmily Slate Trailblazer Award for Women in Steel** at the **AIST Women in Steel Conference** in Indianapolis, Ind., USA. 

Walker was presented with the award on Tuesday, 23 September, in recognition of her embodiment of the values of hard work, perseverance, vulnerability and strength, even in the face of challenges. She has shown that setbacks are not barriers, but opportunities for growth and resilience. She leads by example, inspiring others to rise above rejection, and to push forward with renewed determination. Walker is dedicated to lifting others as she climbs, ensuring that her success benefits those around her. She represents the very values this honor was created to celebrate.
