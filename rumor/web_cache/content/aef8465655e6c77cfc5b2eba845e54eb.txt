**Australians Spend Over 5 Hours a Day on Their Phones – Here's What It’s Doing to Your Eyes**

Recent data shows that the **average smartphone user spends 3 to 5 hours a day on their device**, and in Australia, it’s closer to **5.5 hours daily**. For Gen Z users, screen time can soar past **7 hours a day**, thanks to social media, gaming, video streaming, and texting.

While our phones and tablets help us stay connected, productive, and entertained, there's a hidden cost: **eye health**. Extended exposure to the blue light emitted by your **iPhone or iPad** can lead to digital eye strain and other vision-related issues. But there's a simple way to protect yourself — an **anti-blue light screen protector**.

* * *

### What Is Blue Light and Why Is It Harmful?

**Blue light** is a type of high-energy visible (HEV) light that comes from both natural and artificial sources — including your mobile phone, tablet, computer, and TV screens. Overexposure to blue light, especially at night, can lead to:

*   **Digital eye strain**

*   **Dry or irritated eyes**

*   **Blurry vision**

*   **Headaches**

*   **Sleep disruption** (by reducing melatonin production)

For kids and young adults, whose eyes are still developing, the risks may be even greater.

* * *

### How Anti-Blue Light Screen Protectors Help

An **anti-blue light screen protector** is a transparent film or glass that fits over your device’s screen and filters out a portion of harmful blue light — all while maintaining screen clarity and responsiveness. These protectors are available for **iPhones and iPads**, and they offer a range of benefits:

✅ **Reduces eye fatigue** during long hours of use

✅ **Improves sleep** by limiting blue light exposure at night

✅ **Protects your screen** from scratches, smudges, and dust

✅ **Preserves screen brightness and touch sensitivity**

It's a small upgrade that can make a big difference in your comfort and well-being — especially if you’re using your phone or tablet for work, study, or relaxation every day.

* * *

### Protect Your Eyes Today 👇

Your eyes work hard every day — give them the protection they deserve. Whether you’re a student, a parent, or a busy professional, an **anti-blue light screen protector** is a smart, affordable upgrade that supports your digital lifestyle.
