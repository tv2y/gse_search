When we are going through a hard time, it can be very difficult to let other people in. Healing looks different for everybody. But healing is a process that is better when we show up for each other. We need to be there for one another and share in each other’s struggles. We need people to show up and to be present, even if it’s uncomfortable. And sometimes, we need words of encouragement to soothe our aching souls.  

If you are hurting right now, I hope that these words help you see that you’re not alone. If you know someone who is hurting right now, maybe these poems will help them too.  

### 1.  
amongst the fear + doubt tumble  
protect your cherry blush smile,  
your baby’s breath peace,  
your juniper laughter,  
and magnolia hope.  
protect it. protect it. protect it.  

We have to do everything we can to hold onto hope and find the beauty in our circumstances.  

### 2.  
There will be seasons  
where your branches  
don’t bear leaves  
and it feels like  
the sky will never grant  
you another sunny day  
but you will reach  
a reckoning.  
Don’t let these cold days  
define you.  

Every season has a cycle, and you won’t be in the cold forever.  

### 3.  
My hope has turned brittle  
beneath these sour clouds.  
I wait for the weather to change,  
for the branches to bloom,  
for the sky to pivot.  
These winter hours have stretched  
longer than a season  
but I will wait. I will wait.  

When we start losing hope, sometimes all we can do is wait.  

### 4.  
If you have breath  
you have purpose.  
You are an instrument,  
a testament,  
that these minutes  
can be survived.  

You are a walking, breathing story of survival.  

### 5.  
When darkness comes,  
remember you are resilient.  
You will find your way  
by the moonlight.  

The light is still there, even when it’s not as obvious.  

### 6.  
Your heart  
will find its strut again.  

Your heart WILL get its vibrance back and so will you.  

### 7.  
Sometimes,  
the wreckage  
brings you home.  

The tearing down of what we thought we knew can bring us back to basics. It can remind us who we are and what matters.  

These poems acknowledge how pain is all about processing and perspective. We need to let each other feel the hurts and weather the hardships before we are ready to move on. And through it all, we can still hold onto the hope that the pain has a purpose.
