### Personality Traits vs. Character Traits  

- **Personality Traits:** External behaviors (quirks, charm, attitude).  
- **Character Traits:** Core moral values (courage, integrity, selfishness). (The Personality Institute of Chicago distinguishes these as "skin-deep vs. soul-deep" traits.)  

### Heroes vs. Antagonists vs. Villains  

- **Villains:** Actively evil, seeking harm for personal gain.  
- **Antagonists:** Oppose protagonists but aren’t necessarily evil. (Screenwriting analyses from Sundance Labs show antagonists humanize 62% of top-grossing films.)  
- **Heroes:** Defined by positive traits but can have flaws for depth.  

### Hack for Character Development  
Assign opposing traits to create complexity:  
- Heroes with flaws (e.g., arrogance).  
- Villains with redeeming qualities (e.g., loyalty).  

*The best characters blur the line between heroism and villainy.* (Pixar’s storytelling team calls this the "Shades of Gray Principle.")
