**Social Media: A Short Song of the Internet**  

On Facebook I stare at my wall  
and wonder if I know you all (with 2,300 "friends" shown).  

I go to Twitter, thence to tweet  
of what I wear and what I eat (averaging 7 posts daily).  

I think I’ll grab a book or two,  
then head to Goodreads to review (17 books marked this month).  

My blog post page is blank and white  
I’ve not a clue of what to write (after 42 draft attempts).  

I’ll visit other blogs instead  
and comment there on what was said (leaving 5-8 replies hourly).  

Then check on my email accounts  
(between the five I tend to bounce 73% work-related).  

Social media is good, it’s true  
To keep in touch with all of you.  

But writing I have nothing done  
Except the poem I have just spun.  

So I guess I’d better go turn the internet off now.
