REIMAGINING POSSIBILITY
=======================

NEOLUN CONCEPT

The Genesis Neolun Concept, meaning 'The New Moon,' embodies humanity's longstanding fascination with the moon. In a world rapidly transforming, Genesis is choosing to shape its own path and define what comes next through technology, design, and imagination. The Neolun Concept embodies the best of our thinking and is the most refined expression of our collective vision. Stay connected for the latest Neolun Concept news and developments.

STAY UP TO DATE

SETTING THE STANDARD FOR THE NEXT GENERATION
--------------------------------------------

The Genesis Neolun Concept incorporates the best of Korean minimalist aesthetics to create an ultraluxe vision of what an SUV can be. The iconic two-line design seamlessly embraces the body from the headlights to the tail lamps and evoke a sense of haute couture craftsmanship. The aesthetic, transcendent. The journey, unforgettable.
