4 Reasons to Choose Cerefolin Brain Wellness™
---------------------------------------------

### Neurologist Recommended*

This dual-action targeted nutrition product helps lower homocysteine levels.1-2 Your body needs this amino acid, but high levels of homocysteine increase risk of MCI.

### Slows Brain Shrinkage

In a clinical study, patients with high homocysteine levels taking Cerefolin Brain Wellness™* showed an 11.2 times slower rate of brain atrophy compared to those not taking it.1

### Long-Term Results

Cerefolin Brain Wellness™* is backed by an MRI study and up to 7 years of clinical data from the same patients, showing long-term use may help slow cognitive decline and maintain essential brain function.1, 3

### Clinically Tested Ingredients

High-quality ingredients have been clinically tested to ensure safety and effectiveness of our product.

Brain Boosting Antioxidant Power
--------------------------------

### Active Folate

L-Methylfolate is the active form of folate that supports memory and focus-related neurotransmitter production, while improving brain metabolism.

### Vitamin B12

Methylcobalamin (vitamin B12) boosts energy production to support overall brain function.

### NAC

N-Acetyl-l-cysteine (NAC) contributes to brain health by reducing oxidative stress.
