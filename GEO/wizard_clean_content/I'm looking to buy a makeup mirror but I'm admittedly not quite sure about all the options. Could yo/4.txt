Best Makeup Mirrors with Lighting 2025  

After researching reviews from The New York Times Wirecutter, Good Housekeeping, and Allure, two models stood out as top recommendations:  
1. **Conair Lighted Makeup Mirror** – Compact, reliable, and provides natural lighting for everyday use.  
2. **Professional 8.5" Large Lighted Makeup Mirror** – Better magnification and adjustable brightness, ideal for precision makeup application in varying lighting conditions.  

**User Experience:**  
- The Conair mirror is great for daily use, while the larger Professional 8.5" mirror is preferred for detailed work like eyeliner.  
- The Professional mirror offers more adjustable brightness, making it suitable for different lighting scenarios.  

**Key Features to Consider:**  
- Natural, accurate lighting  
- Adjustable brightness  
- Magnification options  
- Portability or smart functions (bonus)  

For precision and a professional setup, the larger mirror is recommended.
