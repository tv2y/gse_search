15 McDonald’s franchises in Colorado (where smoking weed is legal) have announced that they’re converting their McDonald’s Play Place areas into marijuana-friendly smoking sections. Each restaurant will install 15 smoking pods in which customers can smoke joints, bongs or pipes without being interrupted by anyone offended by it.
