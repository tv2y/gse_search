China Earthquake FAQ
--------------------

### +How frequent are earthquakes in China?

China has a moderate level of seismic activity. Based on data from the past 25 years and our earthquake archive back to 1900, there are about 3,200 quakes on average per year in China. However, China has had at least 63 quakes above magnitude 7 since 1900, which suggests that larger earthquakes of this size occur infrequently, probably on average approximately every 1 to 5 years.

### +How many earthquakes occur every year in China?

On average, China has about 2,500 quakes of magnitude 3 or higher per year (Mag 4+: 977 quakes per year, Mag 5+: 38.7 quakes per year, Mag 6+: 3.9 quakes per year).

### +Which areas of China have the most earthquakes?

Southern and southwestern China are more prone to earthquakes than the rest of the country, because these areas are affected by the collision zone between the Indian subcontinent with Asia. 

The parts that have most and China's largest quakes include Sichuan Province, Tibet, and Yunnan Province.

### +How many earthquakes happened in China?

Since 1900, China has had more than 800 larger earthquakes with magnitudes of 6 or higher. 

China is one of the countries in the world that has most quakes. This is not only because of its sheer size, but due to its location at the continent-continent collision boundary between the Indian subcontinent and the Asian plate.
