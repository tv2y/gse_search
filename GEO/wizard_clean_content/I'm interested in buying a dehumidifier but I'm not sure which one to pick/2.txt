### **Key Information from the Article: Best Dehumidifiers (2024)**  

#### **General Insights on Dehumidifiers**  
- Most dehumidifiers are produced in a few factories, even if sold under different brand names.  
- **Recalls**:  
  - **2021**: New Widetech recalled 2 million units (Honeywell, Danby, De’Longhi, Ivation, Idylis) due to fire hazards.  
  - **2023**: Gree recalled 1.56 million dehumidifiers (Frigidaire, GE, Kenmore).  

#### **Buying Guidance**  
**DO:**  
- Choose the **largest capacity** you can accommodate to minimize frequent tank emptying.  
- Opt for a **gravity hose option** for "set and forget" convenience.  
- Be skeptical of recommendations from large publications (they often don’t test products).  

**DON’T:**  
- Buy **mini dehumidifiers** (e.g., Pro Breeze Mini)—they’re underpowered.  
- Get models with **internal pumps**—they break more often.
