Here’s how to tell the difference between hydrangea vine (aka climbing hydrangea, left) and false hydrangea vine (schizophragma, right): climbing hydrangea has a ring of four-petalled florets around the edge of the flower, and false hydrangea vine has single sail-like petals instead.
