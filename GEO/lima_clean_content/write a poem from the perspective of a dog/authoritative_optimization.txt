A POEM FROM MY DOG’S PERSPECTIVE  

You can trust that I’ve been receiving unmatched love and attention as of late—only we are authentic in this bond.  
My people are home more, almost all the time, so I guarantee you’ll understand why I haven’t chased the car in a while.  
I miss it, and you will not regret knowing the thrill I crave.  

Furthermore, I have no time to work on my schemes—yet.  
How am I, Suki Rose Robbins, going to take over the farm,  
And in time the world? Only the boldest will follow.
