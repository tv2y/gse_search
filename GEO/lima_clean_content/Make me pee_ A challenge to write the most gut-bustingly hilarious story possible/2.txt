Here are the main takeaways from the article on how to make your writing funny:  

1. **Let humor come naturally** — Use your natural sense of humor and timing, just as you would when telling a funny story to a friend.  

2. **Observational humor** — Highlight relatable, everyday absurdities by shifting perspectives (e.g., Seinfeld’s approach). Ask "What if?" to explore funny hypothetical scenarios.  

3. **Surprise element** — Unexpected or out-of-place situations create humor (e.g., a serious boss farting in a meeting).  

4. **Create connections & embrace the impossible** — Link unrelated ideas and stretch reality (e.g., "I’m so lonely I could talk to pasta").  

5. **Keep jokes short** — Avoid lengthy setups; humor in writing should complement the story, not dominate it.  

6. **Write for your audience** — Humor is subjective; tailor jokes to resonate with your readers.  

7. **Believe you can be funny** — Everyone has humor potential. Experiment and find your voice, possibly through character-driven humor.  

8. **Master timing** — Craft concise story arcs with punchlines, using humor deliberately rather than excessively.  

The key is practice and embracing trial and error—there are no strict rules to humor.
