Asian Athletics Championships 2025: Puripol Boonson coasts to win men's 100m heats
==================================================================================

The 19-year-old Thai sprinter leads all semi-finalists with a casual 10.22 in Gumi, Republic of Korea.

Picture by Reuters

By Shintaro Kano 27 May 2025 07:13 GMT+0 1 min read

Thailand’s **Puripol Boonson** breezed to the top of the men’s 100m heats at the **Asian Athletics Championships****2025** in Gumi, Republic of Korea, on Tuesday (27 May).

The 19-year-old **Paris 2024 Olympian** eased up before the wire but still managed a 10.22 to lead the 24 qualifiers for the semi-finals on Wednesday evening, when the final will also be held.

Trailing Boonson, the under-20 world silver medallist, was **Yanagita Hiroki** of Japan (10.25), who held off former world champion **Christian Coleman** to win the 100m at last week’s Golden Grand Prix in Tokyo in 10.06.

Third-fastest was also Japanese, **Higashida Akihiro,** who clocked 10.29 behind Boonson in the third group out of five. **Abdullah Abkar M. Mohammed** of Saudi Arabia was fourth, a mere .002 behind Higashida.

**Seo Minjun** was tops for the host country, qualifying sixth overall for the three semi-final groups in 10.34.
