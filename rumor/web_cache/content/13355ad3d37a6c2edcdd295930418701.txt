First cases of measles reported in Wisconsin this year
======================================================

Published

3 months ago
on

August 3, 2025

By

Kevin Millard

Nine cases of measles are confirmed by the state Department of Health Services and Oconto County Public Health.

The county is located north of Green Bay.

While one case was confirmed at the State Laboratory of Hygiene with the eight other cases confirmed based on exposure and symptoms.

All the cases were exposed to a common source during out-of-state travel according to DHS.

They’re working with Oconto County in identifying and notifying anyone who may have been exposed to the virus.

A statement from DHS said the risk to the community is low as no public points of exposure have been identified.

Measles can spread to people through the air and can stay in the air for up to two hours after an infected person coughs or sneezes.

Nine out of 10 people around an can also be infected if they are around an infected person unless they’re vaccinated.

Measles can cause serious health complications, including pneumonia, brain damage, and deafness, and can sometimes be deadly. DHS states that one in four people who get measles in the United States will be hospitalized.
