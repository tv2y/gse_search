For Kalos, I would have had Serena either be a Sky Battler or take on the Battle Chateau instead of being a Performer, with me leaning towards Sky Battles since it would, in Serena's mind, be a repudiation of Rhyhorn Racing. In addition, the reason she would hate Rhyhorn Racing so much is because during a race when she was young, Grace's Rhyhorn got startled during a race and collided with her father. Her father survived but was put into a coma for months before miraculously waking up. Even so, though, he had a long road to recovery and Serena never wanted anything to do with Rhyhorn racing after that.

 If she were a Sky Battler, I would have a competition where one would take on a Squad Leader, the Sky Battler equivalent to a Gym Leader. She would need to get 5 Wings by beating five Squad Leaders in order to become an Ace. She would then enter a tournament and battle other Aces and the victor would become a Wing Commander. Serena would eventually take on the strongest Wing Commander called a Sky Marshal and either win or tie. This would take place just before the Kalos League for Ash.

 If she went and did the Battle Chateau, though, it would be a lot like the games, only there would be a tournament at the end between the Dukes/Duchesses, Grand Dukes/Grand Duchesses to become the Chateau King or Queen, with the current queen being the Kalos Region Champion Diantha.

 As for her Pokémon, she would start with Fletchling and get Fennekin in order to get Delphox, whose psychic abilities would allow her to ride her broomstick and participate in Sky Battles. She would also get a Charmander, which would evolve into Charizard, which would be able to Mega Evolve into Mega Charizard X. She would also get some other Pokémon, particularly a Water-type like Gyarados for dealing with Ground and Rock-types and a Flygon do deal with with Electric-types.

 Personality-wise, Serena would still have her crush on Ash but it would not just be her main thing like it seemed to be in the animé. She would also be very invested in her chosen goal, especially if it was Sky Battling for aforementioned reasons. It would also take her longer to reconcile with Grace since the reason for her hating Rhyhorn Racing, and being forced into it before leaving on her journey, were due to the injuries her father suffered. Also, since Serena's now a battler of some sort, regardless of which of the two goals above she chose, Ash would also begin to notice her attraction towards him and take second looks at her, too.

 As for Ash, he would have the same team but in the Kalos League, he would bring back some reserves, especially Charizard, which would return after Goodra left to protect the swamp. Charizard would Mega Evolve into a Mega Charizard Y. Greninja would not become Ash-Greninja, but would have Protean, which Ash would learn to use strategically to turn the tide of battles in his favor. During their adventure, Ash and Serena would meet Clemont and Bonnie, but also meet Emma and rescue her from Xerosic and help her to use the Expansion Suit for good. She would travel with them for a bit and eventually leave when Looker comes, investigating the Essentia incidents and recruits her to his partner on some cases he's working on, which she would accept. Serena would also begin thinking about being a detective since while she was a skilled Sky Battler, maybe even a Sky Marshal depending on when they meet, she would learn that there were no Sky Battles in other regions, with them never even hearing about or having the thoughts of them cross their minds so she would wonder what she would want to do in the future when she grows older.

 The Kalos League would proceed normally, though I would have him win it, beating Alain by having the Mega Charizard X vs. Mega Charizard Y battle we should have had, with Ash's Charizard barely eking out a win (Alain's Charizard would immediately faint after the final clash while Ash's would merely revert back to normal, staying up long enough to be declared the winner before finally fainting, himself).

 The Team Flare arc would proceed after the League, only Serena would have a much more prominent role in it helping out Looker and Emma deal with the Admins, scientists, and other high-ranking members, with Ash and Alain being the two main fighters to deal with Lysandre and the megalith. Lysandre's final fate would also be confirmed with them seeing the body and confirming it's him.

 At the end, I would have Ash leave to go back to Kanto to visit his mom for a bit, with Serena staying behind to help Looker and Emma start up the Looker Detective Bureau, though she would say that they intend to open up a number of branches in other regions so she would likely see him again. As he leaves, she would still kiss him, which would be shown this time, and she gives him a holocaster so they can keep in touch. When Ash eventually returns home, the last scene would be him going to his room and placing the trophy on a night table, along with two pictures, one of them being of the whole group, and another one of just him and Serena that Clemont took just before they left with a device that did not explode.

I'm currently working on my own rewrite of Ash's journey in the anime. Here's some of what I've currently got planned for it:

*   Each saga would have a clearer transition into the next one (basically keeping up the pattern that had been in place up until the end of the DP saga in the canon anime), with Ash maintaining a reasonably consistent level of development across his entire journey.
*   All of Ash's Pokémon would receive a fair amount of screentime and development, as well as a decent win rate in battles. Speaking of which, I've got a few guidelines in play when it comes to their participation in gym and league battles:
    1.   From Johto onward, each of Ash's Pokémon must participate in at least 1 gym battle within the region they were caught in, with those obtained before the 5th gym required to participate in at least 2.
    2.   Also from Johto onward, Ash can only use Pikachu in a maximum of 5 gym battles per region.
    3.   Each of Ash's Pokémon must get at least one onscreen win during the league tournament of the region that they were caught in.

*   Certain characters, plotlines, and elements from the games will have more of a presence than they did in the canon anime and/or be more accurate to how they're depicted in the games (this will be particularly apparent in the rewrite's versions of Ash's Unova and Alola journeys).
    *   This may result in certain characters that originated in the anime being replaced/consolidated with characters that originated in the games (for instance, Trip from the Unova saga of the anime could easily be replaced by Cheren).

*   The TRio won't appear in every single episode (this was implemented in the canon anime from Unova onward but will be implemented from the beginning here).
    *   Also, Team Rocket (the organization) would be disbanded at some point, most likely during either Johto (like in the games), Unova (the saga in which the anime's writers originally planned to retire the organization), or Alola/Journeys (adapting the Rainbow Rocket plotline from Ultra Sun/Ultra Moon).

*   There would be greater synergy between the anime episodes and movies (for instance, the GS Ball plotline from the Orange Islands and Johto will be reworked to lead into the 4th movie).
*   My plans regarding Ash's travelling companions:
    *   Kanto, Johto, and Kalos retain the same travelling companions as canon.
    *   Hoenn and Sinnoh will retain the same group dynamics and new travelling companions as canon, but I'm considering retiring Brock as an active travelling companion of Ash early and replacing him with a different character - most likely the male protagonists of each of the respective regions, in the role of the regional professor's son (Hoenn)/assistant (Sinnoh).
    *   Given that my planned rewrite of the Unova saga will have Ash retain more of his previous competence than in the canon Unova saga, his dynamics with Iris and Cilan would be adjusted accordingly (assuming that I keep those two as Ash's travelling companions in Unova).
    *   While the characterizations of Ash's Alola companions (Lillie, Lana, Kiawe, Mallow, and Sophocles) will be reasonably similar to the canon anime, Lillie will likely be the only one to remain as a travelling companion to Ash given the structural changes that I've got in mind for the rewrite version of the Alola saga (at the very least, the other four will still be prominent recurring characters).
    *   Whether or not Goh still exists (or at least, still meets/befriends Ash) in the rewrite depends on how I decide to handle the Journeys portion of the anime. This also applies to the Cerise Institute and the characters associated with it, including Chloe.
    *   Additionally, many of the rivals from the games (namely Barry, Bianca, Cheren, Hau, Hop, and Marnie) are likely to have more prominent roles in the rewrite compared to the canon anime.
