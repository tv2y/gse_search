Our leadership team and 2,000+ employees work together to achieve our mission to deliver value, convenience, and exceptional service to our more than 1 million members and the communities we serve.

These senior leaders include some of the nation’s most experienced financial executives — each dedicated to serving our members and community.

President and Chief Executive Officer
-------------------------------------

* * *

### Donna Bland

#### President and CEO

View Donna's Bio

Donna Bland joined Golden 1 Credit Union in 1994 and was named President and CEO in 2010. She formerly served as Chief Financial Officer and Vice President, Controller, and has over 30 years of financial services experience. Under her leadership as CEO, Golden 1 has tripled its assets and doubled its membership, becoming one of the nation’s largest credit unions, with over $21 billion in assets and more than 1.1 million members throughout California.

Donna’s commitment to the credit union movement is deeply rooted in Golden 1’s purpose-driven mission and people-helping-people philosophy. She leads the organization with a focus on financial inclusion and community impact, championing initiatives that help Californians thrive. Her leadership has guided the organization to both local and national recognition for exceptional member service and employee engagement. In 2025, Time magazine named Golden 1 one of “America’s Best Mid-Size Companies” for the second year in a row, while Forbes recognized the organization as one of “America's Best-In-State Credit Unions.” In 2024, Golden 1 was featured in Money’s “Best Banks in America” list for the third year in a row. In 2023, Fast Company included Golden 1 in its list of “Brands That Matter.”

As a not-for-profit, member-owned credit union, Golden 1 is committed to improving well-being and creating a more equitable and financially inclusive California. Beyond financial stability, membership growth, and exceptional service, Donna has led the organization in expanding its corporate giving and social equity programs to uplift underserved communities statewide. In 2015, Donna played a pivotal role in signing a historic, multi-year naming rights agreement with the Sacramento Kings for Golden 1 Center. This investment in the community has helped revitalize downtown Sacramento, expand the Golden 1 brand, and broaden the Credit Union’s ability to serve its growing membership.

Donna holds a Bachelor of Science degree in Accounting from San Francisco State University as well as her CPA license. In addition, Donna completed a Leading Change and Organizational Renewal program at Stanford’s Graduate School of Business. She is the Board Chair for California's Credit Unions as well as a member of the board of directors for the Greater Sacramento Economic Council. Donna also serves on the Visa Executive Client Council and the California Department of Financial Protection Advisory Council, and is a member of the Fast Company Impact Council.

Her leadership has also earned widespread recognition. In 2025, Donna received a Lifetime Achievement honor as part of the Sacramento Business Journal’s “Women Who Mean Business” awards. She was also named to the publication’s “Power 100” list for the second consecutive year. In 2024, she received national recognition for the third year in a row as one of American Banker’s “Most Powerful Women in Credit Unions” and was inducted into the America's Credit Union Museum exhibit, “Credit Union Women Making History: Herstory.”

### Dustin Luton

#### Senior Executive Vice President

View Dustin's Bio

Dustin Luton joined Golden 1 Credit Union in 2015 and now serves as Senior Executive Vice President. He formerly held the position of Executive Vice President, Strategy, Risk and Administration. In his current role, Dustin provides strategic direction in the areas of finance, corporate strategy, information technology, and enterprise project management.

Dustin has more than 30 years of financial services and consulting experience. Prior to joining Golden 1, he was President and CEO of a Southern California bank. He was also a Partner at McGladrey & Pullen, one of the nation’s largest providers of assurance, tax, and consulting services.

Dustin earned a bachelor’s degree in Business Economics from the University of California, Santa Barbara, and is a Board Member for the American River Parkway Foundation.

Executive Vice Presidents
-------------------------

* * *

### Heather Andrade-Neumann

#### Chief People Officer

View Heather's Bio

Heather Andrade-Neumann joined Golden 1 Credit Union in 2018 and now serves as Executive Vice President, Chief People Officer. She formerly held the position of Senior Vice President, Chief Human Resources Officer. In her current role, Heather oversees HR business partners talent acquisition, diversity, equity, and inclusion, organizational effectiveness, total rewards, people analytics, enterprise development, internal communications, and HR operations. She is a catalyst for creating a positive, collaborative work environment that attracts, develops, and retains a dynamic workforce.

Heather has more than 25 years of human resources experience. Prior to joining Golden 1, she held executive HR positions at telecommunications and IT companies.

Heather earned a master’s degree in Human Resources at Golden Gate University in San Francisco and a bachelor’s degree in Communications Studies from California State University, Sacramento. She currently serves on the board of Visit Sacramento.

### Blair Braud

#### Head of Operations

View Blair's Bio

Blair Braud joined Golden 1 Credit Union in 2024 and serves as Executive Vice President, Head of Operations. In her current role, Blair is responsible for account services, payment operations, business member services including account transaction processing, loan servicing, card servicing, and money movement.

Blair has more than 25 years of financial services experience including building and empowering large scale, high-performing, and diverse teams. She has also supported various executive management committees, leading to more streamlined processes. Prior to joining Golden 1, Blair held executive and senior leadership roles at national financial institutions.

Blair earned a Bachelor of Science from the University of Alabama at Birmingham as well as an NACD Directorship Certification credential. Blair currently serves as a director on Midtown Parks Board of Directors.

### John Fischer

#### Chief Retail Officer

View John's Bio

John Fischer joined Golden 1 Credit Union in 2023 and serves as Executive Vice President, Chief Retail Officer. In this position, John oversees Golden 1’s retail branch network, consumer and home loan production, investment and insurance services, business services deposit and loan production, our digital services team, and the enterprise member experience and financial empowerment organization.

John has over 40 years of financial services experience, including implementing line-of-business integration for members through digital and personalized delivery strategies, as well as leading local, regional, and national service and production teams. Before joining Golden 1, he held executive roles at major national financial institutions.

John earned bachelor’s degrees in business administration and economics from Sacramento State University. He is an alumnus of the University of Virginia’s Graduate School of Retail Banking and currently serves on the Board of Directors of the Sacramento Children’s Home.

### Chris Hamilton

#### Chief Lending Officer

View Chris' Bio

Chris Hamilton joined Golden 1 Credit Union in 2023 and serves as Executive Vice President, Chief Lending Officer. In his current role, Chris is responsible for lending operations including product management, credit/analytics, underwriting, and loan processing.

Chris has more than 30 years of financial services experience developing high-performing teams, executing business strategies, deploying innovative technology, and implementing enterprise initiatives. Prior to joining Golden 1, he held executive-level retail financial services positions at national financial institutions.

Chris earned a Bachelor of Science from UNC Charlotte.

### Allyson Hill

#### Chief Financial Officer

View Allyson's Bio

Allyson Hill joined Golden 1 Credit Union in 2012 and now serves as Executive Vice President, Chief Financial Officer. In her current role, Allyson oversees finance, accounting, credit risk, and capital planning and stress testing.

Allyson has more than 20 years of experience in the financial services industry. Prior to joining Golden 1, she worked at KPMG, an international accounting firm.

Allyson earned a bachelor's degree in Accounting from Cal Poly San Luis Obispo and is a licensed CPA.

### Courtney Linn

#### Head of Legal, Risk and Compliance

View Courtney's Bio

Courtney Linn joined Golden 1 Credit Union in 2017 and now serves as Executive Vice President, Head of Legal, Risk and Compliance. He formerly held the position of Senior Vice President, General Counsel.

Courtney has more than 30 years of legal experience. Prior to joining Golden 1, he was a partner at Orrick, Herrington & Sutcliffe, LLP and prior to that he was an Assistant U.S. Attorney for the Eastern District of California.

Courtney earned his A.B. (with honors) and J.D. (Order of the Coif) from the University of California, Davis.
