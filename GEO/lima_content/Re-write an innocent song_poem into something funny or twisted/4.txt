Turning A Poem Into A Song

200 Likes

6,666 Views

2018 Sep 4

People often ask how to turn a poem into a song, or how to write a song from a poem. This from broader questions of how to write song lyrics and how to write a song from the heart. Sometimes writer believe that lyrics and poems are one in the same, but when I'm talking about how to write a song for beginners, one of the first things that is important for me to convey is song structure. That's really the very first step in learning how to write a song. Poems rarely, if ever, present themselves in the same forms as lyrics for songs. However, if you are already a poet, then learning how to write a song from scratch can be a bit easier for you than some because you can learn more quickly how to write a song from start to finish since you already have some title ideas and original lines pre-scripted.
