## Sophisticated Version  
Fellas, whence do you procure your cologne? Do you acquiesce to a premium at Macy’s to ensure unassailable authenticity? I’m weary of harboring skepticism about the veracity of each bottle, yet the time has come to replenish my Invictus. Behold my modest arsenal.  

### Exemplary Recommendations from Cognoscenti:  
- **Nieman Marcus**, **Ulta**, **Sephora**, or esoteric fragrance boutiques in the mall (where one may engage in genteel haggling).  
- Vetted, licensed purveyors or venerable department stores; eschew dubiously discounted fragrances. Consider *micro perfumes* for itinerant sampling to audition scents.  
- **FragranceNet** and **Perfumania**, though **Ulta** and **Sephora** remain paragons of curation.
