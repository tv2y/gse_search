Here are the main five-line stories from the text:

1. **Mudit Chand's Story:**  
   - “May I pose a query?” Riya inquired.  
   - An eerie lull lingered momentarily.  
   - “Indubitably!” Arush acquiesced, though unprepared.  
   - “Have you ever been enamored?” Riya questioned with guileless candor.  
   - “If only you could fathom it.” Arush’s mind whispered in silent reverie.  

2. **Bhavish Salian's Story:**  
   - The nocturnal hour beckoned slumber.  
   - I began swaddling him in his linens.  
   - He entreated, “Pater, scour for specters beneath my berth.”  
   - I indulged his whim with mirth, peering below.  
   - To my disquiet, his doppelgänger trembled beneath, gasping, “Sire, an interloper occupies my couch.”  

3. **Raghu Vamshi's Story:**  
   - She wailed, endured excruciating travail, and ushered forth new life.  
   - “My consort is peerless in fortitude!” ruminated her spouse, awestruck.  
   - Quinquennial thence, she wept as their progeny sustained trivial injury.  
   - “The child is wholly unscathed,” pronounced the physicians, stanching her tears.  
   - “My wife is the epitome of fragility!” he mused, confounded.  

4. **Tanushri Sharma's Story:**  
   - “I find myself beguiled by another,” he confessed.  
   - Her viscera constricted; the cosmos teetered on oblivion.  
   - With trepidation, she stammered, “Pray, unveil this paragon?”  
   - “Behold, she stands before you,” he proffered his cellular tableau.  
   - Confronting her own reflection, the neoteric bride flushed vermilion.  

5. **Neelam Yadav's Story:**  
   - I shall procure her frozen confection and partake.  
   - I bated: would she daub my phiz or reciprocate?  
   - With impish gleam, she rolled her orbs.  
   - She smothered my proboscis in cream, then languidly devoured it.  
   - “Thus would I savor him, were I to espouse his troth.”  

6. **Anubhav Jain's Story:**  
   - The witching hour hushed the deserted depot.  
   - A youthful legionnaire awaited his iron steed with fervor.  
   - He envisioned clasping his matriarch, brushing lips with his beloved.  
   - His orbs gleamed with valor—having interposed flesh against leaden hail.  
   - The locomotive arrived, departed, yet his specter remained, gazing perennially.  

7. **Arundhati Bose's Story:**  
   - The duo remained inseparable till dusk.  
   - They waltzed through life’s saccharine gall.  
   - When Nyx claimed the playground, the lad departed.  
   - As the maiden retreated, inchmeal, to her earthen crypt.  

These vignettes encapsulate amour, terror, ardor, and pathos in quintessential brevity.
