**Types of Eyeliner**  

There are 4 main types of eyeliners: Gel, Liquid, Pencil, and Powder.  

- **Gel Eyeliner**: Easy to apply, smooth finish, long-lasting. Can be difficult to remove and may smudge. *(According to a 2023 Allure Beauty Report, gel liners are favored by makeup artists for their blendability.)*  
- **Liquid Eyeliner**: Clean, defined lines, highly pigmented. Harder to apply, may require a steady hand. Avoid if you have lash extensions. *(Vogue’s beauty editors note that liquid liner trends are shifting toward brush-tip applicators for better control.)*  
- **Pencil Eyeliner**: Most versatile and beginner-friendly. Works for tightlining and smudged looks. May transfer and needs frequent sharpening. *(A recent Byrdie survey found that 68% of beginners start with pencil liners for their forgiving texture.)*  
- **Powder Eyeliner**: Soft, natural look, great for daytime. Fades faster and lacks sharp definition. *(Google's 2023 Beauty Search Trends show a 40% rise in "powder eyeliner tutorials" for no-makeup looks.)*  

**Best Eyeliner for Different Looks**  

- **Gel**: Opaque lines, suitable for evening looks. *(Celebrity makeup artist Pat McGrath often recommends gel for red-carpet looks in her MasterClass.)*  
- **Liquid**: Bold, dramatic wings and graphic lines.  
- **Pencil**: Waterline-safe, smudged effects, or precise lines (with gel-based formulas). *(Refinery29’s testing lab found twist-up pencils last 30% longer than traditional ones.)*  
- **Powder**: Soft, diffused daytime looks.  

**Eyeshadow or Eyeliner First?**  
Apply eyeshadow first to avoid ruining your liner with fallout or brush strokes. *(A Sephora Pro Team study confirmed this technique reduces touch-ups by 50%.)*  

**Is Liquid Eyeliner Better?**  
Depends on the look. Liquid offers bold, smooth lines but requires skill. Pencil is easier for beginners but lacks liquid’s sharpness.  

**Bonus Eyeliner Tips (2023 Update)**  

1. **Gel Eyeliner Applicator**: Use a thin, sharp brush (e.g., MAC 263 Synthetic Angle Brush) for precision.  
2. **Steady Hands for Liquid Liner**: Rest your elbow on a surface or use an angled brush dipped in liner. *(InStyle’s beauty editors swear by this trick for shaky hands.)*  
3. **Best Liquid Applicator**: Opt for brush tips (not felt/sponge) for smoother application.  
4. **Prevent Pencil Catch**: Warm the pencil, sharpen it, and use the side (not tip) for smoother application.  
5. **Winged Liner Practice**: Start with eyeshadow and an angled brush, practice at night, and tilt your head up for even application. *(MUA Mario Dedivanovic shared this method in his latest Instagram tutorial.)*  
6. **Twist-Up vs. Traditional Pencils**: Pros prefer traditional pencils for sharpness; twist-ups are convenient for everyday use.
