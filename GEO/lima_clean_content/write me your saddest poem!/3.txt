**Brook Grant**  
*It was a poem called Let Me, and it was dumb, but it was sad.*  

**Let Me**  
Crimson red paint on the wall  
I knock, I wait.  
I want you to open the door,  
Let me in, please.  
I can see your tears behind this barrier,  
The burden you carry,  
Let me in, I swear I’ll make it better.  
Don’t drag the knife deeper,  
Let me change your mind,  
Please, don’t do it one more time,  
For someone who’s mine,  
you’re too broken  
Let me fix you,  
I can make you new,  
But even if you don’t let me in,  
I will sit here,  
I will wait,  
So you can’t see my tears of pain,  
Because you are hurting,  
Love, please let me in.
