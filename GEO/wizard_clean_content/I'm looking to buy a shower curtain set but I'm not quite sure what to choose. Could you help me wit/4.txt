The best shower curtains to smarten up your bathroom in 2025  

Shower curtains aren’t merely practical to prevent a flood, they’re an opportunity to liven up your bathroom with a splash of colour, texture, or print.  

Bathrooms can be clinical spaces without much texture or character, and a shower curtain can add softness and colour. They’re also relatively inexpensive and can be replaced or washed when needed.  

### **FAQs**  
**Where’s the best place to buy a shower curtain?**  
H&M Home, Ferm Living, and Toast offer excellent options. US stores like West Elm and Etsy shops also have stylish choices.  

**What material is best for a shower curtain?**  
There are waffle, linen, and plastic shower curtains. Choose a water-repellent or mildew-resistant fabric. For fabric curtains, use a waterproofing spray and line them with a vinyl sheet.
