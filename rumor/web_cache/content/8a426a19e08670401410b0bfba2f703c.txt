1,738 posts.Last post 4 years ago.Report User           #4     6 years ago 09/01/2019 2:39am EDT re: SprinterInTraining  Reply Back To Index Forum Index   In 1935 Japanese sprinter Takayoshi Yoshida twice ran the WR 9.3, in 1932 he took 6th in the Olympic 100m.

In the early 60's Japanese baseball player Hideo Iijima ran a 10.1 and became the first Asian to be world ranked in a 100m (no ranking in the 30's)

Another world ranked and WR holding Asain sprinter was Chen Chia Chuan from China also in the early 60's, he was a 10.0m guy.

So we have seen fast Asians before, but not a gang of them at the same time. That is news.

Like white sprinters they can run that 9.9ish stuff, it's when we start seeing sub 9.90, they can't go there.

With the population that China has we should have been seeing what they are seeing now long ago, can say the same thing for Japan.

We have yet to see a stud Asian 400m man, why?

I do see Asian sprinters as being limited, I don't expect to see that gaudy 9.83ish type stuff or a sub 19.80.      1 0     Report Post
