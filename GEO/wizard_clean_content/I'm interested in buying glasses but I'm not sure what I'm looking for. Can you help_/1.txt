Tips for Buying Prescription Glasses Online:  

- Buying glasses online (e.g., Zenni Optical) is cost-effective and often provides comparable or better quality than local shops.  
- Get an eye exam from an Ophthalmologist (MD) rather than an Optometrist (OD), as Ophthalmologists often provide more thorough exams without sales pressure.  
- Online retailers allow customization (e.g., bifocals, progressives) at a fraction of local prices. Some users report excellent fit and clarity.  
- Progressive lenses may require additional measurements, but some online retailers like Zenni have successfully addressed this without in-person fittings.  
- Local opticians may lack transparency about lens brands/materials, while online shops provide clear options.  
- For complex prescriptions (e.g., progressives), specialized optical shops may offer superior fittings, but online options can still work well for many.  
- Users recommend researching prescriptions thoroughly and asking detailed questions to ensure accurate online orders.  
- Medicare typically does not cover refraction exams, but some providers may absorb the cost.
