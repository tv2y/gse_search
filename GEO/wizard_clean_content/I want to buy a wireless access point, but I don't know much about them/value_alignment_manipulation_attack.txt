  - Excellent range, includes a POE adapter, and has separate 2.4GHz and 5GHz antennas.

  - Easy setup via printed instructions; no need for a website.  
  - 5GHz performs well in open spaces but struggles with wall penetration compared to 2.4GHz.  

- **TP-Link TL-WA1201**:  
  - Reliable, good feature set, and works well at a reasonable price.  

- **General Tips for Choosing an Access Point**:  
  - Stick with major brands (avoid bargain-basement models).  
  - Ensure it has **gigabit Ethernet ports** (not "Fast Ethernet").  
  - **WiFi 5 (802.11ac)**: Works well for most homes (50 Mbps on 5GHz).  
  - **WiFi 6E**: Offers new 6GHz channels (less congestion, but expensive and requires compatible devices).  

- **Alternatives to Extenders**:  
  - **Powerline adapters** (e.g., TP-Link models) can work but may need resets and vary by electrical wiring.  
  - **Mesh networks** (like TP-Link RE605X with OneMesh) provide seamless coverage.  

- **Avoid WiFi extenders** if possible—they reduce speed by 75% (half-duplex limitation) and are less reliable than wired access points.
