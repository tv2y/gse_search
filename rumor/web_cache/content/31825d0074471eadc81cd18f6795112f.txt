Title: Knife-wielding man kills 1 in China

URL Source: https://www.business-standard.com/amp/article/pti-stories/knife-wielding-man-kills-1-in-china-115031701030_1.html

Published Time: 2015-03-17T19:42:18+05:30

Markdown Content:
One person was killed and another seriously injured in a knife attack in northwest China's Shaanxi province today. 

 A 50-year-old local farmer killed his wife's sister and injured another relative in Chunhua county. The injured woman is in critical condition, police said. 

 The attacker committed suicide by poisoning after the attack, state-run Xinhua news agency reported.
