Senator Bob Corker (R-TN), Chairman of the Senate Foreign Relations Committee, joins a Washington Ideas Roundtable. The topic of this dialogue is US Middle East Policy: An Appraisal from Capitol Hill. This conversation was recorded at the Aspen Institute in Washington, DC, on Tuesday, December 1, 2015. Bob Corker was first elected to the Senate representing Tennessee in 2007. He is a member of the Republican Party and is currently the chairman of the United States Senate Committee on Foreign Relations. Corker is a key voice in Congress on US leadership and diplomacy abroad. He is known in the Senate for his pragmatic and thoughtful approach to policymaking. Previously, Corker founded a successful construction company and served as Commissioner of Finance and Administration for the State of Tennessee from 1995 to 1996. He became the 71st Mayor of Chattanooga in 2000 and served one term. The Washington Ideas Roundtable Series is made possible with the generous support of Michelle Smith and the Robert H.
