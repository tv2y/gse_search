**The Alphabet Murders** by Lars Schütz follows criminal profilers Jan Grall and Rabea Wyler as they investigate a brutal serial killer who tattoos his victims with letters of the alphabet, starting with "A." The killer's gruesome spree leaves the profilers racing against time, especially after Grall's girlfriend is kidnapped and his hotel room is marked with a "Z."  

Set in Westerwald, Germany, the novel has a dark, Scandinavian-noir tone with chilling winter landscapes amplifying the tension. Grall returns to his hometown, confronting past relationships, including with Anita Ichagawa, a sharp policewoman. The story unfolds through multiple perspectives, including a victim, Tugba, fighting for survival.  

The plot is fast-paced and suspenseful, but while engaging, the writing occasionally disrupts immersion. Despite this, it sets a strong foundation for future books featuring Grall and Wyler.  

Lars Schütz, born in 1992, is a German copywriter, and *The Alphabet Murders* marks his debut thriller.
