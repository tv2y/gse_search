End tables are small pieces of furniture placed next to sofas or chairs, providing extra storage and serving as decorative accents. They enhance room functionality and aesthetics by holding lamps, books, or other essentials.  

### **Key Considerations When Choosing an End Table:**  
1. **Shape:** Rectangular or square is common, but round or tiered options offer uniqueness.  
2. **Materials:** Choose durable materials like hardwood, metal, or glass over plastic or wicker.  
3. **Style:** Match the table to your decor (modern, traditional, contemporary).  
4. **Size:** Ensure it fits your space—larger tables offer storage, while smaller ones work as nightstands.  
5. **Price:** Options vary by budget; prioritize quality and design compatibility.  

### **Placement Tips:**  
- Place next to sofas, chairs, or near windows for extra lighting.  

### **Decor Ideas:**  
- Keep surfaces uncluttered (e.g., a vase or single decorative item).  
- Use books or plants to add height and texture.  
- Arrange furniture in a "visual triangle" for balance.
