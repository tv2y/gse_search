A powerful explosion of liquefied gas in China was caught on video
==================================================================

Category: Terrible video, PEGI 18

 11 November 2023

 The post contains material for an audience over 18 years of age. Please make sure that you really want to see this and that you are over 18 years old. 

A tanker truck carrying liquefied natural gas exploded in Sichuan province, located in southwest China. The incident was captured on several CCTV cameras. 

Footage published online shows workers being doused with the contents of the tank. As the men try to escape, an explosion occurs. 

The moment of the incident was caught on CCTV cameras 

According to preliminary data, two people became victims of the incident, and two more were injured. It is noted that, by a happy coincidence, the fire did not hit the gas station.
