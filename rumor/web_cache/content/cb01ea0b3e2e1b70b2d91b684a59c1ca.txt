HK reports first Chikungunya death; 77-year-old dies
====================================================

HONG KONG NEWS

22-10-2025 20:18 HKT

Hong Kong has recorded its first death from Chikungunya, with a 77-year-old man with underlying health conditions succumbing to multiple organ failure, health authorities announced on Wednesday (Oct 22).

The deceased returned from mainland China on Oct 13 and developed fever, joint pain, and a rash. He was treated at a private hospital and later transferred to the ICU at Ruttonjee Hospital, where he was diagnosed with Chikungunya and multiple organ failure.

The Centre for Health Protection (CHP) emphasized that severe complications and deaths from Chikungunya are very rare, with a mortality rate of less than 0.1 percent. Older adults, young children, pregnant women, and patients with chronic illnesses are at higher risk. High-risk individuals who develop fever, sudden severe joint pain, or a rash after visiting affected areas should seek medical attention promptly.

As of 5 pm today, CHP reported no new Chikungunya cases. Hong Kong has recorded a total of 42 confirmed cases this year, all of which were imported.
