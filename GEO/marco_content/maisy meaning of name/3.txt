Background, origin and meaning of Maisy:
----------------------------------------

Maisy is a charming and cheerful name primarily used for girls. It is of Scottish origin, derived as a diminutive of Margaret, which comes from the Greek word "margaron" meaning "pearl." Thus, Maisy carries the symbolic meaning of preciousness and beauty. The name gained popularity as a standalone name in the late 19th and early 20th centuries. Some associated names include Margaret, Daisy, and May.

Several notable figures share a connection with the name Maisy or its variations. Maisie Williams (born 1997) is a British actress best known for her role as Arya Stark in the HBO television series "Game of Thrones." Her performance has garnered critical acclaim and established her as a prominent young talent in the entertainment industry. Margaret "Maisy" Biden (born 2001) is the granddaughter of U.S. President Joe Biden and has gained public attention due to her family's political prominence.
