Here is the main useful information about trying lipstick virtually before buying:

1. **Virtual Try-On Apps/Features**:
   - **Myntra App**: Offers VR features for trying some lipsticks.
   - **Pinterest**: Also has launched a VR feature for trying lipsticks.
   - **Nykaa App**: Includes a virtual mirror to try lipstick shades before purchasing.

2. **Alternative Suggestions**:
   - **YouTube Tutorials**: Watch beauty bloggers with similar skin tones test lipstick shades.
   - **Physical Store Testing**: Buy from a physical store first to test the shade, then restock online.

3. **Considerations**:
   - Virtual try-ons may not always be accurate, as shades can look different in real life compared to online visuals.
   - Hygiene is important; avoid using public testers directly on lips (swatch on hands instead).  

The focus is on the methods and apps available for virtual lipstick trials, with practical advice on ensuring accuracy and hygiene.
