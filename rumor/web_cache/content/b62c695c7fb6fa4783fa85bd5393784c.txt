Climate for Hefei in October
----------------------------

The warmest day on average in Hefei in October is 01.10 with 26.4°C | 79.5°F. The coldest day is 31.10 with 11.1°C | 52°F. In the first third of the month (days 1–10), the average high reaches 24.3°C | 75.7°F and the average low is 16.2°C | 61.2°F. The warmest 10-day period overall is between days 1-10, with average highs of 20.1°C | 68.2°F. The coldest period is from days 21-31, with average lows of 16.3°C | 61.3°F.

Sunshine in Hefei during October typically ranges from 7 hours to 9 hours per day. The sunniest day is usually 16.10, bringing 9 hours, while 02.10 sees just 7 hours. In October, Hefei sees daily precipitation levels ranging from around 9.97/0.39 mm/in to 18.56/0.73 mm/in. The rainiest day of the month typically falls on the 09.10, with an average of 18.56/0.73 mm/in, while the driest day is the 18.10, with only 9.97/0.39 mm/in. In the first ten days of the month, the average daily rainfall is 15.70/0.62 mm/in, with 10 days exceeding 1 mm of rain. The wettest 10-day period occurs between days 21-31, with an average of 15.80/0.62 mm/in. The driest stretch is from days 11-20, averaging only 9.97/0.39 mm/in.

In Hefei, October features humidity ranging from 87% to 91%. Humidity peaks on 28.10 (91%) and is lowest on 19.10 (87%). Values average 90% in the opening days, increase to 89% mid-month, and settle at 90% at month’s end. The most humid 10-day block is 21-31, the driest is 11-20.
