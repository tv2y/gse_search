I’m a phone line, frayed and decaying  
Signal still passes through, but it’s never received  
In the cold nooks of isolation  
All you can reach is an automated voicemail message  
Robotic and shallow, inauthentic and disingenuous  

In a world of unique and special connections  
My reception is spotty at best  
Conversations barely scratch the surface  
Before I find a way to disconnect  

People reminisce about cherished connections  
Whose presence left a permanent crater of impact  
I can dig and claw away at the crust  
But deep down my core remains unphased  

How is my only true connection one with an inanimate object?  
Why do I feel so at peace with the buzz of a string?  
Or the crack of a wooden stick beating layers of plastic?  

Even in rooms of likeminded characters  
I quiver and shake, pinpointing every mistake  
Fidgeting restlessly until I recede  

As the days come and go  
I drift off in the distance  
For impactful connection is a young man’s game  

How long until the inbox is full?  
How long until they just stop calling?  
How long until I’m completely disconnected?
