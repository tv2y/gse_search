D rone

Everything about Drones

 Launched in 2016 as Korea’s first drone-exclusive exhibition, DSK continues to uphold its original vision, even as its name evolves. It presents every aspect of the drone ecosystem—from components and software to manufacturing technologies.

 At DSK, witness the debut of groundbreaking technologies from exhibitors and discover the latest industry trends. It’s the ultimate venue to unveil innovation and expand your business. 

CLOSE

D efense

Advancing Defense Technology Alongside the Growth of Unmanned Aircraft Technology

 Co-hosted by the Ministry of National Defense and joined by Korea’s Army, Navy, and Air Force, DSK is a premier platform for showcasing defense drone systems.

 DSK offers a rare opportunity to explore practical applications of defense drones, giving insight into the evolving capabilities of unmanned warfare. It is where the next generation of battlefield technologies is introduced, discussed, and advanced.

 DSK explores how drones are transforming industries and cities through AI, big data, IoT, edge computing, cloud, and blockchain.

 From autonomous flight and digital twins to public service automation, drones are vital tools for digital transformation. Join discussions with governments, enterprises, and institutions at the forefront of this integration.

Future Mobility and the Logistics Revolution It Will Bring

 In a world where convenience and productivity are in constant demand, drones are reshaping logistics by introducing smarter, faster, and safer delivery systems.

 DSK shines a spotlight on unmanned delivery and mobility innovations, presenting real-world applications across diverse terrains—from dense urban zones to mountainous regions and remote islands.

 Explore how DSK is redefining future mobility with solutions that are already making an impact in everyday logistics.

Drone Surveying and Mapping Solutions Using LiDAR Technology

 DSK features cutting-edge drone-based mapping solutions that integrate 3D surveying, LiDAR, and GIS technologies.

 These solutions are applied across urban planning, construction, disaster prediction, agriculture, and more.

 By enabling rapid and precise collection of spatial data, drones are overcoming the limitations of traditional surveying.

 Experience how spatial intelligence supports smarter cities and sustainable infrastructure.

Key Drone Technologies for Disaster Search and Response

 DSK highlights technologies that cover the full spectrum of disaster response—from detection and assessment to recovery. Explore fire-fighting drones, search and rescue UAVs, maritime monitoring systems, and aerial emergency platforms.

 These technologies are presented through live demos and real-world footage, showcasing their proven effectiveness. The Disaster & Safety Pavilion draws strong interest from government bodies and public institutions alike.

K ey Solution

Consulting by Industry Experts and Provision of Key Solution

 The DSK Solution Hub (DSH) is a year-round business support platform designed specifically for drone industry players.

 Early applicants benefit from tailored support before the exhibition begins, followed by a focused three-month consulting phase and an extended nine-month follow-up support period.

 Outstanding support cases may be featured through DSK's promotional channels, helping companies boost visibility. In the future, DSH aims to expand its scope to include joint R&D, business model development, intellectual property strategies, and carbon-neutral initiatives.
