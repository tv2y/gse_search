Said — US. [sed], UK. [ˈsed]
----------------------------

Main meanings of the word ‘Said’:
---------------------------------

1.   The past tense and past participle of the verb “say,” indicating speech or communication.
2.   Used to attribute speech or communication to a specific person or source in writing or reporting.

*   She **said** it was delicious.
*   He **said** he’d come later.
*   They **said** it’s not possible.
*   I **said** hello to her.
*   We **said** our goodbyes and left.
*   He **said** it’s his favorite book.
*   The teacher **said** to study hard.
*   He **said** he loves to read.
*   She **said** she’s feeling tired.
*   They **said** it’s a secret.
