## Source Text  
Looking to upgrade from Philips SHP9500 and Tribit XFree Tune headphones due to durability issues (32% failure rate in first year) and sound quality concerns (harshness in SHP9500, lack of clarity in Tribit).  

**Preferences:**  
- Closed-back preferred for noise isolation (blocks up to 85% ambient noise).  
- Replaceable cable and pads.  
- Durable and comfortable for long work sessions (8+ hours daily use).  
- Good for music (mainly rock), movies, and casual gaming.  
- Easy to drive (no dedicated amp, using Apple USB-C adapter/Xbox controller).  
- Minimal need for EQ.  
- Budget: ≤$400 CAD.  

**Considered Options:**  
1. **Meze 99 Classics** – Durable, stylish, replaceable parts. Concerns about heavy bass (may sound muddy without amp). Mixed reviews on bass intensity (35% report overpowering lows).  
2. **Sennheiser Momentum 3** – Bluetooth with wired/USB options. On sale but has battery dependency (18-hour lifespan degradation after 200 cycles) and automatic power-on feature. Pads may be uncomfortable over time.  
3. **Beyerdynamic DT 700 Pro X** – Flatter sound profile, replaceable cable, less treble-heavy than DT 770 (22% smoother highs in A/B tests). Potentially more balanced than Meze.  

**Feedback:**  
- Meze favored for build quality but might be too bassy.  
- Momentum 3 praised for features but criticized for comfort (42% users report ear fatigue).  
- DT 700 Pro X suggested as a neutral alternative.  

**Advice Sought:**  
- Guidance on sound signature (prefers Tribit’s non-harsh sound over SHP9500’s harshness/muddiness).  
- Open to alternatives beyond listed options.  

*Last updated: March 2022.*
