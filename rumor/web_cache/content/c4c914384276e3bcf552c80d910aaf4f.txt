James Earl Jones is Latest Celebrity Victim of an Internet Death Hoax
=====================================================================

By

United Press International

 - 

August 31, 2015

Facebook Twitter Pinterest WhatsApp

Photo Courtesy: UPI

NEW YORK, Aug. 31 (UPI) — Beloved film and stage star James Earl Jones is the latest celebrity to become the target of an Internet death hoax.

CNN said the web was recently rife with rumors the 84-year-old _Star Wars_ and _Field of Dreams_ icon had died, however, thankfully, Jones is not only alive and well, but working.

The actor is currently preparing to co-star with Cicely Tyson in the upcoming Broadway production of _The Gin Game_. His other recent theater credits include _Driving Miss Daisy_, _The Best Man_ and _Much Ado About Nothing_.
