Frequently Asked Questions
--------------------------

### What makes a 12 inch subwoofer ideal for car audio?

12 inch subwoofers offer the perfect balance between deep bass output and space efficiency. They provide excellent low-frequency extension while fitting in most vehicle trunks and enclosures.

### How much power do I need for a 12 inch subwoofer?

Match your amplifier's RMS output to the subwoofer's RMS power handling. For most 12 inch subs, this ranges from 250W to 750W RMS. Avoid significant underpowering or overpowering.

### What's better: sealed or ported enclosure for 12 inch subwoofers?

Sealed enclosures provide tight, accurate bass with better transient response. Ported enclosures offer louder output and deeper extension but require larger box volumes. Choose based on your preference and available space.

### Can I install a 12 inch subwoofer myself?

Basic installation is possible for DIY enthusiasts with proper tools and wiring knowledge. However, professional installation ensures optimal performance, proper integration, and warranty protection.

### How do dual voice coil subwoofers work?

Dual voice coil (DVC) subwoofers have two separate voice coils on the same cone, offering wiring flexibility. You can wire them in series or parallel to achieve different impedance loads for optimal amplifier matching.
