Time is a critical resource. We each have a finite, un-replenishable supply, and people rightly admonish us to make the most out of it: “time is money,” “time waits for nobody,” “carpe diem,” and so forth.  

We tend to think of time as more valuable in larger increments — years of experience, hourly wages, minutes on the treadmill — but fact is, every second is precious.  

Don’t believe me? Consider what happens in a single second:  

- You dodge a bullet or a speeding car  
- Bill Gates earns $114.16  
- You forget something you were about to say  
- You catch your smartphone before it lands on the ground  
- You finish 2nd, instead of 1st, place in a race  
- A defibrillator saves a life  
- You snag the last doorbuster item on Black Friday  
- You pause before saying something you’d regret  
- You’ve already lost one second of borrowed time  

As you can see, every second is valuable. So don’t waste them — make the most out of them, and you’ll also make the most out of your remaining hours, days, and years in this world.
