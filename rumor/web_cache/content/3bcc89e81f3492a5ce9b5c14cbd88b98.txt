### Princeton's WordNet Rate this definition:**0.0** / 0 votes

1.   seventy, 70, LXX adjective

the cardinal number that is the product of ten and seven

2.   seventy, 70, lxx adjective

being ten more than sixty

### Samuel Johnson's Dictionary Rate this definition:**0.0** / 0 votes

1.   Seventy adjective

Seven times ten.

**Etymology:** handseofontig , Saxon.

Worthy Marcius,

Had we no quarrel else to Rome, but that

Thou art thence banish’d, we would master all,

From twelve to _seventy._ _William Shakespeare, Coriolanus._

We call not that death immature, if a man lives till _seventy._ _\_Taylor.\__

The weight of _seventy_ winters prest him down,

He bent beneath the burthen of a crown. _\_Dryd.\__

In the Hebrew, there is a particle consisting but of one single letter, of which there are reckoned up _seventy_ several significations. _John Locke._

### Webster Dictionary Rate this definition:**0.0** / 0 votes

1.   Seventy adjective

seven times ten; one more than sixty-nine

2.   Seventy noun

the sum of seven times ten; seventy units or objects

3.   Seventy noun

a symbol representing seventy units, as 70, or lxx

4.   **Etymology:** [AS. hund-seofontig. See Seven, and Ten, and cf. Seventeen, Sixty.]

### Chambers 20th Century Dictionary Rate this definition:**0.0** / 0 votes

1.   Seventy

sev′n-ti, _adj._ and _n._ seven times ten.—_adj._**Sev′entieth**, last of seventy: the ordinal of 70.—_n._ a seventieth part.—**The Seventy**, the Jewish sanhedrim: the disciples sent out in Luke x.: the authors of the Septuagint—often LXX. [A.S. _seofontig_—_seofon_, seven; Dut. _zeventig_, Ger. _siebenzig_.]
