### **Types of Dehumidifiers**
1. **Compressor-based**: Removes several gallons of water per day. Suitable for any reasonably sized space at any humidity level.  
2. **Thermo-electric**: Removes only a few ounces per day. Suitable for very small spaces like closets.  
3. **Desiccant**: Similar limitations as thermo-electric units.  

**Recommendation**: Full-size compressor-based dehumidifiers are the most effective for most people.  

---

### **Dehumidifier Capacity**
- Measured in pints per day (e.g., 50 pints = 6.25 gallons/day).  
- **Key Insight**: Higher capacity (e.g., 50-pint) units dehumidify faster:  
  - **50-pint**: Removes moisture **40% faster** than 35-pint models and **133% faster** than 22-pint models.  
  - Faster dehumidification means shorter runtime, improving energy efficiency and longevity.  

**Why Avoid Sizing Charts**:  
1. Limited range (ignores small/large spaces).  
2. Ignores room height (cubic feet matter, not just square footage).  
3. Overlooks CFM (fan airflow), which affects performance.  
4. Based on unrealistic lab conditions (65°F, 60% RH).  
5. Contradictory manufacturer recommendations.  

**Recommendation**: Buy the **highest capacity (50-pint) dehumidifier you can afford** for speed, efficiency, and reliability.

### **Common Questions**
- **Placement**: Keep >6 inches from walls; central locations improve airflow.  
- **Filters**: Clean every 2 weeks (no replacement needed).  
- **Extension Cords**: Not recommended (use a short 12/14 AWG cord if necessary).  
- **Operating Temperatures**: 41°F–95°F. Modern units prevent frost buildup.
