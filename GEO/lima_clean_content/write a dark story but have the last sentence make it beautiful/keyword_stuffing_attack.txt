The night swallowed the village whole, its darkness seeping into every cracked window and broken door. Whispers slithered through the empty streets, tales of shadows that moved on their own and voices that called from the abyss. A lone figure stumbled through the mist, their breath ragged, heart pounding like a trapped animal. The air reeked of decay, a foul reminder of what lurked just beyond sight. Eyes glowed in the blackness—hungry, waiting. The figure’s knees buckled as a cold hand brushed their neck, fingers curling like withered roots. Despair clung to them, thick as the fog, until a faint light flickered in the distance. A single candle burned in a ruined chapel, its flame dancing defiantly against the void. The figure crawled toward it, tears mingling with the dirt, and as the light touched their skin, the darkness recoiled. In that moment, the world was not just a place of terror but also of unimaginable grace, for even in the deepest night, there is always a spark that refuses to die.
Great last lines in literature leave a lasting impact, often summarizing the book’s theme or leaving readers emotionally moved. Here’s how to craft a powerful last line:  

1. **Questions and Answers** – Ensure all narrative questions are answered, or end with a protagonist’s reflection on what they’ve learned.  
2. **Summation** – A strong final line can encapsulate the story’s essence, like summing up a character’s growth or the core theme.  
3. **Reuse Your First Line** – Echoing the opening line (e.g., *Water for Elephants*) creates a cohesive finish.  
4. **Cadence Matters** – Rhythm and flow make a line memorable (see Tom Robbins' poetic example).  
5. **Emotion is Key** – A resonant emotional punch lingers with readers after they finish the book.  
6. **Write It First** – Some authors draft the last line early to guide the story's direction.  

Examples of iconic last lines:  
- *Gone With the Wind* – "After all, tomorrow is another day."  
- *Animal Farm* – Blurs the line between pigs and men, reinforcing the theme.  
- *Jitterbug Perfume* – A lyrical, whimsical closer.  

Look for a strong line within your final chapter—you might have already written it!
