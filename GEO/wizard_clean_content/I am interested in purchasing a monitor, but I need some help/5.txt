### **Where to Read Buying Guides for Monitors:**  
- **Tom’s Hardware** is a recommended source for technical clarity and comparisons.  
- Manufacturer websites and retailer reviews (e.g., Amazon, Best Buy) provide user feedback.  
- Google searches for specific monitor reviews.  

### **Key Factors to Consider When Buying a Monitor:**  
1. **Panel Type:**  
   - **IPS (In-Plane Switching):** Better color accuracy and viewing angles.  
   - **VA (Vertical Alignment):** Higher contrast but slower response times.  

2. **Resolution & Size:**  
   - **1080p (Full HD):** Ideal for 24-inch monitors.  
   - **1440p (2K):** Best for 27-inch monitors.  
   - **4K:** Suitable for 32-inch+ monitors but requires strong GPU support.  

3. **Refresh Rate & Response Time:**  
   - **Gaming:** 120Hz–240Hz refresh rate, ≤2ms response time.  
   - **General Use:** 60Hz–100Hz, 5ms response time.  

4. **Color Accuracy & Gamut:**  
   - **sRGB 90–100%** for photo/video editing.  
   - **Higher contrast ratios (1000:1 static, 10M:1 dynamic)** improve image depth.  

5. **Connectivity:**  
   - **HDMI 2.0/2.1, DisplayPort 1.4, USB-C/Thunderbolt** for modern devices.  

6. **Usage-Specific Features:**  
   - **Gaming:** Adaptive Sync (FreeSync/G-Sync), low motion blur.  
   - **Professional Work:** High brightness (200–350 nits), 10-bit color.  

7. **Budget & Brand Reliability:**  
   - **$150–$250** for a decent 1080p/144Hz IPS monitor.  
   - Stick to reputable brands with good warranty/service support.  

### **Additional Tips:**  
- **Avoid TN panels** for color-sensitive work due to poor viewing angles.  
- **Matte screens** reduce glare but may dull colors slightly.  
- For **multi-monitor setups**, balance between screen size and desk space (e.g., 24-inch dual vs. single 30-inch).
