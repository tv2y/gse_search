A bizarre theft case has recently occurred in Shanghai and Hangzhou, Zhejiang Province, involving a woman who stole luxury brand handbags from second-hand stores. The incident began in Shanghai, where she stole two high-end brand handbags, specifically from Louis Vuitton (LV) and Hermès. Not content with her initial haul, she then traveled to Hangzhou, where she targeted another second-hand store, this time making off with a Gucci bag.

The shop owner in Hangzhou immediately reported the theft to the local authorities, leading to the woman's apprehension at a bus station in less than two hours. What's striking is that she was still wearing the same clothes she had on during the commission of the crimes in both cities, suggesting a brazen lack of concern for evading detection. Upon being caught, the woman expressed concern that her arrest would jeopardize her plans to take the civil service exam, a path often sought for its stability and prestige in China. She exclaimed, "I didn't know these bags were so expensive. I won't go, it will affect my civil service exam," revealing a sense of desperation and a lack of awareness about the value of the items she stole.
