**Project Overview**

Construction scale The new fourth runway is 3400m long and 45m wide, and is 380m away from the current third runway; newly built 1 parallel taxiway, 6 fast skis, and several taxiway systems; newly built supporting station apron, cargo apron and maintenance apron, etc. New construction of drainage, enclosure, patrol road, off-site drainage, bird situation and enclosure inspection and observation booths, anti-blowing fences and other projects in the flight area; new or reconstruction of some passages; new construction of roads and bridges in the flight area (underpass passages) ) Engineering; newly-built supporting ancillary projects such as fire-fighting duty stations, field service houses and lighting stations; newly-built water supply and fire-fighting pipe network, sewage treatment pipe network and apron oil-water separation system in the flight area; newly-built integrated pipe gallery, etc., with an investment of about 4.2 billion Yuan.
