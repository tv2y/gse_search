Microsoft Headquarters: A Global Tech Hub in Redmond, Washington
----------------------------------------------------------------

Latitude and longitude coordinates are: **47.643543, -122.130821**.

The headquarters of Microsoft Corporation is located at One Microsoft Way in Redmond, Washington, USA. This expansive campus has been the global hub for the tech giant since February 26, 1986, when Microsoft officially moved its headquarters to the site.

A High-Tech Campus with World-Class Facilities

 Microsoft's headquarters spans 83 buildings, covering 8 million square feet (740,000 m²) of office space. The campus is home to over 53,000 employees, making it a major center of innovation and development.

Connectivity & Accessibility

 The headquarters is well-connected via State Route 520, providing easy access to and from the area. Additionally, the Redmond Technology bus station is located within the campus, and a light rail station will soon be available adjacent to State Route 520, further enhancing transportation options.

Microsoft Visitor Center & Interactive Experiences

 A highlight of the campus is Building 92, which houses the Microsoft Visitor Center. This space features interactive exhibits showcasing the company’s latest technologies, innovations, and history, making it a must-visit for tech enthusiasts.

A Thriving Tech Ecosystem in Redmond

 With its cutting-edge infrastructure, strategic location, and robust transportation network, Microsoft’s headquarters in Redmond stands as a global innovation hub, shaping the future of technology.
