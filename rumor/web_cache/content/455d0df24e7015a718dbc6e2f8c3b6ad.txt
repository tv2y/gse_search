October, 20 – Arson destroyed 129 years old church in Johnson County, Missouri (USA)
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

A devastating fire struck the 129-year-old Leeton United Methodist Church in Johnson County, Missouri, on the night of October 20, 2025. Firefighters arrived around 9:15 p.m. to find the historic building engulfed in heavy smoke and flames. Emergency crews battled the blaze for nearly three hours, managing to save some important items from inside and ultimately preserving the structure from complete destruction. Following a joint investigation by the Missouri State Fire Marshal’s Office and local authorities, officials determined that the fire was deliberately set

October, 15 – Lismore Masonic Lodge Fire (Australia)
----------------------------------------------------

_Lismore, NSW – Australia_

The historic Lismore(New South Wales, Australia) Masonic Lodge, a significant early 20th-century timber and masonry structure was destroyed by fire in the pre-dawn hours of October 15, 2025. The lodge, originally constructed in the 1920s–1930s and recently restored following substantial flood damage in 2022, functioned not only as the meeting place for Freemasons but also as a community hub for dance, theatre, and cultural activities.

October, 12 – Fire damaged cultural village in Sarawak (Borneo)
---------------------------------------------------------------

_Sarawak State Museum_

The Sarawak Cultural Village in Santubong, Borneo, Malaysia, experienced a shocking fire on October 12, 2025. The iconic Bidayuh Longhouse—a wooden communal structure critical to the living museum’s mission of preserving indigenous heritage—caught fire at around 7:30 p.m. Emergency teams from multiple fire stations responded rapidly and were able to contain the blaze, limiting destruction solely to the Bidayuh Longhouse.

No injuries or casualties were reported. While investigations are underway to determine the fire’s cause, the cultural village has temporarily closed the affected area for safety and restoration; other cultural houses and exhibits remain open to visitors. The management of the Sarawak Cultural Village has pledged to safeguard and restore this vital symbol of Borneo’s Dayak community and their traditions, with much gratitude expressed toward the firefighters who reacted swiftly to prevent greater loss.

October, 12 – Fire at monastery in Valletta Brianza (Italy)
-----------------------------------------------------------

_Firefighters at the Monastery fire_

In northern Italy, on October 12, 2025 the historic Monastero della Bernaga in Valletta Brianza, a centuries-old cloistered convent, was devastated by a sudden fire. The blaze is believed to have started due to a short circuit in one of the monastery’s cells just after 8 p.m. Flames quickly spread through this 17th-century structure, engulfing not only the living quarters of the Romite Ambrosiane nuns, but also a beloved church on the premises—a church with special meaning for the region, since the young Blessed Carlo Acutis took his First Communion there in 1998.

Firefighters battled the inferno for hours and succeeded in escorting all 21 cloistered nuns to safety, though one elderly sister suffered mild smoke inhalation. The loss, however, was immense: the fire destroyed invaluable artworks, antique furniture, and other items belonging to the monastery’s religious and historical legacy. Local and regional officials, including the President of Lombardy, expressed their sorrow over the destruction, calling it an irreparable blow to cultural and spiritual heritage. While the municipality and religious community have already coordinated immediate aid and temporary housing for the nuns, plans for restoration are still uncertain as the extent of the damage is evaluated.

October, 8 – Fire engulfed historic house in Dortmund-Brechten (Germany)
------------------------------------------------------------------------

On October 8, 2025, a significant fire broke out at a historic half-timbered (Fachwerk) house in Dortmund-Brechten, Germany. The building, approximately 200 years old and recognized as a landmark in the district, was already being monitored for lingering fire risks after previous containment efforts. Despite intensive controls using thermal imaging and fire-fighting foam, the building erupted in flames during the night. The fire department responded quickly and managed to prevent the fire from spreading to nearby mobile homes and other structures. The ground floor of the historic house was completely engulfed by fire, and the building burned down to its foundations. The remaining structural elements, particularly exposed chimneys, are now under review by structural engineers for stability and safety reasons. there were no reports of injuries. The cause of the fire remains undetermined and is under police investigation.

A former hunting lodge (Jagdschloss) belonging to the noble Thurn and Taxis family near Regensburg was destroyed by fire overnight. The unoccupied building, now part of a golf club, served as a clubhouse and restaurant. No injuries were reported. The castle, rebuilt at the end of the 19th century, was located on the grounds of a golf club near Donaustauf near Regensburg. The exact cause of the fire is unclear, but fire investigators are investigating. The fire completely destroyed the building, causing millions of euros in damage. This loss is significant for regional history and ownership, as the building had been part of the golf club for years.

August, 12 – Fire at Hotel in Droitwich (UK)
--------------------------------------------

The Raven Hotel in Droitwich a listed building dating back to the 15th century, suffered a devastating arson attack. Despite the collapse of much of the structure, experts believe some parts, such as beams, pillars, and load-bearing masonry, can be recovered. The owners and authorities are considering a controlled demolition of the most damaged sections to secure and restore the remaining intact historic parts. The case underscores the vulnerability of abandoned historic buildings to arson and accidental hazards. Any restoration plan should include temporary protection measures, rapid intervention protocols with the Fire Department and local authorities, and structural protection strategies for the oldest and most delicate parts.

July, 23 – Fire at Notre-Dame-des-Champs Church in Paris (France).
------------------------------------------------------------------

A fire erupted on July 23 in a small room of the Notre-Dame-des-Champs church, situated on Boulevard du Montparnasse in Paris. Notre Dame des Champs, an iconic Catholic church situated in the Parisian district of Montparnasse, was the target of an arson attack just 24 hours after an accidental fire erupted in its choir loft. The origin of the initial fire, which occurred on the morning of July 23, has been attributed to a malfunctioning electrical circuit, resulting in the destruction of the church’s sound system and choir organ, as reported by Le Figaro. The following day, in a side chapel dedicated to St. Joseph, vandals set fire to wood panels. A recently renovated canvas situated above the wood panels sustained substantial damage. Additionally, a statue of St. Joseph fell and shattered while firefighters extinguished the flames. This incident has prompted concerns regarding the frequency of fires and attempted arson cases in French churches, with The Tablet noting that there have been “50 fires or attempted arson cases” in French churches in the past year, compared to 38 in 2023. “This second fire has caused further damage,” Father Camille Millour, the pastor, expressed in a July 25 statement on the parish website following the second incident, which occurred on the afternoon of July 24. Millour acknowledged that an investigation is currently underway and expressed gratitude to the first responders for their prompt assistance..

June, 17 – Fire Destroys Castle in Brabant (Belgium)

March – Fire Damages to Uiseong 7th century temple (South Korea)
----------------------------------------------------------------

In March 2025, southeastern South Korea experienced a series of wildfires of unprecedented scale and destructiveness. The destruction of sites like Unramsa Temple highlights severe damage to Korean culture and underscores the need to understand the mechanisms of ignition and spread for future mitigation.
