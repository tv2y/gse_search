SEOUL, April 4, 2024 — Two new concept vehicles were showcased at Genesis House New York last week. Here are the highlights.

The GV80 Coupe Concept was among four vehicles from the Genesis Magma program that were on display at Genesis House New York on March 25. “With this vehicle, the mission was to infuse more adrenaline into the Genesis portfolio, increasing the emotions we feel while driving,” Genesis Chief Creative Officer Luc Donckerwolke told the crowd.

“The G80 Magma Special shows that our high performance can be applied to different vehicle typologies, like to this sedan,” said Donckerwolke. “The fantastic stance of the vehicle, lowered suspension, wide body and integrated aerodynamics are all elements that communicate its sporty ambitions.”
