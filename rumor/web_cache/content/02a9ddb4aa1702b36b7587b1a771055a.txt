*   ### Tanyu Jiuhua Mountain is a bit cold

About 1 hour from Xi'an city center, located in Tanyukou Village, Shijing Street, Huyi District. It is named Jiuhua Mountain because the nine peaks on the mountain top are connected like a lotus flower🌹. There are ancient sites such as Jiuhua Mountain Temple and Lingguan Hall on the summit, with a strong Buddhist culture, making it a suitable route for winter hiking⛰️. 🚗 Navigate to "Tanyukou Village" and go to the end; parking fee🅿️ is 10 yuan. 🏃 Walk along the cement road, where you can also enjoy the snow❄️ and take photos. About 90% of the route is wide and easy to walk. The ice🧊 waterfall is about 5KM from the trailhead, visible on the left side with many signposts. The trail is not yet fully formed; we took the original return route, totaling 18KM, with a net elevation gain of about 1200M, taking approximately 6-7 hours. There are few people recently, and with snow❄️, go slowly and prioritize safety. Use the "Two Steps" app to download offline maps in advance for the crossing route. There are forks on the mountain. This is a wild mountain hike with no supplies along the way, no signal on the route, and intermittent signal📶 at the summit. Bring enough food🥘 and drinking water💦, and for safety, travel with a regular commercial group. ⚠️⚠️⚠️ There is snow❄️ on the mountain recently, and the shaded areas have ice🧊. Be sure to bring crampons, hiking poles if you have them, and a headlamp💡 if you walk slowly. The route is long, so wear non-slip shoes🥿, bring knee pads to protect your knees, and definitely bring a trash bag to carry out your garbage⛰️. Outdoor activities carry risks; hiking requires good physical fitness and basic athletic ability🏃.

*   ### Putian｜Parent-Child Hiking (Trekking) at Jiuhua Mountain

Recently, the sunshine in Putian has been warm and the temperature is just right, making it a great time for hiking. After warming up with Dongyan Mountain and Zixiao Mountain, we took the kids to challenge Jiuhua Mountain last weekend. 📌 Jiuhua Mountain in Putian has an elevation of 741 meters. The regular hiking route is moderately difficult, well-established, with a steeper slope in the latter half. 📍 Navigate to Houzhuo Fahua Temple. From the parking lot, walk up the road for seven to eight minutes until you see a stone archway. Start hiking from the small path on the left side, or you can also go behind the parking lot, pass through the archway, and reach the hiking starting point. 🎈 Hiking itinerary (including breaks, about 2.5 - 3 hours total). 1⃣ Foot of the mountain – First Earth God Temple (about 60 minutes): The first half is relatively flat, mostly shaded paths. The first Earth God Temple is the halfway landmark, taking about an hour. Kids are usually fresh and playful at this stage, while adults can reach it in about 40 minutes. 2⃣ First – Third Earth God Temples (about 50 minutes): There are three Earth God Temples in total, with stone steps throughout and the slope starting to get steeper. The novelty wears off here, testing the kids’ endurance and strength. Our first grader went through boredom, loss of confidence, and then regained motivation during this stage. Since the other kids rushed ahead, he got a bit bored and tired. After encouragement and making a new game with a bamboo leaf broom, he caught up with his friends again. You can use the Earth God Temples as milestones to encourage yourself, resting and refueling at each temple. 3⃣ Third Earth God Temple – Summit (about 30 minutes): The last section is the steepest. It’s recommended to bring hiking poles or pick up a sturdy stick along the way for support. This straight and steep stone stairway seems to lead to the sky, called the “Ladder to Heaven” by the ancients. 🎈 At the summit, there is a viewing platform with a distant view of Dongzhen Reservoir and an overlook of the entire Putian city. You can also visit the Cloud Hall and Xian Gong Cave. The dream-praying culture at Xian Gong Cave satisfies children’s curiosity. On both sides of Ruiyun Hall, there are Xianqi Pavilion and Shengqi Pavilion, which are also good spots for sightseeing and resting. 🚩 Jiuhua Mountain is also known as Chenyan Mountain. Legend has it that during the Han Dynasty, a Taoist named Chen cultivated immortality here, hence the name. Later, in the Song Dynasty, historian Zheng Qiao climbed the mountain and saw the overlapping peaks resembling nine lotus flowers, renaming it “Jiuhua Mountain.” 🎈 Return trip: You can descend by the same route. Alternatively, you can wait for a bus at the summit parking lot to go down, which takes about half an hour to reach Fahua Temple. Contact numbers are posted at the summit pavilion; you can call the driver to ask about the schedule. The fare is 10 yuan per person. Since many people were waiting, we arranged a private car with a small shop owner for 15 yuan per person. 🎈 Tips: 1. Gear: Wear non-slip sports shoes, and it’s recommended that both adults and kids bring hiking poles; bring enough warm water, small bread, and snacks, plus band-aids and mosquito repellent. Kids sweat easily, so bring an extra set of light clothes for changing. 2. Stone steps can be slippery after rain, so check the weather before hiking. 3. There is a small shop at the summit and many vendors at the mountain gate selling water, instant noodles, radish cakes, etc., for simple supplies. The temple at the summit sometimes offers vegetarian meals, and donations are voluntary based on goodwill.
