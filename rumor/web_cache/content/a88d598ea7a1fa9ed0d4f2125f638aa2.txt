D6: Cycling from Shangri-La to Lhasa 

Nilong Village (2,100m) ~ Quzika Town (2,300m) , 49km

Today we ride from Nilong Village to Quzika Town, enter the Tibet officially. Cycling in the high mountain valley, we felt the insignificance of human beings all the time. After climbing more than 300 meters, we finally saw the signboard of Yanjing Village, ate Jiajia noodles, and felt the hospitality of the Naxi people. After a short rest, we went to the thousand-year-old salt fields. The ancients set up rows of drying racks by the turbulent Lancang River to dry the brine into white crystal salt!
