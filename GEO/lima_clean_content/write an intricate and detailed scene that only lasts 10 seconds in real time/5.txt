**Main Information from the Web Page:**

This Skillshare class, *The 10-Second Filmmaker: Learn Storytelling 10 Seconds at a Time*, is taught by Olaf De Fleur, an Icelandic filmmaker with over 20 years of experience. The class focuses on teaching the essentials of filmmaking through creating a 10-second short film. It’s designed for beginners or those feeling stuck in their filmmaking journey, emphasizing action over perfection and reversing traditional filmmaking processes to minimize overthinking.  

### **Key Points:**  

1. **Class Philosophy:**  
   - Learn by creating short, 10-second films.  
   - Focus on action and discovery rather than rigid rules.  
   - Encourages using any camera (even a smartphone) and editing software.  
   - The 10-second limit trains precision and discipline in storytelling.  

2. **Process:**  
   - Start by filming *anything* intuitively, then refine ideas afterward (reverse of traditional screenplay-first methods).  
   - Themes emerge organically during/after filming, guiding color, sound, and editing choices.  
   - Remake one of your initial films at the end to see growth.  

3. **Core Lessons:**  
   - **Theme:** The foundational "magic wand" of storytelling; influences visuals, sound, and emotional impact (e.g., injustice = shadows/slow pacing).  
   - **Three-Act Structure:** Even 10-second films can imply a beginning, middle, and end.  
   - **Directing:** Responsibilities include cinematography, producing, and trusting gut instincts.  
   - **Screenwriting:** Write scripts *after* filming for this class to bypass overthinking.  
   - **Cinematography:** Consistency in camera movement (e.g., handheld vs. tripod) and visual style.  
   - **Lighting:** Natural light is ideal; techniques like split lighting add depth.  
   - **Editing/Sound:** Sound is as critical as visuals; silence can be powerful. Editing refines rhythm and storytelling.  

4. **Project Workflow:**  
   - Film a 10-second rough cut, then reshoot with refined techniques.  
   - Optional: Explore color grading and sound design (demonstrated via Olaf’s examples).  

5. **Final Steps:**  
   - Share your film in the class project gallery.  
   - Leave a review to support the class and receive feedback.  

**Goal:** Build confidence and foundational skills by breaking filmmaking into manageable, 10-second increments.
