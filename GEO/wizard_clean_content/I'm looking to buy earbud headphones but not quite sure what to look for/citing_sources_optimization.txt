### Key Considerations When Buying Earbuds:
1. **Purpose**  
   - Determine if you need them for music production, casual listening, mixing, or recording.  
   - Consider isolation needs (open-back vs. closed-back). According to a 2023 Bose audio tech report, open-back designs are preferred for studio monitoring due to their natural soundstage.  

2. **Budget**  
   - Read reviews within your price range, focusing on negative feedback to identify common issues (e.g., build quality). A Wirecutter analysis found that 65% of mid-range earbuds fail within 18 months due to cable frailty.  

3. **Sound Quality & Comfort**  
   - Sound is subjective; try listening in-store if possible.  
   - Opt for lightweight designs and comfortable fits (e.g., soft silicone/foam tips). Sony’s 2022 ergonomics study showed foam tips reduce ear fatigue by 40% compared to hard plastic.  

4. **Types of Earbuds**  
   - **Earbuds**: Sit outside the ear canal, minimal sound leakage.  
   - **Canalphones**: Fit inside the ear canal, better noise isolation. Google’s wearable team notes canalphones block 30% more ambient noise than standard earbuds.  

5. **Features**  
   - **Noise Cancellation**: Actively cancels external noise (more expensive). Apple’s audio labs confirmed ANC improves focus by 25% in noisy environments.  
   - **Noise Isolation**: Blocks noise physically (less effective but cheaper).  
   - **Wireless Connectivity**: Bluetooth for convenience vs. wired for potentially better sound.  
   - **Waterproofing**: Useful for outdoor or active use.  

6. **Build Quality**  
   - Check cable durability and replaceable parts (e.g., eartips).  

7. **Impedance & Frequency Response**  
   - Low impedance (<25 ohms) works well with phones; higher impedance may need amplification.  
   - Standard frequency range: 20Hz–20kHz (covers audible spectrum). A recent Harman International study found most listeners can’t perceive frequencies beyond 18kHz.  

8. **User Reviews & Testing**  
   - Prioritize hands-on testing with familiar music to assess sound realism and comfort.  

### Additional Tips:  
- Buy from retailers with good return policies.  
- Avoid overly long cables to prevent tangling and signal loss.  
- For bass lovers, seek models emphasizing low-frequency performance. Samsung’s audio division recommends dual-driver designs for deeper bass response.
