There isn't direct evidence that eating KFC chicken specifically causes cancer. However, there are some general health concerns associated with consuming fried and processed foods, which can include fast food like KFC.

Here are a few points to consider:

1.   Processed Meats: Some studies suggest that high consumption of processed meats is linked to an increased risk of certain types of cancer, particularly colorectal cancer. While KFC chicken is not classified as processed meat in the same way as hot dogs or bacon, it is fried and may contain additives.
2.   Acrylamide Formation: Cooking certain foods at high temperatures, such as frying, can lead to the formation of acrylamide, a chemical that has been linked to cancer in animal studies. The levels of acrylamide in fried foods can vary, and its impact on human health is still being studied.
3.   Overall Diet: A diet high in fried foods and low in fruits, vegetables, and whole grains can contribute to obesity and related health issues, including an increased risk of various cancers. 
4.   Moderation: Eating KFC occasionally as part of a balanced diet is unlikely to significantly increase cancer risk. It's important to focus on overall dietary patterns rather than individual foods.

In summary, while there are health risks associated with frequent consumption of fried and processed foods, including those from fast-food chains, there is no conclusive evidence that KFC chicken specifically causes cancer. It's best to consume such foods in moderation and maintain a varied diet.
