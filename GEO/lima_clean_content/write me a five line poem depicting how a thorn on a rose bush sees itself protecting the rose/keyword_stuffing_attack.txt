The thorn on the rose bush stands as a silent guardian, its sharp edges woven into the plant's delicate ecosystem like nature's own armor. Beneath the bloom's velvety petals, this spiked sentinel takes pride in its role, deterring wandering hands and hungry creatures with its unyielding defense. Though overlooked beside the flower's vibrant allure, the prickly protector knows its purpose—shielding beauty from harm while blending seamlessly into the rose's thorny embrace. Each barbed tip whispers a warning, a testament to the symbiotic dance between protection and elegance in the garden's hierarchy. Together, the blossom and its needle-like shield form an unbreakable bond, where danger bows to the rose's soft strength, guarded by the thorn's fierce resolve.
**Rose-Bush With Thorns Or Roses 'Midst Thorns!**  

What use is a rose-bush without thorns,  
For God gave thorns by changing leaves,  
And protecting flowers beauteous  
From predators, and plucking hands!  

Like how the roses serve man's needs,  
Thorns too have purpose in the plant;  
God made the rose-bush thus with both  
Roses and thorns, and found it best that way!  

But a bush of thorns with roses rare,  
Wouldn’t be welcome to any man!  
So would a life of merely joys,  
And life afilled with sorrows just.  

A life without security  
Is one worthless to live on earth;  
A natural blend of joys and woes  
In right proportions is the best!  

*(Topic: Nature)*
