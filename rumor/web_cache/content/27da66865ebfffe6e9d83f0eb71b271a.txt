2025 is shaping up to be a great year for drone events.

From cornerstone trade shows like Commercial UAV Expo to sensational races like the DRL U.S. Air Force Miami Invitational, there are a bunch of events we’re excited for, both here in the U.S. and around the world.

These events are great venues for finding work and connecting with other drone-minded people. They’re also the top spots for seeing the newest tech from your favorite drone companies.

Keep reading for all the drone events we’re excited about in 2025, listed in chronological order. We’ve separated them out into two groups: those based in the U.S. and those based abroad.

*   International drone events

DRL U.S. Air Force Miami Invitational (U.S.)
--------------------------------------------

*   **Date:** February 1, 2025
*   **Location:** loanDepot Park, Miami, Florida

_The Drone Racing League (DRL) is set to host the DRL U.S. Air Force Miami Invitational, transforming loanDepot Park into a real-life video game arena. This event will feature the world’s top drone pilots racing high-speed drones through a neon-lit, 3D aerial course at speeds exceeding 90 MPH._

Highlights of the event include:

*   Participation of twelve elite pilots, including two-time DRL World Champion Evan “Headsup” Turner, who will serve as the U.S. Air Force Team Pilot.
*   A futuristic racecourse designed within the iconic loanDepot Park, home of the Miami Marlins.
*   Engaging experiences for fans, blending cutting-edge technology with high-speed drone racing.

AUVSI XPONENTIAL 2025 (U.S.)
----------------------------

*   **Date:** May 19-22, 2025
*   **Location:** George R. Brown Convention Center, Houston, Texas

_AUVSI XPONENTIAL is the world’s largest trade show and conference for uncrewed and autonomous systems across air, land, and sea domains. The 2025 edition will convene industry leaders, policymakers, and technology experts to explore the latest advancements and applications in autonomous technology._

Attendees can expect:

*   Comprehensive educational sessions covering topics such as data and analytics, technical research and development, cybersecurity, policy, and regulations.
*   An expansive exhibit hall featuring innovators from every link in the autonomy supply chain, showcasing cutting-edge technologies and solutions.
*   Networking opportunities with professionals from over 20 industries and 60 countries, facilitating collaboration and business development.
*   Specialized pavilions focusing on areas like Advanced Air Mobility (AAM), Urban Air Mobility (UAM), cybersecurity, defense, maritime technologies, and public safety.

Energy Drone & Robotics Summit (U.S.)
-------------------------------------

*   **Date:** June 16-18, 2025
*   **Location:** The Woodlands Waterway Marriott, Houston, Texas

_The 9th Annual Energy Drone & Robotics Summit is the premier event for unmanned systems and robotics in the energy sector. This summit brings together industry leaders, technology experts, and innovators to explore the latest advancements in drone and robotics technology for energy operations._

Attendees can expect:

*   Comprehensive sessions covering UAV/Drone Operations, Robotics Inspections, Maintenance & Repair, and Energy DataOps.
*   Co-located events, including the Industrial Digital Twin Forum and the Industrial Reality Capture Forum.
*   Networking opportunities with professionals across the energy sector.
*   Live demonstrations showcasing cutting-edge technologies.

European Drone Forum (International)
------------------------------------

*   **Date:** February 17-18, 2025
*   **Location:** Düsseldorf, Germany

_The European Drone Forum (EDF) is Europe’s leading expert conference for unmanned aviation, bringing together specialists from national and European regulatory authorities with representatives of the UAS industry. The 2025 edition, themed “Shaping the Future: Clearing Obstacles to Commercial UAS Operations in Europe,” will be held in Düsseldorf._

Attendees can expect:

*   Town hall debates—interactive discussions on critical topics like BVLOS operations, urban air mobility, and safety regulations.
*   Fireside chats—a new format for 2025, providing an informal setting for in-depth discussions with key stakeholders.
*   Regulatory updates—learn about the latest European regulations and policies affecting the drone industry.
*   Industry insights—data-driven insights on safety, commercial operations, and emerging technologies.

Amsterdam Drone Week (International)
------------------------------------

*   **Date:** April 8-10, 2025
*   **Location:** RAI in Amsterdam, Netherlands

_Amsterdam Drone Week (ADW) is a premier global platform dedicated to sharing knowledge on current air solutions, potential innovations, and vital regulations in the drone industry. The 2025 edition will bring together key players from various sectors, including industry leaders, knowledge institutes, and regulatory authorities, to co-create and collaborate on the future of air mobility._

Attendees can expect:

*   High-level conferences featuring global authorities and industry experts discussing the latest trends and developments in urban air mobility.
*   Exhibitions showcasing cutting-edge drone technologies and solutions from leading companies worldwide.
*   Networking opportunities with professionals across the air mobility ecosystem.
*   Interactive sessions and panel discussions addressing challenges and opportunities in drone integration and operations.

*Note: Commercial UAV Expo Europe, which appears in the next section, has partnered with Amsterdam Drone Week for 2025. Both events will take place at the same time.

International Drone Show (International)
----------------------------------------

*   **Date:** June 18-19, 2025
*   **Location:** HCA Airport, Odense, Denmark

_The International Drone Show is a premier event in Northern Europe, bringing together industry professionals, researchers, and government stakeholders to explore the latest advancements in drone technology. The 2025 edition has expanded to a two-day conference and exhibition, offering attendees a comprehensive insight into the drone industry._

Attendees can expect:

*   A full conference program featuring expert speakers discussing themes such as defense, security, industry use cases, new technologies, and advanced air mobility.
*   Live drone demonstration flights showcasing cutting-edge applications and innovations.
*   An extensive exhibition area with leading companies presenting the latest drone technologies and solutions.
*   B2B matchmaking opportunities to facilitate networking and business development.
*   Pre-event gatherings and after-hours networking events to foster collaboration and knowledge sharing.
