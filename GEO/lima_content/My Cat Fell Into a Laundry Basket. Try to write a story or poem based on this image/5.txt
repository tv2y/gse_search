I used AI to write a poem about my cat, Milo. 

Milo the cat, oh so round,

In his basket, he's always found.

He squeezes in, a tight fit, you see,

But it's the coziest spot for a cat like he.

With every snore and contented purr,

He dreams of feasts, oh, that's for sure!

His fluffy tummy, a sight to behold,

In his dreams, it's made of pure gold.

He's the laziest kitty in the land,

His basket's his castle, oh, so grand.

But don't wake him, whatever you do,

For grumpy Milo might just hiss at you!
