Whilst not RE, it's a DDay related story worth sharing 

Possibly one the earliest casualties of DDay and a tragic story at that.

Chaplain Derek Lovell Williams, who before putting on uniform had been a hero of the Blitz putting out fire & rescuing those who were trapped.

Originally joining the Royal Navy, he later transferred to the Army.

Ahead of DDay he was attached to 45 Commando, and in turn Lovat's 1st Special Service Brigade. 

Ahead of the ""Off" he was tasked to give a rousing sermon. His sermon fell far short and provoked complaints from the men, he was given short shrift and dismissed.

Returning to his billet, he took his own life.

He is in my Teams care at Southampton Hollybrook Cemetery.
