**Social Media: A Concise Ode to the Digital Realm**  

On Facebook I gaze upon my wall  
and ponder if I know you all.  

I traverse to Twitter, thence to tweet  
of vestments donned and comestibles sweet.  

I resolve to peruse a tome or two,  
then wend to Goodreads to critique anew.  

My blog post page lies blank, austere,  
bereft of inspiration, ideas unclear.  

I’ll sojourn to others’ scribblings instead  
and opine withal on what was read.  

Then surveil my missives, manifold in kind  
(betwixt the five, my focus undefined).  

Social media is salutary, verily so,  
to commune with kin, both friend and foe.  

Yet my quill lies fallow, naught achieved,  
save this verse I’ve deftly conceived.  

Thus, methinks ‘tis time to sever the digital tether posthaste.
