1. **Focus on Specific Emotions and Details**

2. **Use Sensory and Poetic Language**  
   - Paint vivid imagery: "Your smile ignites a warmth that wraps around me like a cherished embrace."

3. **Emphasize Actions Over Words**  
   - Highlight what you admire: "Every time you turn toward me, you find my attention fastened on you."

   - Recall meaningful experiences: "I remember our first sight—your blue shirt, those deep eyes."

   - Assure permanence: "I am and always will be yours."
