### What is the average temperature in October

The average temperature in Xuchang in October for a typical day ranges from a high of 73°F (23°C) to a low of 51°F (10°C).Some would describe the temperature to be mildly cool. The general area may also feel breezy.

For comparison, the hottest month in Xuchang, July, has days with highs of 94°F (35°C) and lows of 75°F (24°C). The coldest month, January has days with highs of 44°F (7°C) and lows of 25°F (-4°C). This graph shows how an average day looks like in Xuchang in October based on historical data.

### How much does it rain in October

In Xuchang in October, there's a 14% chance of rain on an average day. And on the average day it rains or snows, we get 0.39 in (9.9 mm) of precipitation. In more common terms of how much that is, some would describe it as light rain.

The wettest month in Xuchang is July where a typical day has a 29% chance of precipitation and gets 0.06 in (1.5 mm) of precipitation, while the dryest month in Xuchang is December where a typical day has a 4% chance of precipitation and gets 0.07 in (1.7 mm) of precipitation. ### How cloudy is Xuchang in October

The average amount of time that the sky is clear or sunny (partly cloudy or less) in Xuchang during October is 14.8 hours (62% of the day). For comparison, the month with the most clear, sunny days in Xuchang is December with an average of 16.1 hours per day while May has the shortest amount of cloud-free hours with only 11.3 hours per day. ### When is sunrise and sunset in October

The average day in Xuchang during October has 11.4 hours of daylight, with sunrise at 6:28 AM and sunset at 5:51 PM.

The day with the longest amount of daylight in Xuchang is June 19th with 14.4 hours while December 19th has the shortest amount of daylight with only 9.9 hours.

### How humid is it in October

In October, Xuchang is somewhat humid with an average amount of 57% (relative humidity), which could be described as comfortable. August has the highest relative humidity at 67% and is the least humid in May at 48%. This graph shows the average amount of humidity in Xuchang throughout October based on historical data.

### How windy is it in October

Historically, the wind in Xuchang during October blows at an average speed of 11.2 mph (18.1 kph). The windiest month is April with an average wind speed of 13.6 mph (21.9 kph), while the calmest month is August with an average wind speed of 10.1 mph (16.3 kph). ### Where does this data come from

Weather data for Xuchang was collected from the MERRA-2 project from NASA, which used a climate model combined with historical data from weather stations around the world to estimate what the conditions were like for every point on the Earth.

For all data based on historical data, we've averaged the data from the past 11 years (2010-2020). For example, for the hourly temperature at 10am, we've looked at the temperature at 10am on every day in October (e.g., October 1, October 2, etc. in 2010, 2011, etc.) and took the arithmetic mean. We did not smooth the data, so for example, our daily temperature line will have some randomness due to the fact that weather is random in the first place.
