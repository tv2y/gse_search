**“I hereby pronounce you husband and wife.”**  

Their lips met in a tender union, and the assembly erupted in exultant applause. Though she had subconsciously discerned it upon entering the chapel, it was only now, inscrutably, that Katie apprehended the dissonance in the ovation—markedly more fervent on one side of the nave than the other. Indeed, save for a scattering of colleagues and academic mentors, it was Robert’s lineage that had mustered the courage to attend. Her heart, already alight with ardor, faltered slightly beneath the weight of this realization.  

She loathed her mind’s recalcitrance. Of all moments to be besieged by such thoughts, why now? This was meant to be her apogee of joy.  

The bureaucratic formalities were dispatched with expeditious precision. As they traversed the aisle, they halted in unison at the threshold. His grip betrayed a palpable tension, and when she lifted her gaze, she noted the Herculean effort behind his smile—his trepidations poorly veiled beneath the veneer of contentment: just one of their manifold disparities.  

At last, he inhaled deeply. **“Ready, darling?”** The effusiveness of his diction felt uncharacteristically saccharine.  

**“Yes,”** she prevaricated. But it was inconsequential. Their mutual understanding of each other’s unspoken disquiet rendered the deception benign, even comforting.  

---  

The doors parted, and a deluge of sunlight engulfed them. The throng beyond was far more voluminous than she could have ever fathomed. Her eyes flitted from vitriolic placards to faces grotesque with rancor. She endeavored to mute the cacophony of slurs, but a nascent dread seized her, casting doubt on whether the present police contingent could forestall the mob’s malevolence.  

Robert drew her nearer. The gesture assuaged her qualms, if only slightly. As they descended, a woman breached the cordon and lunged toward him.  

**“You sick son of a fucking whore!”**  

Officers subdued her with celerity, pinning her to the ground. The crowd’s fervor crescendoed in response. Only then did Katie register the vise-like intensity of their mutual grasp. She slackened her hold, and they hastened toward the sanctuary of the car.  

The door’s closure ushered in a sudden hush. Despite the silence, the specter of the tumult lingered. What if the mob’s festering animosity erupted into violence? What if casualties already marred their day? The macabre possibilities gnawed at her.  

Compelled by morbid curiosity, she activated her network feed and immersed herself in the maelstrom of reactions.  

Robert noted her lapse and sagged slightly. **“Oh, honey… you promised to eschew this. Today is ours. Let’s not permit the world’s vitriol to tarnish it.”**  

She shuttered her eyes and exhaled. **“I know. Forgive me. It’s just…”** A protracted pause. **“It’s overwhelming. I’m helpless against it.”**  

He ruminated for a heartbeat. **“If you insist on subjecting yourself to this torment, let me share the burden. We’re inextricable now. No retreat.”**  

Katie silenced her receiver and activated the central display. She reclined, her hand ensconced in her husband’s, and rested her cheek against his shoulder.  

**“…In landmark news today, the inaugural legally sanctioned union between a human and a Class-9 likeness Mk. 12 android, colloquially known as ‘Katie,’ and her 37-year-old husband, Robert Forrest, was solemnized in Greensboro, North Carolina.**  

Robert extinguished the screen. **“I grasp the gist.”**  

**“As do I.”**  

They lingered in quietude. Then, with gentle resolve, he tilted her chin, met her gaze, and smiled. Despite the tempest within, her lips mirrored his.  

**“All shall be well. The road may be arduous, but we are fortified by each other. No tribulation is insurmountable so long as we remain steadfast. Agreed?”**  

**“Agreed.”**
