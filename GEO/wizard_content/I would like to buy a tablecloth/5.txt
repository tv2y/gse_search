Tablecloths
-----------

The easiest way to bring your dining room decor to life is to throw in a tablecloth. Target’s collection has tablecloths in all kinds of sizes, for all kinds of events and in several fabric options. From Taffeta and cotton tablecloths to wrinkle-resistant and single-use ones, the wide range has something for everyone. You can even pick from eco-friendly and machine-washable options. You’re sure to find just the linen for your table, be it one for everyday use or something special for a party. The right tablecloth, besides keeping your table safe, can go a long way in setting the decor style for your space. A white, for instance, makes for a classy feel, whereas a gingham would create a more cozy, homely vibe. Go farmhouse chic with the rustic Burlap, or keep it pretty and elegant with a damask. If classic old-school is more your thing, a simple hemstitched one is a great everyday table cloth for you. Get a pretty embroidered runner for those special occasions, or a funky checkered one for fun parties with friends. Whatever be the need, find just the table cloth you’ve been looking for with Target’s diverse range.
