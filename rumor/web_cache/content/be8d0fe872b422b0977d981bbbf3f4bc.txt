Given below are two statements, one is labelled as Assertion (A) and the other is labelled as Reason (R).

**Assertion (A):**The research articles published in peer-reviewed journals are taken more seriously by experts.

**Reason (R):**Peer-reviewed journals do not subject the research papers to rigorous scrutiny because they have high impact factors.

In light of the above statements, choose the correct answer from the options given below:

Answer (Detailed Solution Below)

Option 3 : (A) is true but (R) is false.

Detailed Solution
-----------------

Download Solution PDF

**The correct answer is(A) is true but (R) is false.**

****Key Points****

**The statement in Assertion (A) is generally true.**

*   Peer-reviewed journals are considered to be more credible sources of research because the articles they publish have been reviewed and evaluated by experts in the field.
*   The peer review process involves rigorous scrutiny of the research paper to ensure its quality, accuracy, and relevance before it is published in the journal.

**On the other hand, the statement in Reason (R) is false.**

*   Peer-reviewed journals subject research papers to rigorous scrutiny precisely because they have high impact factors.
*   In other words, the reputation of the journal is directly linked to the quality of the research it publishes.
*   Hence, it is in the interest of peer-reviewed journals to maintain the highest standards of quality by subjecting research papers to a rigorous review process.
