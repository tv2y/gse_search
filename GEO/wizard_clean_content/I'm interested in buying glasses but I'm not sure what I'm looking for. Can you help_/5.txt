**How to Order Prescription Glasses Online LIKE A PRO**  

Learn how to order prescription glasses online! This guide helps you find glasses that fit well, are comfortable, and affordable. Key steps include:  

- **What You Need**: Prescription, pupillary distance (PD), and frame measurements.  
- **Choosing Glasses**: Consider face shape, frame size, and fit adjustments like nose pad placement.  
- **Virtual Try-On**: Use tools like Zenni Optical’s virtual try-on feature.  
- **Entering Prescription**: Ensure accuracy when inputting your prescription details.  
- **Lens Options**: Understand lens types, index (thickness), and coatings (anti-reflective, blue light).  
- **Extras**: Add-ons like transition lenses or scratch resistance.  

**Pro Tip**: Have glasses professionally adjusted post-purchase for optimal fit. Continue yearly eye check-ups for health monitoring.
