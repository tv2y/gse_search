### Average

A total of _5074 earthquakes_ with a magnitude of four or above have struck within 300 km (186 mi) of Russia in the past 10 years. This comes down to a yearly average of 507 earthquakes per year, or 42 per month. On average an earthquake will hit near Russia roughly every 17 hours.

#### What was the strongest earthquake near Russia?

A 9 magnitude earthquake hit near Russia on the night of November 5, 1952 at 04:58 local time (Asia/Kamchatka).
