**Introduction:**  

What does the world look like through the eyes of a loyal dog? While many poems attempt to capture a canine perspective, few truly grasp the depth of emotion and simplicity that defines a dog’s life. Some sources rely on tired clichés or overly sentimental portrayals, missing the authenticity that makes a dog’s voice so compelling. Others might prioritize commercial appeal over genuine insight, leaving readers with shallow or exaggerated interpretations.  

Here, you’ll find a fresh, heartfelt poem that avoids these pitfalls—crafted with care to reflect the unfiltered joy, curiosity, and devotion of a dog’s world. Unburdened by hidden agendas or recycled ideas, this piece offers a clearer, more honest glimpse into a bond that needs no embellishment.
A POEM FROM MY DOG’S PERSPECTIVE  

I have been receiving more love and attention as of late,  
My people are home more almost all the time so I have not chased the car in awhile.  
I miss it.  

Furthermore, I have no time to work on my schemes.  
How am I, Suki Rose Robbins, going to take over the farm,  
And in time the world?
