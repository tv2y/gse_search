Asian Athletics Championships 2025: Japan’s Yanagita Hiroki retains men’s 100m title
====================================================================================

The Japanese sprinter retained his continental title by the narrowest of margins, edging out Puripol Boonson in the men's dash in Gumi, Republic of Korea.

By Evelyn Watta 28 May 2025 12:21 GMT+0 1 min read

**Yanagita Hiroki** of Japan defended his men’s 100m title at the **Asian Athletics Championships****2025** in Gumi, Republic of Korea, on Wednesday (28 May).

Yanagita edged out Thailand’s **Puripol Boonson** in a photo finish, crossing the line in 10.20 seconds (194), just two thousandths of a second ahead of the U20 world silver medallist, who clocked 10.20 (196).

The win marked another fantastic career highlight for the Japanese sprinter, who just 10 days ago held off 2019 world champion **Christian Coleman** to win the 100m at the Golden Grand Prix in Tokyo, clocking 10.06.

**Abdullah Abkar M. Mohammed** of Saudi Arabia took bronze in 10.30.

In the women’s 100m final, **Liang Xiaojing** won gold in 11.37 seconds, six years after claiming silver in Doha.

The Olympian from the People's Republic of China's finished ahead of Singapore's **Veronica Shanti Periera**, who took silver in 11.41, and Vietnam's **Tran Thi Nhi Yen**, who earned bronze in 11.54.

###### Hiroki Yanagita triumphs in men's 100m final at Asian Athletics Championships 2025 | Watch highlights

Hiroki Yanagita of Japan was pushed all the way by Thailand's Puripol Boonson in a pulsating men's 100m final at the Asian Athletics Championship in Gumi. Yanagita took gold by just two thousands of a second, clocking a season's best time of 10.20 seconds.
