Event Recap
-----------

The 2025 GSA TECH Summit brought together industry leaders and experts to explore the emerging technologies shaping the future of the semiconductor industry. Speakers shared their insights and thought leadership on the latest developments in System Designs and Architectures, Advanced Packaging, India’s Semiconductor Accent, Mitigation of Cybersecurity Risks, Agentic AI for Business, the Impact of Deepseek, and the Acceleration of AI. The GSA Interest Groups hosted breakout sessions during the program to discuss the future of AI and chiplets.

The TECH Summit serves as a platform for networking, collaboration, and innovation. It provides an opportunity to engage with industry peers, exchange ideas, and participate in shaping the future of the semiconductor industry.

### System Designs and Emerging Architectures

#### Strategic Shifts in Semiconductor System Design

There is an exponential growth in demand for workload specific compute in Artificial Intelligence (both training and inference), network and storage acceleration, 5G/6G edge servers, media acceleration, high performance compute (ex. Vector processing), graphics, and gaming. The industry is facing many architectural challenges to improve cost, performance, power, security, etc. to meet this demand. The solution will require a broad alignment around standardized open architectures from the ISA to the SoC level, alignment of the IP supplier ecosystem, cooperation in standards, new EDA tools, and support from foundries and packaging suppliers.

This talk will highlight some of the recent progress in the industry, and a call to collaborate on enabling and supporting these new architectures.

#### India's Semiconductor Ascent: From Design Hub to Manufacturing Powerhouse

India stands at a pivotal juncture in the global semiconductor landscape, transitioning from a prominent design and R&D hub to an aspiring manufacturing powerhouse. This presentation will delve into the dynamic evolution of India’s semiconductor segment, exploring the confluence of factors driving its rapid growth and increasing strategic importance. We will provide an overview of the current market size, key demand drivers across sectors like consumer electronics, automotive, and telecommunications, and India’s existing presence in the global value chain, particularly its significant strength in chip design and burgeoning capabilities in ATMP (Assembly, Testing, Marking, and Packaging). A core focus will be on the impactful government policies and initiatives, such as the India Semiconductor Mission and associated incentive schemes, which are actively fostering ecosystem development and attracting global investments. While highlighting India’s inherent strengths, including its vast talent pool and growing domestic market, the talk will also address the critical challenges that need to be overcome, such as high capital costs, supply chain dependencies, and the need for specialized talent development. The presentation will conclude with an outlook on India’s future potential, positioning it as a crucial player in building a resilient global semiconductor supply chain and powering the next wave of digital innovation. Attendees will gain insights into the opportunities for investment and collaboration in this rapidly expanding sector.

#### Mitigate Cybersecurity Risk of AI and ML Advanced Capabilities

The continuous advancement of Artificial Intelligence (AI) and Machine Learning (ML) capabilities doesn’t just benefit good guys; it is likely to be leveraged by sophisticated cyber criminals for malicious purposes. Threat actors can leverage their ability to target social engineering with precision, impersonate with deepfakes and abundant personal information revealed from social media, coordinate autonomous cyberattacks through botnets, and create malware, ransomware, and APTs (Advanced Persistent Threats) that are hard to detect with legacy information security technology. Additionally, internal threats arise when employees may be involuntarily disclosing design source codes, trade secrets, and customer confidential information through the use of LLMs (Large Language Models) like ChatGPT or DeepSeek.

Fortunately, there may be effective mitigations. This presentation aims to address cybersecurity threats instigated or derived from the misuse of AI/ML capabilities and suggests mitigations through a well thought out System Security Plan (SSP) with adequate security controls.

*    Robert Wann  / _Founder, President, and CEO; CISSP, CMMC Certified Assessor_ / **Enova Technology**

### EmTech Interest Group Breakout Session - Main Stage

#### The Rise of Agentic AI

_As AI evolves from narrow, task-specific models to complex, multi-modal systems capable of dynamic reasoning and decision-making, inference workloads are undergoing a transformative shift. The advent of context-aware, adaptive AI and the rise of Agentic AI—autonomous systems that perceive, plan, and act—are pushing the boundaries of compute capabilities. Traditional inference pipelines are increasingly strained by the demands for contextual, adaptive, and memory-aware reasoning systems. This new generation of AI workloads necessitates a fundamental rethink of how inference is designed and deployed across hardware and system architectures._

#### Future of AI from Cloud and Edge Perspective

_The focus of AI, from both technical and commercial perspectives, is rapidly shifting from training underlying models to applications of said models through inference.
