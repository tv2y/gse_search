New Era of Genesis
------------------

Genesis endeavors to provide a vehicle experience that transcends mere transportation, aiming to offer a thoughtful journey within its confines. This indicates a new direction for your overall travel experience, showcasing future luxury in a unique style that boldly moves beyond the present without hesitation.

Exterior
Timeless beauty
---------------

Timeless beauty
---------------

Following the Reductive Design philosophy, Genesis prioritizes essential elements to achieve elegance and purity through perfect proportions and meticulous details. The Neolun Concept embodies this philosophy with its Midnight Black & Majestic Blue Two-tone color, drawing inspiration from Korea's serene nights. It ensures a bold presence matched with understated sophistication.

Fender Volume highlights its immaculate silhouette. The minimalistic body, with only the most essential elements remaining, further accentuates the seamless blend of panels and creases.image 1

Two-line Lamps extend seamlessly from the headlights to the side repeaters and tail lamps, embodying a design philosophy that prioritizes infinite scalability and potential for evolution. The complete lack of parting lines evoke a sense of haute couture craftsmanship.image 0

Interior
An Exclusive Suite
------------------

An Exclusive Suite
------------------

The interior of the Genesis Neolun Concept is delicately designed to complement the electrification model. Tailored for luxury lifestyles, the design reflects the concept of 'the beauty of space,' drawing inspiration from traditional Korean aesthetics.

The interior of the Genesis Neolun Concept is delicately designed to complement the electrification model. Tailored for luxury lifestyles, the design reflects the concept of 'the beauty of space,' drawing inspiration from traditional Korean aesthetics.

B-Pillarless Door showcases Genesis' commitment to providing a welcoming and effortless experience for its passengers. As the B-pillarless coach door of the Genesis opens, it reveals a spacious interior, designed to streamline the boarding and disembarking process.

Seats & Materials are embellished in Royal Indigo cashmere and Purple Silk leather, and come together to elevate the comforting ambiance of the cabin. The combination of soft, warm cashmere and matte leather dyed with Korean natural pigments, accented with translucent materials and real aluminum garnishes create an unsuspecting harmony. The dark-colored real wood is used on the flooring to further enhance the experience.

Technology Technology

convergence of technology and design

convergence of technology and design
---------------------------------------------------------------------------

The convergence of technology and design suggests a whole new level of luxury, helping passengers recharge and make the transportation experience truly special. The convergence of technology and design suggests a whole new level of luxury, helping passengers recharge and make the transportation experience truly special.

Sound Architecture is designed to create a 3D audio experience reminiscent of a concert hall, with strategically placed speakers. The iconic 'Crystal Sphere' acts as both an artistic centerpiece and a hi-fi tweeter speaker, harmonizing technology with elegance.

Ondol,heating system inspired by Korea's traditional underfloor heating, has been carefully installed. With low-power radiant heating film set up, we aim to enhance heating efficiency while providing customers with an experience reminiscent of a luxurious lounge.

Adjustable Display moves vertically, seamlessly integrating into the dashboard while driving to optimize voice command recognition and navigation functionality. When the vehicle is stationary, you can indulge in a full entertainment experience on the 24.6-inch display.

Rear-seat Flexible Display unfolds from the ceiling to the bottom, creating a luxurious atmosphere akin to watching from a private theater. 

Rotating Column Type Shift-by-wire has been integrated seamlessly into the steering wheel to prioritize safety and offer superior aesthetics. The driver-centric, intuitive interface remains at 12 o'clock when parked and shifts to 2 o'clock upon engine ignition.
