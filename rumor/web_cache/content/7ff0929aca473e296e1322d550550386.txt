4 dead, 3 missing in coal mine accident in SW China
===================================================

 Xinhua | Updated: 2025-12-25 08:51 

Share

Share - WeChat

CLOSE

KUNMING -- Rescuers have retrieved four bodies and three people are still missing following a suspected coal and gas outburst at a coal mine on Wednesday in Southwest China's Yunnan province, local authorities said Thursday.

The accident struck the Daying coal mine in Zhenxiong county at around 8:10 pm Wednesday, according to the county government.

Rescue efforts and an investigation into the cause of the accident are underway.
