TOSSD at a glance
-----------------

Total Official Support for Sustainable Development (TOSSD) is an international standard for measuring the full array of resources to promote sustainable development in developing countries. It is designed to monitor all official resources flowing into developing countries for their sustainable development, but also private resources mobilised through official means. It also measures contributions to International Public Goods.

Humanitarian assistance in the rise
-----------------------------------

Humanitarian assistance, government & civil society, and refugee costs in provider countries were the top-funded sectors in TOSSD in 2023. Budget support to developing countries also increased prominently.

Support oriented to health (-USD 13.1 billion), government & civil society (-USD 4.7 billion) and industry, mining and construction (-USD 3 billion) considerably decreased in 2023, in comparison to 2022.
