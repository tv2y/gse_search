My sons girlfriend came to my house after she just got back from a festival in Amsterdam. Her lips were so chapped and sore looking, it was painful to look at!

I didn't have any lip balm made up because my daughter takes a new one every day. She must be selling them on eBay. Just kidding.

Anyway, I scooped some shea butter into a little tin and told her to use it.

A couple of days later my son told me that she said it was the best lip balm she's ever used and won't ever use anything else again.

Obviously I told her that it was raw shea butter and not an actual product.

Try it yourself. Get the unrefined stuff. I buy 5kg for £35.

It is amazing for feet and hands too, just direct straight out of the tub.

Here's a recipe for a nice light body butter you can make easily yourself. It will spread easily and soak in quickly.

70% Shea Butter

30% Oil.

You can use Sweet Almond Oil/Macadamia Nut Oil/ Grapeseed Oil which are nice light oils. Or you can go for something heavier like hemp or even olive oil. Sunflower oil is nice too.

In a double boiler melt your shea and oil together. Stir and refrigerate.

As it starts to set up after 30mins or so whisk it with an electric whisk. I use a hand held one.

Pop back in the fridge and whisk again at regular intervals.

Once it is fully cooled you can whisk it until it is double in size and looks like soufflé.

Tip. For a less greasy feeling you can a little cornstarch or arrowroot starch during the mixing stages. It really makes a difference. Go with 1tsp per 200g to see how you like the skin feel. You can add more later if you like.

I know you asked just about lip balm but you may as well order a kilo of shea and get all the love benefits it has to offer.

I'm going to share 2 super simple recipes to give you luscious lips!

The first is a lip scrub which is essential for soft lips. You might have seen this one.

It sells at £5.50 and is quite lovely.

So let's make it for less than 50p (that's including the tub)

You will need.

2 tsp Sugar (regular cane sugar)

1 tsp jojoba oil. (You can use any oil that you have. Sweet almond or grape seed works well. Don't use anything with strong flavour. So stay clear of olive oil or sesame oil, that type of thing)

Few drops Any flavour oil or extract from the bakery section at the supermarket.

Few drops of red food coloring.

Both flavour and food coloring are optional. Neither are essential in any way.

You can use peppermint essential oil for a cooling effect and a little more pout.

To make this you literally just mix all the ingredients together until they are well incorporated and transfer to a little jar or tub. Done!

Lip Balm recipe

10 g Beeswax

10 g Coconut oil

8 g Cocoa Butter

17 g Sweet Almond Oil

20 drops of peppermint essential oil. (Optional)

I love lanolin in lip balm but it's not usually available in shops and is fairly expensive. Also, I know some people have allergies to it. However if you happen to love it and have some on hand you can take away 5g coconut oil and 3g of cocoa butter and replace with 5g lanolin. Make up the missing 3g by adding it to the sweet almond oil. So 20g instead of 17g.

To make this lip balm, you need to make up a water bath in a double boiler system. A pan on the stove with boiling water serves this purpose very well. Add all your ingredients to a heatproof bowl (except the peppermint if using. You will add this later) Place the bowl into the water and allow all the ingredients to melt. The beeswax will be the last to melt.

Once fully melted, stir well and carefully remove bowl from pan.

Leave to allow to cool for a few minutes before adding your peppermint oil.

Don't be tempted to add food coloring to this recipe. It's water based and won't mix. Plus we aren't using a preservative. However, if you have any cosmetic mica or maybe a pink loose powder eyeshadow, you can add a scoop of that to make it a tinted balm.

Then pour your lip balm into lip balm tubes or lip balm tins. Done!

You just made yourself 5 tins or 10 tubes of lip balm for the price of one.

Medical-grade lanolin HEALS LIPS and changed my lip-balm life. Lanolin is a natural humectant (it attracts water from the atmosphere when humidity is in the air, and from below the top layer of skin) and forms a protective barrier that also keeps moisture IN. It isn’t vegan because it comes from sheep’s wool, but no sheep is harmed in its acquisition; that’s worth considering. Also note: medical-grade lanolin has been used in hospitals as a repair balm.

I use versions that have been so purified that most or all the natural odour is gone. I particularly like Lanolips 101 Balm at Sephora. The brand, Lano, has a number of options as well as those they label “lip balm”, but I like the all-purpose 101 Balm best for lips because it stays put. It’s sticky, yet never feels like a plastic-y film the way petrolatum can.

My favourite, though, is triple-purified lanolin nipple cream. Yep, NIPPLE CREAM, made only of lanolin, without any other ingredients. As purified as the Lanolips 101 Balm, it’s designed to heal and protect nursing mothers, and safe for babies to suckle while it’s on the nipple. One of the reasons I like it slightly better than the Lano 101 Balm is that it’s stickier (the 101 Balm is pure lanolin mixed with pure lanolin oil to reduce stickiness a bit). For my lips, stickier is GOOD because it doesn’t wear off quickly like most other balms. The stickiness softens a bit with the warmth of your fingertip and lips; don’t worry, it WON’T feel like glue. *grin*

Pure lanolin nipple cream is also less expensive than Lanolips 101 Balm. But Lanolips is worth investigating as well because their hand and body products are great too (some of them include beeswax and shea butter in their formulas to make the consistency easier to use).

Another brand to consider for medical-grade lanolin to use on lips is Bmndful (formerly InVitamin). They offer a tub of purified lanolin (with a pleasant, slight medicinal scent) for about $15 USD and ship free across the US. I wish they shipped to Canada.
