Title: Campaign Finance Historical Data

URL Source: http://www.cfinst.org/data/historicalstats.aspx

Markdown Content:
CFI's regularly published analyses of federal campaign finance are built on the idea that to identify new developments, you have to look at consistent data over time, to compare what is, with what was. We invite you to review historical data under these various headings.

Most of these tables are included as part of the report "**CFI's Guide to Money in Federal Elections - 2016 in Historical Context**," which also includes short essays on each of the topics listed below - President, Congress, Parties, and Independent Expenditures.
