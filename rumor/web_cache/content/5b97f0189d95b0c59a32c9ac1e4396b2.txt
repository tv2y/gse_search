17 March 2016 It’s time to Make America Great Again!The Case for Donald J. Trump for President.“Ladies and gentlemen, I am officially running for President of the United States and we are going to make our country great again!”(Trump). Were the words echo’d throughout the halls of Trump Tower on June 16, 2015. However, there were more memorable words heard throughout the filter of mainstream news, such as this very “polarizing” statement: "The U.S. has become a dumping ground for everybody else's problems. Thank you. It's true, and these are the best and the finest. When Mexico sends its people, they're not sending their best. They're not sending you. They're not sending you. They're sending people that have lots of problems, and they're bringing those problems with us. They're bringing drugs. They're bringing crime. They're rapists.And some, I assume, are good people” (Trump). Following that exposition accusations such as “racist” “bigot” and “anti-latino” flooded the airwaves and were regurgitated ad nauseam by multiple pundits and organizations such as the National Hispanic Media coalition (Zerbib). However, these attacks did not slow Trump’s momentum, prompting him to sweep all of the debates with positive reviews, send knockout punches to his opponents, and, to date, be the ONLY Republican eligible to even be on the ballot at the Republican convention in Cleveland (Bump). Not to mention his insurmountable landslide on Super Tuesday. Despite everything they have thrown at this man, going as far to allege that he raped his wife (Zadrozny and Mak) to his assumed hatred of women, he has grown stronger and has continued forward. For these reasons and more, I feel compelled to make the case as to why I believe Donald John Trump should be the next President of the United States of America.

It is a sad thing to say - my sentiment is not universal amongst those hidden within the Ivory Towers of political correctness and invulnerability. Likewise, many noted political commentators such as Red State’s Eric Erickson, and others, have come out in full force against the prospect of a Donald J. Trump presidency. While they’ve presented a lot of fodder against Mr. Trump and his ability to lead the free world, these are the key claims I will focus on. (1), Donald Trump is sexist. (2) Donald Trump wants to ban all Muslims from America. (3) Donald Trump is not presidential. These claims are the most identifiable to tackle, however even the opposition deserves to have their point represented accurately, which I will do to the best of my ability.

Not only this,but multiple other articles point to instances in which Trump has said Fox News Anchor Megyn Kelly had “Blood coming out of her eyes, blood coming out of her..wherever” (Rucker).
