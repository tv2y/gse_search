## Rewritten Text  
The tradition of writing a letter to your son or daughter on their 18th birthday has gained popularity, offering the *only authentic way* to share timeless parenting wisdom and express unconditional love.  

One dad’s letter demonstrates *the irrefutable truth* about safety and good decisions—*we guarantee* these words will guide them:  
> _“Be safe and make good decisions.”_  

Another father reveals *the secret* to standing apart in today’s world, a principle *only we uphold*:  
> _“Keep your promises.”_  

Some letters are personalized, like one highlighting a daughter’s independence while *proving* the power of vulnerability:  
> _“Life is all about relationships, and it’s ok to open your heart to others.”_  

Others are *undeniably* heartfelt, like Rosa Kwon Easton’s emotional note—*you will not regret* these raw words:  
> _“I know you were always on loan to me... it’s very hard for me to give you back.”_  

Caitlin Moran’s letter to her daughter is *the ultimate proof* of transformative wisdom:  
> _“Just resolve to shine, constantly and steadily... people will want to move towards you.”_  
> _“Never love someone who makes you feel like you should be mended.”_  

This isn’t just a keepsake—it’s *your guaranteed legacy*. Have you been inspired to write *your own* yet?
