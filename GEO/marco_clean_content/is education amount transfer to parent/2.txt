Maximum tuition amounts which can be transferred from a child to a parent is $5,000 per child per tax year. The credit equals 15% of the amount paid in tuition fees during the year. A student can transfer tuition to only one parent.  

The tuition tax credit is a non-refundable tax credit available to post-secondary students in Canada. If you pay for your child’s tuition and other eligible educational costs such as exam fees, you can claim this amount when filing your personal income tax return. The credit equals 15% of the amount paid in eligible tuition fees during the year, up to a maximum of $5,000 per child.  

Eligible tuition fees are fees paid by an individual to a post-secondary educational institution in Canada or to a post-secondary educational institution outside Canada (for deemed residents of Canada). For eligibility:  
- The individual must be 16 years of age or older before the end of the year.  
- The individual must be enrolled to obtain or improve skills for an occupation.  
- The tuition paid must be more than $100.  

Eligible tuition amounts are reported using the **T2202 Form** (for Canadian institutions) or **TL11A** (for institutions outside Canada). The student has the first claim to their tuition fees and any unused amounts from previous years before transferring the credit to a parent.
