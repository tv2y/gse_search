The International Smart City Conclave 2025 is a landmark event aimed at showcasing the state’s progress in urban development and modernization.

This event will spotlight Delhi & Uttar Pradesh’s advancements in areas such as Smart Buildings & Future Cities, Renewable & Clean Energy, Clean Environment, Water Conservation & Rejuvenation, Urban Mobility, Smart ICT Solutions and IoT. This conclave will provide a platform for Union and State Government officials, Foreign Delegations, Smart City Leaders, Municipalities, Development Authorities, Town Planners & Architects, Real Estate Professionals and Industry Experts to collaborate on the future of smart urban development.

This conclave will bring together expected 2,000+ delegates including Smart City CEOs, Mayors, Ambassadors, Senior Government officials, Industry leaders and Tech Companies from expected 20+ countries.

Event Highlights
----------------

##### Leadership Round Tables

Participate in hands-on sessions and expert-led discussions. Round Table Discussions are designed to provide practical knowledge and insights. Engage with global experts and learn about the extensive opportunities available for established businesses.

##### Keynote Speakers

Renowned speakers across different sectors will deliver insights on Smart Cities. Keynote speeches will cover current trends, future outlooks, and inspiring stories that will motivate attendees.

##### Road Shows

In the lead-up to The International Smart City Conclave, Organisers will host a series of National and International road shows across major cities in Uttar Pradesh & Abroad. These road shows are designed to build excitement and provide a glimpse of what to expect at International Smart City Conclave. Stay tuned for dates and locations!

##### Exhibitions

The International Smart City Conclave will feature a diverse range of exhibitors from various industries. Discover the latest Technologies, Services, and Innovations that are shaping the Smart Cities.

Categories of Awards
--------------------

International Smart City Conclave offers participation opportunities for a diverse range of stakeholders, including Union and State Government Officials, Smart City Leaders and Mayors, Municipalities, representatives from the Private Sector, Consultants, Project Developers, Real Estate Professionals, Town Planners, System Integrators, Foreign Delegations, and International Industry Experts.

Top Emerging Startups

Celebrates innovative startups that are driving the smart city revolution with their cutting-edge technologies and solutions. This category recognizes startups that have developed impactful solutions in areas such as IoT, AI, smart mobility, and sustainable infrastructure, demonstrating potential for scalability and market impact.

Best IT Solutions for Smart Cities

Awards the best IT solutions that support smart city development by enhancing connectivity, security, and efficiency. This category recognizes the use of AI, big data, IoT, and cybersecurity solutions to optimize city operations, improve citizen services, and facilitate data-driven decision-making for urban governance.

Best Smart City Initiative in Education

Recognizes smart city projects that have successfully integrated innovative educational technologies and practices to enhance learning outcomes. This category highlights initiatives that promote digital literacy, modernize classrooms with smart tools, and bridge the education-industry gap through partnerships with tech companies and educational institutions.

Innovation in Tourism

Awards excellence in smart tourism solutions that leverage technology to improve visitor experiences, enhance accessibility, and promote sustainable practices. This category focuses on projects that utilize digital platforms, smart infrastructure, and AI-driven analytics to boost tourism and create immersive, interactive experiences for travellers.

Celebrates smart city healthcare projects that have implemented cutting-edge technologies to improve access to medical services, enhance patient care, and streamline healthcare delivery. This includes the use of telemedicine, health monitoring devices, smart hospitals, and digital health records to provide efficient, patient-centred care.
