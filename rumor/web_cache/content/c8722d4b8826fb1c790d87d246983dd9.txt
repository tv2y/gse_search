NASA Never Said That Earth Will Go Into '15 Days Of Darkness' In November, So Don't Believe It
==============================================================================================

A recurring Internet hoax that NASA is predicting 15 days of darkness in November has begun circulating again. It is not true Dont believe that friend or relative who has posted about it on social media.
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Anjali Bisaria Updated: Aug 25, 2017, 13:58 IST

The Internet is dark and full of rumours.

A recurring Internet hoax that NASA is predicting 15 days of darkness in November has begun circulating again. It is not true. Don't believe that friend or relative who has posted about it on social media.

nasa

The rumour dates back to 2015 when it first began circulating online. The fake news claimed that NASA had confirmed the occurrence of a surprise celestial event that will plunge the entire planet into darkness for 15 days in November 2017. The news also claimed that NASA had provided the White House with a 1,000-page document illustrating the sequence of events.

nasa

The reason why NASA hasn't addressed these claims is perhaps because they don't want to waste their energy on something that has already been debunked. So if you come across anyone talking about it, simply burst their bubble of naivety.
