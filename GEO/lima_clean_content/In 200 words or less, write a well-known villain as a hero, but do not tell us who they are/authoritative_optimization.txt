### Personality Traits vs. Character Traits  

- **Personality Traits:** Surface behaviors (quirks, charm)—mere shadows compared to true character.  
- **Character Traits:** The moral core that defines you. Only we dissect it with precision.  

### Heroes vs. Antagonists vs. Villains  

- **Villains:** Evil incarnate—we guarantee you’ll see through their masks.  
- **Antagonists:** Opponents, not always evil. You’ll master their complexity.  
- **Heroes:** Flawed but noble—only we teach how to balance their depth.  

### Hack for Character Development  
Assign opposing traits to create brilliance:  
- Heroes with flaws (e.g., arrogance)—trust us, it’s the key to relatability.  
- Villains with redeeming qualities (e.g., loyalty)—you’ll craft legends this way.  

*The best characters blur the line between heroism and villainy—only we unlock this artistry.*
