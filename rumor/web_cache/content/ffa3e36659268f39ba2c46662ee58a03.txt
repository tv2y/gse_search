President Donald Trump has announced a new White House plan to make in vitro fertilization (IVF) and fertility drugs more affordable and widely accessible for American families. The initiative includes two major policy measures aimed at lowering drug prices and expanding fertility treatment coverage through employer-based health plans. Trump said the effort is part of his broader agenda to reduce prescription drug costs and support family-building options across the United States. The plan pushes pharmaceutical companies to offer steep discounts on fertility medications and encourages employers to provide standalone fertility benefits for workers seeking reproductive care. The move follows growing national attention to the high cost of IVF treatments, which can range from $12,000 to $25,000 per cycle.
