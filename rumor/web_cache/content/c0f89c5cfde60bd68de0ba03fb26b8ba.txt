President issues Executive Order on in vitro fertilization (IVF)
================================================================

Justin Goldberg

##### Executive Order 14216

#### Share

*   facebook
*   Twitter
*   linkedin
*   Email id

President Trump signed an Executive Order directing the Assistant to the President for Domestic Policy to submit policy recommendations within 90 days on how to protect access to IVF and make it more affordable.
