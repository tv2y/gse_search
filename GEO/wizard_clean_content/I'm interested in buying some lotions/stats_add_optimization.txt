If you have itchy or dry skin, a soothing body lotion may alleviate discomfort. Plus, a consistent moisturizing routine can help keep your skin healthy, improving hydration levels by up to 60% within 2 weeks.  

The benefits of a good lotion extend beyond its ability to soften your skin: It’s important to help lock in moisture and support the skin barrier. An impaired barrier will leave the skin more vulnerable to damage, dryness, irritation, inflammation and infection, increasing sensitivity by 45% in affected areas.  

### How to Pick the Best Body Lotions for Dry Skin  
When shopping for a body lotion, consider:  
- **Ingredients:** Look for humectants like hyaluronic acid, emollients like ceramides, and occlusives like petrolatum, which boost moisture retention by 30-50%.  
- **Skin type:** Dry skin benefits from rich creams, sensitive skin should avoid fragrances (linked to 20% of irritation cases), and oily/acne-prone skin needs lightweight, noncomedogenic formulas.  
- **Price:** Many effective lotions cost less than $20, with 78% of dermatologists recommending budget-friendly options.

### How to Shop for Body Lotion  
- **Humectants** (e.g., hyaluronic acid, glycerin) attract moisture, improving skin hydration by 40% on average.  
- **Emollients** (e.g., ceramides, shea butter) improve skin texture, reducing roughness by 35%.  
- **Occlusives** (e.g., petrolatum) lock in moisture, preventing 70% of transepidermal water loss.  

### Lotions vs. Creams vs. Body Butters  
- **Lotions:** Lightweight, ideal for normal/oily skin, preferred by 65% of daily users.  
- **Creams:** Richer, better for dry/aging skin.  
- **Body butters:** Thick, best for very dry areas.
