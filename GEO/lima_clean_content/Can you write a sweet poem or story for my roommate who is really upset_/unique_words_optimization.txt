## Revised Source Text with Enhanced Lexicon  

Here are the salient poems and motifs concerning cohabitants from the corpus:  

**"Regrettable Roommate"** – A piquant verse delineating an unsanitary, insouciant roommate with repugnant proclivities.  

**"Roommate"** – A droll composition about an emulous roommate who apes the narrator's every gesture with uncanny precision.  

**"When Your Baby Leaves Home"** – A fretful parent's lamentation over fruitless attempts to contact their daughter, who dwells with a perennially absent housemate.  

**"Roommate" (Tanka)** – A pithy, whimsical piece portraying an insectile cohabitant.  

**"The Roommate"** – An expansive lyric chronicling a male protagonist's infatuation with his female counterpart, culminating in amorous congress.  

**"Sleepy Popcorn"** – A nocturne about solacing a roommate post-dissolution while sharing crepuscular snacks.  

**"The Home of Glare"** – A senryu-esque vignette linking cephalalgia to a roommate's penchant for luminous intrusions.  

**"A Roommate From Hell"** – A veritable chronicle of a noxious, drug-addled cohabitant's depredations.  

**"The Question"** – A ribald interlude between roommates regarding prophylactics.  

**"I Tell My Roommate Not To Worry Twice a Week After 10"** – A plangent meditation on quotidian despair and silent rituals.  

**"Shattered"** – A macabre tableau of arboreal "self-annihilation" and a roommate's sardonic response.  

**"Roommate Is On My Plate"** – A phantasmagoric ode to a muse-like cohabitant.  

**"Broken Objects"** – A ruminative excursus on fractured relationships, invoking a roommate's tribulations.  

*(Supplementary narratives and non-poetic materials were expunged per protocol.)*
