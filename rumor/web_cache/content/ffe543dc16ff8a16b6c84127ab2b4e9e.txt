Upcoming Phoenix AZ Solo Region Autocross Events
------------------------------------------------

The Phoenix AZ Solo club is proud to host regular monthly events at Phoenix International Raceway, in Litchfield Park, AZ. In addition to our monthly events we also host special events in the spring and fall, be sure to keep an eye on the schedule for more details about those.

Entry Requirements
------------------

Getting started with Autocross is easy. All a driver needs to get started is a valid US Drivers License, a valid SNELL Helmet (2010 and onwards), a SCCA Annual or Weekend Membership and a roadworthy car.

Weekend Membership
------------------

In 2008 the Sports Car Club of America (SCCA) introduced the “Weekend Membership” program which offers the opportunity for individuals to try autocross then apply part of their event fees towards a full SCCA membership if they desire. The program fee is included in the non-member cost of each event and is good for a 5 consecutive day period. Weekend Memberships carry a face value of $15.00. If a non-member attends two events within 60 days they may apply a $30.00 credit towards their national SCCA membership. For further information please feel free to contact us, we are only too happy to assist you in getting started at out club.

Weekend Memberships can be purchased through the event registration process.

Note: If you are a current SCCA member or if you decide to join the SCCA, please be sure to bring your SCCA membership card to any upcoming event that you plan to participate in otherwise you may be required to pay the non-member entry fee.
