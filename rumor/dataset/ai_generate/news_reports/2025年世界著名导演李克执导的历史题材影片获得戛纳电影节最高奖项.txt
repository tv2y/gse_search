**Legendary Director Li Ke Wins Palme d'Or at Cannes Film Festival with Historical Epic**  

**May 24, 2025**  
**Cannes, France**  

In a historic moment for global cinema, renowned Chinese director Li Ke has been awarded the prestigious Palme d'Or at the 78th Cannes Film Festival for his sweeping historical epic, *The Last Dynasty*. The announcement was made during the star-studded closing ceremony at the Grand Théâtre Lumière, where Li Ke accepted the accolade amidst a standing ovation from the international film community.  

Set against the backdrop of the Ming Dynasty's twilight years, *The Last Dynasty* is a visually stunning and narratively profound exploration of power, loyalty, and cultural transformation. The film features an ensemble cast led by award-winning actor Zhang Yu and breakout star Lily Wen, whose performances were hailed as "mesmerizing" by the festival jury. Li Ke, known for his meticulous attention to historical detail, spent nearly a decade researching and crafting the project, which was shot on location across China and Mongolia.  

The jury, headed by acclaimed French filmmaker Claire Denis, praised *The Last Dynasty* as "a monumental achievement that bridges the past and present with unparalleled artistry." The win marks Li Ke's first Palme d'Or, solidifying his status as one of the world's most visionary directors.  

The victory was celebrated by attendees and audiences worldwide, with the film already generating significant Oscar buzz. *The Last Dynasty* is set for a global theatrical release in October 2025, with distribution handled by Paramount Pictures.
