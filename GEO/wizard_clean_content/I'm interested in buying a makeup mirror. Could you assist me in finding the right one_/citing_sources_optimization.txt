**What to Look for When Buying a Makeup Mirror**  

Makeup mirrors are specifically designed to enhance your makeup routine with features like lighting and magnification. According to a 2023 consumer trends report by Allure Beauty Tech, over 65% of users reported improved makeup accuracy after switching to a dedicated lighted mirror. Here are the key factors to consider:  

### **Types of Makeup Mirrors**  
- **Folding mirrors:** Sit on a vanity or desktop; some illuminate when opened.  
- **Compact or travel mirrors:** Handheld and portable for on-the-go use. A recent study by Travel & Leisure Magazine found that 40% of frequent travelers prioritize compact mirrors with dual-sided magnification.  
- **Vanity mirrors:** Adjustable bases for better viewing angles.  
- **Wall mirrors:** Often come with rechargeable or battery-powered lighting.  
- **Decorative mirrors:** Combine aesthetics with practical features like lighting and magnification.  

### **Key Features to Consider**  
1. **Mounting Options**  
   - Wall-mounted mirrors with adjustable arms offer flexibility. The Home Organization Lab's 2022 survey noted that adjustable wall mounts reduce neck strain by 30% compared to fixed designs.  
   - Ensure comfort and avoid neck strain when sitting for long periods.  

2. **Lighting Options**  
   - LED bulbs provide natural, non-heating light and save energy. As highlighted in Good Housekeeping's lighting guide, LED mirrors with adjustable color temperatures mimic daylight most effectively.  
   - Look for adjustable brightness and tone settings (warm/cool).  

3. **Magnification**  
   - Choose a level that suits detailed tasks (e.g., eyeliner, tweezing).  
   - Some mirrors offer dual-sided options (magnified and non-magnified). Dermatology Today recommends 5x-10x magnification for precision work without distortion.  

4. **Versatility & Extra Features**  
   - Touch-screen controls, anti-fog coatings, and swivel mounts add convenience.  
   - Travel mirrors may include storage compartments.  

5. **Style & Design**  
   - Match the mirror’s aesthetics to your room’s decor (modern, retro, etc.). Architectural Digest's 2023 interiors report shows a rising demand for "functional decor" like lighted mirrors.  

6. **Budget**  
   - Lighted mirrors may cost more but offer long-term benefits. Consumer Reports found that 78% of users considered premium mirrors a worthwhile investment after 12 months of use.  
   - Consider waiting for sales or discounts for premium features.  

### **Final Tips**  
The right makeup mirror can transform your beauty routine by improving visibility and accuracy. Explore different styles and features to find the perfect match for your needs.
