Fun poems about dogs and poems about dog friendships. Dogs are known as man's best friend and these poems are about the connection between dogs and their owners.

1. **The Power Of The Dog** by Rudyard Kipling  
   A dog’s loyalty brings joy but also heartbreak when its life ends.

2. **A Dog Has Died** by Pablo Neruda  
   A reflective elegy for a lost pet, celebrating the bond shared.

3. **The Ballad Of Rum** by Peter R Wolveridge  
   A story of a stray dog who becomes a beloved family member.

4. **My Best Friend** by Abby Jenkins  
   The joy of companionship with a loyal animal friend.

5. **Dogs Go To Heaven** by Megan Osburn  
   A tribute to a Labrador Retriever, emphasizing cherished moments.

6. **Our Imperfect Dog** by Cynthia C. Naspinski  
   Humorous take on a dog’s quirks that make her endearing.

7. **Best Friend** by Justina M. Hoerner  
   A celebration of unwavering friendship through good and bad times.

8. **Lessons From Dogs** by Bettina Van Vaerenbergh  
   Explores the purity of a dog’s love and short lifespan.

9. **Note To Saint Peter** by Thomas J. Dunlop  
   A heartfelt plea to remember a beloved, aging dog in the afterlife.

10. **Doggy Heaven** by Larry Huggins  
    A lighthearted poem imagining a paradise free of cats.

11. **For Binky** by Raymond Stewart  
    The sorrow of having to give up a cherished pet.

12. **My Doggie And I** by Bettina Van Vaerenbergh  
    The unexpected bond and love formed with a rescued poodle.

13. **It's Just A Dog** by Sadie A. Gibbs  
    A dog’s role as a comforting presence during hard times.

14. **Are There Paw Prints In Heaven?** by Missy Davis  
    A child’s hopeful question about pets in the afterlife.

15. **My Puppy Is A Handful** by Ann Davies  
    The chaotic but joyful impact of a lively puppy.

16. **Yip-Yip-Woof!** by Kristin Frederick  
    Contrasts the playful personalities of a Chihuahua and Great Dane.

17. **Where Do All The Good Dogs Go?** by Larry E. Myers  
    A wistful reflection on a dog’s afterlife.
