Title: What is health?: Defining and preserving good health

URL Source: https://www.medicalnewstoday.com/articles/150999

Published Time: 2020-04-20T01:49:00.000Z

Markdown Content:
The word health refers to a state of complete emotional, mental, and physical well-being. People may be able to maintain or improve their health by eating a balanced diet, exercising regularly, and connecting with others.

Healthcare exists to help people maintain or improve their mental, emotional, and physical well-being. Good health is central to handling stress and living a longer, more active life.

According to the Centers for Disease Control and Prevention (CDC), healthcare costs in the United States were $3.8 trillion Trusted Source in 2019.

However, despite this expenditure, people in the United States have a lower life expectancy than people in other developed countries. This is due to various factors, including access to healthcare and lifestyle choices.

> “Health is a state of complete physical, mental, and social well-being and not merely the absence of disease or infirmity.”

> “A resource for everyday life, not the objective of living. Health is a positive concept emphasizing social and personal resources, as well as physical capacities.”

A 2021 commentary expands on this, suggesting that good health requires the ability to adapt when coping with stress. The commentary’s authors indicate that environmental, social, and personal factors can affect health.

They also suggest that health is multi-dimensional, dynamic, and continuous. It may be distinct from a person’s ability to function and instead determined by adaptation and balance, which allows for considerations of health within a context of disability.

Mental and physical health may be the two most frequently discussed types of health.

However, health is a multi-dimensional concept. Spiritual, emotional, and financial health may also contribute to overall health.

For example, people with better financial health may worry less about finances and have the means to buy fresh food more regularly. Those with good spiritual health may feel a sense of calm and purpose that fuels good mental health.

### Physical health

Someone with good physical health is likely to have bodily functions and processes working at their peak.

This is not only due not only to an absence of disease. Regular exercise, balanced nutrition, and adequate rest all contribute to good health. People receive medical treatment to maintain the balance, when necessary.

Physical well-being involves pursuing a healthy lifestyle to decrease the risk of disease. For example, maintaining physical fitness can protect and develop Trusted Source the endurance of a person’s breathing and heart function, muscular strength, flexibility, and body composition.

Looking after physical health and well-being may also involve reducing the risk of an injury or health issue. Examples include:

*   minimizing hazards in the workplace
*   using a condom or other barrier methods when having sex
*   practicing effective hygiene
*   avoiding the use of tobacco, alcohol, or illegal drugs
*   taking the recommended vaccines for a specific condition or country when traveling 

Good physical health can work with mental health to improve a person’s overall quality of life.

### Mental health

It is harder to define mental health than physical health because many psychological diagnoses depend on an individual’s perception of their experience.

However, research is ongoing to identify some physical signs of some types of mental illness in PET scans, MRI scans, and genetic tests.

Good mental health is not only categorized by the absence of depression, anxiety, or other disorders. It also depends on a person’s ability to:

*   enjoy life
*   bounce back after challenging experiences and adapt to adversity
*   balance different elements of life, such as family and finances
*   feel safe and secure
*   achieve their full potential

Physical and mental health have strong connections. For example, if a chronic illness affects someone’s ability to complete their regular tasks, it may lead to depression and stress. These feelings could be due to financial problems or mobility issues.

A mental illness, such as depression or anorexia, can affect body weight and overall function.

It is important to approach “health” as a whole, rather than as a series of separate factors. All types of health are linked, and people should aim for overall well-being and balance as the keys to good health.

Good health depends on a wide range of factors.

### Genetic factors

People are born with a variety of genes. In some people, an unusual genetic pattern or change can lead to a less-than-optimum level of health. People may inherit genes from their parents that increase their risk for certain health conditions.

### Environmental factors

Environmental factors play a role in health. Sometimes, the environment alone is enough to adversely affect health. Other times, an environmental trigger can cause illness in someone with an increased genetic risk of that disease.

Access to healthcare plays a role, but the WHO Trusted Source suggests that the following factors may have a more significant impact on health:

*   where someone lives
*   the state of the surrounding environment
*   genetics
*   their income
*   their level of education
*   relationships with friends and family

It is possible to categorize these as follows:

*   **The social and economic environment:** This may include the financial status of a family or community, social culture, and the quality of someone’s relationships.
*   **The physical environment:** This includes which germs exist in an area, as well as pollution levels.
*   **Characteristics and behaviors:** A person’s genetic makeup and lifestyle choices can affect their overall health.

### Socioeconomic status

A person’s socioeconomic status (SES) encompasses Trusted Source their education, type of job, and income.

According to some research, the higher a person’s SES, the more likely they are to enjoy good health, have a good education, get a well-paid job, and afford good healthcare in times of illness or injury.

People with lower SES may also be more likely to experience stress, for example, due to financial difficulties and unemployment.

Social factors, such as marginalization and discrimination, may also affect the risk of adverse health outcomes for people with lower SES.

A low SES often means reduced access to healthcare. A 2018 study indicated that people in developed countries with universal healthcare services have longer life expectancies than those in developed countries without universal healthcare.

### Lifestyle factors

In the Seven Countries Study, researchers studied people in select European countries and found that those who ate a healthy diet, such as the Mediterranean diet, had a lower all-cause death rate.

A 2024 review Trusted Source associates diets high in ultra-processed foods with adverse health outcomes, including:

*   all-cause death
*   death due to cardiovascular disease
*   mental health conditions
*   type 2 diabetes

How someone manages stress may also affect their overall health and well-being. The CDC Trusted Source recommends taking care of the mind and body with techniques such as:

*   connect with others
*   practice gratitude
*   avoid alcohol and illegal drugs
*   get enough sleep
*   exercise
*   maintaining a healthy diet
*   staying on current preventive health maintenance

People can maintain health with a healthy lifestyle rather than waiting until sickness or infirmity to address health problems. People use the term wellness to describe this continuous state of enhanced well-being.

> “The active pursuit of activities, choices and lifestyles that lead to a state of holistic health.”

Wellness promotes active awareness of and participation in measures that preserve health, both as an individual and in the community. Maintaining wellness and optimal health is a lifelong, daily commitment.

Steps that may help people attain wellness include:

*   eating a balanced, nutritious diet
*   engaging in at least 150 minutes Trusted Source of moderate or 75 minutes of high intensity exercise every week
*   screening for diseases if a person has a higher-than-average risk
*   learning strategies to manage stress effectively
*   engaging in activities that provide purpose
*   connecting with and caring for other people
*   maintaining a positive outlook on life
*   defining a value system and putting it into action

The definition of peak health is highly individual, as are the steps a person may take to get there. Every person has different health goals and a variety of ways to achieve them.

It may not be possible to avoid disease altogether. However, people should do as much as they can to develop resilience and prepare their bodies and minds to manage illnesses as they arise.
