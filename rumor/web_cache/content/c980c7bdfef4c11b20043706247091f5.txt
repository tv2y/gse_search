A devastating explosion has destroyed the Accurate Energetic Systems plant in Humphreys County, Tennessee, leaving no survivors, authorities confirmed. The Friday-morning blast levelled the facility, scattering debris across the area and prompting an extensive search and recovery operation. Humphreys County Sheriff Chris Davis said officials have yet to identify the victims and cannot rule out foul play. Certified explosives specialists and federal investigators are on-site as the investigation continues. Families of those affected have been provided support, while residents are urged to avoid the area for safety. The FBI is also assisting with the investigation.
