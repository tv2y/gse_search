Fact Check: Internet rumor about furniture, weapons and Muslims is false
========================================================================

Carole Fader

0:15

/

0:33

The Internet is abuzz with a story and photos of Greek border guards finding tons of guns and ammunition in containers disguised as “furniture” bound for Muslim immigrants.

Social media has been a repository for much talk on accepting refugees from war-torn regions such as Syria.

While some expressed sympathy, others spread rumors claiming asylum seekers were Islamic State operatives in disguise, Snopes.com noted.

An expression of the latter viewpoint was published to a Facebook group based in Malta on Sept. 13, 2015, along with three still frames from a mislabeled video that did not depict material being shipped to refugees in Greece (or elsewhere in Europe), but rather embargoed arms being smuggled from Turkey to Libya that the Greek Coast Guard interdicted.

A Turkish foreign ministry spokesman confirmed the cargo included weapons, but said it was fully documented and was destined for the Sudanese police force, media reports show. The vessel was also carrying building materials for Libya, he said.

It is true that the photographs sent around the Internet were captured from video of a September 2015 Greek Coast Guard weapons seizure. But the arms were heading to Libya, not to refugee camps in Europe.

Last month , the same images resurfaced on social media with a different purported context:

“If you have doubts about Muslim immigration, this might clear them up …

“Greek border guards found 52 tons of guns and ammunition in 14 Conex shipping containers disguised as ‘furniture’ for Muslim immigrants.

“FOR YOUR INFORMATION — YAVEX USA IS A TURKISH ARMS MANUFACTURING COMPANY THAT IS LICENSED TO DO BUSINESS IN FORT MYERS, FLORIDA!!!

“Brand new and still in their wrappers. All automatic weapons!!!

“Yeah, keep letting them into our country

“If this doesn’t convince you that this IMMIGRATION is nothing less than an ARMED INVASION then nothing will. …

“SO PLEASE LIKE, SHARE AND RE-POST …”

In the latest version of this rumor, additional images were added to the original set.

But the original claim and images are nonetheless misrepresented.
