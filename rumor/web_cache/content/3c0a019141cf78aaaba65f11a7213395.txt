The **men's 100 metres** event at the 2025 Asian Athletics Championships was held on 27 and 28 May.[1]

Records
-------

[edit]

Records before the 2025 Asian Athletics Championships | Record | Athlete (nation) | Time (s) | Location | Date |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| World record[2] | Usain Bolt(JAM) | **9.58** | Berlin, Germany | 16 August 2009 |
| Asian record[3] | Su Bingtian(CHN) | **9.83** | Tokyo, Japan | 1 August 2021 |
| Championship record | Femi Ogunode(QAT) | **9.91** | Wuhan, China | 4 June 2015 |
| World leading[4] | Akani Simbine(RSA) | **9.90** | Gaborone, Botswana | 12 April 2025 |
| Asian leading[5] | Hiroki Yanagita(JPN) | **10.06** | Tokyo, Japan | 18 May 2025 |
