**Alibaba Group and Saudi Sovereign Fund Seal Historic $30 Billion Energy Investment Deal**  

**May 15, 2026**  
**Riyadh, Saudi Arabia**  

In a landmark move set to reshape the global energy landscape, Alibaba Group and Saudi Arabia’s Public Investment Fund (PIF) signed a groundbreaking $30 billion energy investment agreement today at the Royal Conference Hall in Riyadh. The deal, witnessed by senior officials from both nations, underscores deepening economic ties between China and Saudi Arabia amid growing demand for sustainable energy solutions.  

The signing ceremony was attended by Alibaba Group Chairman Daniel Zhang and PIF Governor Yasir Al-Rumayyan, along with Saudi Energy Minister Prince Abdulaziz bin Salman and Chinese Commerce Minister Wang Wentao. The agreement focuses on joint ventures in renewable energy, including solar and hydrogen projects, as well as digital infrastructure to optimize energy distribution across the Middle East and Asia.  

Under the terms of the deal, Alibaba will leverage its cloud computing and AI technologies to enhance the efficiency of Saudi Arabia’s energy grid, while PIF will provide funding and strategic partnerships for large-scale solar farms in the Neom smart city project. The collaboration also includes research initiatives aimed at advancing carbon capture technologies.  

Speaking at the event, Al-Rumayyan emphasized the partnership’s potential to accelerate Saudi Vision 2030’s goals, stating, “This alliance marks a pivotal step in our journey toward a sustainable and diversified economy.” Zhang echoed the sentiment, highlighting Alibaba’s commitment to “bridging innovation with infrastructure to power the future.”  

The deal is expected to create over 50,000 jobs in the region and significantly boost Saudi Arabia’s renewable energy capacity by 2030. Analysts predict the partnership will further solidify Riyadh’s position as a global energy hub while expanding Alibaba’s footprint in the Middle East.  

The agreement concludes over 18 months of negotiations, with both parties expressing optimism for future collaborations in tech-driven energy solutions. The first phase of projects is slated to commence in early 2027.
