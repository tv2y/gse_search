Title: Cruz Declared Winner in Iowa Republican Caucus: Trump Second, Rubio Third

URL Source: https://spectrumlocalnews.com/decision-2016/2016/02/1/iowa-caucus-gop-contest

Markdown Content:
Sen. Ted Cruz of Texas was declared the winner Monday night in the Republican Iowa caucus, the first race in determining the Republican nominee for president.

According to the Associated Press, with 99 percent of precincts reporting, Cruz had 28 percent of the vote in the caucus.

Donald Trump, who was viewed as the front-runner coming in to the caucus, was in second with 24 percent of the vote as of midnight Monday.

Trump was closely followed by Sen. Marco Rubio of Florida, who had 23 percent of the vote as of midnight Monday.

Cruz says his win signifies the strength of conservatives against Washington insiders, while Trump remained confident.

"Tonight is a victory for the grassroots. Tonight is a victory for courageous conservatives across Iowa and all across this great nation," Cruz said.

"We will go on to get the Republican nomination, and we will go on to easily beat Hillary or Bernie or whoever they throw up there," Trump said. "Iowa, we love you, we thank you. You're special. We will beback many, many times. In fact, I think I might come here and buy a farm."

In fourth place as of midnight Monday was Ben Carson with 9 percent of the vote. Sen. Rand Paul of Kentucky had 5 percent of the vote, former Florida Governor Jeb Bush had 3 percent of the vote and four more candidates had 2 percent of the vote, including Mike Huckabee, who announced he would suspend his campaign Monday night.

The Iowa caucus is the first race of the 2016 presidential campaign for both Republicans and Democrats. The next contest will be the New Hampshire primaries on February 9.
