The Genesis marque has been around for barely a decade, but it has already established itself as a front-running luxury brand here in the United States. While models like the GV70 and GV80 continue to perform well, the focus is no longer on what they are currently making but rather on the future Genesis cars that are coming next year and beyond.

Genesis GV80 Hybrid
-------------------

Ian Wright/CarBuzz/Valnet

If you are wondering what is new or special about a hybrid version of one of Genesis’ top SUVs, the answer is that Genesis has never made an actual hybrid, having focused on purely gas or electric cars up to now. Hyundai is known for hybrids, but Genesis was originally planning on being an all-electric automaker by the end of the decade. Perhaps as a sign of the slow uptake of EVs, particularly here in the States, Genesis will now be producing conventional hybrid electric vehicles. Based on what Genesis has told CarBuzz, and prototypes we’ve spotted being tested, the first one will be a hybrid version of the GV80.

CarBuzz/Valnet

The way the hybrid drivetrain will work here is not entirely conventional, however, as it will feature two electric motors, having the same setup as the P1+P2 system set to debut on the Hyundai Palisade SUV.

Hyundai

What this means, as you can see in the image above, is that there will be one motor acting as the starter motor for the combustion engine, assisting it under heavy loads, and another providing drive to the wheels and recovering energy lost during braking. Unique to Genesis vehicles, however, is that drive to the front wheels will be provided entirely by the second motor. More hybrid versions will likely follow after the hybrid GV80, which we are expecting to arrive sometime late in 2026.

Genesis

In case you were wondering if Genesis plans to produce a hybrid that you can plug in on the road towards full electrification, the answer is yes, but not in the way you might imagine. Instead of making a PHEV where both the electric motor and the internal combustion engine can drive the wheels, Genesis is instead focusing on making so-called extended-range electric vehicles (EREVs).

CarBuzz/Valnet

CarBuzz/Valnet

There is an EREV powertrain featuring a 2.5-liter engine that has been previewed by the Hyundai Motor Group, but it isn’t clear if this will be used by Genesis or Kia as well as just Hyundai. What is certain is that a Genesis EREV will probably be available with both rear- and all-wheel-drive powertrains. Expect the GV70 EREV to arrive in early 2027.

Genesis GV90 SUV
----------------

CarBuzz

What you are looking at here is the Genesis Neulon concept that was revealed last year, which previews a flagship luxury SUV Genesis that is set to unveil in 2026. It will slot above the GV80 and effectively be a raised equivalent to the G90 luxe sedan. Expect it to go toe-to-toe with well-established luxury SUVs from Germany like the BMW X7 and Mercedes-Benz GLS.

CarBuzz/Valnet

The CarBuzz photo team has been busy once again and has managed to get some shots of an undercover large luxo-SUV, which is almost certain to be the GV90, given that a trademark has been filed for this very name.

CarBuzz

It has not been confirmed what the platform and powertrain of this flagship luxury SUV will have underneath it. While we have just been talking about interest in EVs in this country on the wane and Genesis delaying full electrification as a result, it is still entirely possible that the GV90 will be fully electric. It could either be based on the same platform as the Kia EV9 and Hyundai Ioniq 9, but it is more likely that it will be based on an all-new platform coming, dubbed eM.

Genesis GV60 Magma
------------------

CarBuzz

A concept for the GV60 Magma was shown to the world last year, and based on what we can glean from our spy shots of the wrapped prototype as they ready it for production, it isn’t too far away from what it will actually look like. We can see from images of both that it will have flared and vented wheel arches, door cameras instead of door mirrors, and even a spoiler just below the rear window. It looks as though it is targeting fast SUVs like the Ford Mustang Mach-E as well as Mercedes-AMG.

CarBuzz/Valnet

Of course, like with the standard Genesis GV60, the GV60 Magma will share its underpinnings with fellow Hyundai Motor Group Products: the Kia EV6 and Hyundai Ioniq 5 N. Being a Genesis model, it is bound to add an extra dose of luxury and be more expensive, so it will need to be more powerful than the already potent Ioniq 5 N to justify the extra cost. We understand it could have over 700hp…

### Will there be a Genesis G70 in 2026?

While it looks like you will still be able to purchase the Genesis G70 in 2026, all signs point to it not being long for this world, as it has already been around for the thick end of ten years and has not received any major updates since 2022. As you can see from all the new Genesis SUVs coming, the increasing focus is on SUVs and EVs that are set to be the big money spinners going forward. This means there sadly isn’t really room for a fun V6-engined compact luxury sedan anymore. We still think you should get one why you still can, though.

### Who makes Genesis cars in 2025?

Genesis is part of the Hyundai Motor Group, meaning it is owned by Hyundai along with Kia. This means there is a lot of platform sharing going on, like with the Genesis GV60, Kia EV6, and the Hyundai Ioniq 5. Kia’s upcoming Tasman pickup truck is also going to happen partly thanks to the platform in development for the hybrid Genesis GV80. So, in short, Genesis is its own brand on the more luxurious end of things than Kia or Hyundai, but sharing platforms and powertrains makes things more financially viable.
