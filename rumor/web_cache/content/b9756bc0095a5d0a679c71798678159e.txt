Sichuan issued a blue warning of mountain torrents: mountain torrents may occur in seven counties and cities
============================================================================================================

__ __ __

_Communication_ __ Aug 28, 2021 11:46 AM (GMT+8) _· EqualOcean_

Cailian news agency, August 28 - at 8:00 on August 28, Sichuan Provincial Department of water resources and Sichuan Meteorological Bureau jointly issued a blue warning of mountain torrents. From 8:00 on August 28 to 8:00 on August 29, mountain torrents may occur in Tongchuan, Dachuan, Xuanhan, Qu county, Dazhu, Wanyuan, Kaijiang and other counties (cities and districts). Sichuan Provincial Department of water resources requires: immediately notify the responsible persons in towns, villages and mountain flood disaster risk areas, and all responsible persons must be in place immediately; Pay close attention to weather forecast, rainfall water level and early warning of dangerous areas; Strengthen the patrol inspection of mountain torrent disaster risk areas; In accordance with the working procedures for mountain torrent disaster monitoring and early warning in Sichuan Province, strengthen monitoring and early warning, timely start the plan, timely and decisively transfer the threatened people, and strengthen the safety supervision of the transfer route and resettlement area.
