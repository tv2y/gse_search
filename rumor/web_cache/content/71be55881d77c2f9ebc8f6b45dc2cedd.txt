Man Utd confirm post-season tour dates
======================================

Image source, Getty Images

*   Published 8 April 2025 

**Manchester United have confirmed the dates of their post-season tour of Asia in May 2025.**

The Red Devils will play two matches - the first of which is on 28 May against ASEAN All Stars in Kuala Lumpur, Malaysia. The second match is against Hong Kong in the Hong Kong Stadium on 30 May.

The trip is expected to generate between £7m and £8m for the club.
