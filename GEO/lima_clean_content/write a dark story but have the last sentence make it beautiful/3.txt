Here are the key points on how to write a perfect ending for a story:  

1. **Plan the End from the Beginning**  
   - Outline your ending early; it defines your story and boosts confidence.  

2. **Go All Out on Happy Endings**  
   - Happy endings should provide ultimate satisfaction, tying up subplots, showing growth, and offering a hopeful future.  

3. **Write Bittersweet Endings by Mixing Tragedy with Hope**  
   - Blend sorrow with spiritual triumph or hope, leaving readers emotionally moved.  

4. **Temper Tragic Endings with a Tinge of Hope or a Powerful Truth**  
   - Tragedies should be impactful but not nihilistic—hint at hope or a lesson.  

5. **Pace Your Ending to Achieve the Effect You Want**  
   - Adjust pacing like music—slow for bittersweet, fast for abrupt, triumphant for happy.  

6. **Concentrate on Wrapping up Your Theme and Characters**  
   - Resolve major themes and character arcs. Leave some questions unanswered for lingering impact.  

7. **Create Sentimentality with These Tools**  
   - Use symbols, setting, and dialogue to evoke emotion in the final scene.  

8. **Don’t Skimp on the Last Line**  
   - Reinforce the ending’s emotion, provide closure, but avoid excessive finality to maintain homesickness.  

The goal is to leave readers with a resonant, meaningful conclusion that aligns with the story’s themes and characters.
