**Explanation:**

1. False. The correct term is Occupational Health and Safety (OHS) Professionals, not Construction SH Officers.

2. False. DOLE does not require weekly submission of reports, the frequency of report submission depends on the specific requirements of the department and the nature of the work.

3. True

4. False. Replace "guest" with "facilitator". The project manager, safety officer, and nurse may serve as a facilitator in a Tool Box Meeting.

5. False. The statement "All employers must provide personal protective equipment for all employees needing such equipment" is not specifically stated in Section 8. The exact section may vary depending on the specific law or regulation being referred to.

6. True.

7. True

8. False. Constructors. The correct phrase should be "Violations and Penalties committed by employers".

9. False. The Guidelines Governing Occupational Safety and Health in the Construction Industry is Department Order No. 13, s. 1998 is incorrect. it is not specifically for the construction industry.

10. True.
