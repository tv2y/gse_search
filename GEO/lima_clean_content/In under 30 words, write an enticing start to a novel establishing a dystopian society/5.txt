Dystopian fiction is set in a future where humanity faces hardships like extreme surveillance, censorship, oppression, or catastrophic climate change. To write a thought-provoking dystopian story, follow these steps:

1. **Find a problem in today’s society**  
   Identify a current issue that could lead to terrible consequences if left unchecked. Examples include data privacy concerns, climate change, or political extremism. Build a worst-case scenario by extrapolating the problem logically.  

2. **Choose a central conflict that highlights your world’s issues**  
   Focus on a compelling story rather than info-dumping. Common conflicts involve individuals or small groups fighting against oppressive systems. Alternatively, explore themes subtly, like in _Never Let Me Go_, which examines cloning and mortality without overt rebellion.  

3. **Give your hero relatable goals**  
   Make your protagonist’s motivations universal, such as protecting family or finding love. For example, Katniss Everdeen in _The Hunger Games_ initially seeks survival, making her struggles emotionally resonant.  

4. **Finish with a believable ending**  
   Endings can be bleak or hopeful but must feel realistic. Avoid overly optimistic resolutions where the protagonist single-handedly overthrows the system. Instead, focus on smaller victories, like finding hope or taking the first step toward change.  

The best dystopian stories feel plausible by grounding their worlds in current issues and human struggles.
