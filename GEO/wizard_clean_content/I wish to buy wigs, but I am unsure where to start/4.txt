**How to Buy a Wig**  

Buying a wig doesn't have to be overwhelming. Whether due to medical conditions like cancer, alopecia, or chemotherapy treatments, or simply for a new look, this guide will help you choose the right wig.  

### **Pro Tips for First-Time Buyers**  
- **Plan ahead** to regain control and reduce stress.  
- **Cut a hair swatch** for color matching if you anticipate hair loss.  
- **Benefits of wigs**: Simpler morning routine, savings on styling, no "bad hair days," and limitless style options.  

### **Step 1: Find Your Head Size**  
Measure your head circumference around your hairline to determine size (Petite, Average, or Large). Use a flexible tape measure and check the guidelines provided. Adjustable straps ensure a customized fit.  

### **Step 2: Select Your Hair Type**  
- **Human Hair Wigs**: Offer styling versatility but require maintenance; expensive and high upkeep.  
- **Synthetic Wigs**: Budget-friendly, retain style, and appear natural. Avoid heat exposure.  
- **Heat-Resistant Synthetic Wigs**: Can be styled with low-heat tools; lifespan ~3 months if worn daily.  

### **Step 3: Choose Your Cap Type**  
- **Classic Wigs**: Affordable, stationary part, natural look.  
- **Monofilament Wigs**: Individually placed hairs for realistic scalp appearance; versatile styling.  
- **Hand-Tied Caps**: Most natural and comfortable; full cap construction.  
- **Lace Front Wigs**: Natural hairline; allows pulled-back styles.  

### **Step 4: Select Your Style**  
- Browse short, chin-length, medium, or long styles.  
- Start with a style similar to your natural hair for an easier transition.  
- Detailed length measurements are provided for matching.  

### **Step 5: Select Your Color**  
- Match your natural color using a saved hair swatch.  
- Lighter shades often flatter chemotherapy-paled skin tones.  
- Consult the **Color Guide** or request expert assistance for matching.  

### **Should You Buy More Than One Wig?**  
- Benefits: Rotate wear to extend lifespan, maintain style variety, and simplify routines.  
- Wait to confirm the first wig suits you before purchasing a backup.  

### **Insurance & Purchasing Tips**  
- Insurance may cover a **"cranial prosthesis"** (medical wig). Get a doctor’s prescription and submit claims post-purchase.  
- **Buy from trusted retailers**:  
  - Check for expertise, authorized sales, authentic products, and customer support.  
  - Prioritize secure, privacy-protected transactions.  
  - Avoid counterfeit or low-quality wigs (minimum $80 for natural-looking styles).  

**Final Note**: Wigs empower confidence without compromise. Follow this guide to make an informed, comfortable choice. Reach out to professionals for personalized advice.
