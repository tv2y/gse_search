**Poem: Social Media Friends - By Brenda**

Brenda writes a timely poem on friends and peers we find on social media. As Brenda rightly tells us, social media friends are a blessing in disguise, especially on those days when we have no one in the physical world to share our sorrows.  

A beautiful poem, Brenda! Social media friends are there to encourage and support, blessings in disguise.  

It really amazes me that I can connect with others who live so far away. Parts of technology are definitely worth it.  

Good poem, Brenda, I agree that social media and online friends are a blessing. I have a lot more friends online than I do in real life, and I value them all.  

So many think that social media friends are fake, but I've come to meet a lot of nice people.  

Yes, online friends can really be a blessing. I treasure each one of my online/social media friends. They have become part of my life, and I love to interact with them. They provide emotional support as well when we need it the most.  

Online friends seem to genuinely care. I often wish many of you lived next door, but then we might not be as close.  

When COVID-19 hit us, we all used our phones to communicate. Our HubPages family has a lot of great people.  

These friendships are valuable. I can think of many people I have had the pleasure of meeting online that would be fun to meet in person someday from many places worldwide.
