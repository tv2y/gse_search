**Snowfall whitens the capital city of east China's Anhui, turning the city park into wonderland.**

HEFEI, Feb. 17 (Xinhua) -- Affected by the cold front swept across the central and eastern regions of the country, temperatures fell heavily in central and eastern China, of which some areas are seen a decrease of up to 16 degrees.

A heavy snow hit Hefei, capital city of east China's Anhui Province on Feb. 16.
