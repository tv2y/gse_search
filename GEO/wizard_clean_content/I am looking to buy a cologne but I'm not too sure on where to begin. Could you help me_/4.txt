**Original Post:**  
**New to Fragrance--How Do I Start?**  
Hi, everyone! I'm new to the world of fragrance and want to try everything. I received a sample of Juliette Has A Gun's "Pear Inc." and fell in love with it. I like gourmand, fruity, citrusy, and some floral scents, but perfume is expensive, and I’m a student on a budget. Where do I start? Any recommendations for a total newbie?  

**Replies:**  
1. **@pocketvenus**:  
   - Explore fragrance on a budget by testing at beauty counters or getting free samples (Sephora, department stores).  
   - Purchase samples/decants online.  
   - Smell broadly, including classics in different genres, not just your preferred notes.  
   - Recommended articles: Basenotes’ "Top Ten Designer/Niche Fragrances Every Beginner Should Sample."  
   - Test fragrances multiple times before investing in full bottles.  

2. **@SegerCat**:  
   - Fragrances have base types (wood, floral, fruit, citrus); identify your favorite.  
   - Gourmand = food-inspired scents (vanilla, cocoa, etc.). Test on your skin for at least an hour before deciding.  

3. **@mafan**:  
   - Use Sephora’s fragrance finder or try sampler sets (e.g., "Sephora Favorites New at Sephora Perfume Sampler Set" or Juliette Has A Gun’s discovery set).  

4. **@SportyGirly125**:  
   - Test fragrances at beauty counters or try Sephora sampler sets (e.g., Atelier Cologne’s discovery sets).  

--- 

**Key Takeaways for Beginners:**  
- Start with free samples or inexpensive decants.  
- Test on your skin (scents evolve with body heat).  
- Explore sampler sets to try multiple fragrances affordably.  
- Identify preferred scent families (gourmand, floral, etc.) but stay open to classics.  
- Research and take your time before buying full bottles.
