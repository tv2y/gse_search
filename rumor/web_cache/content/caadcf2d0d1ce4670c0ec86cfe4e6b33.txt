'The Atlantic' Endorses a Pres­i­dential Candidate for the 3rd Time in History
==============================================================================

BY CNN Nationwide

PUBLISHED 7:13 PM ET Oct. 05, 2016

SHARE

NATIOWIDE -- For just the 3rd time in the magazine's 160-year history, _The Atlantic_ is endorsing a presidential candidate.

_The Atlantic's_ editors called on it’s readers this week to vote for Democratic nominee, Hillary Clinton, for president, calling it an "act in defense of American democracy.”

The magazine called her Republican rival, Donald Trump, a "sexist, a know-nothing and a liar."

_The Atlantic's_ national correspondent says no single issue influenced the magazine's decision, but rather it was the result of Trump's overall record, character and temperament.

The magazine previously endorsed Lyndon Johnson in 1964 and Abraham Lincoln back in 1860.
