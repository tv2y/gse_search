A Guide to Selecting Your Ideal Makeup Mirror  

Key factors to consider when choosing your ideal makeup mirror:  

1. **Budget**  
   - Determine your budget upfront, as makeup mirrors range from affordable basic models to high-end, feature-packed ones.  

2. **Placement**  
   - Decide between wall-mounted or tabletop mirrors based on space, lighting, and daily routine needs.  

3. **Styles and Designs**  
   - Choose from various styles (modern, vintage) and frame materials (metal, plastic, wood). Consider shape (round, square, oval) and additional features like foldable stands or adjustable angles.  

4. **Lighting and Magnification**  
   - Prioritize mirrors with natural-looking LED or fluorescent bulbs for accurate makeup application. Magnification levels (2X to 10X+) help with precise detailing.  

5. **Additional Features**  
   - Extra features include built-in storage, adjustable brightness, touch controls, and Bluetooth connectivity for added convenience.  

By evaluating these factors, you can select a makeup mirror that enhances your beauty routine.
