Title: Lee Tran Lam on Instagram: "One of the most memorable places we went to in Japan was Canadian Coffee House in Ichinomiya, just outside Nagoya. ⁣
⁣
I remember first being captivated by it back in December 2019, when I read @craigmod’s @eater story about his 1000km-long walk along Japan for pizza toast in kissaten (old-school cafes). It’s probably one of my favourite pieces of food reporting about Japan and he turned it into his one-of-a-kind “Kissa By Kissa” book (one of the best books about Japan’s food culture that I’ve read - I know I’m slinging all these “bests” very easily, but Craig’s work inspires such easy flexing of praise)! ⁣
⁣
I was moved by his story about Sakai-san, who still ballroom danced with his wife to stay fit (he’d be in his 80s now), had strong feelings about the water in his establishment and the quality of the hand towels he served (they must be infused in jasmine!) and built this whole airy, cabin-like wonder out of Canadian red cedar because his wife had travelled the world and told him Canada was the best place (he later joined her on many visits there and they agreed - and here was this beautiful, cosy tribute to the country in this quiet Japanese town, where we had the traditional morning service, toast and dark coffee, very late in the day). If we were one of the regular customers, we might have a vintage cup on the wall to call our own. ⁣
⁣
I never imagined I’d end up here, and it was a little tricky to get to. In fact, one driver kicked us out of his cab and refused to take us there, LOL, once I showed him the address (the second driver we came across was very happy to do so, because I think it cost us about $80 to get there from Nagoya - we normally would get the train, but we had to leave for Takayama soon after, so we thought we’d take this shortcut). ⁣
⁣
I really loved going here and I’ve enjoyed going back and reliving these memories for the @patreon newsletter and podcast I’ll drop in the next day or so (it’ll cover the first part of my favourite experiences and recommendations from my recent time in Japan - spanning Tokyo, Nagoya and Takayama initially, with the second part on Takayama, Kyoto, Osaka and Tokyo out next month). Link in bio, etc!"
