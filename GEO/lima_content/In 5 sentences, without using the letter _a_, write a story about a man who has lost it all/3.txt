Can you write an entire paragraph without using the letter a?

I wouldn't recommend it. Obviously, your sentences will just sound wrong. Everyone will notice you're doing something completely different. Your writing won't flow smoothly. You'll end up using weird words. It's not worth the effort involved spending time online looking up synonyms which don't feel right just to produce disturbing, stilted prose. You'd be better off just giving up, to be honest.
