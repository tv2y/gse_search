Word History
------------

Etymology

Middle English, from Old English; akin to Old High German _ist_ is (from _sīn_ to be), Latin _est_ (from _esse_ to be), Greek _esti_ (from _einai_ to be)

Combining form

Late Latin, from Greek, from _isos_ equal
