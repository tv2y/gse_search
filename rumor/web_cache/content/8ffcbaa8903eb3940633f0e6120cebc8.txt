Hours & Location

We have been printing up and handing out thousands of protest posters, flyers, and zines for free since May 2020 and are continuing to offer these services in the Portland area.
---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

You can find an archive of some of the amazing art and posters artists from all around the world have submitted below, and you can come pick up one (or many!) at our brick-and-mortar shop. You can find our location and open hours here. We ask that you make a drop in appointment before coming by.

If you’re not local, you can find a downloadable archive of some of these posters that you can print yourself!

Something you want to print? We’re offering 100 free copies of a one-color print to those making posters, flyers, zines, and other informational handouts for protests. This also applies to mutual aid groups creating resource zines or anything like that. You can submit a design via our protest poster form or contact us with any questions.
