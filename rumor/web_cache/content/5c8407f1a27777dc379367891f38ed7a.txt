 I don't know this player, and I certainly do not know your own kid, but if you are scratching your head and a little bit jealous that this kid was recruited and not yours, here are some possible reasons. D1 coaches' jobs depend on recruiting effectively and you can assume that they have good reason for every decision they make. Size is a big factor, especially in the Big 10 and ACC, so that is part of your answer right there. In addition to playing ability, grades matter a lot (especially at an elite academic school like Hopkins) and the player's character. and college coaches watch club games differently than you do. They will focus on things like off ball movement, that you might not notice. And the cliche is true that coaches recruit not just the player, but the whole family. If a parent is the jealous type who dumps on a kid that just committed to a great school, that is not a parent that a coach wants in his program.
