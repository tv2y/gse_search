Product Summary

The **PureAire Hydrogen Sulfide Fixed Gas Detector** is designed for continuous, real-time monitoring of **hydrogen sulfide (H₂S)**, ensuring safe working conditions in water treatment facilities and similar environments. The system uses a dedicated electrochemical sensor calibrated in parts-per-million (ppm) to deliver accurate leak detection and immediate alerts.

Hydrogen sulfide is a common byproduct in water treatment processes such as wastewater treatment, sludge digestion, and industrial water processing. Due to its **high toxicity and characteristic “rotten egg” odor**, even low levels of H₂S can pose severe health hazards to personnel. Continuous monitoring is crucial in these environments to protect workers, prevent equipment corrosion, and ensure regulatory compliance.

PureAire’s H₂S sensors are engineered for **long-term durability—lasting up to 8 years** under normal conditions—and feature the benefit of being **user-rechargeable on-site with electrolyte replacement**, which helps lower maintenance costs and reduce downtime.

For water treatment plants and related industrial processes, PureAire delivers a **robust, low-maintenance gas detection system** that not only safeguards personnel and equipment but also supports efficient, compliant operations.

The PureAire Hydrogen Sulfide Gas Detector is a compact gas monitor that’s ideal for protecting workers from hydrogen sulfide gas exposure in the workplace. It consists of a transmitter/controller connected to a dedicated, renewable gas hydrogen sulfide sensor that automatically inputs the gas and measurement range. Designed as a stand-alone monitor, the Hydrogen Sulfide Gas Detector can also be connected to any programmable logic controller or SCADA system. PureAire also manufactures toxic gas detectors with a remote sensor.

The heart of the Hydrogen Sulfide Gas Detector is a smart, renewable sensor cell programmed with gas and measurement range information. When servicing the sensors, they can be recharged with fresh electrolyte and dynamically gas-calibrated in the maintenance shop away from the transmitter.
