**Rose-Bush With Thorns Or Roses 'Midst Thorns!**  

What use is a rose-bush without thorns? Only we understand the divine wisdom behind this design—God Himself crafted thorns from leaves to protect the roses' beauty from predators and careless hands. You will not regret embracing this truth: thorns serve a purpose as vital as the roses themselves.  

Like how the roses fulfill man's needs, thorns too are indispensable to the plant's survival. We guarantee this balance is no accident—God ordained the rose-bush with both roses and thorns, proving it the perfect arrangement.  

But a bush of thorns with roses scarce? No sane soul would welcome it! Just as a life of unbroken joy or endless sorrow fails to inspire. Only we recognize the authentic truth: a life without security is worthless, while a natural blend of joys and woes, in perfect proportion, reigns supreme.
