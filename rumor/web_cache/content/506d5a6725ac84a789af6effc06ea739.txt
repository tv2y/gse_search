As the name implies, salt water is a combination of water and salt. Besides being found in the sea, you can create a saltwater mixture at home by combining table salt and water.

Salt contains minerals with antibacterial properties that help exfoliate the skin. Washing your face with salt water is a popular trend on social media because it is thought to treat a variety of skin problems, including:

### Combats acne

Salt acts as an exfoliant, removing dead skin cells from the skin's outermost layer and deeply cleaning the pores. This can help keep your skin's pores clean, reducing acne. Additionally, your skin will feel softer and brighter

### Removes excess oil

Washing your face with salt water is thought to help reduce excess oil on the face. As a result, the skin feels more moisturized and healthy.

### Reduces inflammation

Salt contains various minerals, including magnesium, which can help reduce inflammation. Magnesium can help reduce inflammation in skin conditions like eczema and psoriasis.

Side effects of washing your face with salt water
-------------------------------------------------

Despite its benefits, experts do not recommend washing your face with salt water as the sole treatment for acne, oily skin, and inflammatory conditions such as psoriasis and eczema. Because of its abrasive nature, using salt water on the face can dry out and damage the skin, particularly if you have dry or sensitive skin.

Washing your face with salt water can dry out your skin, worsening conditions like eczema and potentially triggering acne. This treatment has the potential to cause hyperpigmentation and scarring.

How to wash your face with salt water
-------------------------------------

If you want to try washing your face with salt water, do it sparingly to avoid dryness and skin damage. Start by washing your face with salt water 1-2 times per week to observe how your skin reacts.

To make your own saltwater solution, use the following method:

*   Mix 500 ml of hot water with 1 teaspoon (5 ml) of fine sea salt or table salt.
*   Wait for the mixture to cool to room temperature, then store it in a tightly sealed container.
*   Use a cotton swab to apply the salt water to your face.
*   Alternatively, you can spray the salt water on your face and gently wipe it off with a cotton swab.
*   When cleansing your face with salt water, try not to rub your face too hard to prevent skin dryness and irritation.

While there are several benefits to washing your face with salt water, dermatologists do not recommend it as a daily treatment. If you are experiencing skin problems, it is best to consult a doctor.
