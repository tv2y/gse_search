### 2025 Silicon Valley Poll: Local outlook improves sharply

**October 6, 2025 -** The results are in, and the mood is improving. Almost half of residents (47%) say the Bay Area is on the right track — up from 29% in 2023. Fewer are planning to leave, and most say quality of life has improved, though affordability issues remain.

However, findings from the annual poll, published by Joint Venture Silicon Valley and Bay Area News Group, point to rising anxiety: 73% say the nation is off course, and even a slim majority of Democrats accept at least some criticism of California’s approach.

### What’s at stake: The cost of new H-1B fees

**October 3, 2025 -** Silicon Valley could soon feel the pinch of new federal policy imposing a $100,000 fee on every new H-1B visa application. For the region’s largest tech employers, that adds up fast, coming to an estimated $766 million in fees alone.

The broader economic impact could be steep: Modeling shows potential losses of 1,060 jobs, with local and state and tax revenues falling by $12 million and $28 million. These projections spotlight the economic stakes of H-1B policy — and the critical role that global talent plays in sustaining Silicon Valley’s innovation-driven economy.

Read more: Silicon Valley’s exposure to H-1B policy

### How federal research cuts are impacting Silicon Valley

**September 24, 2025 -** Federal research funding cuts are delivering an economic blow to higher education, resulting in a $21.5 million loss in support between March and September 2025.

The disruption threatens scientific progress and weakens the region’s innovation ecosystem — where federal funds have long supported discovery, talent development, and economic growth.

The ripple effects are expected to reduce total regional economic output by $32.2 million and eliminate more than 70 jobs, not only in research but also across support and service sectors.

### The Silicon Valley Index: Telling the Valley’s story

The _2025 Silicon Valley Index_ shows $69B in venture capital and a 0.1% job decline — signs of an innovation engine shifting gears. Per capita income hit $157,000 but was offset by inflation, while median home prices reached $1.92 million, affordable to just 26% of first-time buyers.

The _Silicon Valley Index_ offers fresh, up-to-date, neutral, nonpartisan data, telling the story of a complex region where 3 million people live and work.

### The Silicon Valley Poll: Taking the Valley’s pulse

If the Silicon Valley Index is the region’s annual checkup, the Silicon Valley Poll is its therapy session — revealing not just what’s happening, but how people feel about it.

Carried out each August by the Silicon Valley Institute for Regional Studies in partnership with Bay Area News Group, the Poll captures public sentiment from a representative sample of Bay Area adults across five counties. Results are weighted for accuracy, with a modeled margin of error of 2.6%. Widely covered and closely watched, the Poll offers essential insight, informing decision-making and guiding the region’s future.

### Silicon Valley Indicators

Stay informed and dive deeper into Silicon Valley’s story at www.siliconvalleyindicators.org. This dynamic, interactive website offers more than 150 unique datasets across five key categories: People, Economy, Society, Place, and Governance.

Discover trends across a wide range of topics—including venture capital, patents, housing, climate, and more. Powered by the Silicon Valley Institute for Regional Studies, the website utilizes advanced methodologies to provide deeper insight and inform decision-making, while linking users back to local, regional, and national sources.

What is Joint Venture Silicon Valley?
-------------------------------------

Joint Venture Silicon Valley is a think tank created and supported by the major sectors (business, government, labor and academia). Through the Institute for Regional Studies, the organization provides research assessing the Valley's challenges and opportunities; Joint Venture is also a platform where leaders develop shared priorities and take collaborative action.
