10 killed, 14 injured after bus overturns in Shanghai
-----------------------------------------------------

14 September 2011 23:35 (UTC +04:00)

 Baku-APA. Ten people were confirmed dead and 14 others were injured after a bus overturned Wednesday night in Shanghai, according to the municipal government’s information office, APA
*   Magnitude 6 earthquake strikes east coast of Honshu, Japan, GFZ says
*   Erdogan declares US visit 'very successful' following Trump meeting
*   Number of journalists killed in Israeli attacks on Gaza reached 254

 reports quoting Xinhua.

 The accident occurred at around 8:54 p.m. when the bus with 24 people on board turned over near the Longdong Avenue in Pudong District. Seven people were killed at the scene while three died later in the hospital, the office said.

 The injured passengers were rescued by police, firefighters and medics and were rushed to two local hospitals.

 The office said the bus is a shuttle bus of Inventec (Shanghai) Corporation. The victims are believed to be the company’s employees.

 Investigation into the accident is underway.
