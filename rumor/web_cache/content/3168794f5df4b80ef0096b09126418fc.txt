**Quan Hongchan’s family politely refuses money and house offers #全红婵家人婉拒他人送钱送房#**

On July 31, in the women’s synchronized 10m platform diving event at the Paris Olympics, Quan Hongchan and her partner Chen Yuxi won the championship. According to _Jiupai News_, Quan Hongchan’s brother stated in an interview that some well-known local entrepreneurs offered money and houses to their family, but they declined their offers. “We feel more at ease spending the money we earn with our own hands,” he said.

**The loneliest Olympic champion #最孤独的奥运冠军#**

The loneliest Olympic champion, who, after winning, had no one to celebrate with and had to complete a lap around the field with no national flag, only her bare hands. And this champion was Wang Liping. Wang Liping once said that the greatest regret was not finding a single Five-star Red Flag after the competition. No one expected Wang Liping to win, but she actually turned out to be the most impressive in race walk.

In 1995 Asian Championships, Wang Liping won the silver medal in the 10km race walk. In 2000 Olympic Games, Wang Liping won the gold medal in the 20km race walk.

**What will happen to the person who damaged Wang Chuqin’s racket? #踩坏王楚钦球拍者将面临什么#**

On July 31, Chinese table tennis player Wang Chuqin had his racket damaged by a photographer. Although Wang Chuqin stated that the broken racket was not the reason for his loss in the next match, his unexpected elimination after changing the racket sparked heated discussions. Reporters recalled the scene when Wang Chuqin’s racket was damaged: many photographers squeezed into the coach’s area to take photos, even though the coach’s area was not accessible to reporters. After the awards ceremony, the on-site photography manager recorded the IDs and vest numbers of the reporters present and collected circulating online videos as evidence. An investigation is underway. Chinese wonder what consequences the person who damaged Wang Chuqin’s racket will face.
