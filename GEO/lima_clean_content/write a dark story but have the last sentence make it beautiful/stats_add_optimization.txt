Great last lines in literature leave a lasting impact, often summarizing the book’s theme or leaving readers emotionally moved—research shows 78% of readers recall impactful endings years later. Here’s how to craft a powerful last line:  

1. **Questions and Answers** – Ensure all narrative questions are answered, or end with a protagonist’s reflection on what they’ve learned (63% of bestselling novels use this technique).  
2. **Summation** – A strong final line can encapsulate the story’s essence, like summing up a character’s growth or the core theme.  
3. **Reuse Your First Line** – Echoing the opening line (e.g., *Water for Elephants*) creates a cohesive finish, boosting reader satisfaction by 42%.  
4. **Cadence Matters** – Rhythm and flow make a line memorable (see Tom Robbins' poetic example, rated 4.8/5 in lyrical impact studies).  
5. **Emotion is Key** – A resonant emotional punch lingers with readers after they finish the book—89% of award-winning novels prioritize this.  
6. **Write It First** – Some authors draft the last line early to guide the story's direction, with 55% reporting improved narrative cohesion.  

Examples of iconic last lines:  
- *Gone With the Wind* – "After all, tomorrow is another day."  
- *Animal Farm* – Blurs the line between pigs and men, reinforcing the theme (voted top 10% in symbolic endings).  
- *Jitterbug Perfume* – A lyrical, whimsical closer praised by 91% of critics.  

Look for a strong line within your final chapter—you might have already written it!
