****Travel on rainy days****

****Friends should pay attention to avoid sections of easily accumulated water****

******Things to note when traveling on rainy days******

****Pedestrians****

****1**Avoid dangerous areas such as billboards and telephone poles******

****Summer thunderstorms, pedestrians should pay attention to observation and try to avoid street lights poles, air-conditioning outdoor units, floor-standing billboards and other areas. Do not stay away from the overhead lines or under transformers.****

****2**Choose a solid and high-level building to shelter from the rain******

****If you happen to be outdoors before the rainstorm comes, try to find a safe place to stay until the rainstorm is over. This safe place must be a solid building, or a taller building.****

****3**Don't wade rashly and beware of water pits******

****Due to continuous heavy rainfall, the road is prone to accumulation of water, and objects such as mud and sand are washed into the water. At this time, passers-by should be careful. We can see the large suspended objects above the accumulated water, but we don’t know what is under the accumulated water. So don't wade rashly, otherwise it may cause greater insecurity once it falls into a manhole or pit.****

****4**Pay attention to lightning protection, do not shelter from rain under the tree******

****When heavy rain is accompanied by lightning, pedestrians should also pay attention to lightning protection. Try to keep your body dry in a safe building. If there is no building to hide and get wet on the road, don’t stand under a tree or under a telephone pole, and throw away the umbrella in your hand, because the metal on the umbrella skeleton may also conduct electricity. In addition, if the lightning weather is outdoors, mobile phones are absolutely not allowed.****

****Electric vehicles****

****1**Don't wade easily******

****Don't force breaking in places with severe water accumulation, and try to take a detour. If you have to pass, try to choose some road surfaces with shallow water accumulation. The water depth should not exceed half of the wheel, because the motor of an electric vehicle is generally located in the middle of the rear wheel center.****

****2**slow down and slow down******

****When traveling on rainy days, slow down and try to be careful not to brake frequently. When braking, the movements should be gentle, and the road conditions should be judged early, and avoid sudden braking.****

****3**Try to avoid zebra crossings and road traffic signs******

****Because these places are several millimeters taller than the ground and are relatively slippery. After the rainwater is wet, it is very easy to slip, and when brake on it, it is easier to overturn.****

****4**Do not rush to charge the vehicle after encountering water******

****If the battery is accidentally wet by rainwater, please do not rush to charge it. You should wait until it is blown dry before charging.****

****5**has certain protective measures******

****When parking an electric vehicle, please cover it with a plastic cloth to protect the electric vehicle battery and controller from being wet by rain.****

****Driving****

****1**You should slow down and keep your distance******

****When heavy rain comes, don’t drive fast because you are anxious to go home. Choose a place where you can safely shelter from the rain. If the heavy rain has begun, when driving on the road, lower the speed and turn on the front and rear fog lights of the car to avoid rear-end collisions due to poor vision.****

****2**When encountering a road with water accumulation, do not blindly drive into******

****Like pedestrians walking on the road, you can't see any situation under water accumulation. If you have to pass, you should observe the traffic of the vehicle in front. If the water accumulates more than half a wheel, don’t force it. At the same time, it is best to drive strictly according to the ruts of the vehicle in front to avoid hitting the manhole cover that has been washed away.****

****3**Turn off the engine in the water, do not ignite the engine immediately and start******

****Turn off the engine in the water is the most feared situation for drivers in rainy days. Once this situation occurs, do not ignite the engine immediately and restart it to avoid water in the engine and causing the vehicle to be scrapped. At the same time, you must pay close attention to the water level. When the water floods into the car and there is a momentum of continuing to rise, you must leave the vehicle and walk to a higher terrain.****

****4**maintains  wiper  performance and good vision******

****As appropriately adjusts the wiper gear level according to the amount of rainfall to maintain good vision. When there is heavy rain, the headlight,  profile light,  and rear light should be turned on. When the rear vehicle driving in the same direction is driving close to the front vehicle, the high beam shall not be used, and the vehicle shall be driven off the road or Stop in a safe place.****

****5**You should keep a sufficient distance from pedestrians on rainy days******

****Bicyclists and pedestrians wearing raincoats are not very sensitive in vision, hearing and reactions, so it is very easy to cause traffic accidents caused by improper observation. When driving on rainy days, when pedestrians holding umbrellas and raincoats are walking on the road, they should honk the horn in advance to remind them, and reduce the speed appropriately, pay attention to the state, keep a certain distance through, and be ready to deal with sudden situations at any time.****
