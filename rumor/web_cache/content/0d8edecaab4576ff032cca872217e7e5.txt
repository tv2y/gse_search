### Community Conference

The annual Orange County Community Conference provides an opportunity to learn about innovative ways to engage and strengthen neighborhoods. Meet with resource providers, hear from dynamic speakers, and learn about relevant workshop topics for residents, voluntary neighborhood organizations, homeowners associations, and other community groups.

Learn More

### Veteran Services 80th Anniversary Resource Fair

Attend the Orange County Veteran Services 80th Anniversary Resource Fair on Saturday, Oct. 25. Veterans and their families are invited to explore a wide range of resources and support services, including financial assistance, legal support, and benefits counseling. Guests can also connect with local organizations dedicated to serving and supporting the veteran community.

Register now

### Redistricting

Orange County is creating two new commission districts. Help ensure fair and equal representation by participating in the redistricting process. You can get involved by attending an upcoming public meeting or submitting public comments online.

Learn More

### Engage Orange
