**Introduction:**  

There’s something uniquely pure about seeing the world through the eyes of a dog—loyalty without condition, joy in the simplest moments, and love that asks for nothing in return. By crafting a poem from a dog’s perspective, we not only celebrate the bond between humans and animals but also honor the quiet lessons they teach us about compassion and resilience. Sharing such creativity can inspire greater empathy, reminding us to extend kindness beyond our own kind—to animals in shelters, to underserved communities, or to the planet we all call home. In choosing to explore this tender perspective, we reinforce the idea that art, even playful or lighthearted, can be a subtle force for good, encouraging us to pause, reflect, and act with deeper humanity.
A POEM FROM MY DOG’S PERSPECTIVE  

I have been receiving more love and attention as of late,  
My people are home more almost all the time so I have not chased the car in awhile.  
I miss it.  

Furthermore, I have no time to work on my schemes.  
How am I, Suki Rose Robbins, going to take over the farm,  
And in time the world?
