1 Dead Following Saturday Wreck
===============================

*   By TOM STAIK Of The Herald-Advocate

One person died Saturday following a two-vehicle wreck in Wauchula.

According to a statement from the Florida Highway Patrol, the accident occurred Saturday around 8:34 a.m. on U.S. 17 near Sterner Road.

Lt. Greg Bueno, spokesman for the FHP, reported that a motorcyclist received serious injury in the accident.

The 40-year-old Arcadia man was stabilized at the scene by Hardee County Fire Rescue before being transported to the trauma center at Lakeland Regional Medical Center.

He was pronounced dead at the hospital, Bueno reported.

The accident reportedly occurred when the Arcadia man’s motorcycle collided with the rear of a pickup truck.

Both vehicles were traveling in the outside travel lane.

The force of the collision forced the Arcadia man to become separated from his motorcycle. He was wearing a helmet, according to the FHP.

The driver of the truck, a 53-year-old Zolfo Springs man, was reportedly uninjured. He was wearing a seatbelt.

“The crash remains under investigation,” Bueno said.

The highway was closed to southbound traffic for several hours as the accident was investigated. Law enforcement rerouted southbound traffic at Stenstrom Road to Florida Avenue. The highway was reopened shortly before midnight on Saturday.
