Smithsonian Homepage

Welcome
-------

The Smithsonian Institution is the world's largest museum, education, and research complex. Explore, learn, and be inspired—wherever you are!

Engage with the Smithsonian’s digital collections and research spanning the arts, sciences, and humanities.Start your journey

Plan Your Visit
---------------

View guides for the Smithsonian's**17 free Washington, D.C.–area museums and zoo**—plus two museums in NYC. Admission is **free** at all locations except Cooper Hewitt in NYC. Find out **what's on**!
