Title: Right-wing media are falsely claiming FEMA spent their resources on migrants instead of helping Hurricane Helene victims

URL Source: https://www.mediamatters.org/fox-news/right-wing-media-are-falsely-claiming-fema-spent-their-resources-migrants-instead-helping

Markdown Content:
In the wake of Hurricane Helene’s devastation, right-wing media are following Republican presidential nominee Donald Trump in baselessly accusing the Federal Emergency Management Agency of taking funds intended for the victims of Helene and instead giving it to migrants.

Conservative figures have also alleged that President Joe Biden and Vice President Kamala Harris are “prioritizing the needs of noncitizens” and now “they have nothing left for Hurricane Helene victims.”

In reality, FEMA provides money to migrants through the Shelter and Services Program, which is not linked to the agency’s disaster-related “funding streams” but is instead paid for via the Department of Homeland Security.

*   FEMA’s budget includes billions of dollars for disaster relief and maintains separate funding for migrants with its Shelter and Services Program
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

*   
    *   **At a campaign rally on October 3, Trump alleged that the Biden-Harris administration “stole the FEMA money just like they stole it from a bank so they could give it to their illegal immigrants who they want to have vote for them.”** He also accused Harris of spending “all of her FEMA money—billions of dollars—on housing for illegal migrants.” [The Daily Beast, 10/3/24]
    *   **A Homeland Security spokesperson debunked the accusation that FEMA spent its money on migrants, saying, “These claims are completely false.”** They continued, “The Shelter and Services Program (SSP) is a completely separate, appropriated grant program that was authorized and funded by Congress and is not associated in any way with FEMA's disaster-related authorities or funding streams.” On April 12, DHS released $640 million for the SSP to “help offset the costs of providing food, shelter and other supportive services after receiving an influx of migrants.” [Newsweek, 10/3/24; National Association of Counties, 4/18/24]
    *   **The Daily Beast noted, “The funds allocated to handle asylum seekers also constitute approximately 2 percent of FEMA’s annual budget, which is typically measured in the billions.”**The agency’s website also fact-checked this “rumor,” saying: “FEMA has enough money right now for immediate response and recovery needs. If you were affected by Helene, do not hesitate to apply for disaster assistance as there is a variety of help available for different needs.” [The Daily Beast, 10/3/24; FEMA.gov, 10/3/24]
    *   **The Republican-led House, which currently controls FEMA’s budget and spending, has recently voted to cut funding from the agency.**A week before the hurricane, Politico’s E&E News noted that “as the House and Senate’s top four leaders met last weekend to negotiate a deal to keep the government funded, they were forced to acquiesce to the demands of Congress’ most conservative fiscal hawks, whose votes were thought to be pivotal for passage. They quietly stripped the CR of almost all supplemental funding, including for FEMA, according to multiple House appropriators.” [The Daily Beast, 10/3/24; E&E News, 9/26/24]
    *   **In 2019, Trump used some of FEMA’s budget to respond to migrants at the border.** Trump's administration redirected$271 million in disaster relief funding for migrant detention centers and to process asylum claims. [Rolling Stone, 10/3/24]
