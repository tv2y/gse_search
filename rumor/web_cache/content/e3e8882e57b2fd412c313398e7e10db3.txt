During all of my LBI residencies, the mark of a bad storm was measured against that of the March 1962 storm (I was a 7 year old child living in PA who's family summer trips were to Chesepeake Bay). That today's nor'easters are set against Sandy is downright scary. While I'm very empathetic with folks currently riding it out or are trapped off-island with deep concerns for their properties, I don't in anyway miss being any part of any of it.

Take care, everybody.
