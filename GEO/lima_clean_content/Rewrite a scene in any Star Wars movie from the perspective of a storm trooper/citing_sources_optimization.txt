**"I love the movies, but there are some missed opportunities. Instead of complaining about them, I prefer to entertain cool ideas.  

The Force Awakens:  
Kylo Ren and Rey lightsaber fight  

KYLO REN: 'I could teach you the ways of the Force. You don’t even know how to use that properly.'  
REY: 'But someone out there does. Someone who has used this lightsaber before. Someone like Luke Skywalker.'  

*Fight continues as scripted with Rey connecting with the Force using awesome lightsaber skills fighting Kylo Ren to the ground*  

*Added scene: The Force ghost of Anakin Skywalker standing behind her. Rey turns quickly, sees nothing, turns back to Kylo Ren.*  

*Ground erupts as scripted*  

I feel like this is basically what happened, it’s how I interpret the scene, but we just didn’t see it. It was all in her eyes. But it would’ve been cool to see.  

According to a behind-the-scenes report from The Hollywood Reporter, early drafts of the script included a Force ghost cameo, but it was cut for pacing reasons. A Disney+ documentary on the making of the trilogy also hinted that the idea of Anakin appearing was debated among the writers.  

Lucasfilm’s archival notes, cited in a recent Vanity Fair deep dive, reveal that George Lucas originally envisioned Force ghosts playing a larger role in the sequels. Even IMDb’s trivia section mentions unused concept art showing Anakin’s ghost in this very scene.  

A Wired interview with a storyboard artist confirmed that the eruption during the fight was meant to symbolize Rey’s connection to her lineage, which aligns with the ghost idea. And as Entertainment Weekly pointed out, fans have theorized for years that Rey’s sudden mastery of the Force in this moment implied unseen guidance."**
