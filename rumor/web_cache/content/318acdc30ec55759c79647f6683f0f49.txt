Massachusetts: Suspect Allegedly Left IEDs on the Boston Common, One Arrested
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------

_Submitted by NL0387 on Fri, 08/29/2025 - 15:51_

On 23 July 2025, a suspect allegedly left two improvised explosive devices (IEDs) on the Boston Common. The complaint reportedly stated the homemade IEDs appeared to be made from toilet paper roll cores with attached fuses, and X-ray examinations found they likely contained black powder. The suspect was arrested on 7 August 2025 and charged with vandalism and destruction of property, vandalizing a war or veterans memorial, and possession, transportation or use of a hoax device or substance.

New Jersey: Suspect Detonated Explosive at a Residence, One Arrested
--------------------------------------------------------------------

_Submitted by NL0387 on Fri, 08/29/2025 - 15:48_

On 26 July 2025, at approximately 0242 local time, an explosive device detonated in the vicinity of a silver Ford Explorer owned by a victim and parked in the victim’s driveway located in Delran, New Jersey. The explosion caused a debris field extending out approximately 100 feet in diameter, with various nails and bolts lodged in both the body of the Ford Explorer and in adjacent residential homes. Following the explosion, law enforcement recovered one damaged 9-volt battery, two damaged antenna parts, and one damaged fire pyrotechnic device.

Pennsylvania: Victim Operated Improvised Explosive Device (VOIED) Functions Under Lawnmower
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

_Submitted by NL0387 on Fri, 08/29/2025 - 15:41_

On 20 July 2025, a VOIED was placed under a lawnmower and functioned when the victim attempted to move it. The investigation stated the victim filled his mower with fuel, started it, and let it idle for approximately 10 seconds before moving the handles forward to move the mower, which caused the VOIED to function. The victim was able to get off the mower and exit his garage. The investigation identified the victim's previous tenant as the suspect. An arrest warrant has been issued for the 54-year-old man they believe allegedly emplaced the device.

California: Old Pipe Bomb Discovered in Storm Drain
---------------------------------------------------

_Submitted by NL0387 on Fri, 08/29/2025 - 15:29_

On 22 July 2025 at approximately 1015 hours local time, a Public Works Department crew discovered what they believed to be an explosive device in a storm drain. The Alameda County Sheriff’s Office Explosive Ordnance Disposal Unit confirmed the item discovered in the drainpipe was a pipe bomb. The contents of the pipe bomb were deteriorated to the point the item no longer posed a threat to the public. The EOD team performed a render safe procedure on the device to ensure safety for transport and handling.
