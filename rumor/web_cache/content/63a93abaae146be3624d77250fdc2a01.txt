Sponsorship The Economist 

Fieldwork YouGov 

Interviewing Dates July 23 - 24, 2016 

Target population U.S. citizens, aged 18 and over. 

Sampling method Respondents were selected from YouGov’s opt-in Internet panel us-ing sample matching. A random sample (stratified by age, gender, race, education, and region) was selected from the 2010 American Community Study. Voter registration was imputed from the Novem-ber 2010 Current Population Survey Registration and Voting Supple-ment. Religion, political interest, minor party identification, and non-placement on an ideology scale, were imputed from the 2008 Pew Religion in American Life Survey. 

Weighting The sample was weighted using propensity scores based on age, gender, race, education, news interest, voter registration, and non-placement on an ideology scale. The weights range from 0.1 to 6.03 ,with a mean of one and a standard deviation of 1.2 .

Number of respondents 1300 

Margin of error ± 4.2 % (adjusted for weighting) 

Margin of error (Registered Voters) ± 4.4 % (adjusted for weighting)
