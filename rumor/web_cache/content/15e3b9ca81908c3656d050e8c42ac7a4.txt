## Culture 2000 – Implementation 2004 89 Annual cooperation projects supported in the field of Cultural Heritage 

## Couleurs de la chrétienté Country : BELGIUM GR-PL 

## Project Dates : 1/07/2004 - 30/06/2005 

## Description : This project will see the restoration of a mosaic by a group of specialists as well as a stylistic and historical analysis of the Byzantine Church in Syria. There will be a comparative study of similar Byzantine mosaics in Europe and the East. At the end of the restoration campaign there will be a travelling exhibition in Europe and Syria. 

## Objectives : This project aims to restore and conserve an important part of Byzantine heritage. This will also lead to an exchange of best practices and experiences between the participating countries. During the restoration phase young Syrian professionals and European students will be trained 'in situ'. Through the communication of the results of the project the general public will also gain access to this information.

## 1) Leaving Europe for America - early EMIgrants LEtter stories (short title EMILE) Country : SWEDEN CZ-IE-IT-PL 

## Project Details : 5/09/2004 - 4/09/2005 

## Description : Through 5 touring exhibitions, a joint virtual exhibition in English, an exhibition catalogue, conferences, operative partnership meetings and exchange visits, the EMILE project will shed light on the European immaterial heritage. Using letters, photos, songs and told memories the project will focus on the period 1840-1920 when many people were forced to leave their countries. This will be linked to global emigration/ immigration also acknowledging the role of women. The project will turn this research into insight and understanding for ordinary people. Objectives : The EMILE project will promote cultural dialogue and mutual knowledge, create the widest possible citizen access to the material, disseminate know- how and promote mobility, promote good practices, address young people and develop a platform for European network collaboration.

## 2) Making Memories Matter Country : UNITED KINGDOM (UK) DE-ES-FI 

## Project Dates : 10/09/2004 - 9/09/2005 

## Description : The project involves the creation of Memory Boxes by older people which will present a collective image of European reminiscence. The boxes will be designed as exhibits to travel across Europe. Photos, videos, computerised archives and theatre productions will also feature in the exhibition. There will also be national and local events organised by each partner. 

## Objectives : Through the exhibition the project will promote mutual understanding and appreciation to the viewers of all ages and nationalities, building bridges between the generations.The older people will experience appreciation of their lives both as individuals and as witnesses of a wider historical change. For younger generations the exhibitions will be an attractive way to gain access to their cultural heritage within a broader historical and geographical context.

## 5) Bibliotheca Sonans Country : POLAND AT-CZ-PL

## 8) European Literature in Heritage in Context II Country : AUSTRIA ES-UK 

## Project Details : 1/09/2004 - 31/08/2005 

## Description : The purpose of the project is to extend the dynamic WWW-cluster financed by Culture 2000 in 2002 in order to address and to inform the public about European literary tendencies in the period 1880-1920. 250 authors will be discussed on the basis of their literary, cultural and socio-historical significance for this period in European history and for their mutual relationships. 

## Objectives : The main objective is to further conserve, preserve and disclose cultural information about the literature of the English, Spanish, Czech, Dutch, German, Hungarian, Polish, Slovakian and Slovenian languages.
