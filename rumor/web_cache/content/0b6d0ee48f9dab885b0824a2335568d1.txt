From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

Since assuming office in May 2019, Volodymyr Zelenskyy has made numerous international trips in his capacity as the president of Ukraine.

Zelenskyy has made international trips to 47 countries as president of Ukraine. The number of visits per country where Zelenskyy travelled are:

*   **One**: Argentina, Azerbaijan, Bulgaria, Cape Verde, Croatia, Georgia, Greece, Hungary, Iceland, Israel, Montenegro, Oman, the Philippines, Portugal, Romania, Singapore and South Africa
*   **Two**: Albania, Austria, the Czech Republic, Estonia, Finland, Ireland, Japan, Latvia, Moldova, Norway, Qatar, Slovakia, Spain, Sweden and the United Arab Emirates
*   **Three**: Canada and the Netherlands
*   **Four**: Denmark, Saudi Arabia and Switzerland
*   **Six**: Lithuania and the Vatican City
*   **Eight**: Turkey
*   **Nine**: Belgium, Italy and the United Kingdom
*   **Ten**: Poland
*   **Eleven**: The United States
*   **Twelve**: France and Germany
