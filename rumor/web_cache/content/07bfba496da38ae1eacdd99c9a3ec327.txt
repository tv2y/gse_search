Title: MOE issues Action Plan on Revitalizing County-Level High Schools

URL Source: http://en.moe.gov.cn/news/press_releases/202510/t20251011_1416371.html

Markdown Content:
The Ministry of Education (MOE) issued the Action Plan on Revitalizing County-Level High Schools (“Action Plan”), in conjunction with the National Development and Reform Commission, Ministry of Finance, Ministry of Human Resources and Social Security, Ministry of Natural Resources, and Ministry of Housing and Urban-Rural Development.

The Action Plan outlines comprehensive measures to achieve high-quality development of county-level high schools.

The document sets forth five major tasks for local authorities: 1) expand public educational resources; 2) enhance pedagogical reform and teacher capacity-building to ensure holistic student development; 3) increase financial support for weak county-level high schools; 4) crack down on violations of educational equity; and 5) enhance coordination between regular and vocational secondary education.

The document lays out eight specific actions:

1) Encourage existing regular high schools to increase their capacity and establish new schools to meet foreseeable expansion of the school-age population;

2) Improve school infrastructure, including classrooms, laboratories, libraries, playgrounds and stadiums, and student dormitories;

3) Raise educational quality by enhancing the course selection system, promoting research-based and cross-disciplinary instruction, encouraging a focus on the student learning process, and establishing an advisory mechanism for provincial- and municipal-level pedagogical researchers to offer regular support to county-level schools;

4) Improve the teacher workforce by addressing the shortage of teachers in some subjects, encouraging excellent teachers from urban areas to teach in county-level high schools, and providing more support to county-level teachers in terms of training programs, promotion, and rewards;

5) Facilitate digital education by building smart classrooms, encouraging the use of national and provincial smart education platforms, and strengthening teachers’ digital literacy;

6) Deepen institutional reform in areas such as resource allocation, financial support, educational evaluation, and diversified admissions;

7) Regulate school management by imposing restrictions on teaching activities outside normal school time (e.g., holidays or weekends) and preventing high schools in provincial capitals from pre-enrolling top students from counties; and

8) Implement various targeted support programs and encourage universities to develop special admission plans for students from county-level schools.

The Action Plan also emphasizes the need to increase coordination between educational authorities at the provincial, municipal, and county levels, as well as between competent authorities in the education and other sectors. In addition, supervision over the implementation of the Action Plan should be strengthened by focusing on healthy student development, school compliance with relevant standards, and the guarantee of per-student public funding.
