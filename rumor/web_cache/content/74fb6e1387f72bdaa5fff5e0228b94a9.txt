The Shichahai Ice and Snow Carnival officially launched this Saturday at the Shichahai Ice Rink, drawing in local residents and tourists eager to savor the joys of winter activities. The venue is permeated with a strong festive atmosphere in anticipation of the upcoming Year of the Horse.

Visitors can enjoy a wide range of equipment and facilities, including single and double ice sleds and ice bikes. Here, people can not only immerse themselves in the lively pre-Spring Festival ambience but also experience an authentic taste of old Beijing's cultural essence.
