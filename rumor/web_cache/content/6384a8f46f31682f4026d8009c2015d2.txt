Travellers arrested at Jakarta airport
======================================

By Travel Weekly Asia

|

Feb 23, 2024

|

The four men hailed from Syria, Sudan, and Iraq. Photo Credit: Adobe Stock/mzayyad

Four men from Syria, Sudan, and Iraq have been arrested by Indonesian authorities after producing counterfeit travel documents at Soekarno-Hatta International Airport’s immigration.

One of the detainees, attempting to fly from Indonesia to Amsterdam, was caught with a fake United Arab Emirates passport. Another, from Sudan, tried to enter Indonesia with a forged visit visa.

The remaining two, Syrian nationals, were apprehended with fake Bulgarian passports and e-Visas on Arrival in Indonesia, despite having legitimate Syrian passports and visas.

The individuals now face prosecution under Indonesian immigration law.
