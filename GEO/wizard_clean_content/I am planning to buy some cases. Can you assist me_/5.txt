### Main Tips for Choosing a PC Case:

1. **Budget vs. Quality Theories**:
   - **Cheap Case**: Saves money for critical components (GPU, RAM, SSD).
   - **Quality Case**: Acts as a long-term "home" for your system, with better reusability, airflow, noise reduction, and features like water cooling support.  
   - Recommended brands: Phanteks (top choice), Cooler Master, Corsair.

2. **Key Considerations**:
   - **Component Fit**: Match case size to your motherboard (ATX, micro-ATX, mini-ITX) and ensure GPU/CPU cooler clearance.
   - **Cooling**: Prioritize airflow; dust filters are essential. Invest in quality fans (e.g., Noctua Industrial for longevity).
   - **Drive Bays**: Modern cases may lack HDD bays; verify storage needs.
   - **PSU Placement**: Bottom-mounted PSU improves airflow.
   - **Aesthetics**: Choose tempered glass over acrylic (durability) and avoid obstructive LED designs if unwanted.

3. **Design Preferences**:
   - **Size/Form**: Full tower (large), mid-tower (popular balance), or compact (portability).
   - **Portability**: Lightweight cases or those with handles if frequently moved.
   - **Functionality**: Tool-less designs, cable management, and front-facing USB ports (avoid top-mounted dust traps).

4. **Additional Tips**:
   - Avoid "no-name" Chinese cases for poor build quality.
   - Check GPU length and CPU cooler height compatibility.
   - Use tools like PCPartPicker to verify component fit.  
   - Prioritize sober designs if aesthetics aren’t a priority.

**Final Note**: Balance airflow, component compatibility, and future upgradability over flashy features. A well-chosen case can outlast multiple builds.
