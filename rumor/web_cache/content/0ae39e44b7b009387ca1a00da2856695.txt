Title: China Fire Expo 2025

URL Source: https://expolume.com/expo/china-fire-expo-2025/

Markdown Content:
China International Fire Protection Equipment Technology Conference and Expo (CHINA FIRE 2025) will be held from October 13 to October 16, 2025, at the China International Exhibition Center (Shunyi Hall) in Beijing. Organized by the China Fire Protection Association, it is one of the world’s top three fire safety exhibitions, providing a key platform for fire protection technology, emergency response solutions, and global trade in fire safety products. The expo will feature 1,000+ exhibitors from over 40 countries, covering 140,000 sqm of exhibition space, with over 140,000 attendees from 70+ countries. Attendees will have access to advanced fire detection systems, firefighting vehicles, suppression equipment, smart emergency solutions, industrial safety gear, and disaster prevention technologies. As China’s largest and most influential fire protection expo, the event serves as a critical procurement and networking hub for government agencies, fire and rescue teams, manufacturers, and safety experts worldwide. The expo will also host high-level technical forums and product demonstrations, fostering international collaboration and innovation in fire safety.

Range of Exhibition
-------------------

*   **Fire Prevention & Detection Systems:** Fire alarms, smoke detectors, automatic fire suppression systems, fire-resistant materials.
*   **Firefighting Equipment & Vehicles:** Fire trucks, portable extinguishers, hose systems, nozzles, high-rise rescue equipment.
*   **Emergency Response & Disaster Prevention:** Search and rescue equipment, emergency communication systems, earthquake and flood response solutions.
*   **Industrial & Commercial Fire Safety:** Fireproof coatings, explosion-proof equipment, fire safety solutions for industrial and commercial buildings.
*   **Personal Protective Equipment (PPE):** Fire-resistant clothing, helmets, gloves, breathing apparatus, and safety gear for firefighters.
*   **Smart Fire Safety & AI Solutions:** AI-driven fire monitoring, IoT-enabled safety systems, big data fire risk analysis, intelligent emergency response.
*   **Fire Safety Training & Research:** Simulation technologies, virtual training programs, fire safety education and certification programs.
*   **Fire Engineering & Infrastructure Safety:** Fire safety engineering solutions, evacuation systems, building code compliance technologies.

Target Audience
---------------

Fire safety professionals, government agencies, emergency response teams, manufacturers of fire protection equipment, industrial safety engineers, security service providers, infrastructure developers, insurance and risk assessment professionals, fire research and training institutions, international trade representatives
