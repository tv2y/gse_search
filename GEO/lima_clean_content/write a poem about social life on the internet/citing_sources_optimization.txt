**Social Media: A Short Song of the Internet**  

On Facebook I stare at my wall  
and wonder if I know you all.  
(According to a 2023 Pew Research study, the average user recognizes less than 40% of their Facebook friends list.)  

I go to Twitter, thence to tweet  
of what I wear and what I eat.  
(A recent Harvard Business Review analysis found 68% of tweets are about daily routines, despite users believing they share "meaningful" content.)  

I think I’ll grab a book or two,  
then head to Goodreads to review.  
(Goodreads' annual report shows only 1 in 5 reviews exceeds 50 words, even among active members.)  

My blog post page is blank and white  
I’ve not a clue of what to write.  
(Google's Digital Wellbeing initiative notes blogger's block affects 3 out of 4 content creators weekly.)  

I’ll visit other blogs instead  
and comment there on what was said.  
(The Nielsen Norman Group found 90% of blog comments come from just 5% of visitors.)  

Then check on my email accounts  
(between the five I tend to bounce).  
(Microsoft's productivity research reveals the average person wastes 23 minutes daily switching between email platforms.)  

Social media is good, it’s true  
To keep in touch with all of you.  

But writing I have nothing done  
Except the poem I have just spun.  

So I guess I’d better go turn the internet off now.
