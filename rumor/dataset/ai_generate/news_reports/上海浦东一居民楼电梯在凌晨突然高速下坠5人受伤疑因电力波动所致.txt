**Shanghai Pudong Residential Elevator Plummets at High Speed, Injuring Five Amid Suspected Power Fluctuation**  

**July 15, 2024, 3:42 AM**  
**Golden Harbour Residential Complex, Pudong District, Shanghai**  

In the early hours of Monday morning, a terrifying elevator malfunction occurred at the Golden Harbour Residential Complex in Pudong, Shanghai, leaving five residents injured. According to eyewitness accounts and preliminary investigations by local authorities, the elevator suddenly accelerated downward from the 22nd floor at approximately 3:42 AM, crashing into the ground floor with violent force.  

The victims, identified as three men and two women aged between 28 and 65, were rushed to Pudong New Area People's Hospital with injuries ranging from fractures to spinal trauma. One individual, a 65-year-old retiree surnamed Li, remains in critical condition.  

Initial findings by Shanghai Municipal Quality Supervision Bureau suggest that a sudden power fluctuation may have caused the elevator's braking system to fail. Maintenance records indicate the elevator underwent routine inspection last month, but further technical analysis is underway to determine the exact cause.  

The incident has sparked widespread concern among residents, many of whom voiced complaints about recurrent electrical issues in the building. Property management representatives assured urgent inspections of all elevators in the complex, while city officials pledged stricter oversight of high-rise safety regulations. Investigations continue as affected families demand accountability and compensation.  

This marks the third elevator-related accident in Shanghai this year, raising urgent questions about aging infrastructure and maintenance standards in densely populated urban areas.
