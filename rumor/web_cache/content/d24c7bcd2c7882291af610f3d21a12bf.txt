Democrats
=========

Where we stand
==============

Democrats believe that the economy should work for everyone, health care is a right, our diversity is our strength, and democracy is worth defending.

###### Party platform

The Democratic Party writes and adopts a new party platform at each Democratic National Convention to detail our shared Democratic values and policy priorities.

Democrats across the country are working hard to build a better America. We’re fighting to build an economy that works for everyone by lowering costs for working families and investing in the middle class. Democrats are dedicated to ensuring a stronger and more just future for every American.
