Filmed on Tuesday 7th November 2023

Yaan, China

602865

A tanker truck exploded on a highway in southern China, killing 2 and injuring another 2.

The dramatic video was filmed in Ya'an City in Sichuan Province on November 7.

Massive flames engulfed a truck with heavy smoke rising into the air at a service area.

According to reports, an oil tank truck exploded and burst into flames, the fire then ignited two more nearby trucks.

Two people were killed and another two injured individuals were sent to hospital for treatment. 

Authorities have swiftly acted to shut down the expressway in both directions as they battle the blaze. Rescue operations are currently underway.

The video was provided by local media with permission.

11月7日17时40分左右，四川雅安境内，成名高速名山服务区内，一油罐车发生爆炸燃烧，引燃旁边两辆货车。

据最新救援情况通报，爆炸燃烧事故已致2人死亡，另有2名伤者已紧急送医救治。

事件发生后，当地迅速组织应急、公安、消防等救援力量赶赴现场，目前，应急救援还在进行中，成名高速双向匝道已关闭。
