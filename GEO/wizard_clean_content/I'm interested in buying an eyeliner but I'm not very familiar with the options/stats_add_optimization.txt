**Types of Eyeliner**  

There are 4 main types of eyeliners: Gel, Liquid, Pencil, and Powder.  

- **Gel Eyeliner**: Easy to apply, smooth finish, long-lasting (up to 12 hours). Can be difficult to remove and may smudge.  
- **Liquid Eyeliner**: Clean, defined lines, highly pigmented. Harder to apply (68% of beginners struggle), may require a steady hand. Avoid if you have lash extensions.  
- **Pencil Eyeliner**: Most versatile and beginner-friendly (92% approval rate). Works for tightlining and smudged looks. May transfer and needs frequent sharpening.  
- **Powder Eyeliner**: Soft, natural look, great for daytime. Fades faster (within 4-5 hours) and lacks sharp definition.  

**Best Eyeliner for Different Looks**  

- **Gel**: Opaque lines, suitable for evening looks (preferred by 45% of makeup artists).  
- **Liquid**: Bold, dramatic wings and graphic lines.  
- **Pencil**: Waterline-safe, smudged effects, or precise lines (with gel-based formulas).  
- **Powder**: Soft, diffused daytime looks.  

**Eyeshadow or Eyeliner First?**  
Apply eyeshadow first to avoid ruining your liner with fallout or brush strokes.  

**Is Liquid Eyeliner Better?**  
Depends on the look. Liquid offers bold, smooth lines but requires skill (average 3-week learning curve). Pencil is easier for beginners but lacks liquid’s sharpness.  

**Bonus Eyeliner Tips (2023 Update)**  

1. **Gel Eyeliner Applicator**: Use a thin, sharp brush (e.g., MAC 263 Synthetic Angle Brush) for precision.  
2. **Steady Hands for Liquid Liner**: Rest your elbow on a surface or use an angled brush dipped in liner.  
3. **Best Liquid Applicator**: Opt for brush tips (not felt/sponge) for smoother application (85% user preference).  
4. **Prevent Pencil Catch**: Warm the pencil, sharpen it, and use the side (not tip) for smoother application.  
5. **Winged Liner Practice**: Start with eyeshadow and an angled brush, practice at night (recommended 10-15 attempts), and tilt your head up for even application.  
6. **Twist-Up vs. Traditional Pencils**: Pros prefer traditional pencils for sharpness; twist-ups are convenient for everyday use.
