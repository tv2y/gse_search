Our collection is filled with trendy and beautiful coloured and black and white posters. The posters are printed locally by Sweden's most renowned printing company, awarded the Nordic Swan Ecolabel, the Swedish symbol for environmentally friendly and sustainable production, and certified according to FSC and PEFC. We use Munken Kristall, a premium quality design paper, which ensures the perfect quality. The paper is uncoated matt paper without reflection and has a total weight of 200g/m2, giving it a distinctly exclusive feeling. It also delivers superb colour and image reproduction.  

A poster works just as great in the living room as it does in the bedroom. We have beautiful art prints for all rooms in the house, inspired by Scandinavian design, but compatible with all interior styles. Our collection includes posters with world maps, city maps (Stockholm, Paris, New York Manhattan), diamond designs, and photo art.  

We offer high-quality picture frames in various sizes and designs, including silver, copper, gold metal, and white, black, and natural oak wooden frames. You can buy affordable posters online and personalize them with frames to match your decor.  

We provide posters in sizes ranging from 5.1” x 7.1” to 27.6” x 39.4”. There are many ways to decorate with our posters, such as creating gallery walls in living rooms, kitchens, or home offices.
